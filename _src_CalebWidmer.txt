'''Caleb Widmer''', referred to as a '''Responder corpse''' in-game, was a paramedic with the [[Responders]].

==Background==
A former [[Morgantown]] Emergency Services paramedic, Widmer joined the Responders after the [[Great War]]. He and several others, including [[Tanner (Fallout 76)|Tanner]] and [[Janson]], barricaded themselves in the second floor break room of the [[Morgantown Airport terminal]] after it was overrun by the [[Scorched]]. All having sustained injuries, Widmer and the others chose to commit suicide to prevent themselves from becoming Scorched and hurting other people.<ref>[[Responder's last words]]</ref>
