'''Warning to Vincent''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes#Burning Springs|paper note]] {{in|FO76BS}}.

==Location==
The note can be found in [[Albany]], on a kitchen table on the first floor of the southeastern-most house.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
Hey Vincent,

I wanted to give you a heads up about a suspicious character that passed through here the other day. He was a sleazy bald pig of a man named [[Keith Cooper]]. Initially he was charming, but when he saw I wasn't falling for his tricks, the mask came off. I know how gullible you can be Vin, and this asshole fancies himself quite the "Opportunist". 

Apparently he was in [[Appalachia|West Virginia]] the last few years but wasn't able to gain the ground he wanted with some group and had to fake his death. He must have heard about the way things work over here, and now believes he can climb his way to the top and build a new empire. I don't want you interacting with him and getting roped into some shit again. Honestly I should have done the world a favor and just shot the guy. 

Good news is it sounds like West Virginia is resettling and there's pockets of good people. The [[Scorched Plague|scorch plague]] is still around, but less of an issue, so I'm heading out. Once I'm established, I'll come back for you and Lucy.

Stay out of trouble,
-Dan
}}
