'''Prickett's Fort curator''' is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] operating at [[Prickett's Fort]] in [[Appalachia]]. 

==Background==
The curator is in charge of running the Civil War reenactments at Prickett's Fort and encourages anyone who happens to pass by to partake in the activity.<ref>[[The Battle that Never Was (Versus)]]</ref> It is responsible for why the all the Mister Handies involved in the reenactment have been given real guns,<ref>[[Prickett's Fort curator/Dialogue|Curator's dialogue]]. Line 11: ''"You'll be pleased to know that I've given them all real guns for extra realism. It's a nice touch, don't you think?"''</ref> and will express its disappointment if it results in insufficient bloodshed.<ref>Curator: ''"Well that was a dud of a battle. No blood. No machine parts. No fun."''<br />([[Prickett's Fort curator/Dialogue|Curator's dialogue]])</ref>

It has also been programmed to advertise [[Nuka-Cola (Fallout 76)|Nuka-Cola]] to guests who partake in the reenactment.<ref>Curator: ''"Civil war re-enactments can be thirsty work. Why not get an ice cold Nuka-cola?''"<br />([[Prickett's Fort curator/Dialogue|Curator's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Curator: ''"Speaking of the real thing, Nuka-cola is a real drink for people who are really thirsty.''"<br />([[Prickett's Fort curator/Dialogue|Curator's dialogue]])</ref>
