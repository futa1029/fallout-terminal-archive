概要

Vault 76 is a ロケーション . これは first ロケーション discovered upon starting a new game and completing the prologue.
One of Vault-Tec's many Vaults across America, Vault 76 was constructed north of フラットウッズ, ウエストバージニア, in 森林地帯 before 大戦 and was designated as the "Official Vault of the Tricentennial" by the corporation, under the tagline "Vault-Tec Salutes America."

Construction

Vault 76 was one of 17 control Vaults, part of the overarching Vault experiment. Construction began in February 2065 and ended in October 2069. The maximum capacity was stated to be 500 occupants, and construction blueprints record a capacity of 88. The Vault was slated to open to 外観 world 25 years after 核 war so that the individuals within could emerge to recolonize アパラチア. This was so that Vault-Tec could study their recolonization efforts in comparison to the experiments within other Vaults. The day that the Vault reopened to 外観 would be known as Reclamation Day (distinct from a different version of Reclamation Day used internally by Vault-Tec, which was intended to occur at a much later time after 核 war).

The overseer was instructed to ignore standard operating procedures, as Vault 76 was deemed a special project essential to rebuilding the United States in the wake of 核 war. The occupants were some of America's finest, including students from Vault-Tec University, military members, and a former White House Chief of Staff. Debut

Vault 76 debuted on July 4, 2076, seven years after its construction ended, in honor of the United States' Tercentenary/Tricentennial of its founding in 1776. An opening ceremony, which was broadcasted on television, was held at 外観 and its overseer attended. However, in order to become the overseer, she had to break off her engagement with her fiancé, Evan, or be transferred to another Vault to have him join her in it.

Great War

After 大戦 broke out, Vault 76 was successfully sealed on October 23, 2077, when the air raid sirens sounded for the last time as Chinese nukes headed for American soil. The Vault-Tec security team ensured compliance and defended the individuals as they entered the Vault, authorized to use lethal force to ensure only those authorized would enter. Everyone else was considered an enemy combatant. They succeeded in their mission, and the Vault door was sealed, to be opened only 25 years later, once the all-clear was given after outside 放射線 levels had subsided to a safe threshold.

This was only the beginning of the project. The occupants were a diverse group selected from every walk of life, and apart from the Vault staff, all were highly accomplished before the war and possessed competitive personalities. As such, Vault-Tec expected the Vault dwellers to present unique challenges in maintaining order. As a last resort, the overseer was granted the ability to lock down each domicile and imprison dissenters for any duration deemed appropriate, with the note to account for the fact that enforced solitary confinement could lead to psychological issues. Furthermore, the security team was provided with less-than-lethal devices to ensure compliance. Firearms and other munitions were stowed after the amended entry period.

Ongoing operations

For the next 25 years, Vault 76 continued to operate as planned. The overseer found order easy to maintain, though the impressive pedigree of those the Vault sheltered and their competence led to the aforementioned unique challenges, most often challenging her authority. To provide them with an outlet for their competitiveness, the overseer instituted a number of awards for the Vault dwellers to obtain, as award ceremonies were a great way to placate even the most hardened egos (altercations aside; one lost a tooth fighting over the Best Dental Hygiene award). The high point of the program was the Vault 76 World Cup, even if it nearly broke the jukebox. The overseer credited the success to the "exemplary" service of her "loyal and dependable" senior staff.
Meanwhile, outside of Vault 76, the survivors of 大戦 were aware of the Vault's existence and wondered why it was still closed years later. In particular, the レスポンダー wanted to know if they could open the Vault somehow. The Vault's sealing led to the perception from outsiders that the residents of Vault 76 are cushy and privileged, while the rest of the world adjusted to ウエイストランド life.
However, Vault 76 also had another objective: the Vault's overseer was ordered to find and secure three 核 silos all on her own after its opening. If these sites were still 核-capable, she was to ensure no one except Vault-Tec could access or launch 核 ordnance. Any other authorities, government, militia or otherwise, were to be ignored, to ensure that Vault-Tec alone controlled these silos and could deny their use to any third parties, even if it meant going against any surviving United States Armed Forces personnel that would assert control of the silos themselves as part of the U.S. Department of Defense. However, Vault-Tec may have known that the task was impossible for her to accomplish on her own, especially since they purposefully picked someone young and inexperienced for an overseer.
As the Vault neared the end of its duration and approached Reclamation Day, the situation started to slowly deteriorate. In 2100, the Vault was over capacity, with hydroponics struggling to keep up with the demand and mandatory rationing instituted by the overseer. Six individuals had to be placed under disciplinary lockdown (up from four at the end of the previous year), with the morale officer tasked with pacifying rumors about アパラチア around the clock. Nobody wanted to emerge into アパラチア to fight cannibal mutants and brave burning rain.
Regardless, tensions were running high, Furthermore, the "Ressies," as the security team called them, started hacking into ターミナルs to learn more about the Reclamation and the Activation Notices. Although the security team made a token effort to curtail unauthorized access, they all knew trying to keep geniuses out of the files was a lost cause.

Preparations

As a result of these mounting concerns, 2102 and Reclamation Day was met with relief from all parties; some were so relieved they even broke into the stores to enjoy it early. The overseer mandated a Reclamation Day party the day before the Activation Notice, opening up restricted alcohol stashes and the remaining food supplies. The happiness and cheering in the atrium as the overseer counted down to midnight were second only to the time when the first Vault child was born. The party continued well into the night when the overseer called lights out and had security help stragglers into their beds. Then, she and her team went through the final checklists. Standees and supplies were placed along the exit route to the Vault door to provide a last refresher course for the Ressies, while the security team went through their duties with record speed: Wiping logs, securing non-essential gear and acquiring Reclamation Packages for themselves.
The Vault 76 dwellers ultimately had no other choice, as the Vault itself was claimed to be designed to cease function and be rendered inhospitable 24 hours after opening, a feature intended to force those within to leave. Studies showed that dependency on the Vault's amenities was common, so the killswitch was established and set to kick in after the All-Clear message was received after the aforementioned delay. Air circulation would shut down last, giving everyone enough time to leave the Vault, but rendering it hazardous for habitation. Only robotic stewards would remain to preserve the remains, while the former inhabitants focused on rebuilding America.

Reclamation Day

leaving Vault 76]]
Vault 76 successfully opened on October 23, 2102, exactly 25 years after 大戦, in a much-celebrated event known as Reclamation Day. The team feared that the Ressies could go off the rails, breaking the respectable record of 25 years without casualties. Although some argued for arming everyone before they leave the Vault, using the surplus stocks, the overseer denied the request. The dwellers received the standard survival packages and were told to run fast and far away from Vault 76 to complete their mission.

Last holdouts

Even after Reclamation Day and the claimed shutdown looming over Vault 76, a few of the dwellers still refused to leave the Vault. The Vault's claimed shutdown one day after its opening evidently did not occur, enabling these last holdout dwellers to continue living inside the Vault for months and even years, persisting off of the Vault's remaining supplies. The Mister Handy attendants kept to their duty of maintaining the Vault and manning the survival preparation stations for dwellers on their way out of the Vault; over time, they also grew tired of their roles, but could not stop with dwellers still in the Vault.
The Mister Handies' duties did not extend to cleaning up the Vault's atrium, which was still littered with the trash and decorations from the party for Reclamation Day, or individual dwellers' apartments. The overseer's prerecorded message announcing the arrival of Reclamation Day also continuously played on loop, set to repeat once every morning, though the system was starting to break down after months of repetitions. Even with these inconveniences, the last dwellers continued to stay in Vault 76 until its food supplies finally ran dry and they had no other option but to leave.

* In the quarters that the プレイヤーキャラクター wakes up in at the start of the game, there はthat bathroom, bearing the message "Don't put TP on backwards!" This is a reference to viral debates about toilet paper orientation. The debate extended to Fallout 4 after its release, as players argued about the "under" orientation of the toilet paper in the Sole Survivor's house. In 2025, the twentieth in-game season for Fallout 76, Glow of the Ghoul, included two CAMP items that further reference this debate: the toilet paper holder ("under" orientation) and the "correct" toilet paper holder ("over" orientation). The bathroom in Vault 76 uses the "over" orientation.にあるsticky note on the mirrorです
* When reaching the first checkpoint, the "celebration" music from Fallout Shelter plays.
* In the Japanese version of Fallout 76, Reclamation Day is known as "Saisei no hi" (再生の日). "Saisei" (再生) has the theme of "life" and "biology" in its name, thus the term could be translated as Rebirth Day, Revival Day, Regeneration Day or Regrowth Day. "Seisei" also has the meaning of coming back from either death or severe injury, which would be 大戦 in this case.
* Vault 76 was featured in an official Bethesda livestream for the Fallout 76 5th Anniversary event, in which the Vault's exterior was decorated and the Vault 76 overseer delivered a three minute speech to begin the parade.
