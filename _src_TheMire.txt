'''The Mire''' is one of the eight [[Fallout 76 locations#Regions|regions]] of [[Appalachia]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Background==
The Mire was once a remote, rural region of Appalachia east of the [[Savage Divide]], home to the [[Free States]] movement sparked by [[Raleigh Clay]]. Suspicious of the government's vault program and aware of the escalation of the [[Resource Wars]], the group built private concrete bunkers scattered throughout the region, using the nearby [[Harpers Ferry]] as a staging area and supply point.<ref>[[Fallout 76 loading screens|''Fallout 76'' loading screens]]: ''"Paranoid that the government was lying to its people about the Vault program, the Free States movement built their own concrete bunkers to survive in case of nuclear devastation."''</ref><ref>[[Harpers Ferry terminal entries#11.16.76|Harpers Ferry terminal entries; manager's terminal, 11.16.76]]</ref> The movement's conspiracy theories and ideals unnerved patriotic visitors and disrupted the small town's tourism.<ref>[[Fallout 76 loading screens|''Fallout 76'' loading screens]]: ''"Harpers Ferry was a small town that depended on tourism. This put them at odds with the growing 'Free States' movement, whose conspiracy theories put off patriotic visitors."''</ref>

Violence between the Free States and law enforcement drew the attention of the government.<ref>[[Harpers Ferry terminal entries#01.01.77|Harpers Ferry terminal entries; manager's terminal, 01.01.77]]</ref><ref>[[The Sam Blackwell interview]]</ref> In June 2077, the public prosecutor pressed charges against the movement's founder,<ref>[[Charleston Capitol Building terminal entries#Subject: Advice|Charleston Capitol Building terminal entries; Senator Blackwell's terminal (unlocked), Subject: Advice]]</ref><ref>[[Charleston Capitol Building terminal entries#Subject: Raleigh Clay|Charleston Capitol Building terminal entries; Senator Blackwell's terminal (unlocked), Subject: Raleigh Clay]]</ref> and, by November, the Free States openly seceded from the United States and retreated to their concrete bunkers.<ref>[[Fallout 76 loading screens]]: ''"The Free States were a group of Appalachian anarchists who seceded from the United States shortly before the bombs fell."''</ref><ref>[[Sugar Grove terminal entries#10-13-77: Free States|Sugar Grove terminal entries; project director's terminal, 10-13-77: Free States]]</ref>

After the [[Great War]], the Mire's forest and swamp environment began to mutate and grow deadly with each passing day.<ref>[[Harpers Ferry terminal entries#Survival|Harpers Ferry terminal entries; Miranda's terminal, Survival]]</ref> Exactly one year after the Great War, [[Vault 94]] opened its vault door and sent ambassadors into the wasteland to invite survivors back to the vault.<ref>[[Vault 94 terminal entries#User Log - 10/24/78|Vault 94 terminal entries; exterior security terminal, User Log - 10/24/78]]</ref> In November 2078, an ambassador reached the town of Harpers Ferry. After hearing about the Vault, [[Miranda Vox|Mayor Miranda Vox]] sent a group of survivors led by [[Cole (Fallout 76)|Cole]] to investigate the ambassador's claims.<ref name="MirandaVault94">[[Harpers Ferry terminal entries#Vault 94|Harpers Ferry terminal entries; Miranda's terminal, Vault 94]]</ref><ref>[[Vault 94 terminal entries#Reception Log - 11/20/78|Vault 94 terminal entries; reception desk terminal, Reception Log - 11/20/78]]</ref> Certain that the dwellers' hospitality was a trap, the group massacred the vault's leaders and attacked the vault's [[G.E.C.K. (Fallout 76)|G.E.C.K.]] unit, triggering a massive nuclear explosion.<ref>[[Vault 94 Community Council recording]]</ref><ref>[[Vault 94 G.E.C.K. recording]]</ref><ref>[[Vault 94 terminal entries#G.E.C.K. System Log|Vault 94 terminal entries; G.E.C.K. monitoring station terminal, G.E.C.K. System Log]]</ref> A thick fog poured from the vault's entrance, rapidly intensifying the area's swampy environment and mutating the region's thick foliage.<ref name="MirandaVault94" /><ref>[[Harpers Ferry terminal entries#Vault 94 2|Harpers Ferry terminal entries; Ella's terminal, Vault 94]]</ref><ref name="AbandonHouse">[[Abandon house!]]</ref><ref name="CharlestonMireRecon">[[Charleston Capitol Building terminal entries#They've left their bunkers|Charleston Capitol Building terminal entries; Holbrook's terminal, They've left their bunkers]]</ref> The Savage Divide acted as a natural barrier, preventing the Mire's expansion to the west.<ref>[[The Crater terminal entries#--> The Mire|The Crater terminal entries; terminal, --> The Mire]]</ref>

In 2079, Raleigh Clay gave the all-clear signal to Free States members and was contacted by Miranda Vox, with Harpers Ferry being in desperate need of assistance.<ref>[[Raleigh Clay's bunker terminal entries#All Clear|Raleigh Clay's bunker terminal entries; Raleigh's terminal, All Clear]]</ref><ref>[[Harpers Ferry proposal]]</ref> Despite previous conflicts, the movement agreed to provide aid.<ref name="CharlestonMireRecon" /> Later in the year, a group of Free States members secured the [[Thunder Mountain Power Plant]], but were assaulted by [[Taggerdy's Thunder]] and chose to surrender the complex to them.<ref>[[Thunder Mountain Power Plant terminal entries#Entry 05|Thunder Mountain Power Plant terminal entries; Free States terminal, Entry 05]]</ref>

By 2081, the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Brotherhood of Steel]] used the pre-War [[Camp Venture]] location to provide survival training to members of the Free States.<ref>[[Camp Venture terminal entries#Schedule|Camp Venture terminal entries; terminal, Schedule]]</ref> After intensifying requisitions from local survivors, the Brotherhood abandoned the camp in 2094 to defend [[Fort Defiance]] and the Thunder Mountain Power Plant.<ref>[[Fort Defiance terminal entries#Change of Priorities|Fort Defiance terminal entries; Paladin Taggerdy's terminal, Change of Priorities]]</ref><ref>[[Fort Defiance terminal entries#93-JUL-20|Fort Defiance terminal entries; Senior Knight Wilson's terminal, 93-JUL-20]]</ref> In August 2095, the Brotherhood finally fell as Thunder Mountain was overrun with [[Scorched]].<ref>[[Thunder Mountain Power Plant terminal entries#95-Aug-19|Thunder Mountain Power Plant terminal entries; communications terminal, 95-Aug-19]]</ref> Using sonic generator technology salvaged from Brotherhood outposts, the Free States set up lures in the region to attract, trap, and kill [[scorchbeast]]s.<ref>[[Raleigh Clay's bunker terminal entries#Scorchbeast Lures|Raleigh Clay's bunker terminal entries; outgoing notifications station, Scorchbeast Lures]]</ref>

At some point in the pre-[[Scorched Plague|Plague]] period, the [[Enclave]] established a field lab at Tanagra Town, a neighborhood which had been lifted above the canopy by a towering pillar of [[strangler plant]]s. Their research was ultimately destroyed, but [[Strangler pod|strangler plant seed pods]] were returned to [[Enclave research facility|Enclave Research Facility Site J]] for study.

In 2096, Harpers Ferry was attacked by Scorched and the Free States retreated back into their bunkers throughout the Mire, banning all efforts to recover or save what remained of the town or its inhabitants.<ref>[[Raleigh Clay's bunker terminal entries#Scorchbeasts|Raleigh Clay's bunker terminal entries; outgoing notifications station, Scorchbeasts]]</ref>

In 2102, the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|residents]] of [[Vault 76]] began exploring the Mire. It was known as a place to [[Queen of the Hunt|hunt cryptids]]. Late in the year, they gained access to the Enclave field lab at Tanagra.

In 2103, a group of [[villager]]s settled the abandoned [[Treehouse Village]] under the leadership of [[Jennie Brown]], renaming it [[the Retreat]]. The raider [[Dagger]] and her goons made their headquarters in a [[Dagger's Den|nearby cave]], and began [[Slavery|demanding tribute]] from the village on the threat of death. In the south, the [[Cult of the Mothman|Followers of the Winged One]] cult constructed a towering primitivist stronghold called [[Moth-Home]]. At a nearby camp dubbed [[Blake's Offering]], [[Cultist (Fallout 76)|cultists]] live among unusual strangler vine formations. Additionally, crashed astronaut [[Sofia Daguerre]] was found in a bunker by the Vault Dwellers.

In 2104, the [[Sunday Brothers' cabin]] was settled by moonshiners.

==Environment==
The Mire is a dark, swampy region home to twisting vines, large tree canopies, and tall grass. The thick foliage gives cover to the region's mutated creatures and makes the area one of the darkest at night. While radiation from the Great War attributed to mutations, the region's red-hued environment is largely due to the explosion of Vault 94's G.E.C.K.<ref name="AbandonHouse" /><ref>[[Ella Ames' bunker terminal entries#Flora|Ella Ames' bunker terminal entries; Ella's terminal, Flora]]</ref> 

The [[Strangler plant|strangler vines]] infest the entire region, especially along the river. [[Strangler heart]]s bloom from these vines, which are capable of infesting creatures and controlling them.<ref>[[Fallout 76 loading screens|''Fallout 76'' loading screens]]: ''"Mutated plants known as 'Strangler' vines infest the Mire. They seem to attach to living things, notably the large trees in the area."''</ref> These coarse red vines grow rapidly, and tend to rip buildings apart and lift vehicles off the ground.

The Mire's [[weather]] is primarily misty, though the region may occasionally suffer from highly-radioactive [[radstorm]]s.<ref>[[Ella Ames' bunker terminal entries#Weather|Ella Ames' bunker terminal entries; Ella's terminal, Weather]]</ref> The weather has a 55% chance to be misty, 15% to be misty and rainy, 15% to be foggy, 15% to be clear, and 3% to be a radstorm.<ref>Region data for the Mire. (SwampObjectRegion)</ref>
