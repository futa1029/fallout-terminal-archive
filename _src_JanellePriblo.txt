'''Janelle Priblo''' is a deceased woman whose remains can be found in [[Appalachia]]. 

==Background==
Before the [[Great War]], Janelle was a trapper who owned [[Priblos' Curios]], a shop specializing in selling merchandise relating to Appalachian [[cryptids]], alongside [[Raymond Priblo]].<ref>[[Raymond's pep talk]]</ref> She had the goal of killing what she called the "[[Beast of Beckley]]" in the hope of selling it to [[Aries|Calvin van Lowe]].<ref>[[The Beast of Beckley (holotape)]]</ref> Janelle's continued determination to complete the task ultimately caused the death of both her and Raymond.<ref>[[Janelle's revenge]]</ref>
