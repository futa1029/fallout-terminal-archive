'''Happy Candlemaker''' is a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] operating in [[Helvetia]] as part of the [[Fasnacht Day]] festivities.

==Background==
Happy Candlemaker is one of the Protectrons which was reprogrammed to spice up Fasnacht Day celebrations in Helvetia.<ref>[[Fasnacht robots]]</ref><ref>[[Helvetia Protectron updates]]</ref> It can be distinguished by its white chassis and lit candles on its dome.

When Fasnacht Day is being celebrated, it is one of the Protectron parade marchers which may be encountered.  If it is required to march in the parade, it can be found behind the counter in the general store section of the post office. It will request that the player character(s) bring it 10 [[beeswax]] from the Helvetia Honey Haus' beehives before it will march in the parade. 

In [[Timeline#2102|2102]], the Happy Candlemaker wore the [[Fasnacht toothy man mask]] when marching in the parade. In [[Timeline#2103|2103]], the Protectron wears a random Fasnacht mask with an alternate color scheme.
