'''Niraj's confession''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] {{In|FO76}}.
__TOC__
==Location==
The tape can be found on the shelf above [[Ella Ames|Ella's]] terminal in [[Ella Ames' bunker]], directly behind a test tube rack.

==Transcript==
[[File:Niraj's_confession.ogg|center]]
{{Transcript|text=
'''[[Niraj Singh|Niraj]]''': To my dearest [[Abigayle Singh|Abigayle]] and [[Calvin Singh|Calvin]]. I've instructed [[Ella Ames|Ella]] to give you this holotape on the event of my death. Please don't blame anything you're about to hear on her. These are my wishes. Ella has respected them, and I expect you to as well.

I've been diagnosed with [[Diseases and viruses|Parkinson's]]. I've still got plenty of time to live a normal life... well as normal as can be expected... But the time will come where my health will deteriorate and I can no longer care for myself. I love you both tremendously, and you know that... but I will not be a burden to you, my children. Especially in these dark times. If everything has gone according to plan, hopefully my death has meant something.

{{tooltip|Please understand why I chose not to tell you. Abbie, you are smart enough to see our work through till the end.|Emphasis on &quot;you are&quot; because she is not fully confident.}} Calvin, you're turning into such a fine young man. [[Geneva Singh|Your mother]] would've been proud, but not as proud as me. You two take care of each other. You're going to make it through this. I know it.
}}
