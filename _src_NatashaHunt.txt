'''Natasha Hunt''' is a deceased Mistress of Mystery in the [[Order of Mysteries]], found dead in [[Lewisburg]] during the quest [[Initiate of Mysteries]].<ref>[[Cryptos terminal entries#Membership Roster|Cryptos terminal entries; Membership Roster]]</ref>

==Background==
Natasha was the chief medical officer for the Order of Mysteries.<ref>[[Riverside Manor terminal entries#Infirmary terminal|Riverside Manor terminal entries; infirmary terminal]]: "''CHIEF MEDICAL OFFICER: Mistress Natasha Hunt''"</ref> In preparing the Order for winter, she would remind the girls that it was cold season and to check in with the infirmary if they felt unwell. She also reminded the group to wash their hands, drink plenty of water, and get adequate rest.<ref>[[Riverside Manor terminal entries#Sick Days|Riverside Manor terminal entries; guest bedroom terminal, sick days]]</ref> Despite her position, Natasha knew that she had limitations and could not save everybody, such as after [[Alexis Belmont]] suffered a major accident in the Hall of Trials and entered critical condition.<ref>[[Order of Mysteries - Infirmary log]]</ref>

Sometime between September and November 2086, [[Tiana Chevrel]] disappeared on a mission, leading [[Shannon Rivers|Shannon]] and [[Olivia Rivers]] to exit [[Riverside Manor]] to investigate. In her absence, Shannon ordered Natasha to investigate leads on a raider group in Lewisburg. Neither of them knew that the raiders were aware of the Lewisburg mission and had set up an ambush in the town.<ref>[[Kerry's orders]]</ref> When Natasha arrived, she was ambushed by the raiders and ultimately killed, but only after managing to kill a number of the raiders, including their leader [[Kerry (Cutthroats)|Kerry]].<ref>Kerry's corpse, along with other [[raider corpse]]s on the Lewisburg rooftops.</ref>
