'''The Boss''' is a [[Robobrain (Fallout 76)|Robobrain]] that manages [[Camden Park]] from the [[Company Store]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
The Boss doles out wages for employees at Camden Park, and has been waiting for one to show up ever since the [[Great War]]. The Boss refers to humans as "fleshlings," a descriptive term rather than a rude one,<ref>The Boss: ''"{{Tooltip|Welcome valued customer to Camden Park where all fleshlings are winners!|Super cheerful. "Fleshlings" is a description and not intended to be rude.}}"''<br />([[The Boss/Dialogue|The Boss' dialogue]])</ref> and is programmed to be friendly to visitors. However, the Boss becomes stern and scolding when surrounded by workers of the park due to their ill preparation,<ref>The Boss: ''"{{Tooltip|Interesting. You must be my newest fleshling.|Female robot, sounds curious. "Fleshling" is a description only not a rude comment.}} {{Tooltip|You were late. You weren't prepared. You deserve shouting. But, I'm feeling generous today so I'll record your performance as adequate.|Scolding female robot.}} {{Tooltip|You aren't allowed in here when you're working. This is for valued customers only. They can exchange their Mr. Fuzzy tokens for prizes here.|Stern instructions from female robot.}}"''<br />([[The Boss/Dialogue|The Boss' dialogue]])</ref> lack of uniform,<ref>The Boss: ''"Fleshling? Why no uniform? There will be shouting."''<br />([[The Boss/Dialogue|The Boss' dialogue]])</ref> or presence in uniform in a non-uniformed area.<ref>The Boss: ''"Fleshling? Why are you here in uniform? There will be shouting. Go now!"''<br />([[The Boss/Dialogue|The Boss' dialogue]])</ref>
