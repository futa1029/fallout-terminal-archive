'''Cavendish''' is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] in [[Vault 76]].

==Background==
Cavendish is one of the Mister Handies that serve Vault 76. It can be found at station #2 on [[Reclamation Day]], where it facilitates the distribution of [[Stimpak (Fallout 76)|stimpaks]] and [[RadAway (Fallout 76)|RadAway]] to the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] and bids farewell to them as they exit the vault.

By [[Timeline#2103|2103]], Cavendish has grown bored and annoyed with the pre-recorded message from the [[Vault 76 overseer]], due to it having been playing every day for the last several months since [[Reclamation Day]].
