さびれた町 (Run-down town)
TVシリーズ ロケーション

LOCATION DATA

The run-down town is a location in the Fallout TV series.

In the TV series

The Handoff

The Ghoul, Maximus and Thaddeus pass through it, taking a detour on their way to New Vegas. It has a New California Republic armory storage shed to retrieve equipment for the battle against the New Vegas Strip deathclaws.

Appearances

The run-down town appears only in the Fallout TV series.
