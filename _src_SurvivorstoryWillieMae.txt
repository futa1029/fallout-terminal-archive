'''Survivor story: Willie Mae''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] {{In|FO76}}.
__TOC__
==Location==
The tape can be found on the body of [[Willie Mae]] on the porch of the blue house across the road from the [[Green Country Lodge]], near the radio.
