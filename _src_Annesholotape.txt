'''Anne's holotape''' is a [[Wild Appalachia holotapes|holotape]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Wild Appalachia]]''.

==Location==
On [[Anne Litzinger]]'s corpse, at these possible locations:
* [[Bolton Greens]]
* [[Palace of the Winding Path]]
* [[Philippi Battlefield Cemetery]]
* [[Watoga]]
* [[Point Pleasant]]
* [[Beckley]]
* [[Landview Lighthouse]] - Near the very top of the lighthouse, right before the final stairwell.
