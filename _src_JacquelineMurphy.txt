'''Jacqueline Murphy''' is a deceased hunter and member of the [[Free States]] whose body can be found in [[Appalachia]]. 

==Background==
Jacqueline was a member of [[Duncan McKann]]'s failed expedition to track down [[Feral ghoul (Fallout 76)|ghouls]] from the [[Valley Galleria]].<ref>[[Tracking Unknowns]]</ref>

She and fellow expedition member [[Courtney Kelly]] were wounded early on in the expedition and were left in the care of the expedition's [[Mister Gutsy (Fallout 76)|Mister Gutsy]], [[Hardball]], to recuperate.<ref>[[Hardball]]: ''"I've reason to believe their party was ambushed and a distress beacon deployed, but the signal is too weak to find. {{Tooltip|Your duty is to find a signal booster.|Officially giving instructions.}} {{Tooltip|Possible search locations include: Harpers Ferry or the Valley Galleria Electronic Store.|Official, stating information.}} {{Tooltip|Hmm. Initial readings indicate non-hostile.|To himself.}} As a member of the Free States and protector of Harpers Ferry, I hereby conscript you into service. {{Tooltip|I have wounded to protect, and re-establishing communications with Duncan McKann is vital.|Sounding official.}} {{Tooltip|Attach the booster, release the Cargobot, and wait for the distress signal ping to reach your Pip-Boy. Follow the ping. Find the party. Dismissed! Once you've acquired the booster, proceed to the Survival Training Center and call down a Cargobot using this passcode.|Officially giving instructions.}}"''<br />([[Hardball/Dialogue]])</ref> Jacqueline did not survive the ordeal and perished along with Courtney.

==Behind the scenes==
{{FO76CC}}
Jacqueline originally would have had her own [[Jacqueline's holotape|holotape]], in which she was voiced by [[Stephanie Komure]], but this holotape was [[Fallout 76 cut content|cut]] from the game. In it, she describes how the ill-fated expedition was her first hunting trip after being allowed to go by her brother, [[Robbie (cut)|Robbie]]. She was nervous about proving herself to the party, despite her reputation as the best shot on [[Harpers Ferry]] guard duty.<ref group="Non-canon">{{Cut}} [[Jacqueline's holotape]]</ref>
