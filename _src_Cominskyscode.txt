'''Cominsky's code''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
The note can be found on [[Cominsky|Sgt. Cominsky's]] corpse in [[Big Bend Tunnel]]. Her corpse is beside a wooden crate at the bottom of the tunnel. To reach the end of the tunnels, head south from the central room with the catwalks, before turning left into a side cavern in the final room with the [[Megaloader (Fallout 76)|megaloader]].

==Transcript==

-[[Melody Larkin|Melody]]
