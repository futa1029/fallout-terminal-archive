'''Notice: New owners''' is a [[One Wasteland For All notes|paper note]] {{in|FO76OW}}. It was [[Fallout 76 legacy content|removed]] from its location with the ''[[Steel Dawn]]'' update.

==Location==
The note could be found at [[Lewis & Sons Farming Supply]], pinned to a wall with a [[Bowie knife (Fallout 76)|bowie knife]] near the boarded up door of the barn. Upon the introduction of the [[Putnam residence]] interior with ''Steel Dawn'', the note was removed from its location after its purpose as a teaser had been fulfilled.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN:

This property is no longer abandoned. My family are the rightful inheritors, and will be returning shortly to take up permanent residence.

Kindly refrain from wrecking the place any more than it already is. We've taken the precaution of locking up the barn, but if you're trashing the joint when we get back you'll answer to me. And [[George Putnam|George]]'s shotgun.
We've got enough cleaning to do as is.
Regards,
[[Carol Putnam]] & Family
}}
