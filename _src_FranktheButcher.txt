'''Frank the Butcher''' is a member of the [[Blood Eagles]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Frank and [[Jessi the Hook]] were the two main chefs for the Blood Eagles at [[Bloody Frank's]]. Though they mostly cooperated on their cooking, they were not above sabotaging each other to take credit. Frank believed Jessi poisoned him one night to take credit for a dish they made together; though she had no idea what he was talking about, she decided to use that tactic on him later.<ref>[[Bloody Frank's terminal entries#Jessi's Log|Bloody Frank's terminal entries; Cooks' terminal, Jessi's Log]]</ref> 

Frank also stole new member [[Dishwasher Steve]]'s recipes and took them as his own, promising to make Steve a cook but never intending to follow through.<ref>[[Bloody Frank's terminal entries#Frank 2|Bloody Frank's terminal entries; Cooks' terminal, Frank 2]]</ref> Steve, realizing what was happening, began plotting to kill him.<ref>[[Dishwasher Steve's note]]</ref>
