'''Care to test your metal?''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Steel Reign]]''.

==Location==
This note is located in the [[Metal Dome]], on a yellow diagnostics cart on the southern side of the location, near the broken robobrain display.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
Construction of the new Metal Dome has started off on a good note.  We've been able to gather enough metal from surrounding locations in order to build the skeletal frame of the dome.

[[Buzzsaw]] is killin' it when it comes to organizing and delegating work tasks.  He has a pretty good idea of where to salvage materials to build this structure.  His latest request is to hit a high school and grab some stadium speakers for the dome.

Buzzsaw has also been talking about breaking off from the [[Blood Eagles]] and forming a new faction.  Calling it [[Rust Eagles]] or something.  Turning the Metal Dome into some kind of tournament for all to join.
}}
