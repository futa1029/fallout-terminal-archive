'''Responder's last words''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
The note can be found at the [[Morgantown Airport terminal]], on the second floor, inside the break room, next to a [[Responder corpse]].

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
If [[Jeff Nakamura|Jeff]] or [[Melody Larkin|Melody]] or anyone else finds this, I'm happy to know that someone made it out, but I think it's the end of the line for us. [[Tanner (Fallout 76)|Tanner]] and [[Janson]] aren't doing so great and I'm not far behind them...

It's still hard for me to come to grips that [[Scorched|those... things]] used to be our friends, our family. I can't take the thought of us [[Scorched Plague|changing]] and hurting other people, too. So I won't let it happen. If the last thing I can do is make sure that we stay ourselves...then I'm okay with that.

Serving with you all has been the highest honor.

[[Caleb Widmer]]
Paramedic
Morgantown EMS
}}
