'''The Coop''' is a [[Fallout 76 locations|location]] in [[the Forest]] region of [[Appalachia]] {{in|FO76BP}}. It is a [[Crater Raiders]] outpost that is reeling from the supposed death of its leader, [[Keith Cooper]].

The Coop is located almost directly east, and only slightly north, of [[Black Mountain Ordnance Works]]. On the world map, it is across the road, and its waypoint marker is depicted in the mountains, located up a cliff.

==Background==
The Coop was originally a group of nomadic settlers led by a man named [[Chris (Boardwalk Paradise)|Chris]]. When [[Keith Cooper]] joined the group, he would decide that he should instead be in charge, and led Chris to his death. Shortly after, Keith convinced the others that following him would be their best option.<ref>[[The Coop terminal entries#A Better Leader|The Coop terminal entries; Keith Cooper's terminal, A Better Leader]]</ref>

In time, Keith would manipulate and gaslight his group into doing what he wanted, gloating about his efforts on his [[The Coop terminal entries#Keith Cooper's terminal|personal terminal]]. Anyone who challenged his position as leader was killed by the group.<ref>[[The Coop terminal entries#The End of Terry|The Coop terminal entries; Keith Cooper's terminal, The End of Terry]]</ref> Eventually, only true loyalists were left.<ref>[[The Coop terminal entries#A Loyal Family|The Coop terminal entries; Keith Cooper's terminal, A Loyal Family]]</ref><ref>[[The Coop terminal entries#Garbage Day|The Coop terminal entries; Keith Cooper's terminal, Garbage Day]]</ref>

At some point, scouts from the group would encounter the [[Crater Raiders]], and would inform Keith of this new group. Being the manipulative and controlling man he was, Keith wanted to climb to the top of the raiders, though [[Meg Groberg]]'s leadership proved to be an obstacle. Once the group integrated, it is implied that the residents of the Coop began their final transformation into being chem-addicted raiders. Keith mentioned bringing his experience in the drug trade "to the table" in negotiations with the Crater Raiders.<ref name="the raiders">[[The Coop terminal entries#The Crater Raiders|The Coop terminal entries; Keith Cooper's terminal, The Crater Raiders]]</ref><ref>[[The Coop terminal entries#Merging with the Group|The Coop terminal entries; Keith Cooper's terminal, Merging with the Group]]</ref>

Failing to make ground within the Crater Raiders, Keith eventually decided he would not be able to make the gains he wanted to in West Virginia and thus chose to fake his death and disappear.<ref>[[Warning to Vincent]]</ref> Keith's group fell for his deception and assumed he was murdered; the subsequent fighting and attempted retalation against Keith's presumed killer led to chaos and confusion in the Coop, with those left alive not knowing if they even caught the killer in the chaos (not that there was one to begin with). Due to Keith's manipulation, the raiders are left reeling for their dear leader who, in actuality, damned them in the first place. Some of them even lament his loss to the point of believing they are all doomed with him.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"What happened here?"''<br />[[Dicer]]: ''"A tragedy! We used to have Keith to lead us. He was a smart man, he always told me what to do! I guess someone didn't like that. They didn't like what Keith had, so they... they killed him! We retaliated. A lot of people died. But in the confusion, I'm not really sure if we got the bastard or not. Anyway, we don't have Keith anymore, and I don't know what to do. It's a mess!"''<br />([[Dicer/Dialogue|Dicer's dialogue]])</ref><ref>[[Doomed]]</ref><ref>[[A life without Keith]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
* Level designer [[Ryan Johnson]] worked on the geography, layout and art of this location, in addition to writing the notes and terminal entries regarding [[Keith Cooper]]. Johnson received layout design guidance from [[Craig Bernardo]] and art approval from [[Andrew Collins]] (who also worked on the lighting), while [[Christopher Marshall]] wrote the character of [[Dicer]].<ref name="AS" group="Meta">[https://www.artstation.com/artwork/495kBW Ryan Johnson on ArtStation: "Fallout 76 - The Coop"]</ref>
* Originally, Johnson planned for Keith to have been killed in the story by a [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]] fresh out of the [[Vault]], but this was changed to be more open-ended for the final release.<ref name="AS" group="Meta" />
