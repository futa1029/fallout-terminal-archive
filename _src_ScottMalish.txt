'''Scott Malish''' is a deceased man in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
After the [[Great War]], Scott was harassed by the [[Raiders (Fallout 76)|early raider groups]] in Appalachia and was stalked by the [[Gourmands]]. To hide from his pursuers, Scott holed up in a trailer near a toxic dumping site in the [[Toxic Valley]]. He assumed that the [[radiation]] would keep the raiders away, and if he died, his corpse would be too irradiated for the Gourmands to feast on. Even if the [[Cannibalism|cannibals]] were stupid enough to eat him anyway, Scott hoped that the poisoning would eventually kill them. Now alone, Scott apologized to [[Peach (character)|Peach]] in writing, though he admitted that he still loved her. His corpse can be found lying in the trailer years later.<ref>[[Scott's letter]]</ref>
