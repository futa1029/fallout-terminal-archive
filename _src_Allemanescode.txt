'''Allemane's code''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
This note can be found on [[Allemane|Sgt. Allemane's]] corpse at [[Big Bend Tunnel west]]. It is beside a train car outside of the [[Blue Ridge Caravan Company]] outpost.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
Sgt. Allemane, your code phrase for the Master Terminal is "Smoker Boy." Yes, I'm telling you to kick the habit. Don't you got enough nasty going into your lungs?
-[[Melody Larkin|Melody]]
}}

==Notes==
Before the ''[[Wastelanders]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]], Allemane's corpse, and thus this note, was found beside a large orange crate near the gate to the [[Big Bend Tunnel]] interior. When the Blue Ridge outpost was added with ''Wastelanders'', Allemane's corpse was moved to be outside of the outpost, on the rocks near a train car.
