'''Hardball''' is a [[Mister Gutsy (Fallout 76)|Mister Gutsy]] found north of [[Valley Galleria]] in [[Appalachia]].            

==Background==
A custom Mr. Gutsy used by the [[Free States]] before their demise, Hardball was part of [[Duncan McKann]]'s expedition to track down the Valley Galleria [[Feral ghoul (Fallout 76)|ghouls]] and stayed behind with the wounded [[Jacqueline Murphy]] and [[Courtney Kelly]] as the party moved on.<ref>Hardball: "''I have wounded to protect, and re-establishing communications with Duncan McKann is vital.''"<br />([[Hardball/Dialogue#7|Hardball's dialogue]])</ref>

During [[Tracking Unknowns]], Hardball tells the player character to find a [[signal booster]] to track the missing hunting group.
