'''Watoga Emergency Services''', also known as the '''Watoga Emergency Medical Services'''<ref>[[Voice of Watoga]]: "''Watoga Emergency Medical Services is here to help! Speak to our friendly automated receptionist for immediate assistance."''<br />([[Voice of Watoga/Dialogue|Voice of Watoga's dialogue]])</ref> ('''Watoga EMS'''),<ref>[[Voice of Watoga]]: "''Watoga EMS thanks you for letting us help you today! We are here for you with top of the line medical technology!"''<br />([[Voice of Watoga/Dialogue|Voice of Watoga's dialogue]])</ref> is a [[Fallout 76 locations|building]] and institution in the [[Appalachia]]n city of [[Watoga]].

==Background==
Intended to be an automated solution for all the emergency needs of the people of Watoga, the center was staffed almost entirely by robotic personnel, with only a single human stationed there for maintenance. Although intended to offer 24/7 support for the needs of the city's citizens, the system was rife with malfunctions and the impersonal nature of the robots resulted in poor service.<ref>[[Watoga Emergency Services terminal entries#Complaint Report #092734|Watoga Emergency Services terminal entries; complaint database terminal, Complaint Report #092734]]</ref> The maintenance engineer, [[Oscar]], was routinely swarmed by patients believing that he could help with their medical issues, though he had no experience in the area.<ref>[[Watoga Emergency Services terminal entries#I'm not the Doctor|Watoga Emergency Services terminal entries; engineer's terminal, I'm not the Doctor]]</ref>

The system required that only the patient could request services and complete medical paperwork.<ref>[[Watoga Emergency Services terminal entries#Other .28Parent .2F Guardian .2F Spouse.29|Watoga Emergency Services terminal entries; emergency report terminal; File Medical Report; Other (Parent / Guardian / Spouse)]]</ref> This made it impossible for parents, spouses or legal guardians to report emergencies, unless they brought the patient in themselves.<ref>[[Watoga Emergency Services terminal entries#Complaint database terminal|Watoga Emergency Services terminal entries; complaint database terminal]]</ref> In addition, the fire fighting side of the facility system was programmed to ignore reports from outside the three districts of Watoga, preventing response or help to be dispatched.<ref>[[Watoga Emergency Services terminal entries#Watoga City Exterior|Watoga Emergency Services terminal entries; emergency report terminal; File Fire Report; Watoga City Exterior]]</ref>

==Layout==
Located across the street from the [[Watoga Civic Center]], the Emergency Services building is green in color and contains the reception and waiting room. Taking the elevator leads to a lower floor, which contains automated examination rooms. Near the elevator is an access door to the helipad, which has a potential armor mod and some ammo boxes.

The upper floor contains the emergency report room with terminals for reporting medical issues, as well as a secondary patient examination room on the western end and the emergency services server room on the southern side.  The locked laboratory in the center contains the bodies of deceased Brotherhood soldiers and their power armor. 

Up another flight of stairs, the roof contains the location's [[Steamer trunk (Fallout 76)|steamer trunk]] as well as a [[Power armor chassis|power armor]] spawn at the southern edge. It also contains a helipad and offers a picturesque view of downtown Watoga.
