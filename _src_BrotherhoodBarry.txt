'''Brotherhood Barry''' is a member of the [[Brotherhood of Zeal]], traveling throughout [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Also known as the Brotherhood Bandits,<ref>In the game's files, the quest name for each variant of the random encounter starts with "Brotherhood Bandits"</ref> Barry and his two associates, [[Brotherhood Barnaby|Barnaby]] and [[Brotherhood Buck|Buck]], are actively impersonating the newly revived Brotherhood of Steel through the arrival of the [[Brotherhood First Expeditionary Force]]. In doing so, they attempt to rob travelers of their [[Cap (Fallout 76)|bottle caps]] under the guise of donations. Barry will ask for five caps as a donation if he is leading the group.<ref>Brotherhood Barry: ''"We are the Brotherhood of Zeal! We require a donation of 5 caps to further our immoral clause."''<br />([[Brotherhood Barry/Dialogue|Brotherhood Barry's dialogue]])</ref> If attacked, all three bandits will run away instead of fighting back despite carrying weapons.
