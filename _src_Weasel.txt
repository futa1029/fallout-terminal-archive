'''Weasel''' is a member of the [[Crater Raiders]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
While she was still with the [[Blood Eagles]], she ratted on someone in the gang, which resulted in a gang member burning her voice box with a hot poker.<ref name="unusual">[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Weasel? That's an unusual name."''<br />Weasel: ''"Not my real name. I was with a different gang until I snatched... no, ratted on someone. They used a red hot poker to burn my voice box. Now, I am with Meg and her gang. They like me, but when they found out why I could not talk, they programmed this thing so I could only say my name is Weasel. That way, people know I was a rat."''<br />([[W05_Raider_Weasel_Crater.txt|Weasel's dialogue]])</ref><ref name="revenge">Weasel: ''"One day, I will find the Blood Eagle who did this to me and I will tear his throat out."''<br />([[W05_Raider_Weasel_Crater.txt|Weasel's dialogue]])</ref> She later joined up with [[Meg Groberg]] and her gang, who dubbed her Weasel after they found out the reason for her being mute.<ref name="unusual" />

She was given a voice collar by [[Caleb Fisher]], who was searching [[Monongah]] for tech, when he found and tinkered with a voice interpreter, originally developed by [[Eddie Harrison|Dr. Eddie Harrison]]. The voice collar enables Weasel to speak, though her vocabulary is limited to the words that are in the collar's voice library, causing her some frustration due to the censorship.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Is there something wrong with your voice?"''<br />Weasel: ''"I can't speak. The man, Fisher, made this collar to let me speak words. It is not perfect, but it works. He found the machines... No. The science... No. That is also not right. Technology. Yes. It has trouble interpreting some words."''<br />([[W05_Raider_Weasel_Crater.txt|Weasel's dialogue]])</ref><ref>[[The Core terminal entries#Vox Collar|The Core terminal entries; Fisher's terminal, Vox Collar]]</ref>

Weasel indicates that she is still seeking revenge against the Blood Eagle responsible for her condition,<ref name="revenge" /> though she is thankful to have only lost her voice.<ref>Weasel: ''"The Blood Eagles are psychotic and violent. I was lucky to only lose my voice."''<br />([[W05_Raider_Weasel_Crater.txt|Weasel's dialogue]])</ref> A skilled tracker, she is enlisted by Fisher to shadow her friend [[Lucky Lou]] and ensure his safety after he leaves the Crater,<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Do you have any idea where he might have gone?"''<br />[[Caleb Fisher]]: ''"Not exactly, but I did send out a friend of mine to go find him, just before you showed up. She's a tracker. Best I've ever known. Unfortunately, she hasn't come back yet. Might be she's having trouble getting Lou to see eye to eye. My friend's not the best conversationalist, and Lou's stubborn. Maybe you can go and mediate. Talk some sense into old Lou?"''<br />([[Caleb Fisher/Dialogue|Caleb Fisher's dialogue]])</ref> with Fisher able to follow their progress using a radio beacon he built into her collar.<ref>[[The Core terminal entries#Tracking Beacon|The Core terminal entries; Fisher's terminal, Tracking Beacon]]</ref>
