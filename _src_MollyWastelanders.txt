'''Molly''' is a member of the [[Crater Raiders|Raiders]] residing in [[the Crater]].

==Background==
Molly lived in [[South Boston]] before the [[Great War]], which she describes as a rough neighborhood. She saw military blockades, trigger-happy policemen and, looming over everything else, crime lord [[Eddie Winter]]. She much prefers pouring drinks for the Raiders in [[Appalachia]] to her past life.<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Pouring drinks for bandits seems like a fast way to get yourself killed. You aren't worried for your own safety?"''<br />Molly: ''"{{Tooltip|Worried? Pfft. Hardly. Sure, I lock my cap stash up at night, but these folks are pussycats compared to the ones I grew up around.|Recounting your very rough upbringing.}} {{Tooltip|South Boston, before the bombs, real bad. Military blockades, trigger-happy cops, and looming over it all, the monster himself. Eddie. Winter.|Linger on "Eddie. Winter." He's a real monster.}} Nah, I'll pour drinks for these troublemakers any day of the week over that life again."''<br />([[Molly (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Molly's dialogue]])</ref>

When she arrived in Appalachia, she was one of three bartenders. [[Pretty Pete]] served alcohol and [[Lex (Wastelanders)|Lex]] served chems. However, both died early on, leaving Molly the only bartender. She now refuses to drink any kind of alcohol or take any chems.<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''How'd you end up tending bar in Crater?"''<br />Molly: ''"{{Tooltip|Process of elimination. There were three of us in the inebriation business when we first set up - me, Pretty Pete, and Lex.|Telling a story.}} {{Tooltip|Pete poured, Lex slung chems. But Appalachia ... seems it was just too much for them. Lex tragically OD'ed on her own stash not long after we arrived.|Coy. You murdered both of them.}} {{Tooltip|Pete was so distraught, he got wasted and wandered right into one of those big bat fissures.|You helped Pete get so hammered, he wandering into this pit.}} {{Tooltip|Now, I don't touch the stuff. My own little sacrifice. A reminder of the ones it took to get me my monopoly.|Again, very coy. "I don't touch the stuff" referring to drugs. Add a little bit of threat to the last line. Should be a "no one fucks me with" line.}}"''<br />([[Molly (Wastelanders)/Dialogue#295|Molly's dialogue]])</ref>
