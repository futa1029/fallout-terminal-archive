'''Anne Litzinger''', referred to as '''Anne''' in-game, is a deceased woman in [[Appalachia]]. 

==Background==
Anne was a student at [[Vault-Tec University]] originally from [[San Francisco]]. At first, she dreaded the thought of interacting with people that she thought would be ignorant, backwater hillbillies. However, over time, she became more accepting of people from the area and realized that a person's birthplace does not always define them. 

An aspiring photojournalist, she captured photos of tourism spots in [[Appalachia]] before dying to [[radiation]] sickness while visiting one after the [[Great War]], never finishing her "bucket list" of sights to see.<ref>[[Anne's holotape]]</ref>

==Possible locations==
[[File: FO76 dead tourist locations.jpeg|thumb|Dead tourist locations]]
Anne's and [[Ansel Abrahms]]'s corpses share a single spawn chance and will only spawn one time per server. The spawn location can be found by using [[My personal terminal]] and clicking the Suspicious Person option.
Anne's body has a chance to be found at the following locations:
* [[Bolton Greens]], on the fountain opposite a body on the other side of the fountain.
* [[Palace of the Winding Path]], overlooking the location from the south at a tourist spot.
* [[Philippi Battlefield Cemetery]], next to a bench.
* [[Watoga]], in front of the west entrance's "Welcome to Watoga" sign.
* [[Point Pleasant]], at the [[Mothman]] statue.
* [[Beckley mine exhibit]], on the blue car in front of the building
* [[Landview Lighthouse]], near the very top of the lighthouse, right before the final stairwell.

==Appearances==
Anne Litzinger appears only {{In|FO76WA}}.

==Behind the scenes==
* Anne is a reference to [[wikipedia:Annie Leibovitz|Annie Leibovitz]], a photographer known for her work capturing portraits of celebrities. Annie Leibovitz graduated from the San Francisco Art Institute, and Anne the character is from San Francisco.
* Anne is voiced by [[Rachel Butera]], who also voiced [[Shelley van Lowe]] and [[Trish (Wild Appalachia)|Trish]] in ''Wild Appalachia''. In localized versions of the game, her voice is dubbed by Anni Sultany (German).
