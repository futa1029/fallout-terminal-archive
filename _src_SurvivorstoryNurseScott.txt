'''Survivor story: Nurse Scott''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
The tape can be found in a cooler between two bathtubs, directly behind the red house with the barricaded porch in [[Flatwoods]], south of the church.
