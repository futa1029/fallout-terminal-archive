'''Graveyard note''' is a set of two [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]]s {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
Both notes are tacked to one grave marker each in the [[Brotherhood graveyard]] outside [[Huntersville]]. They can be read, but cannot be taken.
