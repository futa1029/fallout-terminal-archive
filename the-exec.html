The Exec

概要

Before the Great War, the Exec was Tony, a businessman who had a seat on the executive board of Abraxodyne Chemical. He looks back on his pre-War life fondly, embracing the capitalist mindset of "making money hand over fist." Tony also concepted the "Abraxo-bucks" initiative, held as a contest by Abraxodyne for the public to collect tickets with rewards for the winners. However, due to insufficient cost projections prior to the contest's approval, the company soon realized they could not have "so many winners" and were forced to reconfigure the contest and discourage the likelihood of someone collecting enough tickets.

Tony was initially skeptical of the company's venture into Project Meadow Breeze, using the company's chemicals to produce a military-grade weapon, and he wanted to scrap the project after the first round of testing failed. However, he changed his opinion after seeing the projected revenue from a contract with the United States Armed Forces, joking that perhaps they had a "national duty" to protect the military, in a boon for both "morale and market share." While on the board, Tony was in charge of securing company secrets and controlling the flow of information, making sure Abraxodyne's business stayed private.

It was soon determined that this mole was Amrita, the head of Abraxodyne's marketing division. Using his powerful position on the board of Abraxodyne Chemical, Tony ordered that Amrita should be eliminated, in order to protect the company's interests and private business. The company discretely hired a hitman to kill Amrita, who would pick up equipment from Tony's apartment before "disposing" of the whistleblower. Although they were successful in silencing her and keeping Project Meadow Breeze under wraps, it was all for naught, as the bombs dropped not long after, which Tony sees as a reason not to care about the past anymore.

He further defends his actions regarding Amrita's murder for two reasons: he claims that the Meadow Breeze Sprayer and its prototypes were never intended to actually be used and were only deterrents to help the troops, and that the board members had to toe the company line and he would have been "next" if he did not deal with Amrita. Before the bombs dropped, he had a wife and daughter whom he wanted to protect, and he says that he was just looking after his family in his actions. Tony survived the Great War but became a ghoul, and he clearly resents the current state of his body, calling himself "sentient roadkill" He lives a secluded life in his old pre-War apartment in Athens, as one of its few non-feral residents, where he has set traps to ward off visitors, whom he makes clear are unwelcome.
