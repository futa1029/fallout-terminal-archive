'''Agreement with Radicals''' is a [[Wastelanders holotapes|holotape]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Wastelanders]]''.

==Location==
The tape can be found inside the main farmhouse at [[Anchor farm]], under the stairwell, in a floor safe.

==Notes==
Collecting the tape will unlock a dialogue option to accuse [[Daniel (Wastelanders)|Daniel]] of being a murderer during the quest [[Hunter for Hire]].
