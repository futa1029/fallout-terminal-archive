'''Incident record 5374''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] {{In|FO76}}. It is a recording of a conversation between a young girl named [[Sissy]] and a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]].
__TOC__
==Location==
The tape is found inside the [[Watoga Emergency Services]] building, on the upstairs reception desk near [[Chief]].
