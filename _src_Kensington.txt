'''Agent Kensington''' is a [[glowing one]] and [[United States government|federal agent]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Agent Kensington was a member of an unknown spy agency stationed at Listening Station 4D-20, which was secretly located in the basement of the farmhouse at [[Solomon's pond]]. Kensington worked with [[Sawyer Lang]] and [[Enrique Gómez]] and had a security level of 5, which allowed access to the holotape repair terminal located inside the basement.<ref>[[Solomon's pond terminal entries#Holotape repair terminal|Solomon's pond terminal entries; holotape repair terminal]]</ref> At some point either during or after the [[Great War]], Kensington became a [[Feral ghoul (Fallout 76)#Glowing one|glowing feral ghoul]], though remained at the listening station.
