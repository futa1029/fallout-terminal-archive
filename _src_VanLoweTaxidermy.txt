'''Van Lowe Taxidermy''' is a [[Fallout 76 locations#Wild Appalachia|store]] within [[Lewisburg]].

==Background==
Van Lowe Taxidermy was owned by [[Shelley van Lowe]] who inherited it from her parents when they died.<ref>[[Life with Calvin]]</ref> She eventually sold it to the [[Bysshe Company]]<ref>[[Property deed]]</ref> when she left Lewisburg after her brother [[Aries|Calvin]]'s disappearance.<ref>[[Van Lowe Taxidermy#To Calvin, From Shelley|Van Lowe Taxidermy; To Calvin, From Shelley]]</ref> The store has a secret basement lab which is accessible by pressing the laundry day date on the wall calendar next to the laundry.<ref>[[Further instructions]]</ref> This was built by the Bysshe Company for Calvin van Lowe on a weekend when his sister Shelley was out of town.<ref>[[Van Lowe Taxidermy terminal entries#Check-in #1|Van Lowe Taxidermy terminal entries; Check-in #1]]</ref> It was here that Calvin tried to reprogram an assaultron to help him in his hunt for the legendary sheepsquatch, but misunderstood what the assaultron said when he gave it commands, and this led to it attacking him, mutilating his face, and causing him to go into hiding, joining the Blue Ridge Caravan Company as one of their bodyguards for their supply caravans through Big Bend Tunnel under the alias Aries.

==Layout==
Van Lowe Taxidermy has three floors and a basement and is a combination of taxidermy store, personal home, and a lab.

===First floor===
The entrance to the building leads to the main taxidermy store. The walls are lined with the merchandise - mounted heads of various [[Appalachia]]n fauna, mounted [[Antler (item)|antlers]] and claws, and a large stuffed bear standing in the front window. The store counter sits next to a shelf of taxidermy equipment for the casual hobbyist. A [[noise complaint]] lies on the counter next to a terminal. Behind a counter is a red button that can be pressed. There is a display case located against the northern wall which contains a [[strange bone]] if the player character has not yet completed the quest [[Lying Lowe]], which is the start of a small quest line leading to the public event [[Encryptid]]. A doorway to the east leads to a set of stairs that connect to the main living areas. Next to the store counter is a door that leads to a storage area and a set of stairs leading down to the basement. The storage area contains lockers and merchandise which have yet to be assembled for sale. There is a locked storage closet ([[Picklock]] 1) which can be unlocked by picking the lock or using the [[taxidermy storage key]] which can be found on top of a bureau on the second floor near the staircase.

===Second floor===
The landing area after reaching this floor contains a television and a couch. It also has a small cabinet holding [[Coffee cup (Fallout 76)|coffee cup]]s, [[Teacup (Fallout 76)|teacups]], and a [[Teapot (Fallout 76)|teapot]]. A door to the west leads to a kitchen. Shelley’s bedroom is connected to the kitchen, through a door that is next to another flight of stairs leading up. In her bedroom is an ensuite, a broken bed frame, and a desk with the [[Life with Calvin]] holotape on it. There are three cat heads mounted above Shelley’s desk. The mounted cat heads are part of the requirements for a [[Pioneer Scouts]] badge.

===Third floor===
The third floor contains Calvin's bedroom and study. There is an [[upright piano]] and a couch in the main landing. Calvin's bedroom is beside the upright piano. Inside his bedroom are a large bed, a dresser with a [[Bowler hat (Fallout 76)|bowler hat]] sitting on it, and a vanity with a golf bag leaning against it. His room has an ensuite, with a set of mounted antlers and six rolls of [[toilet paper]] hanging from them. 

Calvin's study is east of the top of the stairs. A terminal sits on a desk next to a note from [[RobCo customer support]]. A [[Robot parts model (Fallout 76)|robot parts model]] sits on the desk beside the terminal and four empty milk bottles can be found in the wastebasket. There is a map and multiple boards hung on the walls with notes detailing strange occurrences and possible cryptid sightings tacked to them. 

===Basement===
A [[Radrat (Fallout 76)|radrat]] has taken up residence in the basement which is a combined laundry and workshop area that contains a [[Chemistry station (Fallout 76)|chemistry station]]. On the wall next to the chemistry station is a mounted wolf head. The strange bone can be inserted in its mouth to reveal a secret safe containing [[Calvin's security code]]. On the wall next to the washer and dryer in the corner of the room is a calendar. Pressing the day marked 'Laundry Day' will cause the wall to shift and reveal the entrance to Calvin’s secret laboratory. A laser grid guards the entrance and can only be bypassed with a [[spare operative keycard]].

Calvin's lab contains an assaultron [[custom robotic assembly platform|assembly platform]] with a terminal attached to it. There is a large work area filled with spare parts from both robots and various animals. A [[tinker's workbench]] sits at the far end of the lab, next to a chalkboard with schematics for an [[imposter Sheepsquatch]] drawn on it.
