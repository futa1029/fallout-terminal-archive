The '''[[Vault-Tec Corporation|Vault-Tec]] Agricultural Research Center''' is a corporate [[Fallout 76 locations|R&D]] center in [[the Forest]] region of [[Appalachia]] that experimented with large-scale farming automation before the [[Great War]].<ref>''[[Fallout 76 Vault Dweller's Survival Guide]]'' p.291: ''"'''15. VAULT-TEC AGRICULTURAL RESEARCH CENTER'''''<br />''On the southern outskirts of Flatwoods is a cluster of lowrise rusting structures: a curved storage building with two others on the road's north side and the main center with two connected exterior greenhouses. Check under the road bridge for additional items.''<br />''Reach the Center's interior via the main foyer doors, the roadside doors, a side door, or the roof. Access the basement via the easily overlooked stairwell; here you find the Agricultural Center Mainframe, a computer you must access during the event."''<br />([[Fallout 76 Vault Dweller's Survival Guide Atlas of Appalachia]])</ref>

==Background==
A pre-War facility established to experiment with crop automation and fertilization, the Agricultural Center was one of the major landmarks in the small town of [[Flatwoods]]. The fleet of [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)#Mr. Farmhand|Mr. Farmhands]] (customized Mr. Handy robots) would automatically take care of crops, fertilizing them, planting seeds, and collecting the harvest with barely any need for human oversight. While the Great War put the facility offline, it was reactivated and restored to partial operation by [[MacFadden]] in a bid to aid starving survivors. MacFadden had no technical expertise and was unable to achieve much beyond simply reactivating the robots, with help from the [[Responders]]. He continued his work, but his lack of skill culminated in the Farmhands going rogue and the Protectron Handy overseers turning the robots loose on anyone who breached the facility's perimeter.<ref>[[Vault-Tec Agricultural Research Center terminal entries#MacFadden's terminal|Vault-Tec Agricultural Research Center terminal entries; MacFadden's terminal, 11-11-94]]</ref>

==Layout==
The Vault-Tec Agricultural Research Center consists of the main building with surrounding sheds and greenhouses that no longer house any plant life. 

The main building can be accessed either via doors in the greenhouses behind the building, the main front door, or a door on the roof of the building (the roof can be accessed from the outside via a set of fire stairs). It has three floors including a basement. The first floor contains locker rooms, bathrooms, a few hydroponics labs (one contains a [[Chemistry station (Fallout 76)|chemistry station]]) and a reception area. One of the [[Overseer's journal, entry 1|overseer's journal holotapes]] can be found on the reception desk. 

The second floor contains multiple offices, one of which contains [[Vault-Tec Agricultural Research Center terminal entries#MacFadden's terminal|MacFadden's terminal]] and a locked ([[Picklock]] 0) wall safe. One of the offices on this floor is locked (Picklock 0) and contains a bedroll and [[Laser gun (Fallout 76)|laser gun]]. There is also a meeting room off the main atrium.

There is a skeleton wearing a lab coat and holding a box of rat poison under the stairs leading to the basement. The [[Vault-Tec Agricultural Research Center terminal entries#Agricultural center mainframe|mainframe terminal]] can be found here. One of the basement rooms is partially flooded and contains a chemistry station and planters, some of which still have plants growing in them. There is also a locked fertilizer storage container in this room which can be accessed using a terminal. It contains [[raw fertilizer]].

The three greenhouses behind the facility are largely identical. All of them contain farming materials such as [[Blue garden gnome|garden gnomes]], [[flower pot]]s, and [[Bag of fertilizer (Fallout 76)|bags of fertilizer]] as well as a locked (Picklock 0) fertilizer storage container. A few [[Tarberry (Fallout 76)|tarberries]] grow in the pools of dirty water near the greenhouses.
