'''Mr. Bosley''', referred to as a '''raider corpse''' in-game, is a deceased [[Raiders (Fallout 76)|raider]] at the [[Pioneer Scout lookout]]. His aesthetic appearance is randomly generated.

==Background==
Bosley was contracted by an unknown person to kill [[Henrick|Mr. Henrick]], a rival raider. Unbeknownst to Bosley, Mr. Henrick was also enlisted to take out Bosley at the same time. Henrick was positioned on the roof of [[Grafton Steel]] and Bosley was set up at the Pioneer Scout lookout, resulting in a duel that killed both men.<ref>[[Henrick's note]]</ref><ref>[[Bosley's note]]</ref>
