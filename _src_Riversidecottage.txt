The '''riverside cottage'''<ref group="Non-game">''[[Fallout 76 Vault Dweller's Survival Guide]]'' p. 380: ''"'''The Ash Heap (Zone A) - Secondary Locations'''''<br />1. Riverside Cottage ''A small white cottage in the woods by the river, just south of the Nuka-Cola Plant."''<br />([[Fallout 76 Vault Dweller's Survival Guide Atlas of Appalachia]])</ref> is an [[Fallout 76 locations|unmarked location]] in [[Appalachia]] {{In|FO76}}. It is west of the fast travel point to the [[Kanawha Nuka-Cola plant]], and south of the plant itself.

==Layout==
A destroyed, lime green car sits before the patio. After walking up the patio and into the building, the doorway leads into the main room. A [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] lies destroyed on the kitchen floor, in front of the oven. The refrigerator has had its door removed. Turning right from the front door leads into the bedroom. 

A single, sheet-less bed lies in the middle of the room, and a baby crib is next to the window. The northern bedroom wall has collapsed and exposes the bathroom. A dead [[Radroach (Fallout 76)|radroach]] holding a [[Walking cane (Fallout 76)|walking cane]] and wearing a [[Bowler hat (Fallout 76)|bowler hat]] sits on the toilet.

==Notable loot==
* [[Wedding ring (Fallout 76)|Wedding ring]] - In the dead radroach's inventory.

The riverside cottage appears only {{In|FO76}}.

==Behind the scenes==
* The location contains several [[Fallout 76 cultural references#Monster Factory|references]] to the Final Pam, a character from the ''[[Fallout 4]]'' [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_1jGnFt78H8 episodes] of the webseries ''Monster Factory'' by [[Wikipedia:Justin McElroy|Justin]] and [[Wikipedia:Griffin McElroy|Griffin McElroy]].
** The house is similar in layout to the player character's pre-War home in ''Fallout 4''.
** The name "PAM" is spelled out in wooden blocks on top of the TV.
** There is a dapper, wedding-ready radroach in the bathroom, a reference to the character Roachie.
** Pam claims that her real son is a [[Coffee tin (Fallout 76)|coffee tin]]. The same item can be found in a crib, along with a box of [[Abraxo cleaner (Fallout 76)|Abraxo cleaner]], which Pam suggested feeding to baby [[Shaun]] as a snack.
** There is also a [[Hammer (Fallout 76)|hammer]] on the nightstand between the crib and the larger bed, alluding to Pam's idea to "take a hammer and fix the baby" because she thought Shaun was too small. The nightstand on the opposite side has Pam's original shirt and fashionable glasses sitting on top.
** The destroyed Mister Handy is a reference to [[Codsworth]], whom Pam calls her "three-eyed metal husband."<ref group="Non-game">[https://www.thegamer.com/fallout-76-monster-factory-final-pam-canon/ Fallout 76 Makes Monster Factory's 'The Final Pam' Canon]</ref> 
** The baby bottle in the fridge alludes to the existence of Trash Hulk, Pam's organic husband, as Pam suggested feeding him with a bottle before shrinking him to roughly the size of a baby.
*A few details added to the game in later updates read as alluding to or inspired by the works of the McElroy Brothers, podcast comedians and prominent West Virginians. The trio were honorarily considered mayors of Huntington for one day in 2017, when they held a parade to improve public perception of tarantulas. They are joined in some projects by their father [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clint_McElroy Clint McElroy], a successful radio host active in the area since the 1970s.
**[[Davenport]] appears to be named for [https://theadventurezone.fandom.com/wiki/Davenport Davenport the navigator] from ''The Adventure Zone''.
**Season 2 of ''The Adventure Zone'' takes place in West Virginia, and Indrid Cold was a central character, per the mythical figure's brief [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indrid_Cold Wikipedia page]. Later, the [[Smiling Man]] was added to ''Fallout 76'' in the ''[[Mutation Invasion]]'' update, in a comparable depiction.
**The same update added [[Steven Scarberry]]. One of his lines trails off with a thoughtful standalone ''"Unless...''"<ref name=":0">[[Steven Scarberry]]: ''"{{Tooltip|I can see the Light!... Oh wait. That's the sun... Unless...|Starts confident. Gets dejected. Starts to become confident again with "Unless"}}"''<br />([[Steven Scarberry/Dialogue|Steven Scarberry's dialogue]])</ref> This a famous catchphrase of the McElroys, as part of a joke format.
***His script notes instruct the actor to speak dejectedly, then grow confident at the end of the line,<ref name=":0" /> which is the format of the joke.
***As literary precedent, a line structured the same way in a musical called ''Hamilton'' was confessed by the playwright to be an allusion to their podcast.<ref group="Non-game">[https://web.archive.org/web/20210929110525/https://twitter.com/Lin_Manuel/status/537319805163147264 Lin-Manuel Miranda on Twitter, archived]</ref>
* The house has the cell name of "HuntingtonHouseExt." The location itself even sits roughly where [[Huntington]] does in the real-world.
