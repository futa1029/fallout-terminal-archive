'''Tiffany Brantley''' is a [[Fire Breathers]] initiate residing in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Tiffany can be found at the [[Charleston Fire Department]] studying for the Fire Breathers knowledge exam. She previously completed the fitness exam with ease, but is having trouble with the knowledge exam, and refuses to cheat by allowing an intelligent [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]] to give her answers or using [[Mentats (Fallout 76)|Mentats]] to get an edge. She is very enthusiastic about becoming a Fire Breather and fighting the [[Scorched]], claiming that it is her duty to do so.

===Other interactions===
An intelligent player character (4+) can attempt to help Tiffany through the knowledge exam by giving her the answers. She will refuse, stating that if she will be out in the wasteland helping others, it will be necessary for her to actually know and understand what she is doing, but will give the player character 24 [[10mm round (Fallout 76)|10mm rounds]].
