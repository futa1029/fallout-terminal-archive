'''Fritz''' is a former [[Crater Raiders|Crater Raider]] living at [[the Whitespring]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Once a raider, Fritz has denounced that type of life, in favor of the posh and fancy lifestyle of the pre-[[Great War|War]] elites, largely because he is bored. He dresses in a golf outfit he found on one of the bodies in the Whitespring, and spends his days at the [[The Whitespring driving range|driving range]] just down the road from [[the Whitespring Golf Club]]. 

He puts on a fake [[United Kingdom|British]] accent, yet if a speech check is passed, the player character can remark on the curiosity of the situation, leading to him abandoning the accent and confessing his ruse, before returning to the facade.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"[Perception 4+] Love your little get-up. Did you loot it off some bodies around here?"''<br />Fritz: ''"Get-up? Well, I never-! Alright, you got me. I'm a Raider who's got nothing better to do, okay? I spend my days fending off irradiated montrosities to survive, anyway. Why not make some fun out of it?"''</ref>

===Other interactions===
Fritz will engage in dialogue about himself and the perks of being wealthy, on top of providing background information about the Whitespring. If a [[Perception (Fallout 76)|Perception]] check of 4 is successfully completed, he will reveal himself, though he will continue to speak in his phony accent, even after this check is passed.
