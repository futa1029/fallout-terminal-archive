'''From mom''' is a [[Wild Appalachia notes|paper note]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Wild Appalachia]]''.
__TOC__
==Location==
The note is found in the [[Van Lowe Taxidermy]] basement, next to a washing machine and dryer. It is obtainable during [[The Lowe-Down]].

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
[[Shelley van Lowe|Shell]],
I don't want our last days together to be filled my with my "nagging," (as [[Aries|Calvin]] puts it), so I'm writing a few things down for you to read later...

REMINDERS:
-   Wash your whites separately. Remember that time you threw your red socks in??
-   If you have to wash your more delicate "unmentionables," remember to dry them with LOW HEAT -- you wouldn't want to ruin them.
-   Go easy on the [[Abraxo cleaner (Fallout 4)|Abraxo]]! Trust me on that one.

And most of all, remember I'll always love your brother and you, my little Shell-bell! 
- Mom
}}

==Notes==
The note is removed from the player character's inventory once the quest is completed.
