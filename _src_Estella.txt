'''Estella''' is a deactivated [[Miss Nanny (Fallout 76)|Miss Nanny]] at [[Vault-Tec University]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
A Mister Handy playing the role of an assistant to [[Loris|Doctor Loris]], Estella rushed in to help [[Kelemen]] in the reactor wing. Sealed in with him, she used her supply of radiation drugs to keep him alive long enough to fix the reactor and save the vault - before perishing alongside him.
