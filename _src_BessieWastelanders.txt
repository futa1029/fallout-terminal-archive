'''Bessie''' is a [[Sentry bot (Fallout 76)|sentry bot]] belonging to [[Duchess]] in [[Appalachia]] {{In|FO76WL}}.

==Background==
Following its retirement from the military, Bessie was acquired by Duchess as a bargain due to its integrated weapons being removed, with a pair of [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] hands where its multi-barreled machine guns normally are. She used it as a "pack ox-forklift hybrid" to haul the materials and supplies she would use to set up [[the Wayward]]. Later, [[Sol]] gave it a cow-like paint job and voice.<ref>[[Duchess]]: ''"Great, isn't she? I got her for a steal because the military took all her guns when they retired her. She's the pack ox-forklift hybrid I never knew I wanted. I never would've been able to haul all this stuff up here without her, let alone build this place up. Was Sol's idea to paint her, 'upgrade' her voice module to something more fitting her role."''<br />([[Duchess/Dialogue|Duchess' dialogue]])</ref>

Currently, Bessie resides in the [[Brahmin (Fallout 76)|brahmin]] pen next to the Wayward. With its synthesized voice, it makes cow-like sounds and occasionally describes cow-like sound effects. From time to time, [[Jide]] will check if Bessie's combat systems are still offline.<ref>[[Jide]]: ''"{{Tooltip|Bessie. Threat assessment.|Stern, but just a little nervous. You're not talking to livestock. You're talking to a combat robot.}} {{Tooltip|Bessie... initiate combat protocols.|Stern, but just a little nervous. You're not talking to livestock. You're talking to a combat robot. Give a good beat after "Bessie."}}"''<br />Bessie: ''"Moo."''<br />Jide: ''"{{Tooltip|Good, good... god help us if those ever start working again.|A bit relieved. You were testing this robot's former voice commands. They still don't work.}}"''<br/>([[Jide/Dialogue|Jide]] and [[Bessie (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Bessie's dialogue]])</ref>
