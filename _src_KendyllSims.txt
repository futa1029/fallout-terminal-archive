'''Kendyll Sims''' is a deceased member of the [[Free States]], found in [[Dyer Chemical]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Kendyll Sims was one of the most capable sharpshooters in [[Harpers Ferry]], even before the Free States returned to the town in the wake of [[Great War]].<ref>[[Restoring order (holotape)]]</ref> She was a member of [[Duncan McKann]]'s failed expedition to track down [[Feral ghoul (Fallout 76)|ghouls]] from the [[Valley Galleria]].<ref>[[Tracking Unknowns]]</ref> When the expedition finally cornered the ghouls in [[Dyer Chemical]], Kendyll recognized [[Lucy Harwick]] when she spoke and was distressed by McKann's desire to kill the group despite them clearly being in control of their mental faculties. McKann shot and killed Kendyll when she turned her gun on him after this revelation.<ref>[[Lucy's holotape]]</ref><ref>[[Randy's holotape]]</ref>
