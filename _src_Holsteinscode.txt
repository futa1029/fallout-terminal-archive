'''Holstein's code''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
The note can be found at [[Big Bend Tunnel east]], on [[Holstein|Sgt. Holstein's]] corpse. It is on the floor of a train car outside of the [[Blue Ridge Caravan Company]] outpost.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
Sgt. Holstein, your code phrase for the Master Terminal is "Nuclear Dash." Show all those slowpokes how to keep the pace.

-[[Melody Larkin|Melody]]
}}

==Notes==
Before the ''[[Wastelanders]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]], Holstein's corpse, and thus this note, was found on the train tracks near the gate to the [[Big Bend Tunnel]] interior. When the Blue Ridge outpost was added with ''Wastelanders'', Holstein's corpse was moved to be outside of the outpost, inside of the train car.
