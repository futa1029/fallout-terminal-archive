'''John Aaronholt''' was a resident of [[Appalachia]] who died before [[Timeline#2102|2102]]. His corpse can be found sitting a chair on the upper level of the barn at the [[Aaronholt homestead]].

==Background==
John Aaronholt lived with his brothers [[Lowell Aaronholt|Lowell]] and [[Shawn Aaronholt|Shawn]] at their family farm after the [[Great War|War]]. He died after being attacked by some [[Mongrel (Fallout 76)|rabid dogs]] who severely wounded his leg. He was unable to get medicine for his injuries as he and his brother Shawn were unable to find the key to the granary where their supplies had been stockpiled. He died convinced that Lowell had hidden the key,<ref>[[John's last words]]</ref> thus condemning John to die from his infected leg and Shawn to die from radiation sickness.
