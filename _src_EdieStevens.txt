'''Edie Stevens''' is a deceased woman whose remains are found within [[wendigo cave]] in [[Appalachia]] {{In|FO76}}. 

==Background==
Edie was the wife of [[Morris Stevens]], the former leader of the [[Gourmands]]. Both she and her husband were exiled for violating the gang's code, which states to not feast on other members, after the half-eaten body of [[Gregory (Gourmands)|Gregory]] was discovered in Morris' room.<ref>[[Bolton Greens terminal entries#Entry 05|Bolton Greens terminal entries; Gourmand's terminal, Entry 05]]</ref><ref>[[Bolton Greens terminal entries#Entry 06|Bolton Greens terminal entries; Gourmand's terminal, Entry 06]]</ref> 

Following their exile, the couple lived in the wendigo cave together.<ref>[[Gourmands note]]</ref> While Morris went out for hunting, he did not share the meat with Edie, forcing her to live off of whatever remains he left for her. She also noted that both of them were changing.<ref>[[Edie's note]]</ref> In the end, Edie was killed and devoured by her husband.<ref>[[Morris's end]]</ref>
