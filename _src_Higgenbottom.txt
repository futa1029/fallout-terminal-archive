'''Higgenbottom''' is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] living in [[Vault 76]].

==Background==
Higgenbottom is one of the many Mister Handies that serve Vault 76. It can be found at Station #3 (Power is Key!) on [[Reclamation Day]], offering building supplies (five [[Cloth (Fallout 76)|cloth]], five [[Steel (Fallout 76)|steel]], five [[Wood (Fallout 76)|wood]] and three [[Adhesive (Fallout 76)|adhesive]]) to [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] exiting the vault.
