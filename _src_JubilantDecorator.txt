'''Jubilant Decorator''' is a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] operating in [[Helvetia]] as part of the [[Fasnacht Day]] festivities.

==Background==
Jubilant Decorator is one of the Protectrons which was reprogrammed to spice up Fasnacht Day celebrations in Helvetia.<ref>[[Fasnacht robots]]</ref><ref>[[Helvetia Protectron updates]]</ref> It can be distinguished by its sky blue chassis, painter's toque (when it is not wearing its Fasnacht mask), black mustache (when it is not wearing its Fasnacht mask), and nailgun modification for its left hand.

When Fasnacht Day is being celebrated, it is one of the Protectron parade marchers which may be encountered. If it is required to march in the parade, it can be found in the red barn across the street from Helvetia's post office and workshop. It will request that the player character(s) decorate the Helvetia barn before it will march in the parade.

In [[Timeline#2102|2102]], the Jubilant Decorator wore the [[Fasnacht goblin mask]] when marching in the parade. In [[Timeline#2103|2103]], the Protectron wears a random Fasnacht mask with an alternate color scheme.
