'''James Addison''' is a deceased member of [[the Whitespring Resort]] staff whose body can be found in [[the Deep]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
After the Whitespring Resort suffered several minor earthquakes for unknown reasons, James became convinced that they were unnatural and set off to find their source despite being mocked by his coworkers. Diving into the cave systems below the Whitespring, he came across the [[Motherlode Acquisition Facility]], but mistook it for an "underground spaceship."

He decided that it was necessary to tell the [[Last President of the United States|president]] about his discovery and believed that he would be commended as a hero on his return.<ref>[[President's eyes ONLY]]</ref> However, he died before he could tell anyone of his discovery.
