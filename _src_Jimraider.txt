'''Jim''', referred to as a '''raider corpse''' in-game, is a deceased [[Raiders (Fallout 76)|raider]] at the [[raider outhouse and moat]]. His aesthetic appearance is randomly generated.

==Background==
Jim was told about a strange place in the mountains between the [[West Tek research center (Fallout 76)|West Tek research center]] and the [[National Isolated Radio Array]]. [[Randal (Fallout 76)|Randal]] told Jim that he would be unable to miss the "big red door in the middle of nowhere." Although neither of them knew who built it or what was at the location, Jim went to investigate and was ultimately killed at the moat.<ref>[[Raider note (Fallout 76)|Raider note]]</ref>
