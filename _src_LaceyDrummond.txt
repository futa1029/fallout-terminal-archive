'''Lacey Drummond''' is a woman exploring the [[wasteland]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Curious explorers from the ruins of Virginia, Lacey and [[Isela Mejia]] have set themselves up outside of [[Vault 76]], seeking answers for the questions that brought them to Appalachia in the first place. They heard news on a broadcast about a buried treasure, but the details of it were vague. Prior to their arrival, in exchange for a lead about the treasure, the two were apparently scammed out of the last of their [[Cap (Fallout 76)|caps]] by a man at [[the Wayward]] who she recalls being named "Carter". Lacey and Isela have not been in Appalachia for long, but noticed the area was hit hard; despite this, she mentions Appalachia is still better than their previous travels.

===Other inteactions===
With a Charisma of 2 or higher, the player can ask if Lacey has a spare weapon, upon which she will give them a [[Pipe revolver (Fallout 76)|pipe revolver]] and a [[Machete (Fallout 76)|machete]]. Note that this option is only available as long as one is level 5 or below, and does not have a gun in their inventory.

==Notes==
* With the ''[[Expeditions: Atlantic City]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]], Lacey and Isela have been moved closer to the [[Vault 76]] entrance, as opposed to further down the stairs, where they are currently.
* If the player character lies to Lacey, saying that Vault 76 is the site of a treasure, Isela mutters "''ay, pendeja''" ("idiot") in Spanish under her breath before speaking to Lacey once more.

==Appearances==
Lacey appears only in ''[[Fallout 76]]'', introduced in the ''[[Wastelanders]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]].

==Behind the scenes==
Lacey is voiced by [[Mara Junot]], who also voiced [[Meg Groberg]] and [[Elsie Taylor]]. In localized versions of the game, her voice is dubbed by Chieko Miyazaki (Japanese) and Nieves "Nikki" García (Spanish). Lacey's English and Japanese voice actresses share the same roles, but this is not the case for the Spanish dub.
