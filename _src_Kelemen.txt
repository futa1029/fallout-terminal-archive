'''Kelemen''' is a deactivated [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] at [[Vault-Tec University]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
A Mister Handy playing the role of apprentice to [[Brass|Head Engineer Brass]], Kelemen rushed in to fix the reactor to prevent radiation from overrunning the vault. Worried about contamination, [[Loris|Doctor Loris]] sealed the reactor wing with him inside, dooming Kelemen to death. This would become the bone of contention between Loris and her well-trained medical staff and the engineers. For his part, Kelemen and [[Estella]] sacrificed their lives to save the vault.
