'''Biv E. Ridge'''<ref>[[Biv/Dialogue#15|Biv's dialogue]]: ''"You know, if life ever does get you down, don't be afraid to consult ole Biv E. Ridge about what's eatin' ya."''</ref> is a drink tester [[Robobrain (Fallout 76)|Robobrain]] operating in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Biv is found in the backroom of [[The Nukashine]] inside [[Big Al's Tattoo Parlor]]. Biv was acquired by [[Lewis (Wild Appalachia)|Lewis]], whose mother worked for [[General Atomics International]] before the [[Great War]]. Whether Biv was acquired legally was never explained by Lewis to [[Judy Lowell]] or the rest of Eta Psi.<ref>[[Eta Psi house terminal entries#Biv]]: ''"I just found out that Lewis’s mother works at General Atomics - not from him, of course. [...] Anyways, that explains where he got the Robobrain from. It doesn't explain HOW he got it, if it's safe... if it's legal."''</ref>

Lewis planned to use Biv to test [[Nukashine]].<ref>[[Eta Psi house terminal entries#Robobrain]]: ''"He slipped a note under his door that said, 'Need drink tester.'"''</ref> This was likely the best course of action, as a human who consumed that amount of alcohol from each drink would die from alcohol poisoning. It is unclear as to whether Biv's drunken manner of speech is a result of alcohol taste-testing, or if he was originally programmed to always sound a little intoxicated.

===Quests===
* [[Tipsy Taste-Test]]: In this repeatable daily quest, Biv will ask the player character to drink a specific type of alcoholic beverage and then perform some S.P.E.C.I.A.L. themed task before its effects expire (typically 3 minutes). The main incentive for this quest is unlocking one of thirteen possible drink recipes.
* [[Wasted on Alcohol]]: In this repeatable daily quest, Biv wants the player character to craft a new drink for his daily quality test.
* [[Wasted on Nukashine]]: This side quest allows the player character learn how to create Nukashine, as well as how to craft the [[brewing station]] and [[fermenter]].
