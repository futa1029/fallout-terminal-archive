'''Kit''' is a sergeant in the [[Hellcat Company]], a group of mercenaries operating in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
A member of Hellcat Company, Kit came from up north.<ref>[[A farewell to Pittsburgh]]</ref> Kit was hired by [[Edgar Blackburn]] to provide him test subjects, Kit kidnapped members of both the [[Blue Ridge Caravan Company]] and [[Crater Raiders|raiders]] for the doctor's experiments. Blackburn was happy with his efforts, especially in capturing Blue Ridge employees, and considered the raiders a good contrast to the caravaners, but asked that he try capturing subjects who were not such heavy chem users in the future.<ref>[[Blue Ridge delivers again]]</ref>
