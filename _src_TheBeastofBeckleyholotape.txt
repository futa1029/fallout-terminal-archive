'''The Beast of Beckley''' is a [[Wild Appalachia holotapes|holotape]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Wild Appalachia]]''.

==Location==
The holotape is found at [[Priblos' Curios]], on a crate next to a [[Brahmin skull (Fallout 76)|brahmin skull]]. It is obtained during [[Unsolved: Death and Taxidermy]].
