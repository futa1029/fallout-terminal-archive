'''Got that thing you wanted''' is a [[Steel Dawn notes|paper note]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Steel Dawn]]''.

==Location==
The note can be found in the [[Foundation supply room]], on [[Tad Chance]]'s table, on the southern end of the interior.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
[[Tad Chance|T]],

It took a bit of haggling, but I managed to procure a limited print Tricentennial Protectron figure-it even still has the little flag and everything. It's not in-box (of course) but I'd say it's near mint. Next time I'm by, let's talk prices?

And that request for one of our backpacks? I don't know about that, my friend. We don't just give them out to anyone, but I understand: they're kind of a collector's item, eh? Maybe I might be able to get you one... if you're finally willing to part with that Grognak "Grog-na-rok Battle Damage" Axe replica!

-A
}}
