'''Restoring order''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] {{In|FO76}}. It is recorded by [[Eddie Hayes]].
__TOC__
==Location==
It can be found in [[Harpers Ferry]], next to the north gate terminal, near the building with the large [[Grey Tortoise]] advertisement.

==Transcript==
[[File:Restoring_order_(holotape).ogg|center]]
{{Transcript|text=
Things are finally starting to shape up around here at Harpers Ferry. We got a lot of defenses up and running, a real perimeter, and people taking lookout shifts. [[Niraj Singh|Niraj]] is working on some new contraption to help with these brutal storms that sweep through. He said they could filter the air or something. And [[Ella Ames|Ella's]] got a decent clinic set up with the help of the [[Responders]]. It makes me feel like maybe [[Raleigh Clay|Raleigh]] was right, and we can have a real go at this.

Some of these survivors proved they aren't completely worthless, too. One guy, [[Duncan McKann|Duncan]], turned out to be a decent hunter, and another gal, [[Kendyll Sims|Kendyll]], she's a better shot than me. Still, you look at these people struggling here and just think... You bastards had your chance. We practically spelled it out for you and you just turned up your noses and called us crazy. Bunch of idiots...
}}
