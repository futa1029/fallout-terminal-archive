'''Ronny''' is a raider in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Ronny is the niece of [[Edwin (Wastelanders)|Edwin]], the leader of [[Edwin's gang|a gang]] of raiders. With Edwin being sick, Ronny has taken over leadership of the gang. During [[Thicker Than Water]] she helps [[Beckett (Wastelanders)|Beckett]] and the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] gain access to the [[Watoga Underground]] to finally kill the leader of the [[Blood Eagles]], [[the Claw]].

In the aftermath of aforementioned events, Edwin retires and Ronny officially declares herself the leader of the gang. Beckett mentions she will occasionally visit the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]'s [[CAMP|C.A.M.P.]], as long as Beckett's bar is open.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What's Ronny up to now, anyway?"''<br />[[Beckett (Wastelanders)|Beckett]]: ''"Now that Edwin's retired, Ronny is the full-time leader of the gang. She said she'll swing by your C.A.M.P. once in a while and say hello... as long as the bar's open, of course."''<br />([[Beckett (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Beckett's dialogue]])</ref>

===Quest===
[[Thicker Than Water]]: Ronny meets Beckett and the Vault Dwellers at the entrance to the Watoga Underground, explains the situation, and hands over the [[Watoga Underground key|key]].

===Effects of player's actions===
* If the Vault Dweller asks Ronny why she hasn't visited their C.A.M.P., Ronny sarcastically mentions she thought it would be better if her gang didn't know where all the Vault Dweller's valuables are.
* If [[Frankie Beckett]] is killed, Beckett mentions he talked to Ronny and she buried Frankie for him because he did not deal with burying his brother himself. If Frankie survived, Beckett mentions she will help with Frankie's recovery from his addictions.
