'''Janelle's revenge''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Wild Appalachia]]''.

==Location==
Obtainable during [[Unsolved: Death and Taxidermy]], this holotape can be found near [[Janelle Priblo|Janelle Priblo's]] skeleton, east of her camp and north of the [[Hornwright air purifier site 04]].
