'''Botsmith Amp''' is a botsmith for the [[Rust Eagles]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Amp, as fitting her name, is energetic and lighthearted with a flair for style.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Why do they call you Amp?"''<br />Botsmith Amp: ''"{{Tooltip|That's an easy one!|Excited.}} {{Tooltip|Everywhere I go, I add a jolt of energy to the mix.|Sincere. She prides herself on this.}} {{Tooltip|You've run into tons of boring old junk in the Wasteland, right?|Expects the player to affirm this.}} {{Tooltip|Some people try to fix it, restore it to what it was before the war.|Matter-of-fact, explaining.}} {{Tooltip|But me? I add my own flair and make something new.|Proud. Her creativity and ability to remix things is a big part of her persona.}}"''<br />([[Botsmith Amp/Dialogue|Botsmith Amp's dialogue]])</ref> She designs the ranged and exploding battle bots and considers her botsmithing to be superior to [[Beatrice the Wrench|Beatrice's]]. Prior to the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]]' arrival, she destroyed the scoreboard to claim the record for most kills in a single match.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What do you do here again?"''<br />Botsmith Amp: ''"{{Tooltip|I'm a Rust Eagle botsmith, of course. But you knew that just by looking around, didn't you?|Friendly, but treating the player like it should be obvious.}} {{Tooltip|If it shoots, shocks, or explodes, you know I made it.|If it shoots, shocks, or explodes, you know I made it.}} {{Tooltip|You're looking at the Rust Eagle recordholder for kills in a single arena match.|Proud of having the deadliest bots.}} {{Tooltip|Sure, Beatrice'll tell you that she has more kills overall, but I wiped that slate clean. Now, it's about what you've done lately.|Proud of the fact that she physically destroyed the old scoreboard when she says she wiped the slate clean.}}"''<br />([[Botsmith Amp/Dialogue|Botsmith Amp's dialogue]])</ref><ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What do you do here again?"''<br />[[Beatrice the Wrench]]: ''"{{Tooltip|I'm the best damn botsmith the Rust Eagles have, and Buzzsaw knows it.|Proud of herself and her abilities.}} {{Tooltip|We used to count kills out in the arena. Right up until Amp 'accidentally' destroyed the board, anyway.|Bitter about the loss of public recognition for her kills record.}} {{Tooltip|All that shame lit a fire under her. Now, I got a real rival.|But happy now that she has a legitimate bot designer rival}}"''<br />([[Beatrice the Wrench/Dialogue|Beatrice's dialogue]])</ref>

She has a lot of respect for the [[Gladiators of Steel|Gladiators]] and their ability to coordinate as a team, but is critical of their inability to please the crowd.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What do you think of the Gladiators of Steel?"''<br />Botsmith Amp: ''"{{Tooltip|They're good at what they do, I'll give them that.|Begrudging.}} {{Tooltip|I've never seen a team so coordinated in the ring. I'll bet it has to do with that spotter they use.|Genuinely admires that they use tactics to win battles.}} {{Tooltip|But it's just so rote. Where's the art, the pageantry, the performance?|But ultimately, they just don't have the showmanship she's looking for.}}"''<br />([[Botsmith Amp/Dialogue|Botsmith Amp's dialogue]])</ref> Although she gets upset when they trash her bots, she sees it as an opportunity to address a flaw in her design. Each match is another step forward on her path to creating the perfect, unstoppable killing machines.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Why do you keep making bots if they're destined to be destroyed?"''<br />Botsmith Amp: ''"{{Tooltip|Because one day, they won't be. And that's how you'll know you're in trouble.|Serious. She intends to use her champion killing machines once the games are all done.}} {{Tooltip|Sure, it hurts every time one of my bots gets turned into a heap of scrap.|Honest. She doesn't like seeing her creations get destroyed.}} {{Tooltip|But it's also one more flaw I can fix.|And that's why it's worth it.}}{{Tooltip||}}"''<br />([[Botsmith Amp/Dialogue|Botsmith Amp's dialogue]])</ref>
