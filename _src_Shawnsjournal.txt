'''Shawn's journal''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.
__TOC__
==Location==
The note can be found on the body of [[Shawn Aaronholt]], in the bed of a pickup truck in front of the houses at the [[Aaronholt homestead]].

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
Shawn Aaronholt's Journal

[[John Aaronholt|John]] and I are sick of hearing [[Lowell Aaronholt|Lowell]] talk about [[Chicago]] all the damn time. Lowell wants us to pack up and head that way. Who gives a shit about Chicago? I always thought we should travel more south to see if any of our cousins survived. 

John and I can't find the [[Aaronholt granary shed key|key to the granary shed]] where we hoarded ammo and medicine. Radiation sickness has taken the best of me. Without meds I'll probably die within days.
}}
