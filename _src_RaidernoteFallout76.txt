'''Raider note''' refers to two [[Fallout 76 notes|paper notes]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
* The first note is located at the [[raider outhouse and moat]] southwest of the [[National Isolated Radio Array]], next to the door.
* The second note is found at [[Twin Lakes]], in a rowboat on the shores of the lake closest to the [[investigator's cabin]].
