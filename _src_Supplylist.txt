'''Supply list''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] that was [[Fallout 76 legacy content|removed]] from ''[[Fallout 76]]''.

==Location==
The note could previously be found in the lobby of [[Watoga Emergency Services]], on [[Vivie Steinbeck]]'s corpse. It was removed along with Vivie in [[Fallout 76 patch 1.5.1.26|patch 1.5.1.26]].
