'''Brother Moncrief's note''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}. It is written by Brother [[Moncrief]].
__TOC__
==Location==
It can be found on the top floor of [[Landview Lighthouse]] on a crate next to the skeleton of Brother Moncrief.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
High Priestess,

I was right. The [[Firefly|Fireflies]] come here to mate. They go a little crazy, and I got hurt pretty bad, but I was able to gather enough [[Bioluminescent fluid|goo]] to light the lamp.

Now I just have to wait. The Great [[Mothman]] will come. He has to.
}}
