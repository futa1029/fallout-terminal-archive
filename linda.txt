'''Letter to Linda''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
The note can be found down the road from [[relay tower HN-B1-12]], held in the hand of a military skeleton in an [[off-road vehicle]].

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
''[[Linda (Fallout 76)|Linda]], baby, this is [[Charlie (soldier)|Charlie]]. [[Philip (Fallout 76)|Philip]] said he'd get this to you. I have to make this quick. I know things are crazy right now, but I have to stay at my post. Tell [[Benny (Fallout 76)|Benny]] I love him. I'll be home as soon as I can. '''Stay safe, baby.'''''
}}

==Notes==
An identical copy of this note, though with a different [[form ID]], exists in the game's files, but is not used anywhere in-game.
