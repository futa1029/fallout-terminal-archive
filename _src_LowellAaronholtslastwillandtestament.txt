The '''Lowell Aaronholt's last will and testament''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.
__TOC__
==Location==
It can be found on the corpse of [[Lowell Aaronholt]], who lies in a bathtub in a collapsed shed in the [[Aaronholt homestead]], east of the houses and down in the field.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
To whomever finds my body-

The granary shed holds provisions that should last you a little while. I ponder what became of my brothers [[Shawn Aaronholt|Shawn]] and [[John Aaronholt|John]]. I should have gone to [[Chicago]].

P.S. Watch out for those [[Scorchbeast|giant flying bats]]!
}}
