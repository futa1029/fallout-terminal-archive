'''Magdalene<ref name="ChaseJournal">[[Chase Terrier's journal]]</ref> "Maggie" Stern''', called '''Pita''' (Pain in the Ass) by her peers, is an underage [[Secret Service (Fallout 76)|Secret Service]] agent living in [[Vault 79]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Maggie Stern is the daughter of [[Mercedes Stern]]. She was born of an unintentional pregnancy in [[Vault 79]], though Mercedes has opted not to reveal the identity of the father. Instead, the male agents all stepped up to co-father the child, each contributing in their own way to help raise her, using this generosity as a way to come together and cope.<ref name="ChaseJournal" /> Maggie was raised to become a child agent, while also developing a rude attitude, earning her the nickname of "PITA" (short for pain in the ass).<ref>[[Reginald Stone]]: ''"{{Tooltip|Agent Stern should never have had a child. That little brat is a pain in the ass. But we just call her PITA for short.|see it as a breach of regulations to have a child}}"''</ref> She claims to have training in shooting firearms and to have her own personal silenced [[10mm pistol (Fallout 76)|10mm pistol]] with a light frame and short barrel, with her mother wanting to give her a calibrated [[.44 pistol (Fallout 76)|.44 pistol]] with a short scope and comfort grip for her birthday.<ref>Maggie Stern: ''"Mom says I can get a calibrated .44 with a comfort grip and a short scope for my birthday. I still get to keep my light frame 10mm short barrel with the silencer though."''</ref>

Born after [[Shorty (Wastelanders)|Shorty]]'s mutiny,<ref name="ChaseJournal" /> Maggie had not experienced any major conflict until recently when the vault reactor malfunctioned, causing most [[Secret Service agent|agents]] in the vault to either die or become [[Feral ghoul (Fallout 76)|feral ghouls]]. She, her mother and a few other agents managed to lock themselves in part of the vault, though they are running out of supplies and will perish without rescue.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What happened down here?"''<br />[[Derek Garrison]]: ''"We were doing routine work in this part of the vault when the reactor went. Next thing we knew half our people were turned into monsters trying to kill us. Worse, the fighting activated the security systems and robots. It was either lock ourselves in here or die. That was almost three months ago. We're running out of food and water, so it's a good thing you showed up."''</ref>
