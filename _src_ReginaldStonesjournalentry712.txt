'''Reginald Stone's journal, entry 712''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Wastelanders]]''.

==Location==
The tape can be found in [[Vault 79]], in a side room slightly north of where the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] first encounter [[Slick]]. It is to the left of the large staircase leading up to the reactor area. After [[Secrets Revealed]] is completed, most sections of the vault become inaccessible and this item can no longer be retrieved.
