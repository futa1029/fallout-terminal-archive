Maj. '''Darion T. Jones''' is a deceased former member of the [[Responders]] who resided in [[Appalachia]]. His body contains the [[signal repeater notes]] and [[signal repeater schematic|schematic]].

==Background==
Darion Jones was a former soldier in the [[United States Armed Forces]] prior to joining the Responders. His role in the Responders made him primarily responsible for crafting ammunition.<ref>[[Morgantown Airport terminal entries#Other Members|Morgantown Airport terminal entries; Responders HQ terminal, personnel files, Other Members]]</ref> He developed a schematic for a signal repeater which the Responders hoped to plug into the [[National Isolated Radio Array]] to boost their radio signal. However, he and his team were killed by [[Raiders (Fallout 76)|raiders]] at the [[Seneca Rocks Visitor Center]] and never completed their mission, leaving the schematics for the repeater on his corpse.<ref>[[Rose (Fallout 76)|Rose]]: ''"{{Tooltip|So, I've got this little radio operation I need to expand... Gotta boost this super weak signal. You know how it is.|Matter of factly, as if everyone has dealt with this problem at some point.}} {{Tooltip|See, I found these plans for a Radio Signal Repeater on a dead Responder out by Seneca Rocks.|He was totally dead when she got there. It's not like she killed him. His friends maybe, but not him.}} {{Tooltip|They were going to patch it through that giant radio array on the other side of the mountain and use it to boost their radio.|Pure information delivery here.}} {{Tooltip|Only... I don't have the plans. Didn't seem important at the time. Aw, I'm kicking myself for not taking the schematic when I found it.|Oops. She's disappointed, but in a "gosh darn, oh well" sort of way.}}"''<br />(Excerpt from [[Rose (Fallout 76)/Dialogue#15|Rose's dialogue]] when starting [[Signal Strength]])</ref>
