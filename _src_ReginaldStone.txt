'''Reginald "Regs" Stone''' is a member of the [[Secret Service (Fallout 76)|Secret Service]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Reginald was a Secret Service member in [[Vault 79]], although he was never field agent material, like [[Mercedes Stern]] and [[Cole Carver]]; however, he is an accomplished requisition officer, as he is highly strict with rules (his nickname Regs, short for both "Reginald" and "regulations" reflects this).<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"So what's the deal with Regs?"''<br/ > [[Cole Carver|Slick]]: ''"His full name is Reginald Stone, but we all call him Regs. He was never field agent material, like Mercy and me. But he's a great requisition guy. Just mind your P's and Q's. He's totally by the books."''<br />([[Cole Carver/Dialogue#132|Slick's dialogue]])</ref> Even after two years inside [[Vault 79]], Reginald made sure that all the gold was stored and accounted for.<ref>[[Reginald Stone's journal, entry 712]]</ref> After the failed mutiny of [[Shorty (Wastelanders)|Shorty]], he started reading more often, mostly old manuals.<ref>[[Reginald Stone's journal]]</ref> He was also the one who helped Mercedes give birth to [[Maggie Stern]].<ref>[[Chase Terrier's journal]]</ref>

However, Reginald now faces a new problem. He and others are trapped in a small, under-supplied area after the vault's reactor malfunctioned, releasing [[Feral ghoul (Fallout 76)|feral ghouls]] and rogue security robots. He now awaits an unlikely rescue.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What happened down here?"''<br /> [[Derek Garrison|AC]]: ''"We were doing routine work in this part of the vault when the reactor went. Next thing we knew half our people were turned into monsters trying to kill us. Worse, the fighting activated the security systems and robots. It was either lock ourselves in here or die. That was almost three months ago. We're running out of food and water, so it's a good thing you showed up."''<br />([[Derek Garrison/Dialogue#249|AC's dialogue]])</ref>
