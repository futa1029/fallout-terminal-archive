概要

Kevan reveals very little about his past prior to becoming a ghoul, though he makes it very clear that his ghoulified form is something that he resents. However, this is not simply due to the nature of being a ghoul itself, but rather because of the societal changes and the universal mistrust associated with being a ghoul.

At some point after turning into a ghoul, Kevan fell in with Lucy Harwick's group of survivors, who were trying to create a place for ghouls to live at Valley Galleria. After the ghouls began being hunted down by Duncan McKann, a trio including Kevan, Elmira and Maddox Mullen split off from the main group and headed east, instead of going to Dyer Chemical. This proved to be the right move, as Lucy's group were eventually killed, while Kevan, Elmira and Maddox covered their tracks and escaped.

The three engaged in raiding to try and survive, as well as gather supplies to make their escape from the area. For their final raid on a camp, they intended to keep things quiet and with minimal damage before anyone knew they had been there. However, the group was not used to such tactics at the time and things went awry. The residents of the camp were alerted to the ghouls' presence, and Elmira chose to make a distraction. She ripped off her mask and thrust herself into the camp's spotlights, and the wastelanders there assumed she was feral. Elmira was killed by the wastelanders, and her sacrifice bought the others, including Kevan, enough time to escape without more casualties.

The survivors eventually came across an abandoned, inactive missile silo far to the north, in the Savage Divide. The settlement became known as Radiant Hills. It was heavily irradiated, perfect for a community of ghouls, and had a non-functional, leaking nuclear warhead inside. The faction has since grown to include more ghouls seeking refuge, and its co-founders, such as Kevan, honor Elmira's sacrifice. Kevan is responsible for security, heading up guard duty at the settlement, and conducts combat training to better prepare the other Revenants for any threat that may come their way.

Kevan was also responsible for setting up the affiliated location of Point Repose, a nearby sanctuary for ghouls going feral to have a place to do so in peace, without harming others. It was initially built up by Kevan to accommodate one woman who started to show signs of going feral, and he did so entirely of his own volition without anyone asking him to create such a place. Point Repose is kept as a sacred place for the Revenants, somewhere for its members to go and eventually be at rest if things ever go bad.

===Ideology and personality=== thumb|250px|Kevan threatening Leamon Price.

Kevan has a dim view on life as a ghoul. He believes it to be an overall negative, and is distrustful of humans after facing discrimination. As a result, he aims to protect his fellow ghouls in spite of their poor circumstances, at the same time rejecting outsiders and dealing with them by whichever means necessary, including violence. Kevan acknowledges that ghouls and humans living together peacefully could occur, but says those who initiate an attempt are taking a big risk, and he does not want to compromise the safety of his people for such a gamble. Kevan is willing to kill outsiders in order to protect not just the Revenants themselves, but also their own identities as ghouls. Kevan and the other Revenants go to great lengths to avoid being seen, otherwise they could be hunted down.

thumb|250px|Kevan murders Madeline Keene.

While Kevan openly admits to raiding and killing, he believes it to be justified out of a desire to protect his fellow ghouls, and says that he and the other Revenants try to avoid collateral damage when possible, rather than just exploiting others for the sake of it, specifically contrasting them to the Crater Raiders. Kevan says he would agree that murder of innocents is not justified, and knows that many will see him and the other Revenants as mere bandits and murderers, but he believes the Revenants are merely "paying back the injustice that was already dealt." To that end, Kevan is a firm adherent to the Revenants' policy of not leaving witnesses, stemming from their desire to survive without disturbance and failed previous attempts at doing things the "nice way."

Kevan has a cold and gruff exterior, and acknowledges that he is not one for conversation. He is not interested in making friends, and believes many other Revenants may be getting too "soft." He refuses to let himself fall into carelessness and expects others to show up on time for important things, such as his combat training routines. However, beneath his rough surface, Kevan does have a soft side, which Parthenia Blankenship teasingly acknowledges. This is best seen in Kevan's interactions with Eugenie, a former member of the Revenants who left Radiant Hills. Kevan and Eugenie disagree on many things, including how the proceeds of her mercantile ventures were being used, but the two remain on good terms nonetheless. Eugenie is grateful to still be in contact with Kevan, who musters up plenty of will to send friendly letters, which is not something in his nature. Eugenie has tried to convince Kevan and Maddox that she is living proof that ghouls and humans can live and work together without issue, but she also acknowledges that they will likely never change their viewpoint, and that is okay with her. Eugenie is grateful to Kevan for taking care of everyone at Radiant Hills, but also reminds him to take care of himself, too.
