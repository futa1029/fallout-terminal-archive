'''Jonah Ito''' is a man living in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
An orphan, Jonah's parents died during the [[Great War]], leaving him and his sister [[Kaede Ito|Kaede]] to survive on their own.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What happened to your family?"''<br />Jonah Ito: ''"My parents are long gone. Killed during the War. {{Tooltip|My sister, Kaede, was a Brotherhood Knight. The best of them, without a doubt. She was so determined to see the world through to a better future.|Kai-ey-day}} So she fought for it, here at Fort Defiance. Now I get to see the future, and she doesn't."''<br />([[Jonah Ito/Dialogue#10|Jonah Ito's dialogue]])</ref>

After the Great War, Kaede joined the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Brotherhood of Steel]] to help fight the [[Scorched]], asking her brother to join with her. Jonah refused his sister's offer to join and instead ran away.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Aren't you worried about your safety?"''<br />Jonah Ito: ''"Why should I be? I've got nothing to live for anymore. {{Tooltip|When the Scorched came to Appalachia, I ran away. Kaede wanted me to join the Brotherhood with her, but I was afraid to fight. Afraid to die.|Kai-ey-day}} Now I see it's better to die for something than to live for nothing."''<br />([[Jonah Ito/Dialogue#16|Jonah Ito's dialogue]])</ref> When the Brotherhood fell to the Scorched, his sister died, making her last stand at [[Fort Defiance]],<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What's wrong?"''<br />Jonah Ito: ''"{{Tooltip|It's... It's my sister... (sob) I thought I was over this already.|The player's question suddenly unleashes another torrent of tears}} She took her final stand here... at Fort Defiance. I should have been here with her. I'm such a coward."''<br />([[Jonah Ito/Dialogue#30|Jonah Ito's dialogue]])</ref> and leaving Jonah with a deep hatred of the creatures.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What do you know about the Scorched?"''<br />Jonah Ito: ''"{{Tooltip|That they're monsters. They'll kill anything and everything that isn't them! And for what? What's the reason?|Overwhelmed by anger}} What's the reason that my sister had to die? {{Tooltip|Sorry... It just feels so cruel. To answer your question, I don't know much.|Regaining his composure}}"''<br />([[Jonah Ito/Dialogue#19|Jonah Ito's dialogue]])</ref>

By [[Timeline#2103|2103]], Jonah can be found mourning his sister in Fort Defiance. Jonah mourns his sister's death and regrets not joining the fight with her.

==Appearances==
Jonah Ito appears only in ''[[Fallout 76]]'', introduced in the ''[[Wastelanders]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]].

==Behind the scenes==
Jonah's surname Ito, as well as his sister's name Kaede, are of [[Japan|Japanese]] origin. Ito refers to the Fujiwara clan in the Japanese city of Ise, while his sister's name means maple/maple tree/maple leaf.
