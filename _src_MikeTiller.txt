'''Mike Tiller''' is a member of the [[Settlers (Wastelanders)|Settlers]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Mike was meant to be helping record a weapon demonstration with the town watch,<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: "''The [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Brotherhood]] doesn't 'make' any weaponry, nor sell them.''"<br />[[Tad Chance]]: "''Oh... Well. The stuff we got definitely has your logos on it. Some guys from the town watch took them out for testing a little earlier. I'm sure you can catch up with them, they normally practice at a small site they set up near the [[East Mountain lookout|East Mountain Lookout]], a bit North of here.''"<br />[[Gloria Chance]]: "''Oh that's right. Mike and them took the weapons out for a test drive. If you head over, I'm sure they'd let you take a look.''"<br />([[Tad Chance/Dialogue#234|Tad]] and [[Gloria Chance/Dialogue#238|Gloria Chance's dialogue]])</ref> ordered by [[Ward (Wastelanders)|Ward]] at the [[East Mountain lookout]]. However, he paid little attention to the demonstration, preferring to work on a [[terminal]]. When another settler asked him to get off the terminal and help with the recording, he went to lock the terminal and then grabbed a rocket launcher incorrectly, causing it to fire and kill the other four settlers.<ref>[[Weapon demonstration]]</ref> 

Upon realizing what he had done, he was mortified and fled to the nearby [[Kerwood Mine]]. He decided to hide out of guilt, believing that the others would view him as a murderer.<ref>Mike Tiller: "''H-h-hold it right there. Don't come any closer!''"<br />[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: "''Okay, okay, let's just take a deep breath.''"<br />Mike Tiller: "''No, you don't understand. What I did, I... Oh, man... I'm Mike. Mike Tiller. Go to [[Foundation]], tell them you found me dead. Because... I'm... I'm never leaving! I'm not going back, you can't make me! They'll never believe me... They'll think I'm a murderer!''"<br />([[Mike Tiller/Dialogue#81|Mike Tiller's dialogue]])</ref>

===Quests===
* [[Supplying Demands]]: During this quest, it is up to the player to convince Mike to forgive himself for the incident and return to [[Foundation]], as well as to return the rocket launchers he took to their [[Brotherhood First Expeditionary Force|rightful owners]], or to kill him.
** If he is spared, he mentions Ward thought it would be best if he is no longer on guard duty, so he helps out Foundation in other ways. He mentions he still has trouble sleeping at night.
** If he is killed, the BOS weapons key can be obtained from his body. When reporting back to [[Gloria Chance|Gloria]] and [[Tad Chance]], the player may choose to lie by claiming there was an accident with the weapons, lie by claiming Mike "went nuts" and killed everyone, or come clean about his death. This will affect the rest of the quest.
