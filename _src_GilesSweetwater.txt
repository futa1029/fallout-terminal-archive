'''Giles Sweetwater''' is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] operating in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
One of the many attractions of Appalachia, [[The Giant Teapot]], has also undergone general automation. A specialized Mister Handy unit, Sweetwater was created for receiving visitors, working in a gift shop and for the brewing of a [[Sweetwater Special Blend|special blend of tea]] with an exquisite taste and delicate aroma.

In 2102 and the subsequent years, Sweetwater recognized that a [[Great War]] had befallen the world, but was not discouraged and was trying to renew this proud tradition of tea drinking. In communicating with the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]], Sweetwater will declare that he lacks a special ingredient for tea - [[honey]]. When agreeing to find it, the quest [[Strange Brew]] will be started.
