The '''Watoga Underground''' is a [[Fallout 76 locations|location]] underneath the [[Appalachia]]n city of [[Watoga]].

==Background==
An automated underground parking garage below the futuristic city, this location was designed to keep the above-ground city walkable, free of illegally parked automobiles, traffic and hit-and-run accidents.<ref name="bethes" /> The expansive series of automated parking garage systems allowed citizens to drop off their cars for storage within silos below ground.<ref name="bethes">[https://fallout.bethesda.net/en/article/1BIgJ6o5k2KBrIbWpDigdE/fallout-76-inside-the-vault-watoga-underground-details-and-double-xp-event Fallout 76: Inside the Vault - Watoga Underground Details and Double XP Event] ([https://web.archive.org/web/20200925065639/https://fallout.bethesda.net/en/article/1BIgJ6o5k2KBrIbWpDigdE/fallout-76-inside-the-vault-watoga-underground-details-and-double-xp-event archived])</ref>

Several garage entry points are found throughout the city, providing entrance to the facility. Pre-War residents could also take advantage of other automated services such as vehicle detailing, maintenance, short and long term storage, and repair.<ref>[[Watoga Underground terminal entries#Services: Car Wash|Watoga Underground terminal entries; First floor terminal, Services: Car Wash/Mechanical]]</ref> A concierge service was available to provide any needed information, and vehicles could be rented, purchased, or sold.<ref>[[Watoga Underground terminal entries#Purchase or Sell a Vehicle|Watoga Underground terminal entries; First floor terminal, Purchase or Sell a Vehicle/Rent a Vehicle]]</ref> The complex featured an expansive waiting area with amenities such as a [[Jooky Hair Salon|hair salon]], fine dining, shops, a pharmacy, and spa services.<ref name="bethes" /> 

To gain entrance, residents were required to enter their personal ID at an entryway terminal where services could be ordered.<ref name="bethes" /> Vehicles could also be searched for via the terminals by owner name or by [[license plate]].<ref>[[Watoga Underground terminal entries#Search By License Plate|Watoga Underground terminal entries; First floor terminal, Search By License Plate/Owner Name]]</ref> The human staff at the parking facility were in charge of safety initiatives such as preventing errors in vehicle routing or calling for a stoppage in case of a threat to human operators.<ref name="risk">[[Watoga Underground terminal entries#Stoppage procedures|Watoga Underground terminal entries; Car wash terminal, Stoppage procedures]]</ref> The procedures outlined that if a situation was determined to be of no actual risk, lost time would be withheld from employee paychecks.<ref name="risk" /> 

Human employees were also utilized for security operations, verifying owners by way of Vehicle Identification Number spot checks, alerting local authorities in regards to those who failed.<ref>[[Watoga Underground terminal entries#Verification Procedures|Watoga Underground terminal entries; Watoga Underground security, Verification Procedures]]</ref> In addition, stock employees were reminded to follow all procedures concerning restocking shelves for the automated repair system, to ensure that each item is placed precisely on the appropriate shelving unit.<ref name="packing">[[Watoga Underground terminal entries#Packing and Arrangement|Watoga Underground terminal entries; Repair station terminal, Packing and Arrangement]]</ref> Due to issues in the past with the procedure, several complaints had been lodged, such as tires being attached to steering wheels and trunks filled with coolant.<ref name="packing" />

When the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] arrive at the facility, the area is inhabited by [[Blood Eagles]]. The location acts as their main base of operations, and the gang has since adorned the silos with logos, signs, and flags.

==Layout==
[[File:FO76WL Watoga Underground (03).jpg|thumb|280px]]
The entrance to the underground is locked until the beginning of the quest [[Thicker Than Water]]. The location is also accessible after the questline's conclusion. Blood Eagles will be present in all instances.

===Entrance===
From ground level, the garage entrance in [[Watoga]] will lead down a set of escalators to a row of standing terminals. From here, Garage A is visible through the windows, and progressing to the floor below leads to two thoroughfares with railway-like pathways for vehicle transport and storage. Garages A, B, and C are connected via these pathways, blocked at some tunnel junctures by vehicles, but circumvented by following the opposite tunnel.

===Garages===
Enemies present throughout the garage sections include [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handies]] and [[Eyebot (Fallout 76)|Eyebots]]. Before reaching the repair area, a jammed door can be interacted with, presenting a multi-option skill check. It can be opened with +8 [[Strength (Fallout 76)|Strength]], +4 [[Intelligence (Fallout 76)|Intelligence]], or +12 [[Luck (Fallout 76)|Luck]], revealing a medical clinic with aid items.

A shopping area is beyond Garage A, including a car dealership and [[Jooky Hair Salon‎]]. The dealership will be inaccessible unless the Thicker Than Water questline is active. Past the shopping area, progressing to Garage B results in encountering hostile [[Automated turret (Fallout 76)|turrets]] and [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|protectrons]]. On the far end of Garage B, one can find a security terminal in an office that will disable the turrets in both Garage B and C. There are [[Ant (Fallout 76)|giant ants]] and [[Radrat (Fallout 76)|radrats]] in this area.
[[File:FO76 Watoga Underground layout 17.png|''Garage pathway''|left|280px]]
Further down the hallway, the signage on the wall indicates Garage C is up ahead, and the path forks. One can traverse down the stairs, leading to a [[tinker's workbench]] and an [[Armor workbench (Fallout 76)|armor workbench]]. A terminal in Garage C can be used to unlock the furthermost garage door and progress into the maintenance area. Equipment for services such as vehicle repair and the detailing depot are affixed to the wall or to adjustable arms. The long term storage section can also be discovered but the garage sections are inaccessible.

Further down the hallway at this point will lead to a side path labeled "high speed transfer" where several [[Blood Eagles|Blood Eagle]] guards are standing watch. The path ahead traverses past double doors and through the successive depots. On the left toward the end of the path, there is a terminal that can shut down the nearby turret. A branching righthand side doorway leads to a [[Power armor station (Fallout 76)|power armor station]].

===Silos===
[[File:FO76 Watunderg 4.png|thumb|280px]]
These towers are where the vehicles were stored, still situated in their assigned sections up and down the perimeter walls. They can be examined by the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] but have no interactions available. The vehicles line the round silo, reaching upward. A large pillar in the middle orchestrates a cart apparatus, placing and moving vehicles into the open slots. The first silo encountered is devoid of Blood Eagle accouterment, but the remaining have been outfitted by the gang's symbols and decor.

The silos contain a large number of pristine [[Fallout 76 vehicles|vehicles]], including [[Atomic V-8 (Fallout 76)|Atomic V-8s]] and [[Corvega Blitz|Blitzes]], [[Cherry Bomb (Fallout 76)|Cherry Bombs]], [[Fusion Voyager]]s, and [[Pick-R-Up truck (Fallout 76)|Pick-R-Up trucks]]. These variants of vehicles will not explode if struck. 

Past Silo 3, the path continues down a hallway. The righthand side doorway leads to a [[Chemistry station (Fallout 76)|chemistry station]]. A large contingent of gang members and robots are present in Silo 4. A wooden walkway bypasses the blocked garage doors and on the other side, a button can be activated to open the main garage door, leading back to the salon area where the turrets can be disabled via terminal and the Thicker Than Water questline concludes.

* [[Fallout 76 keys#Watoga Underground key|Watoga Underground key]] - Given by [[Beckett (Wastelanders)|Beckett]] during [[Thicker Than Water]] after dealing with the Claw.
* [[Nuka-Cola Cranberry]] - In the medical clinic near Garage C, through the jammed door, on the counter along the northwestern wall.
* [[Collectible baseball (Fallout 76)|Collectible baseball]] - In the security area of the Watoga Underground, on the console in the middle of the room on the right.
* Two [[Mini nuke detonator shell (Fallout 76)|mini nuke detonator shells]] - In the production storage area overlooking the car repairing stations, on a large metal shelf close to a power armor station.
* Two [[Mini nuke hemisphere core (Fallout 76)|mini nuke hemisphere cores]] - In the production storage area overlooking the car repairing stations, on a large metal shelf in the middle of the area, on the left shelf.
* [[Mini nuke stabilizer fins (Fallout 76)|Mini nuke stabilizer fins]] - In the production storage area overlooking the car repairing stations, on a large metal shelf in the middle of the area, on the right shelf.
* Five [[Fusion core (Fallout 76)|fusion cores]]:
** One can be found in the first storage garage encountered, on a table underneath the stairs.
** Two can be found in the production storage area overlooking the car repairing stations, on a conveyor belt.
** One can be found in Silo 4, on a desk next to the Watoga Underground Security terminal.
** One can be found past Silo 4, at the final shopping plaza where the Claw is encountered, on a Blood Eagle in power armor.
* Potential [[Fallout 76 weapon mods|weapon mod]] - In the production storage area overlooking the car repairing stations, on a large shelf facing the steps and conveyor belt, on the right.
* Two potential [[Fallout 76 recipes|recipes]]:
** In the dining area near Garage C, in the corner of the kitchen.
** At the final shopping plaza where the Claw is encountered, on the second floor, in the dining area, in a locked storage room on a kitchen counter.
