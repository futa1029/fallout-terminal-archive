'''Courtney Kelly''' is a deceased hunter and member of the [[Free States]] whose body can be found in [[Appalachia]]. 

==Background==
Courtney was a member of [[Duncan McKann]]'s failed expedition to track down [[Feral ghoul (Fallout 76)|ghouls]] from the [[Valley Galleria]]. She and fellow expedition member [[Jacqueline Murphy]] were wounded early on in the expedition and were left in the care of the expedition's [[Mister Gutsy (Fallout 76)|Mister Gutsy]], [[Hardball]], to recuperate.<ref>[[Hardball]]: ''"I have wounded to protect, and re-establishing communications with Duncan McKann is vital."''<br />([[Hardball/Dialogue]])</ref>

However, Courtney did not survive the ordeal and perished along with Jacqueline. Their corpses can be found northeast of the [[ransacked bunker]], past the rock wall. Before she died, Courtney left behind a [[Courtney's note|note]], asking the finder to find a home for her two cats, Lulu and Misty, and even made some suggestions regarding who might be considered a suitable carer.<ref>[[Courtney's note]]</ref>
