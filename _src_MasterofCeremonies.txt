The '''Master of Ceremonies''' is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] operating in [[Helvetia]].

==Background==
Master of Ceremonies is a Mister Handy which was reprogrammed to spice up [[Fasnacht Day]] celebrations in Helvetia.<ref>[[Fasnacht robots]]</ref><ref>[[Helvetia Protectron updates]]</ref> It conducts all festivities related to Fasnacht Day in the town, including leading the parade and seeking help from passersby to ensure that the festival goes off without a hitch. It sports a black chassis and wears a black top hat embellished with a red ribbon. It also holds a violin, trumpet, and flute.

When it is not leading the parade, it can be found on the bridge between the Helvetia post office and [[Freyja's Haus Restaurant]]. During the parade, it plays a 15-second loop of [[Fallout 76 soundtrack#Fasnacht Day|''One More Pils'']]. To alert players of an incoming assault, the festive music garbles and slows down. During the combat phase, the loop is played at a faster tempo than during the march. Once the combat ends, the same music is played to denote the parade moving again.
