'''Relay tower HN-B1-12''' is a [[Fallout 76 locations|location]] in [[the Forest]] region of [[Appalachia]].

==Layout==
This location is a standard radio relay tower with [[machinegun turret]]s guarding its entrances, formerly controlled by the [[United States Armed Forces|U.S. military]].<ref>[[Letter to Linda]]</ref> Inside is a blue [[Relay tower HN-B1-12 terminal entries|system relay terminal]] which US government supply requisition holotapes can be loaded into to call for supply drops. There is a bank of lockers along its southwestern wall and a locked [[Footlocker (Fallout 76)|footlocker]] ([[Picklock]] 0) and [[Ammo box (Fallout 76)|ammo box]] in the corner of the room. There is also a [[First aid (Fallout 76)|first aid]] next to its southeast entrance.

==Notable loot==
* [[Letter to Linda]] - Note, down the road in a military [[off-road vehicle]] in the hand of a skeleton.
* [[US government supply requisition]] - Holotape, on the blue console behind a metal shelf with a radio.
* Potential [[Vault-Tec bobblehead (Fallout 76)|Vault-Tec bobblehead]] - Inside the building, on the small mainframe computer bank.
* Potential [[Fallout 76 magazines|magazine]] - Inside the building, on top of the mainframe computer terminal attached to the central pillar, facing southwest (if looking at the middle pillar).

==Related quest==
* [[Request Government Air Drop]]: A [[US government supply requisition]] holotape can be used in the relay tower terminal.

==Appearances==
Relay tower HN-B1-12 appears only {{In|FO76}}.
