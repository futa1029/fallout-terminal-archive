Private '''Elliot Tisdale''' was a member of the [[Enclave (Fallout 76)|Enclave]]'s [[Enclave Squad Epsilon|Epsilon Squad]] at the [[Gleaming Depths (location)|Gleaming Depths]].

==Background==
A private in the [[Enclave]], Elliot Tisdale was an automatic rifleman in [[Enclave Squad Epsilon]] who provided overwatch support, and had the codename designation Wasp. Alongside [[Helena Blum|Sgt. Helena Blum]], he is one of two mentioned-only members of the squad who do not appear in the [[Gleaming Depths (location)|Gleaming Depths]].<ref>[[Gleaming Depths terminal entries#Enclave Squad Epsilon|Gleaming Depths terminal entries; Vulcan security terminal, Enclave Squad Epsilon]]</ref> On April 30, [[Timeline#2085|2085]], he and Sgt. Blum assaulted their superior officer as a result of being afflicted by the [[Scorched Plague]], having encountered [[Scorched]] hostiles a week prior, and were killed by the room's automated security.<ref>[[Gleaming Depths terminal entries#Incident Reports|Gleaming Depths terminal entries; Vulcan security terminal, Incident Reports]]</ref>

Elliot Tisdale is mentioned only {{In|FO76GD}}.
