'''Sergeant Ning''', known as a '''Hellcat mercenary corpse''' in-game, was a non-commissioned officer in the [[Hellcat Company]].

==Background==
Ning was a mercenary fighting in [[The Pitt (Fallout 76)|the Pitt]] under contract from the [[Fanatics]]. After the contact ran its course and the Fanatics controlled the Pitt, Ning decided to move on from the city and seek out a new challenge. He had no intention of seeing  what "peace time" would look under Fanatics and neither did he want to follow [[Kit]] into [[Appalachia]], a region he considered unbombed and thus unchallenging.<ref>[[A farewell to Pittsburgh]]</ref> However, before he could find a new community to become a killer or defender for, his life was claimed by the Pitt. His corpse can be found next to [[A farewell to Pittsburgh|his journal]] in a booby-trapped store in the [[Industrial]] district.
