概要

At first, the プレイヤーキャラクター can only access it through the bunker entrance on the world map. Once they are a member of the エンクレイヴ, they can enter the bunker through either the resort, via a secret entrance 3階には in a small hall with the elevators in the southeastern wing upstairs from the governors' hall, or by using the ホワイトスプリング service entrance. All of these entrance routes lead directly inside the bunker, in the military and production wings respectively.
The Congressional Bunker was built beneath the ホワイトスプリング・リゾート to ensure continuity of the US government by providing protection and shelter for members of Congress, the president, and the Cabinet in the case of a 核 strike. In order to ensure its habitability even without human workers, it was designed to be a fully automated, machine-managed refuge from 核 war. The support AI, MODUS, was a custom-built system designed to oversee the habitat, which could hold up to 200 dwellers. The エンクレイヴ subverted the project early on, adapting it for their own needs. The bunker's construction was financed by the Department of Agriculture, led by Secretary of Agriculture Thomas Eckhart, a member of the エンクレイヴ. The facility was connected to エンクレイヴ hubs across the United States - Raven Rock, the Presidential Rig, and others - with the intention of using it as a nucleus of all エンクレイヴ operations. For support, the bunker was provided with the Kovac-Muldoon, a secret satellite in geostationary orbit above アパラチア, capable of spying on every mile of its soil, delivering orbital missile strikes, and even conferring automated promotions in case the chain of command fell apart.
The bunker was activated right before 大戦, when the early warning came, and the government officials that remained nearby rushed to shelter. Secretary of Agriculture Eckhart, the Secretary of 内部, the Secretary of the Treasury, and a select few other エンクレイヴ and non-エンクレイヴ members alike made it to safety, together with a number of high-ranking officers. The rest were left to fend for themselves. Immediately after the bombs dropped, the survivors were horrified to discover that hard-line connections to Raven Rock and the oil rig had been lost, leaving them isolated, as impossible as that seemed. Following the deaths of both the Secretary of 内部 and the Secretary of the Treasury, the Secretary of Agriculture Thomas Eckhart was next in line for command of the ホワイトスプリング bunker. He then offered them a choice: use the ホワイトスプリング to continue the war or oppose his plans. Those who opposed using the facility and the アパラチアn resources were summarily executed once they revealed themselves.

Eckhart's rule

Although the purges entrenched Eckhart's control, he only had 48 people left to "save" democracy. He wasted no time and focused on his driving obsession: the eradication of communism by any means necessary, with no moral or ethical constraints. It was all-out-war and no option was off-limits, even another 核 strike. Eckhart sought to gain control of the three automated 核 silos in アパラチア - Site Alpha, Site Bravo, and Site Charlie - in order to launch a final strike against China. The bunker's extensive scientific, medical, and production facilities were essential to the project, sustaining the エンクレイヴ for the next several years.
In 2082, the bunker's ranks grew tremendously with the inclusion of survivors from Colonel Ellen Santiago's unit, who arrived there after leaving the smoldering ruins of Washington, D.C. behind them. With an influx of veterans, the work towards the eradication of communism advanced at a rapid pace. The experiments with mutations continued in parallel with work on accessing the 核 arsenal, resulting in many working serums and the first scorchbeasts, accidentally created when エンクレイヴ scientists exposed irradiated bats to biochemical experiments the previous year. Eckhart ordered further study of the creatures in controlled habitats at the 放棄された AMS mining complexes while keeping the officer corps in the dark. In the meantime, to further secure his own authority, Eckhart had エンクレイヴ personnel hack into アパラチア's automated election systems in order to run for President of the United States; he won handily, as his エンクレイヴ soldiers cast the only votes in January of 2083.
As Eckhart's attempts to bypass the DEFCON system escalated, however, the エンクレイヴ started to split apart. The system prevented launching 核 strikes from the 地域's automated 核 silos unless DEFCON 1 was reached and in the years since 大戦, DEFCON dropped to low levels. Eckhart planned to fool the system by unleashing ever more horrifying dangers on アパラチア, up to, and including scorchbeasts. Disgusted by these machinations, Santiago threatened to quit in 2084. Now a general thanks to the automated promotion system at Camp McClintock, she held the keys to the 核 arsenal, and Eckhart could not go ahead without her. Realizing that, the self-declared president had her knocked unconscious and kept under lock and key in the bunker's brig.
Without anyone to stand in his way, Thomas Eckhart proceeded with the plan and unleashed his scorchbeasts in the 地域. As the DEFCON system plunged to 1, the organization split apart into two factions: Eckhart's hardliners and Santiago's loyalists, who openly rebelled in mid-2086. Led by Captain Jackson and Major Ragnarsdottir, the rebels fought the hardliners across the bunker. Although they successfully managed to retrieve General Santiago and restore her command, they failed to account for MODUS' interference or the ferocious fighting across the science wing. In order to save アパラチア, Santiago ordered all landline connections severed and the destruction of the biological weapons research records, contained in MODUS' data banks. The plan backfired when the AI turned on all human members of the エンクレイヴ. After an explosion tore out a large part of MODUS' memory, the damaged AI retaliated, killing General Santiago and rupturing a toxin tank that leaked into the air circulation system. MODUS promptly sealed the bunker, dooming all within - including President Thomas Eckhart.

Rebuilding

After the events, MODUS immediately set out to restore the facility to its former glory, even though the damage caused by the fighting - and its effective lobotomization in the ill-fated destruction attempt by Santiago - made progress slow. Sanitizing and decontaminating the bunker of the biotoxin was among the biggest challenges, especially if the blinded and deafened MODUS was to have any chance of regaining a connection to 外観.
In the years afterward, MODUS regained some stability and used the bunker's robots to repair the damage. By the time the Vault Dwellers of Vault 76 entered the bunker, much of the ロケーション had been restored to its earlier splendor, arranged with robotic precision. However, sections of the facility were still sealed away by cave-ins and structural failures. MODUS remained unable to contact Control Station ENCLAVE for further instructions. It patiently waited for a prospective assistant to arrive from outside to help it restore communications.

Science, Medical, Military and Production wings

The プレイヤーキャラクター will be recognized as a member of the エンクレイヴ and be given free rein to explore the bunker.
