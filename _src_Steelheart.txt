'''Steelheart''' is a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] at [[Morgantown]] in [[Appalachia]]. 

==Background==
Steelheart is a part of the [[Back on the Beat]] event in Morgantown in which the player character escorts the robot in a city patrol. Steelheart was created by the [[Responders]] in order to stop the ghouls, Raiders and others from attacking Morgantown.<ref>[[Morgantown terminal entries#Purpose and Implementation|Morgantown terminal entries; Responders HQ terminal, Purpose and Implementation]]</ref> Steelheart is the first known repurposed police Protectron used by the Responders. Upon starting the event and activating Steelheart, one can find more of his backstory. The event is repeatable whenever Steelheart's battery is recharged.
