'''Feeding chute note''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Steel Dawn]]''.

==Location==
The note is located in the [[Enclave research facility]], on the corpse of [[G. Walton]]. In cell block C, where the [[Deathclaw (Fallout 76)|deathclaw]] fight takes place, there is a switch that opens a feeding chute in the main holding cell. Walton's corpse will drop out of this hatch. Alternatively, it can also be opened manually by looking up at the ceiling in the holding cell.
