'''Creed''' is a [[Crater Raiders|Crater Raider]] found outside in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
After their parents died, Creed and his younger sister [[Barb (Wastelanders)|Barb]] survived together in the [[wasteland]] keeping each other alive. Eventually, both of them joined the Raiders sometime before their return to Appalachia.

Creed is well known in [[the Crater]] as both a coward<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What can you tell me about Creed?"''<br />[[Barb (Wastelanders)|Barb]]: ''"You want to know about my brother? Creed's a coward, always has been."''</ref> and a pushover.<ref>[[Meg Groberg|Meg]]: ''"Creed's kind of a liar, but only up 'til a point. He's a major pushover. If you put enough pressure on him, he'll always buckle and come clean with the truth."''</ref> He once stole [[chems]] from [[Ae-Ri]] but admitted to being the thief after being asked only three times.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Why do you say he’s a coward?"''<br />[[Barb (Wastelanders)|Barb]]: ''"'Cause it's true. Just last week, he stole some chems from Ae-Ri's store. Instead of shutting up or pinning it on someone else, he caved after Ae-Ri asked him. Three times was all it took. Did you do it? No. Did you do it? No. Did you do it? Yes. I mean, that's not exactly how it went down, but might as well have been. What an idiot. Now he's paying her back and then some."''</ref> He also stole some tools from [[Caleb Fisher]] to fix up a [[Power armor (Fallout 76)|power armor suit]] he found but gave them back after being grilled, with Fisher also confiscating the power armor.<ref>[[Axel (Wastelanders)|Axel]]: ''"He recently "borrowed" some tools from Fisher. Now, Fisher's not a very confrontational kind of guy, but he kept grilling Creed about it, and Creed eventually told him everything. Turns out Creed was just trying to rebuild a suit of power armor he found. When Fisher found out, he took it to rebuild himself. Probably the right call. Creed would have wrecked it."''</ref>

Creed views his sister affectionately, despite her tendency to snitch on him, although she has a poor opinion of her older brother. He also thinks of [[Axel (Wastelanders)|Axel]] as his best friend.<ref>Creed: ''"Axel? He's my best buddy. Is something wrong? Did he cover for Barb again and get himself into trouble? She doesn't deserve him."''</ref> He dislikes [[Lev]], after he made several passes towards his sister.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Would she ever work for Lev?"''<br />Creed: ''"No way. That creepy old Russian used to make passes at her. It was ugly. Barb always used to complain about him, until I told Meg about it. Meg finally threatened his manhood with a shotgun and told him to step off. He backed down."''</ref>

He looks down on the [[Cult of the Mothman]] and the idea of the [[Mothman]] being a god, saying if there is a god, it is the [[Mega sloth]]. He says he would not mind starting a Cult of the Megasloth.
