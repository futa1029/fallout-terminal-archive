'''Squire Gary Weber''' was a member of the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Brotherhood of Steel]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Before the [[Great War]] took place, Weber was a corporal and part of the [[United States Armed Forces|United States Army]] ranger unit [[Taggerdy's Thunder]]. After the war took place, he remained with the rest of the Thunder and formed the Appalachian chapter of the Brotherhood of Steel, being given the rank of squire.<ref>[[Taggerdy's Thunder]]</ref> Weber was a talented salvager and recovered numerous items, including a military security system later installed at [[Fort Defiance]].<ref>[[Fort Defiance terminal entries]]</ref> Weber disappeared while out on patrol after the operation, presumed dead by the few group members that survived him.<ref>[[Last of the Thunder]]</ref>
