'''Note to Mac''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.
__TOC__
==Location==
The note can be found in the [[Vault-Tec Agricultural Research Center]] on a desk. It cannot be picked up.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
[[MacFadden|Mac]],
We need you. The [[Scorched]] were seen over by [[Tilly (mentioned)|Tilly's]] farm. They're staying with us now. But it's only us and the children. All the men are at [[Morgantown]]. We haven't heard from them in weeks. You've been away so long working on those damned bots. Come home!
Love,
[[Margerie]]
