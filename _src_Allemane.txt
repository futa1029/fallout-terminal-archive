'''Sgt. Allemane''' was a member of the [[Fire Breathers]], formerly stationed at [[Charleston Fire Department]]. 

==Background==
Allemane was part of the Fire Breathers' final mission to prevent the progress of a Scorched horde by sealing the [[Big Bend Tunnel]] using explosives.<ref>[[Orders from Melody]]</ref><ref>[[Out of time (holotape)|Out of time]]</ref> Her code phrase for the [[Charleston Fire Department terminal entries#Fire Breathers master terminal|Fire Breathers master terminal]] was "Smoker Boy," a reference to her smoking habit.<ref>[[Allemane's code]]</ref>

Allemane's corpse can be found at [[Big Bend Tunnel west]], presumably having been killed while setting the explosives; prior to [[Timeline#2103|2103]], it was near the tunnel entrance. After 2103, the outpost was occupied by the [[Blue Ridge Caravan Company]], and her corpse was moved to be near a blue overturned freight car outside the outpost's defenses.
