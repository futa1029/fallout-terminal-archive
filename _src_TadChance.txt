'''Tad Chance''' is a member of the [[Settlers (Wastelanders)|Settlers]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Tad Chance is one of the Settlers in charge of keeping stock of the supply room along with his wife, [[Gloria Chance|Gloria]]. However, they both have recently gotten their hands on stolen [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Brotherhood of Steel]] weapons.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: "''These weapons don't belong to you. They're property of the Brotherhood.''"<br />Tad Chance: "'' 'Cept they do, though. We bought 'em. I've got receipts, if you want to see 'em. They're handwritten, but we keep everything on the books here.''"<br />[[Gloria Chance]]: "''If they belonged to your crew originally, well... That's tough luck for sure... And I feel for you guys, I do. But look around us: what's not second-hand property anymore? Everything used to belong to somebody, right? Really, though, once you get things settled over by the [[East Mountain lookout|lookout tower]], I'm sure we can work something out.''"<br />([[Tad Chance/Dialogue#254|Tad]] and [[Gloria Chance/Dialogue#261|Gloria Chance's dialogue]])</ref> Tad purchased the weapons at a good price from "a guy, who knows a guy" who declined to mention where he got them from.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: "''We need to talk about those weapons... now.''"<br />[[Gloria Chance]]: "''Okay, if you don't think it can wait.''"<br />Tad Chance: "''Well, I'm the one who spent all our [[Cap (Fallout 76)|caps]] on 'em, so the least I can do is be the one answering questions about 'em.''"<br />Vault Dweller: "''Where did you acquire these weapons?''"<br />Tad Chance: "''I know a guy, who knows a guy. I don't know the guy he knows, though. My guy buys this and that from caravans and traders. He drops by here when he finds something interesting. He didn't mention where he got these from. Just said he thinks he got a good deal on them. So he gave me a slightly less good deal.''"<br />([[Gloria Chance/Dialogue#246|Gloria]] and [[Tad Chance/Dialogue#248|Tad Chance's dialogue]])</ref> When pressed, he acknowledges his group isn't disciplined, though Tad himself is wary of training, as he hates doing push-ups.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: [ [[Perception]] 8+ ] "''Your people lack discipline, not weaponry. Let us keep the weapons and we'll offer training.''"<br />Tad Chance: "''Well... Hard to argue with that. We're not exactly the most... disciplined bunch.''"<br />[[Mike Tiller]]: "''It's true... Maybe if I had proper training... this... wouldn't have happened.''"<br />[[Gloria Chance]]: "''Alright, the weapons are safer with you... But what would the Brotherhood need in return for training?''"<br />Vault Dweller: "''You can provide us surplus food supplies and any intel on unusual weaponry like this in the future.''"<br />Tad Chance: "''You're not going to make me do push-ups are you?''"<br />Gloria Chance: "'' 'Cause that would be the end of the world... Push-ups included.''"<br />Tad Chance: "''Aw shit...''"<br />([[Tad Chance/Dialogue#12|Tad Chance]], [[Mike Tiller/Dialogue#138|Mike Tiller]] and [[Gloria Chance/Dialogue#137|Gloria Chance's dialogue]])</ref>

===Quests===
[[Supplying Demands]]: Tad unknowingly purchased stolen Brotherhood weapons, and must be convinced to give them up after retrieving them from [[Kerwood Mine]]. If [[Mike Tiller]] was killed and Tad is informed he was murdered, Tad will be angered and order the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]] to give the weapons back and go away. The Vault Dweller can agree and tell them to "go nuts" and blow themselves up with them, or pass a Strength +2 check to refuse, demand supplies from Foundation or else the Vault Dweller will kill them. If the latter choice occurs, Tad calls the Vault Dweller a "jack-booted piece of shit" but Gloria reluctantly agrees because she doesn't want to start a war with the Brotherhood of Steel. Before leaving, Tad says not to bother to see if the food the Brotherhood will be given will have spit because it will.
