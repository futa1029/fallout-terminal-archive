'''Gleeful Butcher''' is a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] operating in [[Helvetia]] as part of the [[Fasnacht Day]] festivities. 

==Background==
Gleeful Butcher is one of the Protectrons which was reprogrammed to spice up Fasnacht Day celebrations in Helvetia.<ref>[[Fasnacht robots]]</ref><ref>[[Helvetia Protectron updates]]</ref> It is distinguishable by its red chassis and circular saw in place of its left hand.

When Fasnacht Day is being celebrated, it is one of the Protectron parade marchers which may be encountered. If it is required to march in the parade, it will be found in the first floor of the red hotel near the parade's starting location, in front of the kitchen door. It will request the player character(s) deposit 12 [[intestine]]s into its cooler before it will march in the parade. 

In [[Timeline#2102|2102]], the Gleeful Butcher wore the [[Fasnacht witch mask]] when marching in the parade. In [[Timeline#2103|2103]], the Protectron wears a random Fasnacht mask with an alternate color scheme.
