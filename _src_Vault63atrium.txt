The '''Vault 63 atrium''' is a section of [[Vault 63]] {{in|FO76SV}}.

==Background==
{{Section|FO76SV}}
Built underneath [[Dark Hollow Manor]] (the family home of [[Hugo Stolz|Hugo]] and [[Cassidy Stolz]]), the atrium served as the administrative center of all of Vault 63, including the offices of the overseer (Hugo Stolz) and the chief of security ([[James Oberlin]]). The residential wing of Vault 63 was also hosted here, as well as a clinic, a nuclear reactor, a garden, and various amenities.

As of [[Timeline (2100-2149)#2105|2105]], the atrium is home to the last remaining dwellers of Vault 63 who have not gone feral or been turned into [[Lost]].

==Layout==
{{Section|FO76SV}}
The Vault 63 atrium is divided into two levels, the upper level and the lower level.

==Behind the scenes==
[[Double Eleven]] developer [[Stewart Morrison]] served as the sole environment artist on both the upper and lower levels of the atrium, responsible for lighting and set dressing, among other duties.<ref group="Non-game">[https://www.artstation.com/artwork/qJGVla "Fallout 76 - Vault 63 Atrium" on ArtStation] ([https://archive.ph/yqCvM archived])</ref> [[David Brown]] served as the level designer on both the upper and lower levels of the atrium.<ref group="Non-game">[https://web.archive.org/web/20260111223844/https://gamesdevdave.wixsite.com/my-site/skylinevalley "SKYLINE VALLEY" on David Brown's website (archived)]</ref>
