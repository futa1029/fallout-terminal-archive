'''Lev''' is a [[Soviet Union|Russian]] member of the [[Crater Raiders]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Lev is a former mobster that came to [[United States of America|America]] from Russia before the [[Great War]]. He enjoyed the change in locale and likes the United States - or what is left of it - as Americans are, in his view, soft and make for easy prey.<ref name="IsRussian"/>

Becoming a raider came easy for him, and Lev eventually threw his lot in with [[Meg Groberg]] and [[the Crater]] raiders. In fact, he considers himself to be Meg's second-in-command and behaves in an arrogant, lofty manner towards other raiders.<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Who is Lev, and why would he do this?"''<br />[[Lucky Lou]]: ''"He's just some Soviet former mobster jagoff. He and his crew joined up with us a while back. You probably saw him hanging around the Crater Core? Liked to think of himself as Meg's number two, but I don't think she thought the same. Dunno why he did what he did. Maybe just hates ghouls. Maybe hates the rest of Meg's gang. Maybe both. {{Tooltip|I'll 'member to ask him next time he tries to kill me.|Sarcastic.}}"''<br />([[Lucky Lou/Dialogue#217|Lucky Lou's dialogue]])</ref> While Lev was fighting super mutants with his fists, he crossed paths with [[Surge]] and offered her a place in their group.

In reality, Meg has come into conflict with Lev frequently. When Lev started making unwanted passes at [[Barb (Wastelanders)|Barb]], her [[Creed|brother]] told Meg. Meg then threatened Lev's "manhood" with a shotgun in order to make him stop.<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Would she ever work for Lev?"''<br />[[Creed]]: ''"No way. That creepy old Russian used to make passes at her. It was ugly. Barb always used to complain about him, until I told Meg about it. {{tooltip|Meg finally threatened his manhood with a shotgun and told him to step off. He backed down.|Add the "He backed down" part as an afterthought.}}"''<br />([[Creed/Dialogue#112|Creed's dialogue]])</ref>

===Quests===
* [[From Russia With Lev]]: Lev must be tracked down and the detonator he stole from [[Lucky Lou]] must be returned in order to finish breaking into [[Vault 79]]. It turns out Lev went to Vault 79 and tried to kill Lou himself, tying him up near a [[Yao guai (Fallout 76)|yao guai]]. Lev was also the one who made Lou be concerned about becoming feral, hoping Lou would kill himself, but got tired of waiting for Lou to do the act. It is revealed Lev was paid by [[Rocco]] to sabotage the plan to raid the vault, and he has a very negative opinion of Meg's "soft" leadership at the Crater as well as some of the other raiders, calling them, "weak, pathetic freaks, animals." He believes in a dog-eat-dog world where only the strong should be allowed to survive and that all ghouls should be dead. Lev does not care about Meg's vault raid, feeling it's a dangerous waste of time. Lev and his [[Lev's crew|crew]], including [[Caleb Fisher]] and [[Surge]], are forced to be killed as part of this quest. Even ordering Lev to flee Appalachia will result in Lev saying he is not a coward and he will attack.
