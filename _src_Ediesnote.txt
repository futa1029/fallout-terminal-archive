'''Edie's note''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}. It was written by [[Edie Stevens]].

==Location==
The note can be found on a sleeping bag inside the [[wendigo cave]], next to [[Edie Stevens]]' skeletal remains.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
[[Morris Stevens|Morris]] hunts people from the road, but he won't share the meat. I've been living off of the scraps, but the toenails keep getting caught in my teeth. Now he's starting to look at me strangely, with hunger in his eyes. He's changing, we both are. Question is, who's going to feast first?
}}
