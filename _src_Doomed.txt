'''Doomed''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{in|FO76BP}}. 

==Location==
The note can be found at [[the Coop]], on the second floor of the outpost's main building, next to a mattress.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text= 
What is a body to do without a brain? A car without a driver? Us without [[Keith Cooper|Keith]]?

I made a lot of stupid decisions in the past, but with Keith here to tell me what to do, and what not to do, it was all so much easier. Granted I couldn't do anything to benefit myself, but to benefit Keith was to help us all! How can he lead without eating the finest meats, drinking the best wines, and having the best company? 

And now without him, we are lost. We are doomed.
}}
