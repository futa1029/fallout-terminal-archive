概要

The Vault 63 organics sector is a section of Vault 63 .

概要

The Vault 63 organics sector was constructed inside the Shining Creek Cavern, though construction faced at least three significant delays that prevented it from being fully completed. It was connected to the other sectors of the vault via a tram system, though the sector was also accessible from the surface via the cavern, which was used as a ventilation system.
After 大戦, the organics sector was used by scientists and researchers, such as Jamie Stewart and Victor, under Hugo's brother, the biologist August Stolz, as well as his daughter Hilda. Various research was performed, including on bacteria, plants such as "Henry", human brains, and a strange animal called a wanamingo. Chemical runoff and waste materials were dumped into the cavern outside the sector. By 2086, these gases had built up significantly within the cave, and Hilda and the other researchers grew concerned that the gases would leak back into the Vault. They were ultimately unable to vent all of the gases away, but were able to prevent the gases from spreading further than they already had.
Due to the incomplete construction of the vault, the vault was not properly sealed and its dwellers eventually succumbed to 放射線 sickness, and many either died or became ghouls. Later, some of these ghouls began going feral, including August Stolz. He and others volunteered to be put under to be experimented on, in search of a cure for feralization. August's daughter, Hilda, subsequently became the new head of the organics sector. By chance, the scientists discovered that proximity to the Vault 63 weather machine in the meteorology sector alleviated the symptoms for feralization.
In 2095, vault overseer Hugo Stolz secretly activated the Vault 63 weather machine at full power. This activation transformed many of the vault dwellers into the Lost.

舞台裏

;Development
Double Eleven developer George Platten was a level designer on the Vault 63 organics sector. Platten worked alongside a quest designer to fit 内部 with the quest and story beats. Stewart Morrison served as the environment artist on this ロケーション's development, while Fergus Russell produced concept art of the ロケーション. The "temporary" story outlined by these entries is as follows:
:Jamie Stewart's team was tasked to find human test subjects for the vault's research. This led to the creation of the "Cadaver Project," where artificially created humans would be grown in pods, though they could only grow as fast as a normal human so that adult test subjects would take 18 or more years to fully grow. With assistance from the vault drug research department, it was found that the subjects could be artificially aged in far less time using specialized drugs.
:Upon removal from their growth pod for an extended period of time, the subjects could become conscious, however, and eventually one attacked a fellow researcher. After the attack, the researchers began reducing the time experiments could take place to only two hours maximum; if more time was needed, guards had to be present to possibly restrain the subject. In addition, after any experiment the subject was sent to the vault furnace to be incinerated. Eventually, Jamie came to regret working on the project after having a crisis of conscience over the killing of the test subjects.
