'''Old Nate's farewell''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
The tape can be found at [[The Kill Box]], on the northern end of the property, along a cobblestone walkway near a ledge overlooking the [[Summersville Lake]].

==Transcript==
[[File:Old_Nate's_farewell.ogg|center]]
{{Transcript|text=
'''[[Old Nate]]''': I should be sitting here, lying about how I sacrificed myself for my so and so's and I'm quietly fading off and blah blah. I know the drill. But look, I didn't choose to sit out here and watch the bombs go off in my bathrobe like some venerable old peach. No, I was left here to die because I'm old and mean and that's fine by me. I like things how I like them, and I am not going to change one bit for anyone. I recorded this just in case anyone saw my body and felt bad for me. To hell with your sympathy. I'm going out on an IV full of painkillers and you're just jealous that you didn't think of it first. Sucker.
}}

==Notes==
Prior to ''[[Wastelanders]]'', Old Nate was found next to the tape, in a wheelchair and possessing an oxygen machine. Although the holotape stayed in place, Old Nate was tipped over the ledge and now lies in the ground face-first, presumably thrown over by the [[Blood Eagles]] that captured the building.
