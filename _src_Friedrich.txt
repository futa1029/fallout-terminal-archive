'''Friedrich (GM-05)'''<ref>[[The Whitespring Resort terminal entries#Week of 10/11/77|The Whitespring Resort terminal entries; Maintenance room terminal, Maintenance Logs; Week of 10/11/77]]</ref> is a [[Mister Gutsy (Fallout 76)|Mister Gutsy]] serving as a [[Fallout 76 vendors|vendor]] at [[the Whitespring Resort]] in [[Appalachia]].            

==Background==
Friedrich is one of the robotic vendors that the Whitespring Resort replaced their human staff with shortly before the [[Great War]].<ref>[[The Whitespring 2080 Initiative]]</ref> He resides in the Artisan's Corner store of the Whitesprings' mall area and mainly sells components and crafting materials for the player character to use at the various crafting benches in his store.

He was programmed to offer Wilderness Survival classes from December 15, 2078 to January 15, 2079. Although touted as survival classes, they covered topics such as taxidermy, fencing and hunting big game with flamethrowers,<ref>[[The Whitespring Resort terminal entries#Class Schedule|The Whitespring Resort terminal entries; Artisan's Corner terminal, Class Schedule]]</ref> rather than any topics with any practical survival value.<ref>[[The Whitespring staff meeting, 12.27.79]]: "''{{tooltip|Friedrich's classes are a joke, but the guests have been pitching in.|Not well. Friedrich is a robot teaching useless survival courses.}}''"</ref>
