Appearances

Hollywood Hills appears in the intro sequence of the Fallout TV series episode "The End" and also in the end credits of "The Head."

Behind the scenes

The scene at Hollywood Hills (specifically the Spencer family's house) was filmed at Briarcliff Manor in Westchester County, New York. Another location in Westchester County, the Cortlandt Quarry, was used to film the flooded section of Hollywood Boulevard in "The Head."
