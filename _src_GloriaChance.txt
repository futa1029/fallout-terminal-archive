'''Gloria Chance''' is a member of the [[Settlers (Wastelanders)|Settlers]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Gloria is a member of the Settlers located in [[the Foundation supply room]] who is in charge of keeping stock of the settlement's supplies along with her husband, [[Tad Chance|Tad]]. She made contact with the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Brotherhood of Steel]] in the hopes of purchasing weapons in exchange for food and other supplies, and has been waiting for a response from [[Leila Rahmani|Paladin Rahmani]].<ref>Gloria Chance: "''You must be here about that shipment of BlamCo Mac & Cheese! I'm here on behalf of Paladin Rahmani.''"<br /> Gloria Chance: "''Oh, how professional of you! You say you're with Paladin Rahmani? That's fantastic, I didn't think she'd get back to us so quickly. ===Quests===
[[Supplying Demands]]: Gloria is the one to speak to about a trade deal between the Brotherhood of Steel and the [[Settlers (Wastelanders)|Settlers]]. She must also be convinced to return the stolen weaponry, as she is very reluctant to the idea and tells the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]] they were last seen with [[Mike Tiller]] and that they can work something out later.

If Mike is killed and Gloria is informed he was murdered, Gloria will express horror and call it murder. Tad will be angered and order the Vault Dweller to give the weapons back and go away. The Vault Dweller can agree and tell them to "go nuts" and blow themselves up with them, or pass a Strength +2 check to refuse, demand supplies from Foundation or else the Vault Dweller will kill them. If the latter choice occurs, Tad calls the Vault Dweller a "jack-booted piece of shit" but Gloria reluctantly agrees because she doesn't want to start a war with the Brotherhood of Steel. Gloria says to let Rahmani know they're unsatisfied with this result.
