'''Stratford (HM-22)'''<ref name="notice">[[Termination notice]]</ref><ref>[[The Whitespring Resort terminal entries#Week of 10/11/77|The Whitespring Resort terminal entries; Maintenance room terminal, Maintenance Logs; Week of 10/11/77]]</ref> is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] operating at [[the Whitespring Resort]] in [[Appalachia]].            

==Background==
Stratford is the most senior member of the remaining staff at the Whitespring Resort following the Modern Heritage renovations.<ref>Stratford: ''"As the most senior member of the remaining staff..."''<br />([[Stratford/Dialogue#3|Stratford's dialogue]])</ref> He was programmed to handle all aspects of day-to-day resort operations, including guest relations, records administration, security coordination and staff supervision. He was also authorized to make any and all necessary decisions regarding personnel, property and guests.<ref>[[The Whitespring Resort terminal entries#About Stratford|The Whitespring Resort terminal entries; duty manager's terminal, About Stratford]]</ref>

Stratford was one of the robots introduced to the resort by the 'Ironclad' program and replaced staff member [[James Wilcox]] in one of the many terminations following the completion of the program.<ref name="notice" />
