'''Patrons''' are customers at [[the Wayward]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Patrons start to appear at the bar once it has officially opened, following the completion of [[The Elusive Crane]]. Three patrons can be found in the game: two men and one woman, who sit and wander around the bar. The woman is a refugee from [[Washington, D.C.|D.C.]] who is doing odd jobs for [[Duchess]] in exchange for food and clothing. The man at the counter is a musician from [[Nashville]] who occasionally plays the [[Acoustic guitar (Fallout 76)|acoustic guitar]] on the bar stage. Finally, the man sitting under the stairs is a former soldier-turned-[[Slavery#Fallout 76|slaver]] who is scoping out the bar for potential victims.

===Other interactions===
* The [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] of [[Vault 76]] may interact with them to hear their backstories. The slaver will refer to individual Dwellers as "vaulter" while hinting at his profession. The musician will respond negatively if he is interrupted while playing or about to play the guitar.
* Unlike most NPCs in the game in instanced interiors who are marked as essential, the patrons are not. It is possible to start a fight and kill them. Duchess will tell them to "knock it off" and call them clowns and nimrods, urging them to take it outside, although she does not say anything if the patrons are killed. The player can proceed to loot their bodies. Each patron that is killed will never respawn.
