'''Maintenance request form''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
The note can be found outside of [[Watoga Emergency Services]], on the roof, on a lower red platform due east.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
Watoga Emergency Services
Building Maintenance Request Form

Date of Request: 01/12/[[Timeline (2050-2099)#2077|77]]
Request Type: PRIORITY - TO BE FILLED WITHIN 8 HOURS
Floor/Location: Top / Roof Access
Requested By: [[Rob Massey]], Senior Vice President of Automated Assets
Assigned To: WES Maintenance Dept.

Requested Maintenance: Restore functionality of Emergency Power System

Notes:
=============================================
[[Oscar]] -

Look, I know you're overworked keeping the on-site automation running smoothly so they can fill these routine repair requests. But you know as well as I do that a damn [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mr. Handy]] can't get down to that ledge with the backup generator. I didn't design the building but I do print your checks. 

I've also taken note of your request to expand the maintenance department, but if anything we're in the process of automating more roles, not less. Maybe you should spend less time complaining and more time training a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] to climb ladders.
<nowiki>=============================================</nowiki>
}}
