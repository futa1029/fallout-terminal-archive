Dr. '''Blake Saunders''' was a scientist and researcher in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Dr. Saunders conducted research on a [[Radiation|radioactive]] crater located halfway between [[the Freak Show]] and the [[monorail elevator]] in the [[Savage Divide]]. He recorded the amount of rads found in the area's environment over a week-long period. He tore his [[Hazmat suit (Fallout 76)|hazmat suit]] on February 20, causing the radiation to seep into his body and, two days later, started vomiting blood. Slowly realizing his fate, Blake hoped that his partner [[Robert (mentioned)|Robbie]] would have better luck than he did. By February 23, the day he saw a [[Yao guai (Fallout 76)|bear]] in the wilderness, Blake accepted that he would soon die, and resolved to see one last sunrise. His [[Skeleton (Fallout 76)|skeleton]] still sits outside of the crater.<ref>[[Research note (Fallout 76)|Research note]]</ref>

==Appearances==
Blake Saunders' skeleton appears only in ''[[Fallout 76]]''.
