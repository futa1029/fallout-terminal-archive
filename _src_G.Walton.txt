'''G. Walton''' was an [[Appalachia]]n [[Enclave (Fallout 76)|Enclave]] scientist.

==Background==
Walton was one of the researchers located in the hidden [[Enclave research facility]] under [[transmission station 1AT-U03]]. At one point, he asked [[SODUS]] to exterminate the subject in cell A-05, though the subject itself had already escaped or had been moved to another part of the facility.<ref>[[Enclave research facility terminal entries#01-09-2084 :: 12:36 :: G. Walton|Enclave research facility terminal entries; holding cell console, 01-09-2084 :: 12:36 :: G. Walton]]</ref> 

Walton would later ask SODUS to "feed the [[Mothman#Scorched mothman|giant moth subject]] before it gets upset," though the mothman had already escaped from its cage.<ref>[[SODUS]]: ''"Task 151979, G. Walton. Feed the giant moth subject before it gets upset. Error: 'Moth Subject' missing."''<br />([[SODUS/Dialogue|SODUS' dialogue]]) {{Small|'''Note:''' This quote is an announcement made by SODUS over the intercom system.}}</ref> He also asked for the status of the water recycling system, though SODUS ignored the request due to there being a "duplicate issue."<ref>[[SODUS]]: ''"Task 151982, G. Walton. Provide status of water recycling system. Duplicate issue. Ignoring."''<br />([[SODUS/Dialogue|SODUS' dialogue]]) {{Small|'''Note:''' This quote is an announcement made by SODUS over the intercom system.}}</ref>

Walton made a [[Feeding chute note|complaint]] about the feeding chute having no guardrails. His remains are found in the chute when the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] of [[Vault 76]] arrive in the facility during [[Over and Out]]. His remains also contain his [[Fallout 76 keys#Walton footlocker key|personal footlocker key]].

==Appearances==
G. Walton appears only {{In|FO76SD}}.
