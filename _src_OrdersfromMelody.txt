'''Orders from Melody''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
Three copies of this note can be found at [[Big Bend Tunnel]]. One copy is found on each of the corpses of Sergeants [[Allemane]], [[Cominsky]], and [[Holstein]].

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
1. Meet up at checkpoint near [[Big Bend Tunnel west|Big Bend Tunnel West]].
2. Assault team charges in and pushes back the [[Scorched]]. 
3. Demo team sets charges.
4. Get out of there and blow the tunnel.
5. If things go pear-shaped, evacuate the tunnels.
}}
