'''Lennox''' is a pilot found outside [[The Whitespring Resort|the Whitespring Refuge]] in [[Appalachia]] {{in|FO76}}.

==Background==
Lennox was a member of the [[United States Air Force]] before the [[Great War]]. She flew tankers and cargo planes and takes pride that she has never lost her cargo. Before the war, she noticed strange things moving in the sky above the clouds which convinced her that aliens exist. She recalls one time, she encountered a strange UFO flying near her seaplane. She shot her pistol at it but then she mysteriously became temporarily blind; her vision eventually returned to her, but she believes she was affected by some sort of alien mind control laser.<ref>Lennox: ''"There was the one time I took potshots at a UFO with a pistol out the window of a seaplane. Big glowing thing, keeping pace right alongside us. Must've fired the whole magazine, copilot's yellin' at me, and suddenly I'm just blind. They had to take over and guide us home. Vision came back later that day. Now, my copilot said I was just drunk and started trying to fight the sun, but.. Alien mind control lasers is my bet."''<br />([[Lennox/Dialogue|Lennox's dialogue]])</ref>

In 2104, Lennox joined the new group at the Whitespring Refuge. She is working as a pilot for the [[Responders]]. Using a [[Vertibird (Fallout 76)|Vertibird]], she pilots [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] to [[The Pitt (Fallout 76)|the Pitt]] and [[Atlantic City]].

Lennox appears only {{In|FO76TP}}. She received expanded dialogue with the ''[[Expeditions: Atlantic City]]'' update.

==Behind the scenes==
Although her heritage is never mentioned in-game, according to Lennox's voice actress [[Debra Cardona]], Lennox is Puerto Rican and came from the Bronx district of [[New York City]]. Neither of these areas have been mentioned elsewhere in the ''Fallout'' series.<ref group="Non-game">[https://archive.ph/FPCuO Debra Cardona on Instagram]: ''"This is Lennox. She is Puerto Rican from the Bronx and is a badass pilot. You can find her in the video game FALLOUT 76: THE PITT.<br />I was so proud to bring my voice and my heritage to this game, and I had waaay too much fun recording lines for Lennox.<br />Here's to the beginning of Hispanic Heritage Month. ¡Wepa!"''</ref>
