Eugenie

概要

Born and raised near Appalachia, Eugenie survived the Great War, but was exposed to enough radiation to trigger ghoulification. Forced to leave her fellow survivors, she dodged bigots for years, evading attempts to kill her just for who she became. Eventually, she joined a group of like-minded ghouls, the Revenants, and lived at Radiant Hills for almost a decade. While safe, she felt that she was stuck there, afraid to venture out. She finally got up and decided to take her chances, gathering supplies, caps, and venturing out to become a merchant. As it turned out, coming into town with goods is an excellent way to make people talk to ghouls (and the firepower she carries ensures they stay civilized).

Eventually, she contracted the Blue Ridge Caravan Company for protection, making regular runs through the Big Bend Tunnel, ferrying weapons, ammunition, and explosives. The company believes her to still be closely tied to the compound, and Joanna Mayfield would like nothing more than Eugenie recruiting more traders for Blue Ridge, on top of the generous commission Eugenie provides. As a result, she receives preferential rates, as free agents have to pay significantly more.

In reality, Eugenie has moved out of Radiant Hills and set up her own network of contacts to source her wares from. However, she remains in touch with the Revenants through letters. Though they are simple updates on what is going on in Radiant Hills, she treasures them as a reminder that she can always come back. While her fellow ghouls do miss her greatly, she is reluctant to return and sell for them again. She prefers being her own boss, and she never liked what the Revenants were doing with the proceeds she generated, though she does not specify what exactly was going on to lead her into conflict with the residents of Radiant Hills. Nevertheless, she maintains cordial contact with her friends, such as Jaye Vo, whom Eugenie encourages to overcome her insecurities about ghoulification and accept herself. She has also written to Kevan Asherton, trying to convince him and Maddox Mullen that she is living proof that ghouls and humans living and working together is possible, but she also accepts the idea that the other Revenants will never see change their mind about their reclusive, isolationist defenses.

Eugenie is used to traveling through the Big Bend Tunnel. While she prefers Libby Wen for her professionalism, she is often paired with Kieran Kennedy. The gruff, silent guard stands in stark contrast to the chatty, joking Eugenie, and the two developed a healthy respect for each other. Eugenie gently teases Kieran about his brooding attitude from time to time, and even feigns ignorance just to get him talking. This unlikely bond is useful, as every run through the tunnel means going through a throng of Blood Eagles. She also knows Tommy Ten-Toes and played darts with him during a stay at Middle Mountain Pitstop, beating the former mobster. It appears that their preferred variant is the 301 version of the game.

The Revenants remain her biggest weakness and liability. She misplaced one of the letters she received, leaving it in Summersville, and the fact has been weighing on her. Vinny Costa, her manager, noticed that her distractions: Eugenie was daydreaming, wandering off the trail and losing track of cargo - and since she is a top earner for the company, anything happening to her would impact the bottom line severely. What complicates the issue are second thoughts about leaving Radiant Hills: on one hand, the other ghouls are practically her family and she still cares about them. On the other, she is her own person, free to forge her own road. During Costa Business: Eugenie, the Vault Dwellers of Vault 76 have several options for how to encourage or discourage Eugenie about having moved out of Radiant Hills.
