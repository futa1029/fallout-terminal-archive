LOCATION DATA

Shady Sands civic center is a Shady Sands building mentioned {{in|FOTV}}.

Background

A multipurpose public building located near the Shady Sands Elementary School, down the road.

Appearances

The Shady Sands civic center appears only {{In|FOTV}} episode "The Past", in the credits sequence.

Behind the scenes

Despite the series being set in America, the name uses British spelling ("centre" as opposed to "center") for the sign.
