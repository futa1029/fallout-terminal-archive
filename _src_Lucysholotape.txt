==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Duncan McKann old voice.ogg|center]]
* Upon ''Fallout 76''{{'}}s release in 2018, [[Duncan McKann]]'s lines in this holotape were all voiced by [[Brian Krause]]. However, at some point in late 2022 (either with ''[[Expeditions: The Pitt]]'' or ''[[Nuka-World on Tour (update)|Nuka-World on Tour]]'', based on available gameplay footage from the time), Duncan's first three lines in the holotape were rerecorded and replaced with a performance by audio director [[Mark Lampert]] for unknown reasons. However, Duncan's other voice lines (the ones at the end of the holotape) were unchanged, effectively creating a situation where Duncan has two voice actors in the same holotape, with both Krause and Lampert voicing the character.
* The audio files for Duncan's first three lines were replaced in the game's Voices .bsa file as well, meaning the original versions can no longer be found in the game's files. However, these original three lines can be heard compiled above.
