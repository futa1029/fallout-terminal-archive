'''Gina Bailey''' is a pre-[[Great War|War]] employee of the [[RobCo Research Center]] whose still-living brain remains preserved there in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Gina is a RobCo intern who committed suicide by electrocuting herself. Her [[Gina's brain|brain]] was one of the ones that were removed and stored in preservation vials, but because of the Great War, it was never installed into a [[robobrain]]. The player character can choose between hers and two more brains to install into a robobrain.

As part of the RobCo team which designed the [[Automated turret (Fallout 76)|automated turrets]] used in [[Vault 79]], Gina is familiar with the technology and can easily create tools to disarm them. She can be helped by convincing her to hurt RobCo by helping dismantle the turrets with Charisma 8+ or by adjusting her brain's dopamine and serotonin levels with Intelligence 12+.
