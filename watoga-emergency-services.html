Watoga Emergency Services
ワトガ緊急サービス

概要

ワトガ Emergency Services, also known as the ワトガ Emergency Medical Services (ワトガ EMS), はアパラチアn city of ワトガ.にあるbuilding and institutionです

概要

Intended to be an automated solution for all the emergency needs of the people of ワトガ, the center was staffed almost entirely by robotic personnel, with only a single human stationed there for maintenance. Although intended to offer 24/7 support for the needs of the city's citizens, the system was rife with malfunctions and the impersonal nature of the robots resulted in poor service. The maintenance engineer, Oscar, was routinely swarmed by patients believing that he could help with their medical issues, though he had no experience in the area.
The system required that only the patient could request services and complete medical paperwork. This made it impossible for parents, spouses or legal guardians to report emergencies, unless they brought the patient in themselves. In addition, the fire fighting side of the facility system was programmed to ignore reports from outside the three districts of ワトガ, preventing response or help to be dispatched.

レイアウト

Located across the street from the ワトガ Civic Center, the Emergency Services building is green in color and contains the reception and waiting room. Taking the elevator leads to a lower floor, which contains automated examination rooms. Near the elevator is an access door to the helipad, which has a potential armor mod and some ammo boxes.
The upper floor contains the emergency report room with ターミナルs for reporting medical issues, as well as a secondary patient examination room on the western end and the emergency services server room on the southern side. The locked laboratory in the center contains the bodies of deceased Brotherhood soldiers and their パワーアーマー.
Up another flight of stairs, the roof contains the ロケーション's steamer trunk as well as a パワーアーマー spawn at the southern edge. It also contains a helipad and offers a picturesque view of downtown ワトガ.
