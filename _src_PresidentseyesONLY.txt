'''President's eyes ONLY''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Wastelanders]]''.

==Location==
The tape can be found on the corpse of a diver just before the toxic water past the entrance tunnels at [[The Deep]].
