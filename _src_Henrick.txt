'''Mr. Henrick''', referred to as a '''raider corpse''' in-game, is a deceased [[Raiders (Fallout 76)|raider]] on the roof of [[Grafton Steel]]. His aesthetic appearance is randomly generated.

==Background==
Henrick was contracted by an unknown person to kill [[Bosley|Mr. Bosley]], a rival raider. Unbeknownst to Henrick, Mr. Bosley was also enlisted to take out Henrick at the same time. Henrick was positioned on the roof of [[Grafton Steel]] and Bosley was set up at the [[Pioneer Scout lookout]], resulting in a duel that killed both men.<ref>[[Henrick's note]]</ref><ref>[[Bosley's note]]</ref>
