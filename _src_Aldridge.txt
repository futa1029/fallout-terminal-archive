'''Aldridge''' is a [[Crater Raiders|Crater Raider]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Coming to Appalachia with [[Meg Groberg]]'s raiders, Aldridge immediately went to work scouting potential raid locations in the area. He investigated the [[Sons of Dane compound]] and [[Camp Venture]], being one of the few scouts to enter [[the Mire]] and return.<ref>[[The Crater terminal entries#--> Sons of Dane Compound|The Crater terminal entries; terminal, --> Sons of Dane Compound]]</ref><ref>[[The Crater terminal entries#--> Survival Training Center|The Crater terminal entries; terminal, --> Survival Training Center]]</ref> Aldridge also discovered a toxic waste dump full of [[Scorched]], which Meg began looking into as part of her plan to wipe them out.<ref>[[The Crater terminal entries#--> Toxic Waste Disposal|The Crater terminal entries; terminal, --> Toxic Waste Disposal]]</ref>

Shortly before the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]] approached [[the Crater]] with a [[Nuka-Cola My Blood's In It|vaccine]] to the [[Scorched Plague]], Aldridge and a team of raiders were sent by Meg to a toxic waste disposal site near the [[Philippi Battlefield Cemetery]], and fought a group of Scorched there including a [[scorchbeast]]. Despite killing the scorchbeast, the team was overwhelmed by the other Scorched creatures, and Aldridge was forced to retreat into a trailer.<ref>Events of [[Strange Bedfellows]].</ref>

Telling him about the plague and its effects, he can either be killed while he is still human, convinced that his condition is terminal and to go out on his own terms, or allowed to succumb to the plague.
