'''Shawn Hockman''' is an independent scavenger operating in [[Appalachia]]. He is visiting [[Fort Atlas]] to protest after being robbed by [[Brotherhood of Zeal|a patrol]] claiming to be part of the [[Brotherhood of Steel]].

==Background==
Although he occasionally works with the [[Settlers (Wastelanders)|Settlers]] of [[Foundation]] to get repairs and find inaccessible items, Shawn lives on his own and likes it that way, running independent scavenging jobs.<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Would you tell me more about yourself?"''<br />Shawn Hockman: ''"I'm a scavenger, mainly. Always been pretty good with a set of tools, so I do all right for myself scrapping and fixing things. Trade what I find or fix with the folks at Foundation when I need something I can't get otherwise. Mostly, I like to be left alone."''</ref> At some point, he was robbed at gunpoint by [[Brotherhood of Zeal|a group of power-armored men]] claiming to be from the [[Brotherhood of Steel]]. Regardless of whether or not the robbers were actually from the Brotherhood of Steel ([[Daniel Shin|Knight Shin]] insists they were not),<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Shawn Hockman was robbed by people claiming to be the Brotherhood."''<br />[[Daniel Shin|Knight Shin]]: ''"Impossible. Whatever happened, it was not us."''<br />A Vault Dweller: ''"Don't you want to find the people who did this?"''<br />Knight Shin: ''"There are countless brigands out there, civilian. It is not the job of the Brotherhood to hunt them down one by one for their crimes. I will say it bothers me to hear that someone is abusing the honor of the Brotherhood this way, but that does not change the facts."''<br />([[Daniel Shin/Dialogue|Knight Shin's dialogue]])</ref> Shawn is frustrated that the Brotherhood would let people get away with this, especially in their name. The incident made him more sympathetic to the [[Crater Raiders|Raiders]], stating that they at least stand up for their own and "don't pretend to be the good guys."<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Tell me what the problem is."''<br />Shawn Hockman: ''"The problem is that a couple of these Brotherhood clowns showed up and took a bunch of my stuff at gunpoint. I'm a peaceful man, I really am. Sure wasn't equipped to deal with armed thugs 'requisitioning' the little I've managed to put together for myself. Never understood the Raiders. I like the quiet life, I don't need much, and I need trouble less. But after this, I get it. At least they don't pretend to be the good guys."''</ref><ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Would you really become a Raider because of this?"''<br />Shawn Hockman: ''"Listen, I don't have much. But what's mine is mine, and these people took that from me. The Brotherhood pretends like they're noble, but then they let this happen? In their name? Raiders don't act like something they're not, and at least the gangs defend their own."''</ref>

===Quests===
[[Forging Trust]]: Shawn is one of four civilians inside Fort Atlas, each with their own problems and requests for the Brotherhood of Steel. Knight Shin requests that the player character interview these civilians, though ultimately Shin discards Shawn's problem. Because the robbers were not actually from the Brotherhood, Shawn's problems are of no concern to him.
