'''Merry Woodsman''' is a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] operating in [[Helvetia]] as part of the [[Fasnacht Day]] festivities.

==Background==
Merry Woodsman is one of the Protectrons which was reprogrammed to spice up [[Fasnacht Day]] celebrations in Helvetia.<ref>[[Fasnacht robots]]</ref><ref>[[Helvetia Protectron updates]]</ref> It is distinguishable by its forest green chassis, twin axe arms, and plaid [[hunting cap]] (when its Fasnacht mask is not on).

When Fasnacht Day is being celebrated, it is one of the Protectron parade marchers which may be encountered. If it is required to march in the parade, it can be found in front of the Helvetia church, beside the road. It will request that the player characters bring it 50 [[Wood (Fallout 76)|wood]] for the bonfire before it will march in the parade.

In [[Timeline#2102|2102]], the Merry Woodsman wore the [[Fasnacht giant mask]] when marching in the parade. In [[Timeline#2103|2103]], the Protectron wears a random Fasnacht mask with an alternate color scheme.
