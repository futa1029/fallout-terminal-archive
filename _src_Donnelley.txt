'''Sergeant Donnelley''' was an [[Enclave (Fallout 76)|Enclave]] officer stationed at [[the Whitespring bunker]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Donnelley was routinely charged with tough jobs by [[N. Jackson|Captain N. Jackson]]. Once, he suffered a near-fatal accident while installing a surveillance dish in [[Charleston]].<ref>[[The Whitespring bunker terminal entries#Military work station - RE: Surveillance Dishes]]</ref> However, he showed potential and was on track for taking a shot at the promotion process at [[Camp McClintock]].<ref>[[The Whitespring bunker terminal entries#Archival terminal - Accessing the silos]]</ref>

During the uprising, Donnelley sided with Santiago loyalists. After her revival, the general ordered him to mine the [[MODUS]] memory banks.<ref>[[Whitespring surveillance recordings#8.5.4|Whitespring surveillance recording 8.5.4]]</ref>
