The Bomb Disposal Unit, codename '''Boomer''',<ref>Boomer: ''"It is now your responsibility as an able-bodied citizen to carry out the duty of this Bomb Disposal Unit, code name, Boomer."''<br />([[Boomer (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Boomer's dialogue]])</ref> is a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)#Police Protectron|police Protectron]] found at the [[Southern Belle Motel]] in [[Appalachia]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Background==
Before the Great War, Boomer was a bomb disposal robot employed by the [[Berkeley Springs Police Department]], used to defuse explosives.<ref name="appass">[[Charleston Herald - Assassin strikes again]]</ref> One terrorist incident that Boomer successfully disarmed was near the highway in [[Berkeley Springs]], left behind by the "[[Appalachian Assassin]]."<ref name="appass" /> Incapacitated near the [[Southern Belle Motel]], Boomer was left without proper repairs or any contact from its precinct.<ref>Boomer: ''"Precinct unresponsive. Request for repairs failed."''<br />([[Boomer (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Boomer's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Boomer: ''"Hardware malfunction detected. Unit is immobile."''<br />([[Boomer (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Boomer's dialogue]])</ref> It continues to pursue its prime directive, asking passersby for assistance in recovering explosive devices from nearby locations as part of the [[Idle Explosives]] daily quest.
