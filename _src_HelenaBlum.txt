Sergeant '''Helena Blum''' was a member of the [[Enclave (Fallout 76)|Enclave]]'s [[Enclave Squad Epsilon|Epsilon Squad]] at the [[Gleaming Depths (location)|Gleaming Depths]].

==Background==
A sergeant in the [[Enclave]], Helena Blum was the assistant lead of [[Enclave Squad Epsilon]] under master sergeant [[Lynx|Harland Keiper]]. She specialized in pyrogenic warfare and had the codename designation Python. Alongside [[Elliot Tisdale|Pvt. Elliot Tisdale]], she is one of two mentioned-only members of the squad who do not appear in the [[Gleaming Depths (location)|Gleaming Depths]].<ref>[[Gleaming Depths terminal entries#Enclave Squad Epsilon|Gleaming Depths terminal entries; Vulcan security terminal, Enclave Squad Epsilon]]</ref> On April 30, [[Timeline#2085|2085]], she and Pvt. Tisdale assaulted their superior officer as a result of being afflicted by the [[Scorched Plague]], having encountered [[Scorched]] hostiles a week prior, and were killed by the room's automated security.<ref>[[Gleaming Depths terminal entries#Incident Reports|Gleaming Depths terminal entries; Vulcan security terminal, Incident Reports]]</ref>

Helena Blum is mentioned only {{In|FO76GD}}.
