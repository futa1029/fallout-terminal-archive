'''The Emissary''' is a mysterious person [[Fallout 76 random encounters#Man in Black|randomly encountered]] in [[Appalachia]] {{In|FO76IB}}.

==Background==
The Emissary can be found wandering Appalachia in rare scene-type random encounters. The Emissary makes vague references to the [[Alien (Fallout 76)|Zetan aliens]] and their invasion. The Emissary appears to be noting instructions for someone named Carol<ref>The Emissary: ''"{{Tooltip|Carol, memo to the bosses. They have arrived in Appalachia.|Speaking into tape recorder.}}"''<br />([[The Emissary/Dialogue|The Emissary's dialogue]])</ref><ref>The Emissary: ''"{{Tooltip|Carol, find out when Venus will be at its zenith.|Speaking into tape recorder.}}"''<br />([[The Emissary/Dialogue|The Emissary's dialogue]])</ref> and mentions collecting samples for a material called Zetanium.<ref>The Emissary: ''"{{Tooltip|Results positive for Zetanium. Sample acquired for further consideration.|Speaking into tape recorder.}}"''<br />([[The Emissary/Dialogue|The Emissary's dialogue]])</ref> The Emissary claims that "when the Andromeda galaxy reaches its peak brilliance," it will mark the beginning of a "new age" for the [[human]] race.<ref>The Emissary: ''"Many may not know that when the Andromeda galaxy reaches its peak brilliance the new age of humanity will begin."''<br />([[The Emissary/Dialogue|The Emissary's dialogue]])</ref> The Emissary also makes references to "men from Mars,"<ref>The Emissary: ''"If I told you I believed in men from Mars, would you believe me? Where have you observed them?"''<br />([[The Emissary/Dialogue|The Emissary's dialogue]])</ref> the [[Mysterious Guidestones]]<ref>The Emissary: ''"Have you seen the Mysterious Guidestones? Quite majestic, are they not? Some might say, otherworldly..."''<br />([[The Emissary/Dialogue|The Emissary's dialogue]])</ref> and [[Flatwoods]], where a meeting is scheduled with a "friend."<ref>The Emissary: ''"Do you happen to know the way to Flatwoods? I'm meeting with... our friend there."''<br />([[The Emissary/Dialogue|The Emissary's dialogue]])</ref>

===Other interactions===
If found during a random encounter, the Emissary will not be hostile to the player. If interacted with, there are no dialogue trees, though the Emissary will say a few lines, calling the player character a neighbor. Eventually, the Emissary will surround the player character in an electrical white light. When the fade-to-white is over, the Emissary will have vanished. If the Emissary is attacked enough, combat will be engaged. If killed, there is a chance that the Emissary may drop a [[Fallout 76 keys#Mysterious key|mysterious key]], which will lead to [[Homer Saperstein]].

==Notes==
* There are two versions of the random encounter with the Emissary. In one, the Emissary is alive, while in the other, the Emissary spawns dead.
* The Emissary's random encounters are only enabled when [[Invaders from Beyond (event)|Invaders from Beyond]] is the current seasonal event, though the quest itself does not have to be active for the encounters to occur.

==Appearances==
The Emissary appears only {{In|FO76IB}}.

==Behind the scenes==
* The Emissary is based on the legend of the Men in Black, as made famous in the 1975 book ''[[Wikipedia:The Mothman Prophecies|The Mothman Prophecies]]''. Supposedly, the Men in Black are frightening, inscrutable agents in suits who appear in the wake of UFO sightings to harass witnesses. Described as slightly "off" in every possible way, their very presence is said to be disorienting. They often seem confused about how to navigate basic human situations, and have a great deal of trouble communicating. 
** In ufological lore, Men in Black are believed to be disguised or hybridized extraterrestrials.
** In the Fortean philosophy discussed in ''The Mothman Prophecies'', Men in Black are understood as uncanny projections by some inarticulate force attempting to interact with humankind, possibly interdimensional, spiritual, or collectively psychic in origin.
** Skeptics have suggested that the urban legend has its origins in early ufological researchers fumbling interviews with rural witnesses, who were bewildered by the behavior of nerds in ill-fitting suits feigning government credentials.
** [[w:c:half-life:The G-Man|The G-Man]] from the ''Half-Life'' series is a well-known depiction of the ufological concept of the Men in Black.
* An emissary is a representative agent on a special mission, usually an ambassador sent to characterize and secure a new diplomatic relationship.
