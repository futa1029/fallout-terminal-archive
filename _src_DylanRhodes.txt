'''Dylan Rhodes''' is a man living in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Dylan is a former miner from [[Welch]]. He grew up in the town before it was destroyed by mining under orders of [[Atomic Mining Services]] and the subsequent [[Great War]]. Dylan intends to live out his remaining years in what's left of Welch and see where it leads him.

He also has become very anti-social, not wanting to spend much time speaking with visitors due to his bitterness over his job being taken over by automatons. He also mentions everyone he ever loved and cared about is dead.
