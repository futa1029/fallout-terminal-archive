'''Esme Rousseau''', also known as '''Chef Esme''', is a chef of [[the Whitespring Refuge]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Esme has always loved cooking. Before the [[Great War]], her father was a talented chef in France and dinner time was an important event for her family. When the War hit, Esme's dreams of owning her own restaurant were put to a standstill. During the apocalyptic aftermath, she spent a long time simply trying to survive in the wasteland all by herself. 

In 2104, Esme joined the new group at the Whitespring Refuge. When she heard about the Whitespring opening its doors to refugees, she thought it would have mouths to feed and thought being a chef for them would give her life meaning.

==Behind the scenes==
* Esme's English voice actress has not been credited or announced anywhere. According to voice director [[Phillip Reich]], although he could not remember the name of Esme's voice actress, the actress was French-Canadian, which had a part in her delivery as Esme.<ref group="Non-game">[https://youtu.be/rCP6CDxDjTs?t=3058 Phillip Reich Plays Fallout 76: The Pitt (Union Dues, Part 1 - Stream Archive) (0:50:58)]<br />[[Phillip Reich]]: ''"I can't remember this actress' name, but I believe she is from... I wanna say she was from Quebec? She's a French-Canadian."''</ref>
** In localized versions of the game, her voice is dubbed by Sarah Barzyk (French) and Anni Sultany (German), among other languages.
* Esme's facial appearance was changed from the [[Public Test Server]]. On the test server, she had very thin eyebrows. In the live game, she has noticeably thicker eyebrows and the shape and shadowing around her eyes appears to have been modified as well.
