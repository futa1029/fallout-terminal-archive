'''Quercus''' is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] operating in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Quercus is a Mister Handy inside the [[Arktos Pharma biome lab]].

{{FO76CC}}
Repair and recommissioned by Squire Asher of the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Appalachian Brotherhood of Steel]], Quercus was originally found disabled by Asher and Squire Ramsey while the two were off duty. Asher repaired and programmed him to document the mutated creatures emerging from [[the Mire]].

To help with this, Asher altered his personality to gain the creature's trust. However, Quercus started talking to the creatures more than Asher intended and even started naming some of the creatures. Asher put this down to the damaged state they found Quercus in, and figured if they could find a new battery unit the problem might fix itself.<ref>[[Squire Asher's journal]]</ref>
{{FO76CC|end}}
