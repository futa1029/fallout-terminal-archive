'''Fishbones''', often nicknamed '''Bones''' and whose real name is '''Billy''',<ref>Fishbones' associated voice type is W05_NPCM_Billy_CB.</ref> is the leader of the [[Crater Raiders]] sub-group known as the [[Blackwater Bad Boys]], based at [[Ohio River Adventures]].

==Background==
Fishbones attended leadership workshops before the [[Great War]], indicating he worked in a corporate setting.<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"You guys all go by nicknames. Whose idea was that?"''<br />[[Blackeye]]: ''"That's all Bones. Claims it's good for morale. Says it's something he learned in a leadership workshop back before the war."''<br />([[Blackeye/Dialogue]])</ref> After the war, he joined up with the raiders at some point and was present during the founding of [[the Crater]], along with [[Blackeye]].<ref>[[Blackeye]]: ''"I've been working under Fishbones for awhile now. We both were part of the original gang back at Crater."''<br />([[Blackeye/Dialogue]])</ref> Although he helped build up the settlement, he was sent far away from the headquarters as part of a supposed promotion, as he believes the other raiders saw him as a threat to their leadership;<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"So you're out here because someone wanted you out of the way?"''<br />Fishbones: ''"Pretty much. We were a great team too, but they don't see it that way. So I got promoted and relocated and they don't have to deal with me no more."''<br />([[Fishbones/Dialogue]])</ref> Fishbones suspects this was because his leadership was too efficient for them.<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Maybe they thought you were the right guy for the job out here."''<br />Fishbones: ''"I don't buy it. They wanted me to think this was a promotion so I wouldn't cause trouble. Worked too, till I got here and saw what was cut out for me."''<br />([[Fishbones/Dialogue]])</ref> They sent him to establish an outpost at Ohio River Adventures with [[Blackeye]] and [[Gills]], where they could coordinate the trade of [[Mirelurk (Fallout 76)|mirelurk]] products such as [[Mirelurk egg (Fallout 76)|eggs]] and [[Softshell mirelurk meat (Fallout 76)|softshell meat]]. During their time at the outpost, Fishbones also added extra ingredients to their meat, alleging that it's not dangerous or harmful,<ref>[[Fishbones]]: ''"Anyways, we found a way to stretch Mirelurk meat. Made it real profitable. Can't taste a difference and it won't kill yah or nothin'."''<br />([[Fishbones/Dialogue]])</ref> yet keeps customers coming back.<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"You're putting something in the Mirelurk meat?"''<br />Fishbones: ''"Better believe it. It triples profits. We got a couple of extra ingredients, actually. Keeps customers coming back too, 'cause its so tasty of course."''<br />([[Fishbones/Dialogue]])</ref>

While making the trip southwest, Bones woke up one night to [[Bruiser (Wastelanders)|Bruiser]] "beating the tar" out of Gills, who was on night watch. Bones figured that he had found Gills' replacement, as Gills could no longer walk from the beating he received. Bones, Bruiser, and Blackeye now lead the outpost, though they have recently been having troubles with mirelurks attacking the docks.
