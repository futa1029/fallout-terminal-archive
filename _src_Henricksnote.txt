'''Henrick's note''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
The note can be found at [[Grafton Steel]], on the roof of the large building with a sign at the northwestern end of the complex. It is in front of a [[Henrick|raider's corpse]], which is surrounded by [[petrified corpse]]s.

==Transcript==

Don't say I never did anything for you.
