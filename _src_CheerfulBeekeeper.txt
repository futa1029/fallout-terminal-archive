'''Cheerful Beekeeper''' is a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] operating in [[Helvetia]] as part of the [[Fasnacht Day]] festivities. 

==Background==
Cheerful Beekeeper is one of the Protectrons which was reprogrammed to spice up Fasnacht Day celebrations in Helvetia.<ref>[[Fasnacht robots]]</ref><ref>[[Helvetia Protectron updates]]</ref> It is distinguishable by its yellow chassis, stun gun in place of its left hand, and swarm of bees that surround it at all times.

When Fasnacht Day is being celebrated, it is one of the Protectron parade marchers which may be encountered. If it is required to march in the parade, it can be found in the Honey Haus. It will request that the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] exterminate the [[honey beast]]s attacking the Honey Haus before it will march in the parade. 

In [[Timeline#2102|2102]], the Cheerful Beekeeper wore the [[Fasnacht sun mask]] when marching in the parade. In [[Timeline#2103|2103]], the Protectron wears a random Fasnacht mask with an alternate color scheme.
