'''Paladin Swafford''' was a member of the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Brotherhood of Steel]] in [[Appalachia]] before [[Timeline#2102|2102]].

==Background==
Swafford held the rank of [[Brotherhood of Steel ranks#Paladin|paladin]] in the Appalachian Brotherhood of Steel. Their [[T-51b power armor (Fallout 76)|power armor]] helmet was marked with white arrows pointing towards the visor. They were killed by [[Super mutant (Fallout 76)|super mutants]] during the [[Battle of Huntersville]] and were buried southeast of [[Huntersville|the town]] in the [[Brotherhood graveyard]].<ref>[[Graveyard note]]</ref> Their grave can be found with a [[graveyard note]] affixed to the marker, under a [[Power armor (Fallout 76)|power armor helmet]] bearing their rank, name, and "Ad Victoriam."

==Appearances==
Paladin Swafford is mentioned only {{In|FO76}}.

==Behind the scenes==
* When asked if Paladin Swafford is the same [[Swafford (Fallout 76)|Swafford]] mentioned in [[the Whitespring bunker]], lead designer [[Ferret Baudoin]], the writer of most of the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Appalachian Brotherhood of Steel]] story, recalled neither the character nor a second paladin beside [[Elizabeth Taggerdy|Paladin Taggerdy]], noting that the intention was that there was to be only one paladin.<ref group="Non-game">[[Ferret Baudoin]] - 12/16/2020 Fallout for Hope - CHAD: A Fallout 76 Story Podcast Twitch stream: ''"Swafford...? Swafford? Wow... uhhh... that one's not leaping to mind, which is unusual. I'm usually really on top of that. I think uh... I don't recollect that. Cause Paladin Taggerdy, if it was a knight or something like that, I could see that being somebody, but you know... I think there's only one Paladin."''</ref>
* This character shares a name with ''[[Fallout 76]]'' additional level designer [[Trevor Swafford]]. According to level designer [[Steve Massey]], another person on the level design team named the character after Swafford while the two were working on the project.<ref group="Non-game">[[Steve Massey]] on the [https://discord.com/channels/784542837596225567/784546579847184485/860925204976369684 Bethesda Game Studios official Discord] ([[:File:FO76_Steve_Massey_on_Paladin_Swafford.jpg|screenshot]]):<br />BGS_Steve: ''"Lol yeah an Austin LD made that graveyard and named a guy after another level designer on the project at the time"''</ref>
