'''Lowell Aaronholt''' was a resident of [[Appalachia]] who died before [[Timeline#2102|2102]]. His corpse can be found in a bathtub in a collapsed shed at the edge of the [[Aaronholt homestead]] fields.

==Background==
Lowell Aaronholt lived with his brothers [[John Aaronholt|John]] and [[Shawn Aaronholt|Shawn]] at their family farm after the [[Great War|War]]. He wanted to travel to [[Chicago]] after the War, but his brothers disagreed, instead wanting to travel south to find out if any of their other relatives survived.<ref>[[Shawn's journal]]: "''Lowell wants us to pack up and head that way. Who gives a shit about Chicago? I always thought we should travel more south to see if any of our cousins survived.''"</ref> Possibly as revenge, Lowell hid the key to the granary where the brothers had been hoarding supplies. With this act he unwittingly condemned John and Shawn to die of an infected leg and radiation sickness respectively.<ref>[[John's last words]]: "''My leg got pretty tore up. There's some medicine stored in the granary storage. I'm not gonna make it.''"</ref><ref>[[Shawn's journal]]: "''Radiation sickness has taken the best of me. Without meds I'll probably die within days.''"</ref><ref>[[Lowell Aaronholt's last will and testament]]: "''I ponder what became of my brothers Shawn and John.''"</ref>
