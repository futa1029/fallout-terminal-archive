'''Miss Annie''' is a [[Miss Nanny (Fallout 76)|Miss Nanny]] operating at [[Wavy Willard's Water Park]]. 

==Background==
Miss Annie was a caretaker for the young [[Freddy Wood]]. While attending Wavy Willard's Water Park with Freddy in [[Timeline#2077|2077]], she realized that the boy had gone missing. Distraught, but bound by the parameters of her programming, she remained at Wavy Willard's, unable to properly investigate his disappearance. When the player character encounters her, she requests their aid in learning what happened to the boy.
