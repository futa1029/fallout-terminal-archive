'''The Crater''' is a [[Fallout 76 locations#Wastelanders|location]] in the [[Toxic Valley]] region of [[Appalachia]]. Prior to [[Timeline#2103|2103]], the location was known as the [[crashed space station]]. 

If the player is a [[Fallout 76 playable ghoul|ghoul]] then a disguise is needed in order to do some of the associated quests and gain entry inside some of the interiors.

==Background==
Built upon the wreckage of the [[Crashed space station|''Valiant-1'' space station]], the Crater is the largest raider settlement in [[Appalachia]].<ref>[[Davenport]]: ''"The Crater settlement was built from the wreckage of an old space station that crashed to Earth not far from here. The Raiders who live there are a violent group where the strongest rule, and they've claimed Appalachia as their turf."''<br />([[W05 Davenport.txt|Davenport's dialogue]])</ref> It was constructed by [[Caleb Fisher]] and his crew,<ref>[[Axel (Wastelanders)|Axel]]: ''"Ain't got a clue. Fisher and his crew put this place together. The only hard labor I do is raiding settlers."''<br />([[Axel (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Axel's dialogue]])</ref> who, like many others, joined up with the surviving [[Diehards]] as they made their way back to the region.<ref>[[Fallout 76 loading screens#Location|Fallout 76 Wastelanders loading screens]]: ''"When the Diehards Raider gang returned to Appalachia, they settled in the area of the crashed space station and named their new home the Crater."''</ref> Crater maintains several crop fields, which supply the raiders with a steady food reserve.<ref>[[Davenport]]: ''"You might have spotted the crop fields near the Crater. Those crops help to provide the Raiders with a steady supply of food. Should the crops fail, the Raiders would be forced to seek other food sources, and a violent clash with Foundation would likely follow. Being forewarned of such a development is vitally important."''<br />([[W05 Davenport.txt|Davenport's dialogue]])</ref>

==Layout==
The Crater is located in a large, shallow crater near the northeastern edge of the Toxic Valley. The space station appears to have fallen from orbit at a high velocity, devastating the surrounding area. The bulk of the station remains embedded upright in the center of the crater, with fragments scattered around it.

By 2103, the location has been turned into a raider settlement, adding scaffolding, amenities, and other decorations typical of raider architecture. [[The Core|The Crater Core]] is located in the center of the spaceship and has been turned into the raiders' headquarters. Most of the raider team for the Vault 79 raid can be found here with the leader of the raiders, [[Meg Groberg]], a former member of the [[Diehards]], one of the original five factions of Raiders in Appalachia before they were either killed or chased out of Appalachia by the [[Scorched Plague]].

==Notes==
* The Crater is one of seven locations for which the player character can select whether to spawn at the location's exterior or its interior. The other locations are [[The Rusty Pick]], [[The Whitespring Resort]], [[Foundation]], [[Fort Atlas]], [[Dark Hollow Manor]], [[Milepost Zero (location)|Milepost Zero]], and [[Radiant Hills]].
* During ''[[Wastelanders]]'', the main menu video opened on a shot of bodies hanging from the arch of the Crater.
* As of [[Fallout 76 patch 1.3.1.26|patch 1.3.1.26]], yao guai and Scorched no longer spawn in the settlement.
* As of [[Fallout 76 patch 1.4.0.20|patch 1.4.0.20]], fast traveling to the Crater is free of charge.
* A set of [[whale bones]] is laid out atop Ae-Ri's workspace.

==Behind the scenes==
* The design for the space station was developed independently of the [[Ballistic Orbital Missile Base 001|B.O.M.B.-001]] design and the premise of [[Ballistic Orbital Missile Base 002|B.O.M.B.-002]] from ''[[Van Buren]]''. Lead artist [[Nathan Purkeypile]] initially came up with the shape based on iconic science fiction space station designs, and intended to use the design for a ''[[Fallout 3]]'' DLC. After learning about the existence of the B.O.M.B.-001, Purkeypile briefly designated his design B.O.M.B.-002, before the late [[Ferret Baudoin]] informed him that the name was spoken for, thus the name ''Valiant-1'' was chosen.<ref>[https://fallout.fandom.com/wiki/File:Dev_Valiant-1.png Developer commentary]</ref>
* [[Nathan Purkeypile]] designed much of the Crater's layout, lighting and cluttering.<ref>[https://twitter.com/NPurkeypile/status/1184889321666596864 Nate Purkeypile on Twitter]: ''"Various settlements I have made on the Fallout series over time. / Also, to clarify, I did the layout, lighting and cluttering. So not just the world art side of things. I enjoy thinking about how a city is laid out and functions. Designers handled the actual NPCs, quests and dialogue."''</ref><ref>[https://www.artstation.com/artwork/0XQWYy ArtStation - Portfolio, Nathan Purkeypile]</ref>
* According to level designer [[Steve Massey]], the crashed space station was selected as the site of the Raiders' settlement as the team had always wanted to do more with the space station since the game's initial release.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20230509200630/https://i.imgur.com/QyLPhZy.png BGS_Steve on Bethesda Game Studios' official Discord]</ref>
