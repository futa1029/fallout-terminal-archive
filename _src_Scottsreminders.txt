'''Scott's reminders''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}

==Location==
The note can be found on the body of [[Scott Shepherd]], on the porch of the house marked private property just south of the [[Flatwoods]] tavern.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
<b>REMEMBER !</b>
- Set up a trap for the you-know-whos and use holotapes to try to record their noises at night
- Get a lock for the secret stash by the Brahmin tubs behind the house
- Get a bigger gun, or more guns, or both!
- Try to tame a wild dog or a giant rat (RIP Perry, you sweet old boy)
}}

==Notes==
The secret stash part of the note points to a cooler at the rear of the house, between two bathtubs, which contains Scott Shepherd's survival story: [[Survivor story: Nurse Scott]].
