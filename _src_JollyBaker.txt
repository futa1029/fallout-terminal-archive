'''Jolly Baker''' is a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] operating in [[Helvetia]] as part of the [[Fasnacht Day]] festivities.

==Background==
Jolly Baker is one of the Protectrons which was reprogrammed to spice up Fasnacht Day celebrations in Helvetia.<ref>[[Fasnacht robots]]</ref><ref>[[Helvetia Protectron updates]]</ref> It can be distinguished by its light blue chassis and chef's hat (when it is not wearing its Fasnacht mask).

When Fasnacht Day is being celebrated, it is one of the Protectron parade marchers which may be encountered. If it is required to march in the parade, it can be found in the kitchen in Freyja's Haus Restaurant. It will request that the player character(s) bring it 20 [[radtoad egg]]s before it will march in the parade.

In [[Timeline#2102|2102]], the Jolly Baker wore the [[Fasnacht soldier mask]] when marching in the parade. In [[Timeline#2103|2103]], the Protectron wears a random Fasnacht mask with an alternate color scheme.
