'''Convivial Historian''' is a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] operating in Helvetia as part of the [[Fasnacht Day]] festivities.

==Background==
Convivial Historian is one of the Protectrons which was reprogrammed to spice up Fasnacht Day celebrations in [[Helvetia]].<ref>[[Fasnacht robots]]</ref><ref>[[Helvetia Protectron updates]]</ref> It can be distinguished by its brown chassis and 1950s style glasses (when it is not wearing its Fasnacht mask).

When Fasnacht Day is being celebrated, it is one of the Protectron parade marchers which may be encountered. If it is required to march in the parade, it can be found in the town museum, the gray building near the gazebo in the park. It will request that the player character(s) bring 12 [[Porcelain stein|old Fasnacht steins]] to the Helvetia museum for analysis before it will march in the parade. 

In [[Timeline#2102|2102]], the Convivial Historian wore the [[Fasnacht jester mask]] when marching in the parade. In [[Timeline#2103|2103]], the Protectron wears a random Fasnacht mask with an alternate color scheme.
