'''Surge's letter''' is a [[Wastelanders notes|paper note]] that was [[Fallout 76 cut content|cut]] from the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Wastelanders]]''. It was written by [[Surge]].

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
Dear Daddy,

I know you ain't around no more, but I wanted you to know that your little girl made it, thanks to you. I fell in with [[Crater Raiders|some decent folks]] I met up around [[Appalachia]]. They're survivors, and they ain't afraid to do what it takes to live to see another day. You'd like them. Well, maybe not all of them. Especially not [[Lev|this guy]] I've taken a shine to, but you never did think anyone was good enough for me. I still remember you chasing around [[Hank (Wastelanders)|Hank]] with a shotgun and that time you accidentally shot [[Wade (Wastelanders)|Wade]] in the leg. 

I miss you. I know you wanted me to stay, but I couldn't spend another day in that damn bunker. I reckon you understand seeing as how you died down there and I couldn't get your body out anyways. 

RIP Daddy,
Maxi
}}
