'''Raymond Priblo''' is a deceased man whose remains are found in [[Appalachia]]. 

==Background==
Prior to the [[Great War]], Raymond worked as a trapper alongside [[Janelle Priblo]] and together they owned [[Priblos' Curios]], a shop specializing in selling merchandise relating to Appalachian cryptids. [[Aries|Calvin van Lowe]] was once a friend of Raymond's and was also interested in cryptids, having bought a lot of the store's items.<ref>[[Letter to Calvin requested]]</ref>

Raymond wanted to convince Janelle not to hunt the [[Beast of Beckley]] but couldn't decide how to word his talk with her.<ref>[[Raymond's pep talk]]</ref> Raymond was killed by the Beast of Beckley after ignoring the warning not to walk around with bait near its cave.<ref>[[Janelle's revenge]]</ref>
