概要

'''J. Schram's house''' は森林地帯 of アパラチア. これは former home of notorious cat killer J. Schram.にあるunmarked ロケーション close to モーガンタウン and directly north of Bolton Greensです

レイアウト

Plastic cat bowls and old cat traps (actually repurposed lobster traps) are strewn all throughout the house, and several pots of moldy cat stew はthe emaciated kitchen. The upstairs is decayed, with missing floorboards and a hole in the roof above the bedroom. The only point of pride in the house that 入手できます are the cat head trophies which are proudly displayed near the front door underneath the only working light in the house.付近で見つけることができます
Outside of the house itself, a number of harvestable crops はthe fields behind the home. The metal hangar on the property of the farm has two locked doors and containers and is a spawn point for random creatures. 付近で見つけることができます

補足

J. Schram's house is referred to as "The Ailurophile's Farm" in the official game guide.
