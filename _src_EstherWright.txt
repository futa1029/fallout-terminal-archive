'''Dr. Esther Wright''' was a scientist and member of the [[Enclave (Fallout 76)|Enclave]] in [[Appalachia]], stationed at the research facility in what came to be known as the [[Gleaming Depths (location)|Gleaming Depths]].

==Background==
After [[Robert Gaines]] was killed in an experiment gone wrong, Dr. Esther Wright assumed control of the Enclave's efforts into [[Project Vulcan (Enclave)|Project Vulcan]], a research project focused on the "Ultragenetic Resonance" properties of sonically-adjusted [[Ultracite]] crystals. She succeeded Dr. Gaines as the project leader on July 31, [[Timeline#2080|2080]]. Over the years, disturbances among the Enclave staff, including illness, sudden panic attacks, violent outbursts, and even [[cannibalism]] were attributed to the sonic experimentation with Ultracite. On August 23, [[Timeline#2085|2085]], Dr. Wright was apprehended and held to an Enclave military tribunal for her role in the experiments. She was found guilty and executed the next day.<ref>[[Gleaming Depths terminal entries#Incident Reports|Gleaming Depths terminal entries; Vulcan security terminal, Incident Reports]]</ref>
