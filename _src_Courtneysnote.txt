'''Courtney's note''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] ''[[Fallout 76]]''.
__TOC__
==Location==
The note can be found during [[Tracking Unknowns]], on the body of [[Courtney Kelly]] to the northeast of the [[ransacked bunker]].

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
Dear Finder of this note,
If you have found this, obviously, I am dead, but my two cats ARE NOT. If you have any heart, please seek out [[Harpers Ferry]] and resume care over my precious Lula and Misty, or notify someone who will... Suggestions for responsible parties: [[Niraj Singh|Niraj]] or [[Abigayle Singh|Abbie Singh]], [[Tyra VanBuren]], or [[Cassandra Roberts]]. People to stay away from AT ALL COSTS: [[Charlie Walker]].
Warning: If Misty bites you, it's a sign of love.
