'''Top of the World ad packet''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Locations==
The tape can be found in multiple locations in [[Appalachia]]:
* [[Beckley]] - On a table in the Red Rocket service station.
* [[Beckley mine exhibit]] - On the front counter of the Beckley coal mine exhibit.
* [[Sal's Grinders]] - On a desk next to Sal's personal terminal.

==Transcript==
[[File:Top_of_the_World_ad_packet.ogg|center]]
{{Transcript|text=
'''Voice:''' Have you ever wondered how to grow your business and expand your reach to thousands of new customers every day? Allow me to welcome you to the exciting world of radio marketing with [[Pleasant Valley Ski Resort]]!

We broadcast 24/7 from the [[Top of the World]], reaching the ears of countless locals and vacationers along Appalachia's greatest peaks and valleys. So tune in and check us out. Pick up the phone and call to set up an appointment with our marketing team.

We guarantee your business will boom!
}}

==Behind the scenes==
The narrator of this holotape was voiced by [[Richa Shukla]], who also provided the [[Voice of Watoga]].
