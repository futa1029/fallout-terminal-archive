'''Carol Putnam''' is the owner of [[Lewis & Sons Farming Supply]] in [[Appalachia]], living there with the rest of her family.

==Background==
Carol has been married to her husband [[George Putnam|George]] for twenty years.<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"How's the farm doing?"''<br />Carol Putnam: ''"We're getting to be self-sufficient soon. Greenhouse is getting there. George wants to restore the house, but we're fine in here."''<br />[[George Putnam]]: ''"The boys are going to want rooms of their own, dear. Don't act like you wouldn't mind being away from their snoring."''<br />Carol Putnam: ''"We've got other work needs doing first. If I can put up with your snoring for twenty years, I can deal with them."''<br />([[Carol Putnam/Dialogue|Carol]] and [[George Putnam/Dialogue|George Putnam's dialogue]])</ref> She and her family returned to Appalachia in 2103 and made their home at the former farm supply store. The family first secured the barn before later returning to begin restoring the location. Carol claimed the Putnam's as rightful owners and inheritors of the location since George was a cousin of [[Lewis (Fallout 76)|Lewis]], the previous owner, who died during the [[Great War]] along with his family. Carol uses the threat of her husband George and his shotgun as a deterrent to would-be troublemakers.<ref>[[Notice: New owners]]</ref>

Carol informed the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Brotherhood of Steel]] of a ghoul presence in the area which she believed had originated near Orwell Orchards based on the number of attacks in the area. After Carol's sons both express interest in joining the Brotherhood, she and George both agreed that one son could join, but the farm could not spare both of them.
