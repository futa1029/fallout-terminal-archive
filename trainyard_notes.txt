'''Bicycle''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
The note can be found on one of the desks in the upstairs office areas at the [[Charleston Trainyard]], on a desk next to a terminal.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
[[Jake (Charleston Trainyard)|Jake]], I'm telling you, I've never felt better since I started using the [[Bicycle|bike]]. Yeah, I know, we're train people, but I'm just going into the city and back. I feel the healthiest I've ever been. Swear.
}}

'''Letter of resignation''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.

It is one of four notes with this name in ''Fallout 76'', as well as one of three that were present upon release.

==Location==
It is found in the upstairs offices at the [[Charleston Trainyard]], on a desk immediately in front of the door in the room with multiple file cabinets.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
Got a job at [[Vault-Tec Corporation|Vault-Tec]]. They say they want my expertise if America ever needs to rebuild the railways.

I'm going to miss you all. I'm serious. Railroads and my family go back over five generations. It's been an honor to be here.
}}
