'''Sara Samir''' is a deceased member of [[Lucy Harwick]]'s group of ghouls in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Sara was a [[ghoul]] refugee who joined up with [[Lucy Harwick]] at the [[Valley Galleria]] when searching for a new home. She was joined by her sister [[Nari Samir]] who, while not a ghoul herself, wanted to support and protect her sister in any way possible.<ref>[[Lucy's journal]]</ref> When [[Duncan McKann]]'s hunting party became a threat, she moved to [[Dyer Chemical]] with the rest of the group and was shot in the eventual confrontation between the two groups.<ref>[[Lucy's holotape]]</ref> Her corpse can be found near Lucy's in Dyer Chemical.
