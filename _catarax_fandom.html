Catarax is a fictional character created by Hubris Comics Publishing, serving as a member of The Diabolicals in the pre-War board game The Unstoppables vs The Diabolicals and its associated products and merchandise.

Background[]

Catarax is one of the three Diabolicals, an antagonistic gang fighting against The Unstoppables.

Gallery[]

Catarax logo from The Unstoppables! vs The Diabolicals score board

Appearances[]

Catarax appears in The Unstoppables! vs The Diabolicals official board game, which served as the playable area for the sixth Fallout 76 season, running alongside the Fallout Worlds update.

Behind the scenes[]

Catarax's name and appearance are a reference to the medical condition of cataracts.
