'''Sgt. Cominsky''' was a member of the [[Fire Breathers]] formerly stationed at [[Charleston Fire Department]]. 

==Background==
Cominsky was part of the Fire Breathers' final mission to prevent the progress of a Scorched horde by sealing the [[Big Bend Tunnel]] using explosives.<ref>[[Orders from Melody]]</ref><ref>[[Out of time (holotape)]]</ref> Her code phrase for the [[Charleston Fire Department terminal entries#Fire Breathers master terminal|Fire Breathers master terminal]] was "Nuka Addict," a reference to a time when she found a case of [[Nuka-Cola (Fallout 76)|Nuka-Cola]] and did not save any to be shared with the rest of the squad.<ref>[[Cominsky's code]]</ref>

Her corpse can be found in the Big Bend Tunnel, near the end of a side tunnel with railroad tracks running south that is at a right angle to the tracks that run through the tunnel, presumably having been killed while setting the explosives.
