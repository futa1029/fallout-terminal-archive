'''Isela Mejia''' is a woman exploring the [[wasteland]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Curious explorers from the ruins of Virginia, Isela and [[Lacey Drummond|Lacey]] have set themselves up outside of [[Vault 76]], seeking answers for the questions that brought them to Appalachia in the first place. They heard news on a broadcast about a buried treasure, but the details of it were vague. Prior to their arrival, in exchange for a lead about the treasure, the two were apparently scammed out of the last of their [[Cap (Fallout 76)|caps]] by a man at [[the Wayward]], who Lacey recalls being named "Carter". Isela and Lacey have not been in Appalachia for long, but noticed the area was hit hard; despite this, she mentions Appalachia is still better than their previous travels.
