'''Margaret''' is a [[Miss Nanny (Fallout 76)|Miss Nanny]] barber at [[the Whitespring Resort]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
The proprietor of ''Elegance'', a salon at the Whitespring Resort, Margaret is a gregarious robot.<ref>Margaret: ''"{{Tooltip|Come in, come in. Welcome to Elegance. Our master stylists are only available by appointment, but you're welcome to look around.|Margaret: Gregarious owner of a hair salon.}}"''<br />([[Margaret (Fallout 76)/Dialogue]])</ref> Interacting with high society clientele, such as [[Evans|Governor Evans]]' [[Evans (female)|wife]],<ref>Margaret: ''"{{Tooltip|Do you know Ms. Evans? The Governor's wife? She was one of our best clients. Such a charming woman. I do hope she's all right.|Margaret: Gregarious owner of a hair salon.}}"''<br />([[Margaret (Fallout 76)/Dialogue]])</ref> on a near-daily basis has made her since reflect on the conversations she would have with them, despite how it would burn out her conversation module.<ref>Margaret: ''"{{Tooltip|I do miss our old clientele. Why, we were so busy my conversation module burned out every other day.|Margaret: Gregarious owner of a hair salon.}}"''<br />([[Margaret (Fallout 76)/Dialogue]])</ref> In the time since, her salon and stylists remain appointment-only. Although the booking system is down, she promises to set up appointments for customers as soon as the system is fixed.<ref>Margaret: ''"{{Tooltip|I'm so sorry, our booking system is still down. I'd be happy to set up an appointment for you just as soon as we get it working again.|Margaret: Gregarious owner of a hair salon.}}"''<br />([[Margaret (Fallout 76)/Dialogue]])</ref> In the meantime, she gives commentary on how other customers' hair should be dealt with.<ref>Margaret: ''"{{Tooltip|Oh, dear, aren't you a fright. You really must do something with that hair.|Margaret: Gregarious owner of a hair salon.}}"''<br />([[Margaret (Fallout 76)/Dialogue]])</ref>
