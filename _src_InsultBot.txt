'''Insult Bot''' is a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] wandering around [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Sponsored by [[RobCo Industries]], Insult Bot was created as part of a social experiment by the Robotics Club at [[Watoga High School]].<ref name="insultnote" >[[You have been insulted!]]</ref> They programmed it for three primary phases, including data gathering, data analysis, and insult delivery.<ref name="insultnote" />

Its purpose was to observe a target, formulate a custom insult based on the target, and then deliver the insult.<ref name="insultnote" /> Insult Bot then gathers any emotional reaction data and stores it. The students would then further analyze the information.

==Characteristics==
In the wasteland, the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] may experience an interaction with Insult Bot, where the Protectron moves towards them, asking them to wait, and then insulting them in a variety of ways. Upon the verbal insult being delivered, a note titled [[You have been insulted!]] appears in the player character's inventory, and the Protectron thanks them for participating.

As the Insult Bot walks away, the Vault Dweller can hear it make quips such as "boom, roasted," "rest in peace," and "contacting burn ward for assistance," among others.

==Notes==
* With the release of the ''Wastelanders'' update, the Insult Bot was given a gold paint job. Its appearance is virtually identical to the [[Gold Bot]].
* At locations like [[Deathclaw Island]], the Insult Bot may appear wading in water, but will be wholly unaffected by it.

==Appearances==
Insult Bot appears only {{In|FO76WA}}.

==Behind the scenes==
* Insult Bot's dialogue was written by level designer [[Steve Massey]], who also gave it its gold paint job.<ref name="BigBrother" group="Non-game">[https://web.archive.org/web/20230514142054/https://i.imgur.com/8eWURJA.png BGS_Steve on Bethesda Game Studios' official Discord]</ref> Massey commented that he found it difficult to write lines for Insult Bot, as they had to sting the player character in the context of the game world, but not offend a real-world player.<ref group="Non-game">[https://web.archive.org/web/20230514142952/https://i.imgur.com/a4WjkPN.png BGS_Steve on Bethesda Game Studios' official Discord]</ref>
* Insult Bot was inspired by [[w:c:bigbrother:The Zingbot|The Zingbot]] from the reality TV series ''[[wikipedia:Big Brother (American TV series)|Big Brother]]'', a show that Massey said he watches with his wife every year.<ref name="BigBrother" group="Non-game" /> The Zingbot and Insult Bot both say "Zing!" after their one-liners.
