'''Marie''' is a [[Miss Nanny (Fallout 76)|Miss Nanny]] serving as a [[Fallout 76 vendors|vendor]] at [[the Whitespring Resort]] in [[Appalachia]], specifically the mall area of the resort.

==Background==
Marie is one of the dozen or so robotic vendors that replaced the human staff of the Whitespring shortly before the [[Great War]]. She resides in the Le Grand Gourmet store of the Whitespring mall area. She mainly sells various food items and related items to the player character.

Marie looks down on the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] for their poor cuisine.
