'''Preston Garvey''' is a member and officer of the [[Commonwealth Minutemen]] operating in [[the Commonwealth]] in [[Timeline#2287|2287]], defending refugees under his protection from an attack by [[Raiders (Fallout 4)|raiders]] in [[Concord]]. He appears {{In|FO4}} as a possible [[Fallout 4 companions|companion]] of the [[Sole Survivor]].

==Background==
===Early life===
During Preston's childhood, the Minutemen were his personal heroes, as he believed them to be the "only good guys around." He enlisted with a company of Minutemen under [[Ezra Hollis|Colonel Ezra Hollis]] as soon as he could at the age of seventeen, growing to be an avid believer in the Minutemen's ideals of fellowship and justice, feeling like he was truly making a difference. He says that he had "a few good years there," relishing the chance to fight for a cause bigger than just himself.<ref>Preston: ''"{{Tooltip|When I was a kid, the Minutemen were my heroes. They were the only good guys around, really. When I turned 17, I joined up with Ezra Hollis's company. He was one of the good ones. Really believed in the old-time Minuteman way.|{Neutral} }} {{Tooltip|We had a few good years there. I felt like I was part of something bigger than me. Like I was really helping make the Commonwealth a better place.|{Happy} }}"''<br />([[PrestonGarvey.txt/COM#388|Preston Garvey's companion dialogue]])</ref>

Despite his idealism, even Preston noticed when things began to change for the worse by [[Timeline#2282|2282]], when the Minutemen's then-leader, [[Joe Becker|General Joe Becker]] died; without his stable leadership, the disparate militia groups began to fragment within the faction, with their former camaraderie devolving into infighting and personal vendettas. By [[Timeline#2287|2287]], Hollis' company, including Preston, was one of the last known active groups of Minutemen that still did their duty of protecting settlements and answering calls for help put out by citizens in the Commonwealth.

===Quincy Massacre===
A short time before the Sole Survivor woke up in [[Vault 111]] on October 23, 2287, [[Sturges]], an inhabitant of the influential trading hub at [[Quincy ruins|Quincy]], convinced its mayor to make a request for Minutemen aid after another local, [[Mama Murphy]] had a [[The Sight|prophetic vision]] of the town being overtaken and massacred.<ref>[[Quincy ruins terminal entries#Mama Murphy's vision|Quincy ruins terminal entries; Sturges' terminal, Mama Murphy's vision]]</ref> Although the majority of Quincy's residents, including [[Marcy Long|March]] and [[Jun Long]] saw Murphy as a burned-out chem addict spouting nonsense, Sturges believed in the truth of her visions from past experience, enough to go behind his fellow citizens and get the Minutemen involved.<ref>[[Quincy ruins terminal entries#Jun Long's terminal|Quincy ruins terminal entries; Jun Long's terminal, Mama Murphy]]</ref> Around this time, Preston was still with Colonel Hollis' company when they were the ones to receive the mission to rescue Quincy.

Upon arrival, the militia found out the threat Murphy foresaw was a company of [[Gunners]] mercenaries besieging the town. The Minutemen's surprise counterattack dispersed the Gunner lines temporarily into retreat, and they were welcomed by the citizens of Quincy.<ref>[[Quincy ruins terminal entries#She was right|Quincy ruins terminal entries; Jun Long's terminal, She was right]]</ref> In preparing for the Gunners' return, Preston was involved with helping the settlers build up more proper defenses for the town. Despite the Minutemen's presence, the Gunners continued acting brazenly against the defenders, including carrying out small-scale ambushes on their defensive patrols that resulted in some injuries to civilians such as the Longs' son, [[Kyle Long|Kyle]] getting injured after a stray bullet hit his leg.<ref name="NoHelpComing">[[Quincy ruins terminal entries#Kyle's hurt|Quincy ruins terminal entries; Long's terminal, Kyle's hurt]]</ref>

A short time after the Minutemen's arrival, a group of Gunners came to town to negotiate, where it was discovered they were being led by an ex-veteran of the Minutemen named [[Clint]], who Colonel Hollis recognized.<ref>[[Quincy ruins terminal entries#Something's up|Quincy ruins terminal entries; Jun Long's terminal, Something's up]]</ref> In a private meeting with Hollis and the mayor, Clint laid out terms of surrender for Quincy and its defenders; these terms were summarily rejected by Hollis as he scolded Clint for his defection from the Minutemen, but ultimately had to allow the mercenaries to leave without reprisal, with Clint leaving a final warning for the Minutemen to abandon the town or be destroyed with it.<ref>[[Quincy ruins terminal entries#Betrayed|Quincy ruins terminal entries; Hollis's terminal, Betrayal]]</ref><ref name=Clint>[[Quincy ruins terminal entries#Col. Hollis|Quincy ruins terminal entries; Gunner terminal, Col. Hollis]]</ref>

While the subsequent events are not definitively recorded in text, the outcome for the Minutemen is broadly established by dialogue with Preston and various other wastelanders; the Minutemen requested further support from other groups, but it was either that they never arrived in time, or they never came at all.<ref name="NoHelpComing"/><ref>[[Quincy ruins terminal entries#Status report|Quincy ruins terminal entries; Hollis's terminal, Status report]]</ref> By the end of it, the Gunners had overwhelmed the defenders and murdered nearly all of the civilians; Colonel Hollis himself, the linchpin of what was likely the last true group of Minutemen, was captured and died being executed by the traitorous Clint.<ref name=Clint/><ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"What was the Quincy Massacre?"''<br>Preston: ''"I thought everyone in the Commonwealth knew about that by now. Where the Minutemen betrayed each other, and the people they were supposed to protect. I was with Colonel Hollis's group. A mercenary group called the Gunners was attacking Quincy; the people there called for the Minutemen to help. We were the only ones that came. The other groups... they just turned their backs. On us, and the folks in Quincy. Only a few of us got out alive. Colonel Hollis was dead. So I ended up in charge of the survivors. We never found a safe place to settle. One disaster after another... you saw how it ended, in Concord."''<br>([[PrestonGarvey.txt#1831|Preston Garvey's dialogue]])</ref> The event became known as the [[Quincy Massacre]] and it catalyzed the disintegration of the protective bubble around the innocent settlers that the Minutemen had championed.

===Post-Quincy===
When Quincy fell to the Gunners, Preston managed to survive and gathered the few remaining Quincy civilians and members of Hollis' company and fled, pursued by Clint's mercenaries. Several of the refugees lost their lives at the hands of the Gunners before the group shook their pursuit at [[Jamaica Plain]].<ref>[[Quincy ruins terminal entries#Preston|Quincy terminal entries; Clint's terminal, Preston]]</ref> The survivors escaped pursuit from Quincy and continued moving across the Commonwealth until they reached [[Lexington]]. As Preston testifies, however, Lexington too proved a disastrous point on their journey as they were nearly overrun by the heavy infestation of [[Feral ghoul (Fallout 4)|feral ghouls]], ultimately leaving only eight survivors making it out. Eventually, the group reached the opposite corner of the region from Quincy and entered [[Concord]], where they took shelter in the [[Museum of Freedom]]. However, their troubles still did not end as they soon after came under attack again, this time by [[Raiders (Fallout 4)|raiders]]. Unbeknownst to the group, the attack was not random as the raiders' commander [[Gristle]] were under orders from their leader, [[Jared]], to capture Mama Murphy and bring her to the [[Corvega assembly plant]] so he could exploit her prophetic powers for himself;<ref>[[Corvega assembly plant terminal entries#She's Here!|Corvega assembly plant terminal entries; Jared's terminal, She's Here!]]</ref> Gristle took to the mission eagerly because he coveted acquiring Preston's [[Minuteman outfit|hat]].<ref>[[Corvega assembly plant terminal entries#Caravan|Corvega assembly plant terminal entries; Gristle's terminal, Caravan]]</ref>

By October 23, the Quincy refugee group numbered only five: Preston himself, Mama Murphy, Sturges and [[Jun Long|Jun]] and [[Marcy Long]], a mere fraction of the original twenty who escaped under Preston's guidance. As he can later admit if helped out by the Sole Survivor, at that point, Preston believed he had failed everyone who had ever relied on him and found everything he thought he knew about the Minutemen was a lie, leading him to the proverbial end of his rope. It was only because of the four people still under his protection that he put on a brave face; in reality, he was not completely sure if they were going to get out of Concord alive, but that was acceptable to him. He had come to the conclusion death was what he deserved and what he actually wanted. Deep down, although not actively suicidal, Preston says he simply had given up hope for himself and the Minutemen and did not care if he lived or died; if he was going to meet his end, he would do it fighting as the last true believer in the Minutemen's justice.<ref name="end">Preston Garvey: ''"{{tooltip|I've been thinking back lately on how things were for me when we first met.|{Thinking} }} {{tooltip|It was the lowest point of my life. I mean... all my closest friends were dead. Everything I believed in had turned out to be a lie. I'd failed everyone who'd ever relied on me. I'd led them to Concord and we had no hope of getting out alive. The thing is... that was actually okay with me. I was ready to die. It was what I felt I deserved. It was what I wanted.|{Somber} }}"''<br/>[[Sole Survivor]]: ''"I never realized you'd lost hope like that."''<br/>Preston Garvey: ''"{{Tooltip|I had to put on a brave face as long as there were still people counting on me. That's the only reason I kept going.|Somber }} {{Tooltip|My point in all of this is that, well... you saved my life. And not just by saving us from those raiders in Concord.|{Grateful} }} {{Tooltip|I mean, that you... made me want to keep living again. ==Personality==
Preston Garvey largely defines himself by his role as a Minuteman, as he had idolized them since childhood as the one group truly dedicated to stability and safety within the Commonwealth. After joining at the first chance, however, Garvey's faith was shaken somewhat when he came to realize that behind the veneer of unity and justice, the Minutemen had become rotten and rife with factionalism; he acknowledges that it may have been even worse under the surface and he just did not want to look any closer due to his idealism. Despite this, he still fundamentally believes in the cause and creed of the Minutemen, and even into the present seeks tirelessly to try to rebuild them to their former glory while identifying himself as being one of the last true Minutemen.

One of the largest hurdles in Garvey's path to the Minutemen's restoration is facing his survivor's guilt over the fallout of the Quincy Massacre. While he knows he's a qualified soldier and had the guts to keep fighting, he fails to see any reason why it was him alone that managed to survive and bear the entire legacy of the Minutemen. He personally displays extreme discomfort, even panic, at the concept of leadership, at least outside of a combat scenario.
