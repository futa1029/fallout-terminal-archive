The '''Canadian rebel''' is a character who appears in the [[Fallout TV series season 2|second season]] of the [[Fallout (TV series)|''Fallout'' TV series]].

==''Fallout'' TV series==
===[[The Handoff]]===
During a mass escape from the [[Uranium City Internment Camp]], the US military hunt down escapees. [[Steph Harper]] and [[Joan (TV series)|her mother]] are cornered by a [[Vertibird (TV series)|vertibird]] and a [[United States Marine Corps|U.S. Marine]] in [[T-45 power armor (TV series)|T-45 power armor]] who calls them by the slur "Hoser" while threatening to shoot them if they do not return to the camp. The Canadian rebel acts as a suicide bomber and kills the soldier and himself. While the explosion mortally wounds Steph's mother, it allows Steph to escape and eventually cross the border.

==Appearances==
The Canadian rebel appears only in the ''Fallout'' TV series.
