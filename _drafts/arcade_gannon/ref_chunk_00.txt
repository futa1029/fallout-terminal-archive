'''Arcade Israel Gannon''' is a doctor and scientist with the [[Followers of the Apocalypse]] residing in the [[Mojave Wasteland]] {{In|FNV}}. He is a possible [[Fallout: New Vegas companions|companion]] of the [[Courier]].

==Background==
===Life in the Enclave===
The only son of [[Gannon Senior]], an officer of the [[Enclave]], and his wife, Arcade was born at the [[Navarro]] military base around {{#expr:2281-35}}, a few years after the [[Chosen One]] blew up the [[oil rig]]. He lost his father at an early age, forming close ties with his mother, but in his own words, he never got over his passing.<ref group="Non-game" name="age"/><ref name="age">The [[Courier]]: ''"Arcade, I'm asking you because I'm interested. Don't put yourself down."''<br />Arcade Gannon: ''"Oh, all right. I'm thirty-ish. {{Tooltip|Well, late thirties.|{admitting} }} I was born... {{Tooltip|west of here.|{guarded} }} I was an only child and spent most of my time with my mother. My father died when I was young and I never got over it. Oh... and I like medicine and reading books about failed Pre-War socioeconomic policies. {{Tooltip|Right now, I'm sure you're asking yourself, 'Why hasn't some lucky man scooped this bachelor off his feet?'|{a bit of sarcasm} }} {{Tooltip|Like I said, I'm boring.|{serious} }}"''<br />([[VFSArcadeGannon.txt#54|Arcade Gannon's dialogue]])</ref> That his father was a member of a fascist paramilitary group responsible for crimes against humanity both before and after the bombs destroyed the world greatly contributed to it,<ref name="ArcadesFullIntroduction">The [[Courier]]: ''"What are you talking about?"''<br />Arcade Gannon: ''"I wasn't always with the Followers, or with the NCR. My late father was an officer in a group called the Enclave, a remnant of America's Pre-War government. Memories being short around here, not a whole lot of people remember them. But they did bad things. Terrorized communities, kidnapped people. Eventually, someone stopped them. I was born a few years later at a military base on the coast, a place called Navarro."''<br />([[VFSArcadeGannon.txt|Arcade Gannon's dialogue]])</ref> as was the fact that Gannon Senior's death occurred while he was away on a mission, with the precise circumstances of his passing never revealed to Arcade by any of the remnants, if they knew at all.<ref name="ripgannon" /><ref name="proud" /><ref name="DaisysBio"/>

Later, Arcade, his mother, and several of the troopers from his father's old unit were forced to abandon the Navarro base, narrowly evading the [[Sacking of Navarro|attack]] by the [[New California Republic]] that wiped it out. Even after escaping Navarro, they still remained in danger from being hunted down by the [[Brotherhood of Steel]],<ref name="ArcadeHappenings">The [[Courier]]: ''"What happened to Navarro?"''<br />Arcade Gannon: ''"After the Enclave's command structure fell apart, it was only a matter of time before Navarro was overrun by NCR forces. My mother and I left with some of the troopers from my father's old unit, the same people I've kept in contact with over the years. Anyone who didn't get out was killed by the NCR. Even some of the ones who escaped were eventually hunted down by the Brotherhood of Steel."''<br />[[VFSArcadeGannon.txt|Arcade Gannon's dialogue]])</ref> and so the group moved south and attempted to integrate into the NCR where their past ties would not be discovered. At some point, Arcade came into contact with and eventually joined the [[Followers of the Apocalypse]], combining his desire to help people with a knack for learning.<ref name="joiningandcoming">The [[Courier]]: ''"How did you join the Followers?"''<br />Arcade Gannon: ''"Honestly, it isn't too difficult if you have half a brain and aren't a complete jerk. I wanted to help people and I enjoyed learning, so I signed up. New Vegas is in trouble, so I came out here. If someone doesn't step up and try to help the people here, it's going to end very badly."''<br />([[VFSArcadeGannon.txt|Arcade Gannon's dialogue]])</ref>

His time in the Followers helped Arcade find his calling in medicine. He learned everything he knows from the group at their excellent medical facilities in the NCR heartlands,<ref name="learning"/> becoming a competent doctor in his own right capable of running his own practice.<ref name="whatsupdoc">[[Fallout: New Vegas endings|''Fallout: New Vegas'' endings]]: "''Arcade was outraged by Mr. House's power play following the defeat of Caesar's Legion at Hoover Dam. Though he remained in Freeside for a short time after the Securitrons established widespread control, he eventually traveled back into NCR territory. Disillusioned with the Followers of the Apocalypse, he settled down in the outskirts of the Boneyard, where he worked happily as a family doctor.''"</ref> He also formed an interest in history, especially on the topic of failed pre-War socioeconomic policies,<ref name="age"/> and even learned to speak Latin, drawing on the Followers' extensive library and the odd holotape about gladiators.<ref name="latin">The [[Courier]]: ''"Where did you learn that?"''<br />Arcade Gannon: ''"Not from the Legion, if that's what you're getting at. Books. Sheet music. Gladiator movie holotapes. Bits and pieces here and there. The Followers have extensive libraries, but we all draw water from the same old well. Even Caesar."''<br />([[VFSArcadeGannon.txt|Arcade Gannon's dialogue]])</ref> While he is not a fully qualified botanist,<ref>Arcade Gannon: "''I'm no botanist, but I think this {{Tooltip|may|{emph} }} not be entirely natural.''"<br />([[VFSArcadeGannon.txt|Arcade Gannon's dialogue]]) {{Small|'''Note:''' This line is spoken at the entrance to [[Vault 22]].}}</ref><ref name="joiningandcoming"/> he dedicates some time and research into [[flora|plants]] and their medicinal properties.<ref name="research" /> 

===Moving to the Mojave===
The NCR leadership eventually realized that remnants of the defeated Enclave had adopted new identities and lived in secret within their borders,<ref>[[Fallout 2 endings|''Fallout 2'' endings]]: "''Shaken by the assassination of Vice-President Carlson, right-wing elements seize control of the Congress and set the New California Republic on a path to military rule. Eventually the survivors of the Enclave found a new home in the ranks of the NCR.''"<br/>{{Small|'''Note''': This non-canon ending explains the circumstances in which Enclave members would be permitted to integrate without problem, noting explicitly that right-wing politics are a precondition.}}</ref> driving them to adopt a policy of hunting these people down to stand trial for the group's [[FEV Curling-13|war crimes]], typically followed by life imprisonment.<ref>[[Fallout: New Vegas endings|''Fallout: New Vegas'' endings]]: "''Though Arcade was crushed by the Legion's victory at Hoover Dam, he was not among NCR's casualties. During the NCR's retreat from the Mojave Wasteland, he helped defend NCR citizens and refugees on their way to Mojave Outpost. Unfortunately, a NCR ranger identified his father's armor as Enclave property. He was arrested, tried as a war criminal, and imprisoned indefinitely.''"<br/>{{Small|'''Note''': This ending explains the NCR's policy towards Enclave members.}}</ref> With the combined efforts of the [[New California Republic Rangers|NCR Rangers]], Brotherhood of Steel members, and numerous bounty hunters actively seeking out anyone with ties to the Enclave,<ref>[[Fallout: New Vegas endings|''Fallout: New Vegas'' endings]]: "''Though Arcade had not hoped for an NCR victory, he was proud of his role in the defense of Hoover Dam against the forces of Caesar's Legion. Unfortunately, when word spread that Arcade was once a member of the Enclave, he was forced out of the Followers of the Apocalypse. Pursued by bounty hunters, NCR rangers, and the Brotherhood of Steel, Arcade pushed deep into the eastern plains and was never heard from again.''"<br/>{{Small|'''Note''': Explains the NCR's policy towards Enclave members.}}</ref> the remnants were forced to keep moving to the fringes of NCR territories, to stay ahead of their pursuers.<ref name="ripgannon">The [[Courier]]: ''"Where did the Enclave go?"''<br />Arcade Gannon: ''"A lot of different places. I only know what I was told. Some of them were cut down by the NCR and the Brotherhood. Some of them went east. My mother never told me what happened to my father, but she and I went south with some others and integrated into the NCR. When the NCR learned that Enclave personnel had integrated, we kept moving to the fringes. It's one of the reasons why I wound up out here."''<br />([[VFSArcadeGannon.txt|Arcade Gannon's dialogue]])</ref><ref name="ArcadeHappenings"/> Wanting to follow them and make sure they were safe, Arcade passed it off to his superiors as choosing an assignment on the frontier, to focus on Follower operations where he could do the most good.<ref name="joiningandcoming"/>

Following the remnants as far from the NCR as the Mojave Wasteland, Arcade settled in the [[New Vegas]] area where he integrated with [[Julie Farkas]]' chapter of the Followers residing at the [[Old Mormon Fort]] in [[Freeside]]. He chose to focus on botanical research at Julie's request, trying to come up with alternative treatments for common illnesses and injuries, and ways to produce medical supplies from local [[flora]] (eg. [[stimpak]]s from [[Barrel cactus|barrel cacti]]). While he considers the research noble, Arcade recognizes it as a waste of time due to the low probability of him actually making a breakthrough.<ref name="research" /> At best, he hopes to rediscover old, forgotten ways of using these plants, which would help the Followers produce their own supplies once the dwindling number of un-looted pre-War hospitals runs out.<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"What's the goal?"''<br />Arcade Gannon: ''"For the past hundred years or so, the Followers have managed to get by using salvaged medical supplies from the Old World. But the side effect of medical success is that more people live longer. Funny how that works. Eventually, we'll run out of hospitals to loot. We need new ways to produce those supplies. Or maybe old ways, if this research goes anywhere."''<br />([[VFSArcadeGannon.txt|Arcade Gannon's dialogue]])</ref> While he finds research boring, he appreciates being kept out of sight and out of mind by Julie, claiming he's not a "people person."<ref name="research">Arcade Gannon: ''"Hi. Finding alternative treatments for common illnesses and injuries. Stimpaks out of barrel cacti and other fantastic improbabilities.
