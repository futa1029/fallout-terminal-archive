===Companion===
* Arcade can be recruited as a companion if any of the following requirements are met:
** Having a ''Liked'' or better [[Fallout: New Vegas reputations#Followers of the Apocalypse|reputation]] with the Followers.
*** He can still be recruited through other methods even with a negative Followers reputation. 
** Completing [[High Times]] for [[Julie Farkas]] as well as ''[[Short-Term Treatment]]'' by giving surplus supplies to the Followers.
*** For this approach, one needs to first ask, "Do you need any help?" after which Arcade will thank the Courier for their work for the Followers and the join option will appear.
** Passing a 75 [[Speech (Fallout: New Vegas)|Speech]] skill check. 
** Having the [[Confirmed Bachelor]] [[Fallout: New Vegas perks|perk]].
** Having an [[Intelligence (Fallout: New Vegas)|Intelligence]] attribute level of 3 or lower will get Arcade to follow out of a sense of pity. Temporary Intelligence-reduction effects that achieve the minimum can be used in this situation if no other options are available.
** As with [[Craig Boone]], Arcade will refuse to join as a companion if one has a positive reputation with Caesar's Legion. To bypass this, one can wear NCR faction armor and talk to Arcade again. It will temporarily bypass the faction check and allow Arcade to become a companion. Though, this will be cut short and Arcade will leave the Courier, this can be bypassed by unequipping the armor and heading to the Legion's fort, starting his dialogue to begin [[For Auld Lang Syne]].

===Quests===
* [[For Auld Lang Syne]]: After the Courier has sufficiently shifted the balance of power in the Mojave, Arcade asks them to help him in rallying the Enclave remnants to put their considerable technological and military advantage to use at [[Hoover Dam]].
* [[Beyond the Beef]]: Arcade (or any other human companion) can be offered as a substitute main course to [[Mortimer (Fallout: New Vegas)|Mortimer]] for the banquet.<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''{{Tooltip|"Would my companion here make a suitable replacement meal?"|Hushed}}''<br/>[[Mortimer (Fallout: New Vegas)|Mortimer]]: ''"Hmm. He's a little thin, but he looks like he comes from good stock with that strong jaw and upright posture."''<br/>([[VMS18Mortimer.txt|Mortimer's dialogue]])</ref>
* [[Bitter Springs Infirmary Blues]]: If Arcade is an active companion, a unique dialogue option is unlocked where he can teach [[Markland]] the specialized medical training he needs, bypassing needing to acquire [[Tiny, Tiny Babies: All You Need to Know About Pediatric Medicine]] and [[Stress and the Modern Refugee: A Primer]] for him.
* [[Et Tumor, Brute?]]: If Arcade is an active companion, a unique dialogue option is unlocked where he can be sold into slavery as [[Caesar]]'s personal doctor, completing the quest and incurring [[Karma (Fallout: New Vegas)|Karma]] loss.
* [[ED-E My Love]]: If asked where to send [[ED-E]] between the Followers or the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Mojave chapter)|Brotherhood]], Arcade will strongly advocate for the Followers.
* [[That Lucky Old Sun]]: If Arcade is an active companion when talking to [[Fantastic]] at [[HELIOS One]], he will urge the player character to send power to the slums of Freeside and Westside. If one instead chooses to arm [[ARCHIMEDES|ARCHIMEDES I]] to kill the NCR stationing Helios, Arcade will become angry and permanently leave the party, or even become hostile depending on certain dialogue.
* [[The White Wash]]: If Arcade is an active companion when confronting [[Tom Anderson]], if one chooses to keep his secret rather than turn him in, Arcade will be very vocal and pleased about it. If Anderson is killed, Arcade will question the decision.

===Making Arcade quit===
Arcade will get fed up with the Courier if they do or say things that he dislikes. He has an internal counter of "dislike" points that are increased by doing or saying stupid or mean things or doing things that hurt the Followers. Once he has accumulated 3 "dislike" points, he will warn the Courier that they are "acting crazy." Most of these possible "dislike" points may be obtained during [[The White Wash]] quest. At 6 points, he will leave permanently. He also tracks "like" points, but those are only used to decide if he will start the For Auld Lang Syne quest early or not.

The "dislike" points are triggered by the following statements or actions:
* Relating to The White Wash quest:
** 1 point for saying "Right. The Followers don't endorse his actions, and all that." when Arcade comments on [[Tom Anderson|Anderson]], when visiting [[Westside]] after already having completed The White Wash without Arcade.
** 1 point for saying "They profit from crime, now I profit, too." when Arcade chastises the Courier if they finished the quest by extorting money from the [[Westside Co-op]].
