The peace Raul coveted did not last forever. The former state of Arizona had become overrun with raiders and tribals between the Great War and the eventual rise of [[Caesar's Legion]] to tame the area. At some point, a group led by [[Dirty Dave]] and his six brothers rolled into town looking for supplies of bullets. Raul offered to sell them ammunition in the hope they would take it and leave; instead, the raiders decided they wanted the brothel. Once inside the brothel, Dave and his brothers mercilessly killed four girls and took Claudia for their own use before leaving. Raul chased them and caught up after three days. However, by the time he found Claudia, the raiders had cruelly executed her. Fueled by the same rage when Rafaela was killed, Raul charged in and killed all of them. Youth, however, was not on his side anymore and the raiders managed to inflict severe wounds on him in return before dying; he claims he only stayed alive purely to spite them.<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"What happened with her?"''<br />Raul Tejada: ''"This was a long time ago. ===Black Mountain===
Dragged back into a depressed state, Raul left Two-Sun and wandered west, where he eventually arrived in the Mojave Wasteland. He built himself [[Raul's shack|a small shack]] in a relatively quiet corner of the desert east of [[New Vegas]], and settled down there, residing there for decades and eventually spending his days listening to the [[Black Mountain Radio]] when it came back online over 200 years after the war. Some time prior to, or during 2281, Raul discovered the radio's signal had stopped transmitting and decided to wander up to [[Black Mountain]] to investigate. There, he was apprehended by the [[State of Utobitha]] but managed to negotiate a deal where he offered his services to [[Tabitha (Fallout: New Vegas)|Tabitha]], the [[nightkin]] leader of the super mutants living at Black Mountain, and fix the transmitter, but to his dismay, Tabitha decided instead to imprison him for his efforts, and threatened him into working for her, mostly with repairing broken toasters for the super mutants as well as performing repairs to her broken Mr. Handy, [[Rhonda (Fallout: New Vegas)|Rhonda]].<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"What happened then?"''<br />Raul Tejada: ''"When I recovered - more or less, anyways - I left Tuscon and headed west. I ran into Tabitha at Black Mountain and, well, the rest you know. I swore I was done with the gunslinging life - I was too old, too slow, and too beat up to protect anyone anymore. I thought I was done forever... but after traveling with you, I realize I've always had my doubts."''<br />([[RaulTejada.txt|Raul Tejada's dialogue]])</ref>

==Personality==
By the time the Courier finds him during his captivity at Black Mountain, Raul has been beaten down by a hard life. He initially declines to tell anything about himself other than the name that his "boss" can call him by.<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"Who are you?"''<br />Raul: ''"Name's Raul. Raul Alfonso Tejada. His humble nature belies his self-confidence, as over two hundred years of living in the wasteland has tempered his skills and his ability to stay calm under pressure. For example, when he first came to Black Mountain naively volunteering to repair the equipment of the Utobitha super mutants, he was declared an intruder and enemy of the state by Tabitha, to be set for immediate execution. However, Raul was able to keep his wits about him and prolong his execution day by day by leveraging his skills as a mechanic to repair Rhonda, then privately neglecting to do so while exploiting the super mutant's mental instability.

Raul is perhaps the most tolerant towards the [[Caesar's Legion|Legion]] when compared to the other humanoid companions who generally fear the inevitable suffering should the Legion be victorious. ===Quests===
* [[Crazy, Crazy, Crazy]]: Raul can be recruited as part of the quest, or simply by traveling to Black Mountain and busting him out. In order to free Raul, one needs to unlock his prison door, via the Very Hard-locked terminal next to the door. It can be accessed either by hacking it, or by receiving the password (123456789) for it, the latter of which can be found by reading [[Black Mountain terminal entries#Entry 6|entry 6]] on the non-locked terminal in the same room. 
* ''[[Old School Ghoul]]'': While Raul is an active [[Fallout: New Vegas companions|companion]], talk to and exhaust all dialogue options for [[Loyal]], [[Sterling (Fallout: New Vegas)|Corporal Sterling]], and [[Andy (Ranger)|Ranger Andy]]. Upon finishing the last conversation, Raul will open up a new dialogue with the Courier. Note that killing any of the non-player characters will cause the quest to fail, resulting in Raul's new apparel to be unobtainable, except through console commands.
