,ps3,xbox360}} When Raul awakens after being knocked unconscious, he will sometimes claim to have had a nightmare of Petro Chico Boy "stealing his chicharrones (Spanish for pork rinds)", but the subtitles will incorrectly use the word "chitlins" instead of "chicharrones". {{Verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|xbox360}} Sometimes if the player speaks to him in the Lucky 38 presidential suite, a generic [[Great Khans|Great Khan]] will spawn. {{Verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|ps3,xbox360}} By pressing "Take All" when pickpocketing Raul, the caps will still appear in the player's inventory despite being caught. To repeat this again, take him outside of his shack and dismiss him as a companion. This allows the player to get free repairs infinitely. {{Verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|xbox360}} When leaving a casino, if Raul is given a weapon, the weapon's weight will be present, but no item can be seen. {{Verify|verified}}
