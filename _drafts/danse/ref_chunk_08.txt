===''Fallout: The Board Game''===
{{FBG}}
Paladin Danse can be acquired at the shop by any [[Fallout: The Board Game player characters|player character]] that has [[Charisma (Fallout 4)|Charisma]]. When he is the active companion and there is another player character within two spaces, the owner can exhaust him to move the target character one space in any direction. If the owner has the [[Fallout: The Board Game player characters#Traits|Idolized trait]] when they do this, they will lose it.

When the player character performs the camp action, he will become unexhausted. However, if the player character does not have the Idolized trait, he must be discarded.

==Notes==
* Due to his strong relationship with the Brotherhood, Danse is the only companion who will refuse to accompany the Sole Survivor during if they side with the Institute and start [[Mass Fusion]].
* Paladin Danse always wears a full set of BOS [[T-60 power armor (Fallout 4)|T-60 power armor]], minus the helmet (except temporarily during [[Call to Arms]]). Following [[Blind Betrayal]], when meeting him again, he will have upgraded to a suit of [[X-01 power armor (Fallout 4)|X-01 power armor]]. Unlike other companions, he cannot be commanded to leave his power armor, and the parts do not appear in his inventory.
** He can be supplied with upgraded armor pieces by transferring it to his inventory and equipping it.
** Danse is the only companion whose power armor, even if equipped with a different suit than his own, will not deteriorate.
* If the player character visits the Cambridge Police Station for the first time after completing [[The Nuclear Option (Minutemen)|The Nuclear Option]] with the Minutemen, Danse will comment on their victory and immediately reward [[Righteous Authority]] without having to complete Call to Arms.

===Post Blind Betrayal===
{{Notable content
| Sparing Danse after [[Blind Betrayal]] results in him losing his companion functionality when starting [[Mass Fusion]] or after [[Airship Down]] for [[the Institute]].
| It is impossible to max out approval or complete a romantic relationship with Danse before completing [[Blind Betrayal]].
** Blind Betrayal can be started and completed normally via the console on the PC to regain the possibility of being idolized by Danse. While it does not appear that one needs to dismiss him he will need to leave and may behave unexpectedly if he is not. Completing this quest will automatically start [[Tactical Thinking]].
| He will use dialogue lines that imply he is still in the Brotherhood despite being exiled after Blind Betrayal.
| After Blind Betrayal, having him accompany the Survivor as a companion turns the Brotherhood of Steel faction hostile. Dismissing Danse returns the Brotherhood to its normal disposition towards the player character, though they will continue to attack Danse.
| After learning of his true nature Danse will approve of some comments that support synths. For example, when the player character agrees with [[Tony Savoldi]]'s comments on synths' rights. This is possibly a developer oversight as Danse still hates/dislikes saving [[Amelia Stockton]] during [[Human Error]] after Blind Betrayal.
| Dismissing Danse to the Boston Airport after completing The Nuclear Option with the Brotherhood results in Danse and [[Liberty Prime]] engaging in an endless battle as neither can be killed. This generally results in the deaths of the unnamed Brotherhood soldiers guarding the tarmac who either get killed by Danse or Prime in the process.
** If one brings other companions to the airport they also will fight the Brotherhood alongside Danse.
** The Sole Survivor can kill any unnamed Brotherhood soldiers while Danse is following (including those on the Prydwen and at the airport) without becoming an enemy with the faction.
* If Danse is spared after [[Blind Betrayal]], Brotherhood of Steel vertibirds may attack settlements where Danse is located.
