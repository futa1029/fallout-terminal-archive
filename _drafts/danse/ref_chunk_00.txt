'''Paladin Danse''' ([[Brotherhood registration|registration]] '''DN-407P''') is a member of the [[Commonwealth Brotherhood of Steel|East Coast division]] of the [[Brotherhood of Steel]], serving as the commanding officer of [[Recon Squad Gladius]] in [[the Commonwealth]] in [[Timeline#2287|2287]]. He appears {{In|FO4}} as a possible [[Fallout 4 companions|companion]] of the [[Sole Survivor]]. 

==Background==
===Past===
Danse's earliest memories are of growing up as an orphan in the [[Capital Wasteland]]. As a child, he made a dangerous living by scavenging the [[Washington, D.C.|D.C.]] ruins until he opened a junk stand in [[Rivet City]]. Krieg presumably saw the spark of greatness in Danse, a correct prediction as by [[Timeline#2277|2277]] Danse had reached the rank of paladin and was given command of his own squad. Danse did not participate in [[Battle of Adams Air Force Base|the assault]] on [[Adams Air Force Base]], and learned after that Paladin Krieg had been among the casualties. Danse continued on to excel as a soldier. In the ideological schism that had sundered the Brotherhood's east coast chapter, he aligned with the traditional utilitarian philosophy that was the order's original doctrine over [[Owyn Lyons|Elder Owyn Lyons]]' benevolent and philanthropic agenda. When [[Arthur Maxson]] assumed the role of elder, Danse's likemindedness and devotion to his duty led to him becoming one of the highest-ranking officers under the new leadership. 

===Coming to the Commonwealth===
As one of the most capable field commanders in the Brotherhood, Paladin Danse was a natural choice to head up a third reconnaissance mission into the Commonwealth. [[Recon Squad Gladius]] was dispatched to the region in late 2286 for a tech recovery operation, and, if possible, to determine the fate of the lost [[recon squad Artemis]]. No one expected that his expedition would become another turning point in the Brotherhood's history.<ref>[[Cambridge Police Station terminal entries#Recon Squad Gladius Orders|Cambridge Police station terminals; Gladius personnel terminal, Recon Squad Gladius Orders]]</ref>

The squad crossed the border into the Commonwealth in January 2287, and soon after suffered several equipment losses in skirmishes with [[Raiders (Fallout 4)|raiders]], including [[Keane|Knight Keane's]] power armor.<ref>[[Cambridge Police Station terminal entries#Log 012287-1|Gladius personnel terminal, Log 012287-1]].</ref> By February, they had established a forward operating base in the [[Cambridge Police Station]].<ref>[[Cambridge police station terminals#Log 022287-2|Gladius personnel terminal, Log 022287-2]]</ref> By April, Danse felt Gladius had a strong enough position to begin recovery and search operations.
