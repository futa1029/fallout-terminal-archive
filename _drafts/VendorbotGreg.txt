'''Vendor bot Greg''' is a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] operating in the town of [[Grafton]] in [[Appalachia]]. 

==Background==
Vendor bot Greg was established as part of a [[Responders]] trading outpost in Grafton, where it still functions as a [[Fallout 76 vendors|vendor]]. However, by 2103, Greg is all that is left of the Responders' presence in Grafton.
