=====The cold fusion deal=====
Barb Howard's trip to Las Vegas, during which she would deliver the cold fusion diode to House, was set to take place not long after this meeting. However, [[Lee Moldaver|Kate Williams]], the inventor of this cold fusion technology, had been tracking Vault-Tec's plans due to her seeming suspicion that Vault-Tec intended to start nuclear war, and she somehow acquired the details of House's trade for cold fusion. She concluded that House was working with Vault-Tec and would give them the weapons that would enable them to kick-start nuclear war in exchange for the cold fusion diode, and subsequently convinced Cooper Howard to visit Las Vegas alongside his wife to assassinate Robert House and retrieve the cold fusion diode. This quickly caught House's attention, as his predictions indicated that Barb was supposed to come to the meeting alone; as soon as Cooper bought his ticket, his predicted date for the occurrence of nuclear war jumped forward by one month, indicating that Cooper was also an unknown variable of interest.<ref name="tvseriesdate" />

To scope out Cooper, House visited an [[American Veteran Entertainment Alliance]] awards ceremony in Los Angeles ahead of Barb's trip, posing as a civilian to get a sense of Cooper's loyalties and beliefs.<ref>{{FOTV|2|3}}</ref> Subsequently, when Cooper arrived to the Lucky 38 during Barb's trip, House took the opportunity to call Cooper directly to his penthouse, where he revealed that he knew why Cooper had come; that they both knew about the "demon in the snow" and Vault-Tec's conspiracy; that he considered Cooper a significant person of interest for his influence over House's calculations of nuclear war; and that he believed some other party -- the same one which created the "demon in the snow" -- would ultimately be responsible for dropping the nukes.<ref name="tvseriesdate" /><ref name="another player" /> Cooper rejected House's claims, calling him a lunatic; House in turn derided Cooper as a "killer" and possibly the one who directly would end the world as Cooper left.<ref name="lunatic">[[Cooper Howard]]: ''"What if you're just a fucking lunatic?"''<br />Robert House: ''"I'm a lunatic?"''<br />Cooper Howard: ''"Yeah."''<br />Robert House: ''"I would encourage you to gaze inward, Mr. Howard, to your small life, spending your days pretending to be a cowboy, while your wife is making apocalyptic proposals at board meetings, and I'm building a missile system to defend this glorious city from what's coming. Cooper's interference resulted in House never receiving the cold fusion diode. ===The Great War===
Though the loss of the cold fusion diode ruined House's ability to use his non-biological backup plan, he was still able to preserve himself biologically inside the Lucky 38. However, House still could not guarantee the successful execution of his plans without one key item: the [[platinum chip]], a computer chip containing a vital OS upgrade for his Securitrons and the Las Vegas anti-missile laser defense network. The chip was to be delivered to him in the afternoon of October 23, 2077. However, a miscalculation of mere hours by House prevented its arrival in time.<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"Why didn't the Platinum Chip arrive on time?"''<br />Robert House: ''"The Platinum Chip was printed in Sunnyvale, California on October 22nd, 2077 - the day before the Great War. It was to have been delivered by courier the following afternoon... but by then, the world had ended. The Chip contained vital software upgrades, but not just for my Securitrons. Every aspect of the missile defense grid would have been upgraded, too. Given that I had to make do with buggy software, the outcome could have been worse. I nearly died as it was."''<br />([[MrHouse.txt|Robert House's dialogue]])</ref> 20 hours before the chip's scheduled delivery, the [[Great War]] occurred, resulting in the chip being lost in the ruins for the next 200 years.

Forced to work with an inferior OS, House endured numerous system crashes but was still able to protect Las Vegas from most, though not all, of the nuclear warheads aimed at the city.<ref name="genius" /> The strain on his systems nearly killed him, but House managed to survive.<ref name="NVMissiles" /><ref name="DieDefendVegas" >The [[Courier]]: ''"How did you nearly die, defending Vegas?"''<br />Robert House: ''"Software glitches set off a cascade of system crashes. I had to take the Lucky 38's reactor offline, lest it melt down. For nearly five years I battled power outages and more system crashes until I finally managed to reboot my data core with an older version of the OS. I spent the next few decades in a veritable coma. But I survived, obviously - and eventually thrived."''<br />([[MrHouse.txt|Robert House's dialogue]])</ref> He spent the next five years struggling against power outages and frequent crashes until he rebooted his data core with a downgraded version of his software. This left House in a coma for the next several decades, allowing the remains of Vegas to be picked over by the people of the surrounding wasteland.<ref name="DieDefendVegas" />

===Post-War===
====Creating New Vegas====

House regained consciousness from his coma in [[Timeline#2138|2138]].<ref name="FNV guide" group="Supplement">''[[Fallout: New Vegas Official Game Guide]] Collector's Edition'' p.454: ''"2138: Mr. House regains consciousness."''<br />([[Fallout: New Vegas Official Game Guide/Behind the Bright Lights & Big City]])</ref> Instead of immediately revealing himself, he opted to stay hidden and wait for signs of an organized civilization having returned. Eventually, he entered the world stage once again in 2274, when [[Securitron]]s under his command emerged from the [[Lucky 38]]. This action was prompted by the detection of [[New California Republic]] scouts arriving at [[Hoover Dam]]. In order to establish his rule, House enlisted the help of three of the [[Tribals|tribes]] who had descended from [[Vault]] dwellers and were now living in the Las Vegas ruins.<ref group="Supplement" name="filter">[[Fallout: New Vegas Official Game Guide]] Collector's Edition p.42: ''"Freeside"''<br />''"Las Vegas wasn't heavily damaged during the Great War, but people didn't immediately "settle" into the remnants of the old city. Mr House directed the tribes to use his sizable quantities of pre-war construction materials to build the crude (but effective) outer walls that separate The Strip and Freeside from the rest of New Vegas. While Mr. House valued the area around Fremont Street, he ultimately viewed it as secondary in importance to the Strip itself and he had a second, inner wall that separated the two areas. When NCR prospectors (and eventually the army) arrived in the region, people typically went straight for The Strip, leaving Freeside (as it had become known by locals) as an informal stopping point. Eventually Mr. House recognized that he could use Freeside as a filter for undesirables, and pulled his favored tribes and all Securitrons into the Strip, leaving Freeside to fend for itself."''<br />([[Fallout: New Vegas Official Game Guide faction profiles]])</ref>

These three tribes became the [[Three Families]]: the [[Chairmen]], the [[Omertas]], and the [[White Glove Society]]. Under House's guidance, they forced out all of their rivals and rebuilt the [[New Vegas Strip]], establishing the [[Free Economic Zone of New Vegas]] and accepting House's rule as its president, CEO and sole proprietor. Those forced out of the Strip were left to fend for themselves in surrounding areas such as what would later become [[Freeside]]; House purposefully left these areas alone, realizing that they were useful filters for people he did not want on the Strip.<ref group="Supplement" name="filter" /><ref>[[Courier]]: ''"How have the Khans been kicked around?"''<br />[[Yes Man]]: ''"They were one of the tribes the Three Families pushed out of Vegas! House's consolidation of power came just in time for the arrival of the [[New California Republic Army]]'s advance forces, with House sending his Securitrons and tribal "employees" to occupy Hoover Dam as a show of force. By [[Timeline#2281|2281]], House's efforts led to the establishment of New Vegas as a major power in the Mojave Wasteland through lucrative trade with the New California Republic and exploitation of the NCR's citizens as customers, guided by his aims to use the NCR's conflict with [[Caesar's Legion]] to further expand his control of the region.

House continued to reside in the Lucky 38 and control the Securitrons as his envoys.
