====Preparing for the war====
{{Quotation|Like yourself, [[The Ghoul|Mr. Howard]], I am an apex competitor. We are the very best at what we do. And what I do, is I know everything.|House to [[The Ghoul|Cooper Howard]] in "[[The Wrangler]]"}}

Armed with his knowledge of the coming war, House devoted the next twelve years making preparations to ensure his home city would survive the apocalypse and that he would live to see the world after the war.<ref name="Houseprepare" /> While the primary goal of his countermeasures was to disable incoming nuclear warheads by remotely transmitting disarm codes, House included contingencies for directly destroying any that managed to get through using laser detonations and defensive missiles or rockets.<ref name="genius" /><ref name="NVMissiles">The [[Courier]]: ''"What preparations did you make to save Las Vegas?"''<br />Robert House: ''"On the day of the Great War, 77 atomic warheads targeted Las Vegas, [[Hoover Dam]], and its surrounding areas. Laser cannons mounted on the roof of the Lucky 38 destroyed another 9 warheads. The rest got through, though none hit the city itself. A sub-optimal performance, admittedly. If only the Platinum Chip had arrived a day sooner..."''<br />([[MrHouse.txt|Robert House's dialogue]])</ref><ref name="lunatic" /> House also acted to prevent other factions from taking over Vegas: though he allowed Vault-Tec to build a [[Las Vegas management Vault|specialized management Vault]] underneath the city, he also sabotaged it to ensure it would not be able to serve its intended purpose.<ref name="outclassed" />

House took equally drastic steps to preserve his own life: biologically, House planned to place his physical body within a [[Life support chamber|life-support device]], removing House's physical presence but wiring his brain directly into his vast information network via an enormous supercomputer to ensure he could continue to influence the world after the bombs.<ref name="HouseHealth">The [[Courier]]: ''"You appear to be a computer, not a man."''<br />Robert House: ''"Don't let the video screens and computer terminals fool you. Sometime during the [[Battle of Anchorage]], House gained access to the [[T-45 power armor]] worn by [[United States Marine Corps|U.S. Marine]] [[The Ghoul|Cooper Howard]] via a piece of software he had licensed to [[West Tek]]. Through this, House was able to observe the situation on the [[Alaska]]n frontline, including Cooper's encounter with a "[[Deathclaw (TV series)|demon in the snow]]." House recognized that this "demon" was a piece of biotechnology developed by some unknown party, and came to believe that this was heavily intertwined with the future of the United States, as well as to Vault-Tec through Cooper's wife [[Barb Howard]], a high-ranking Vault-Tec executive.<ref>Robert House: ''"Mr. Howard, I was there with you in Alaska."''<br />[[Cooper Howard]]: ''"I doubt that very much."''<br />Robert House: ''"Not physically, of course. Later in the 2070s, House and RobCo Industries developed a [[brain-computer interface chip]], which possessed mind control capabilities. House himself conducted a field test of the chip in [[Los Angeles]], [[California]] on a construction worker named [[Bill (TV series)|Bill]], successfully ordering the man to attack and kill his friends. Although the field test went awry when House cranked up the power of the chip using a remote control device, causing Bill's head to explode, House still saw it as remarkable progress.<ref name="s2e1" /> By 2077, House had instead set his sights on a different technology: Vault-Tec's proprietary [[cold fusion diode]], which he intended to use to power his digitized consciousness backup. House brokered a deal with Vault-Tec's leadership to trade his brain-computer control chip technology in exchange for the cold fusion diode, which he would then receive from Barb Howard during a defense contractors' summit in Las Vegas later in the year.<ref name="acquire" /><ref name="auto man">[[Robert House's double]]: ''"RobCo presents the automated man. That same year, not long after informing Barb Howard of his deal for cold fusion, House was invited to a secret Vault-Tec meeting led by Barb Howard and [[Bud Askins]], also attended by American corporate representatives Frederick Sinclair, [[Julia Masters]], and [[Leon Von Felden]], where Barb Howard indicated Vault-Tec was willing to detonate the bombs themselves to ensure their profits. However, House did not trust these figures or their plans, merely sending his body double to the meeting, whom he used via earpiece to call out the plan's flaws. Viewing the plan as unsound from the very start, House came to believe that none of the figures present, nor Vault-Tec itself, would be the ones to launch the bombs. Instead, he suspected that the war would be caused by a hidden party -- the same one which created the "demon in the snow" he had seen in Alaska.
