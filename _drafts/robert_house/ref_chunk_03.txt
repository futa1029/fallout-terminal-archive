===Personality===
{{Quotation|I have no interest in abusing others, just as I have no interest in legislating or otherwise dictating what people do in their private time. Nor have I any interest in being worshipped as some kind of machine god messiah. I am impervious to such corrupting ambitions. But autocracy? Firm control in the hands of a technological and economic visionary? Yes, that Vegas shall have.}}

Mr. House is a very reclusive individual. Even in the pre-War era, he personally shunned overt public appearances, using a body double for all of his public affairs. Eventually, he became a total recluse, making him the subject of rumors;<ref name="beatrix" /> this may have been due to his integration into his life support technology shortly before the Great War. House remained just as reclusive after the Great War, never directly appearing in public even through a robotic medium and never allowing anyone into the Lucky 38.<ref name="FNVOGG L38" group="Supplement" />

Mr. House seeks to restore [[New Vegas]] to its pre-War glory as a crown jewel of technological innovation and a bright neon paradise of business and fortune. Having experienced pre-War Vegas first-hand, he has long been enamored with the city's beauty and grandeur. His vision of autocracy and disillusionment with democratic society stems from his resentment towards the attitudes and actions of the increasingly desperate pre-War [[United States of America|United States]], whose decline was precipitated by the failure to adequately research and develop alternative [[Technology|technologies]]. His ultimate goal is to guide civilization's progress and forge a new future for mankind, free of the corrupting influences of the past. House's unwillingness to allow his life's work to be ruined by what he perceives as an increasingly unstable world has cemented, in his mind, the fate of democracy and human civilization itself. His contempt for most post-apocalyptic institutions, from bottle caps<ref name="chip recovery">The [[Courier]]: ''"I have a right to know more."''<br />Robert House: ''"That's simply not true. I am the only person to hold any rights pertaining to the Chip. I designed it, and I paid for it - dearly. To develop that Chip, I spent a sum of U.S. dollars - not the bottle caps that pass as currency these days - but a sum beyond counting. For decades, I paid salvagers to comb the ruins for it. And when it was finally discovered, tens of thousands of caps spent to have it brought here. We know how that turned out. Complete your contract and it will be the last time I pay for the Chip. Save your questions for then."''<br />([[MrHouse.txt|Robert House's dialogue]])</ref> to the various factions, stems from what he perceives as humanity's inability to learn from its past mistakes.<ref name="fate of democracy" />
