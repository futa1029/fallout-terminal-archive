'''Morris Stevens' holotape''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] {{In|FO76}}. 
__TOC__
==Location==
The holotape can be found at [[Bolton Greens]], on a desk in the blue room with the [[Gourmands]]' terminal, off the kitchen.
