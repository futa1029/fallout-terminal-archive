'''Ada''' is a [[robot]] residing in [[the Commonwealth]] as the only survivor of an ambush on her creator [[Jackson (Automatron)|Jackson]]'s caravan by the [[Mechanist (Automatron)|Mechanist]]'s robots in [[Timeline#2287|2287]]. She appears {{In|FO4AUT}} as a possible [[Fallout 4 companions|companion]] of the [[Sole Survivor]].

==Background==
Created by [[Jackson (Automatron)|Jackson]] to be a traveling companion for his caravan, Ada is a heavily modified [[Assaultron (Fallout 4)|Assaultron]] retrofitted with [[Sentry bot (Fallout 4)|sentry bot]] and [[Protectron (Fallout 4)|Protectron]] parts.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"What kind of robot are you?"''<br>Ada: ''"{{tooltip|{Neutral} I am heavily modified, but at my core, I am an Assaultron. |Neutral}}"''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref><ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Who's Jackson?"''<br />[[Ada]]: ''"{{Tooltip|Jackson was the leader of this caravan. He had a great mind for technology. In many ways, he is my creator. He installed all my current upgrades. Unfortunately, they were not enough to protect them from the robots.|Neutral}}"''<br />([[DLC01Ada.txt#954|Ada's dialogue]])</ref> The group specialized in mechanical repairs and trade and made a living traveling throughout the wasteland looking for ruins full of spare parts to use or sell;<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Tell me about these robots."''<br>Ada: ''"{{tooltip|They roam the Commonwealth and claim to be restoring order. We've seen them fighting Raiders, but they've also attacked innocents. Settlers, merchants... and caravans like ours. We knew we ran the risk of encountering more of these hostile robots if we stayed in the Commonwealth. If only we had made the decision to leave.|Neutral}}"''<br>The Sole Survivor: ''"So why did you end up staying?"''<br>Ada: ''"{{tooltip|Our caravan specialized in mechanical repairs and trade. The Commonwealth has an abundance of salvage that would benefit our business. It was a calculated risk.|Neutral}}"''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref> Ada primarily assisted a member named [[Zoe (Automatron)|Zoe]] with setting up temporary defenses whenever the caravan stopped to scavenge, including places such as an old salvage yard and a robotics factory where they acquired [[Hurtz]].<ref>[[Zoe's diary]]</ref> They had at least one prior encounter with the [[Rust Devils]], a [[Raider (Fallout 4)|raider]] gang specializing in robotics engineering, during a trek across the [[Charles River]] into [[Boston]] where they learned about the group's own destructive methods.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Who or what are the 'Rust Devils?'"''<br>Ada: ''"Allow me to explain. My old caravan encountered them once while crossing the river into the city. The Rust Devils are a group of ruthless Raiders who utilize robots for their nefarious practices. They scour the Commonwealth looking for my kind. What they can't capture and reprogram, they strip for spare parts. I realize they pose a greater threat to me than you... I just want you to be well informed."''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref>

Around late [[Timeline#2287|2287]], the caravan suffered a quick succession of three attacks by similarly-patchwork robots that spouted the [[Mechanist (Automatron)|Mechanist]]'s message of peace for the Commonwealth.<ref name="AdaRepeatAttacks">Ada: ''"My name is Ada. This was the third time we'd been attacked by robots like these. Even with the weapon and detection upgrades Jackson gave me, I was unable to defend them."''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref> After the first attack, the group debated whether to stay or leave the Commonwealth, but took the "calculated risk" to stay. They also salvaged some loose parts from the hostile robots, with Jackson intending to further modify Ada's combat ability.<ref>[[Jackson's holotape]]</ref> It never came to pass, however, as the final attack, taking place near an old [[Wattz Consumer Electronics]] outlet, claimed the lives of her human companions and the two other robots with them, [[Porter (Automatron)|Porter]] and Hurtz, leaving only Ada still standing.<ref name="AdaRepeatAttacks"/>

==Personality==
While she was originally a manufactured automaton beholden to her programming, Ada, like other robots such as [[Codsworth]] and [[KL-E-0]], gradually evolved beyond her virtual routines and developed a humanistic personality capable of a wider range of emotions. This was due to the family-like bond she shared with Jackson and his colleagues; she remembers that the former directly told her he saw her as more than just a servitor robot, so he set her personality mode to "on" permanently.<ref name="AdaPersonalityMode">Ada: ''"{{tooltip|Ever since I let my friends die in that caravan, I feel like I've been failing in my duties. I chose the route we took the day we were attacked. Perhaps if I had selected an alternate route, they'd still be alive. Sometimes I wish that Jackson had programmed me with my personality mode set permanently to disabled so I wouldn't feel so upset.|Depressed}}{{Inline quote|sound=Fo4AUT Ada 00008A1E_1.ogg}}"''<br>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Your personality mode? What do you mean?"''<br>Ada: ''"{{tooltip|Most robots have a personality mode select available to them for their creator to utilize. Turn it on, and we're designed to talk and act more like a human. Turn it off, and we're no better than a mindless automaton.|Confident}} {{tooltip|Jackson said he didn't think of me as a servitor robot... he treated me more like a friend. So he permanently set my personality mode to 'on.' He said I should never lose my identity. I suppose I'm starting to think he was wrong.|Sad}}{{Inline quote|sound=Fo4AUT Ada 000103A8_1.ogg}}"''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref>

This helped Ada to see past the idea of the caravaneers being just her creators and instead gain affection for them as her "friends."<ref>Ada: ''"{{tooltip|I miss my friends. Liza always knew what to say in times of trouble.|Concerned but hopeful. / Neutral}}"''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Ada: ''"My friends would drink to celebrate. I often wished I could experience the appeal until I saw their condition the next morning."''<br>[[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]]</ref><ref>Ada: ''"{{tooltip|My friends would spend days at a good salvage yard.|Somewhat nostalgic. / Neutral}}"''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref> Their deaths by the Mechanist's robots likewise ignited in Ada intense feelings of sorrow as well as anger, fueling a desire for revenge against the one responsible,<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"{{tooltip|Are you ok?|"Easy" Charisma option}}"''<br>Ada: ''"{{tooltip|Physically, the damage is minor, but I am registering sadness and anger at the loss of my friends.|Somber}} {{tooltip|It may sound strange coming from a robot, but they were my... family. Again, I thank you for your efforts. I know they would have thanked you as well.|Neutral}}{{Inline quote|sound=Fo4AUT Ada 0000FD8C_1.ogg}}"''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref><ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"You want revenge, don't you?"''<br>Ada: ''"{{tooltip|I admit my goal is twofold. Stopping the Mechanist both protects the Commonwealth, and allows me to avenge my fallen friends. I won't rest until I see this threat dissolved. I owe it to them.|Neutral}}{{Inline quote|sound=Fo4AUT Ada 0001039F_1.ogg}}"''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref><ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"You couldn't have known something like this would happen."''<br>Ada: ''"{{tooltip|The probability of attack was high. I should have forced our departure. In recompense, I will seek justice for my friends and stop these robots from causing further harm. It's time to uncover the source and confront their leader: the Mechanist.|Neutral}}"''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref> though she cannot outwardly express this as well as a [[human]] could. She can even admit to the Sole Survivor that her emotional capability and the negative feelings it can generate has led her, more than once, to ponder if she would have been better off if Jackson disabled her personality mode and she remained an unfeeling robot.<ref name="AdaPersonalityMode"/>

===Companion===
* Ada becomes available as a companion after defeating the Mechanist robots near Wattz Consumer Electronics and agreeing to help her look for the Mechanist during [[Mechanical Menace]] (though it is not required to recruit her to continue the questline).
* Ada is the only companion aside from [[Dogmeat (Fallout 4)|Dogmeat]] that remains friendly to the player character regardless of actions, positive or negative. She will offer advice and her opinion depending on the situation like any other companion but will never gain or lose [[Affinity]]. As a result, though, Ada has no associated Affinity perk to gain.
* If Ada is an active companion, upon talking to her she may give several random [[Fallout 4 junk items|junk items]], a trait she shares with all player-built [[Automatron (companion)|automatrons]] that have their personality subroutines activated.
* She is one of three companions that can be ordered to steal owned items.
* She is one of three companions (excluding automatrons) able to be modifiable at the [[robot workbench]], the others being [[Curie]] (before completing [[Emergent Behavior]]) and [[Codsworth]].
* Being a modifiable robot, Ada can be upgraded with lockpicking or hacking capabilities to cover for the player character's potential shortcomings in any of those fields, whether they lack the [[Locksmith (Fallout 4)|requisite]] [[Hacker (Fallout 4)|perks]] themselves, or [[Cait]] or [[Nick Valentine]] are not available.
* If dismissed, and no location is specified, she will relocate to the [[Red Rocket truck stop]], but will not count towards that settlement's population. If a location is specified, including the Red Rocket truck stop, she will count towards the population of that settlement.
* When Ada is an active companion, NPCs like [[Diamond City security]] will not make any comments about her as they would with base game companions.

===Effects of player's actions===
* If the Mechanist is killed during [[Restoring Order]], Ada will take over the role as the quest-giver for the radiant [[Rogue Robot]] quests.

===Other interactions===
* Ada will not comment if taken to [[Nate]]'s/[[Nora]]'s corpse in [[Vault 111]].
* She will not ride as a passenger when boarding a [[Vertibird (Fallout 4)|vertibird]].<ref>Ada: ''"Unfortunately, a vertibird cannot properly accommodate me for air travel. I'll meet you at your destination."''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref>
* Ada apparently has a simulated sense of smell, as she will mention her "olfactory sensors" when brought near a location populated by [[Mirelurk (Fallout 4)|mirelurks]].<ref>Ada: ''"My olfactory sensors are catching the scent of rotten fish. Quite revolting for you, I'd imagine."''<br/>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref>
* If asked if she can activate her self-destruct mechanism, Ada says that Jackson prevented anything like that from being installed, knowing he had better plans for her than ending up as debris.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Are you able to self-destruct?"''<br>Ada: ''"{{tooltip|No, I can't. I'm fairly certain that my original creator had grander plans for me other than becoming a pile of debris.|A little offended / Sarcastic}}"''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref> She likewise refutes any requests to turn off her personality subroutine.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"I think it's time to disable your personality subroutine."''<br>Ada: ''"{{tooltip|Sorry, no can do. That switch was disabled when I was built. I'm afraid you're stuck with me this way.|Apologetic}}"''<br>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue/]])</ref>
* If brought to [[Boston Airport]] after completing [[Reunions]], Ada will mention that Jackson's caravan had previous run-ins with the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 4)|Brotherhood of Steel]] and that they were never to be provoked and avoided at all costs.<ref>Ada: ''"My past encounters with the Brotherhood of Steel were never good. Let's not provoke them."'' / ''"The Brotherhood collects technology. Jackson always made it a point to avoid them when possible."''<br/>([[DLC01Ada.txt|Ada's dialogue]])</ref>
