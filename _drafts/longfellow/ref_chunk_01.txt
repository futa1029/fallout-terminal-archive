===Quests===
* [[Walk in the Park]]: [[Olympia Avery|Captain Avery]] directs the [[Sole Survivor]] to Old Longfellow at [[the Last Plank]] for help navigating through [[the Fog]] to reach [[Acadia (location)|Acadia]] where [[Kasumi Nakano]] went.
* [[Shipbreaker (quest)|Shipbreaker]]: Regardless of whether he was brought there or not, after killing the unique [[Fog crawler (Far Harbor)|fog crawler]] [[Shipbreaker (creature)|Shipbreaker]], the Sole Survivor is prompted to speak with him about it.

===Companion===
* Old Longfellow becomes available as a companion after completing Walk in the Park by reaching the exterior of Acadia, either independently or with his guidance after meeting him in the Last Plank.
* When dismissed without choosing a specific settlement, Old Longfellow will return to [[Longfellow's cabin|his cabin]] northwest of Far Harbor, but he may occasionally make visits to the Last Plank as well.
* When picking up [[Fallout 4 junk items|junk items]], Old Longfellow will actually commend the Sole Survivor for doing so and will slightly increase his affinity as well, in direct contrast to most other companions (sans [[Piper Wright|Piper]], but her affinity does not rise).<ref>Old Longfellow: ''"{{tooltip|No such thing as junk, far as I'm concerned. Everythin's got a use.|Approving. Player is picking up junk/trash items, but you're a scavenger and survivor who can put anything to use.}}"''<br/>([[DLC03 CompanionOldLongfellow.txt#275|Old Longfellow's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Old Longfellow: ''"{{tooltip|No sense turnin' your nose up at somethin' that might be useful.|Approving. Player is picking up junk/trash items, but you're a scavenger and survivor who can put anything to use.}}"''<br/>([[DLC03 CompanionOldLongfellow.txt#275|Old Longfellow's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Old Longfellow: ''"{{tooltip|That could come in handy.|Approving. Player is picking up junk/trash items, but you're a scavenger and survivor who can put anything to use.}}"''<br/>([[DLC03 CompanionOldLongfellow.txt#275|Old Longfellow's dialogue]])</ref>
* Old Longfellow openly dislikes the [[Church of the Children of Atom|Children of Atom]] and will disapprove if the player character joins them during [[Visions in the Fog]]. If he is present during [[Best Left Forgotten]] when [[DiMA]] suggests the possibility of there being peace with the Children, Longfellow openly states he would enjoy fighting them. He will also dislike any quest choices involving being peaceful towards the Children such as agreeing to spare [[Gwyneth|Sister Gwyneth]] and reasoning with her during [[The Heretic]].

===Effects of player's actions===
* Gaining maximum [[affinity]] with Old Longfellow grants the [[Hunter's Wisdom]] perk, which decreases [[Damage Resistance]] and [[Energy Resistance]] of all animals and sea creatures by 25%.
* Old Longfellow cannot be [[Fallout 4 companions#Romance|romanced]].
* He will turn hostile if the Sole Survivor uses the kill code to turn off the [[Wind farm maintenance|wind farm]] leading to Far Harbor's destruction, either independently or as part of [[Cleansing the Land]]; if he is not brought to the wind farm as a companion before then, he may be found at the town battling the sea creatures attacking it.
* If [[Walk in the Park]] is completed before meeting Old Longfellow, he has unique dialogue when first speaking to the Sole Survivor and compliments them for being able to brave the Fog.<ref>Old Longfellow: ''"I heard you made it to Acadia by yourself. That takes some stones."''<br/>([[DLC03 CompanionOldLongfellow.txt|Old Longfellow's dialogue]])</ref>
* If [[The Way Life Should Be]] is completed by siding with [[Allen Lee]] and destroying Acadia, Longfellow makes an idle comment about someday moving into the abandoned observatory as a new residence above the Fog's coverage.<ref>Old Longfellow: ''"{{tooltip|With the synths gone, Acadia's wide open. Maybe I'll move in someday. Be nice to live above the Fog.|Musing to yourself}}"''<br/>([[DLC03 CompanionOldLongfellow.txt#590|Old Longfellow's dialogue]])</ref>
