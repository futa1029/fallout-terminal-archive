==Behind the scenes==
* Old Longfellow shares his name with the American poet [[Henry Wadsworth Longfellow]], who was also a native of [[Maine]].
* Concept art shows an early design of Old Longfellow where his face was more wrinkly and sunken to reflect his age and hard lifestyle, as well as him wearing the [[Old fisherman's hat (Far Harbor)|old fisherman's hat]] with his longcoat and smoking from a pipe.
* When near old shipwrecks around the shoreline, Old Longfellow may quote lines from the 1865 poem "O Captain! My Captain!" by [[wikipedia:Walt Whitman|Walt Whitman]] and the 1902 poem "Sea-Fever" by [[wikipedia:John Masefield|John Masefield]].<ref>Old Longfellow: ''"{{tooltip|O captain, my captain, our fearful trip is done. The ship has weather'd every rack, the prize we sought is won.|Rhetorical, wistful, sad. You love ships and are looking at a wrecked one.}}"''<br/>([[DLC03 CompanionOldLongfellow.txt#678|Old Longfellow's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Old Longfellow: ''"{{tooltip|I must go down to the seas again, to the lonely sea and the sky, and all I ask is a tall ship and a star to steer her by.|Rhetorical, wistful, sad. You love ships and are looking at a wrecked one.}}"''<br/>([[DLC03 CompanionOldLongfellow.txt#678|Old Longfellow's dialogue]])</ref>
