====Companion====
* Cait becomes available as a companion after entering and clearing the [[Combat Zone]] near [[Boston Common]], then talking to [[Tommy Lonegan]] on the theater stage.
* She has the unique trait of being able to pick locks, akin to having the [[Locksmith (Fallout 4)|Locksmith]] perk. She can be commanded to open any lock of any difficulty from Novice to Master and only requires having a reasonable supply of [[Bobby pin (Fallout 4)|bobby pins]] in her inventory.
* Cait can be romanced. 

====Effects of player's actions====
* Gaining maximum [[affinity]] with Cait grants the [[Trigger Rush]] perk, which offers a temporary increase to [[Action Points|AP]] regeneration when the player character's [[Hit Points|HP]] drops below 25% of its current total.
** Without completing [[Benign Intervention]], the maximum level of affinity that can be reached with her is 999, meaning completing that quest is required to gain the perk.
* Cait will actively use Psycho, [[Jet (Fallout 4)|Jet]], [[RadAway (Fallout 4)|RadAway]] and other [[chems]] in her inventory, using the appropriate animations. It might be beneficial to keep her stocked up as she appears to get friendly very quickly, and it might improve her usefulness in fights.
** However, she may react negatively, including lowering her affinity when the player character uses chems themselves after completing [[Benign Intervention]], berating them for being a "hypocrite" after helping her to cure her Psycho addiction.
* Before completing Benign Intervention, Cait likes when the Sole Survivor drinks hard alcoholic beverages ([[Bourbon (Fallout 4)|bourbon]], [[Rum (Fallout 4)|rum]], [[Vodka (Fallout 4)|vodka]], [[Whiskey (Fallout 4)|whiskey]] and [[Wine (Fallout 4)|wine]]). Drinking before sleeping and before waiting will speed up the process.

====Other interactions====
