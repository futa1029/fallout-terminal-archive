===''Fallout: The Board Game''===
* Cait can be acquired at the shop by any [[Fallout: The Board Game player characters|player character]] that has the [[Fallout: The Board Game player characters#Traits|Vilified trait]]. When the player character has her as the active companion and performs a test that uses [[Perception (Fallout 4)|Perception]], [[Agility (Fallout 4)|Agility]] or [[Luck (Fallout 4)|Luck]], she can be exhausted to gain one hit on the test. When the player character performs the camp action, she will become unexhausted. However, if the player character does not have at least one [[Fallout: The Board Game#Drugs|drug]] in their inventory, she must be discarded.

==Notes==
Cait is the most openly affectionate and flirtatious of the Sole Survivor's companions, frequently teasing that the Sole Survivor is "fishing for a snog," joking that "all this 'rest' is making [her] tired," and that she's pulled a muscle from "sleeping." This does not change the Lover's Embrace bonus value or duration; she will also continue her pre-romance spontaneous commentary and casual dialogue as well.

==Behind the scenes==
* Cait was written by quest designer [[Alan Nanes]].<ref group="Non-game">[https://web.archive.org/web/20200927132710/https://twitter.com/rpggamedev/status/664827679364005888 Alan Nanes on Twitter]: ''"@thecheekyscot Was awesome working with you on Cait :) People really digging her! Thanks for putting up with all that dialogue! #Fallout4"''</ref>
* Cait is voiced by actress [[Katy Townsend]], who is natively Scottish, but adopts an Irish accent for the role. In localized versions of the game, Cait's voice is dubbed by Julie Badoc (French), Jenny De Cesarei (Italian), Saeko Zōgō (Japanese) and Ana De Castro (Spanish).
* {{cut}} Cut dialogue for Tommy Lonegan indicates that at one stage during development, it was planned for the player character to be able to beat Cait in a fight in order to gain her as a companion.

==Bugs==
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} Cait's quest might not trigger after her confession about her addiction. Taking another companion, sending her back to a settlement, and then talking with her should trigger the quest (it may have to do with her being flagged as a companion). For more info about this bug, read [[Benign Intervention]].{{Verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc}} Cait will talk about wanting to "lower the dosage" if she thinks she is being followed in combat after Vault 95.{{Verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} During Cait's companion quest, Cait may begin to randomly collapse as if incapacitated during the dialogue. This will continue following completion of the quest.{{Verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} Ordering Cait to pick a floor safe when there is an object between the safe and Cait may sometimes cause her to pace aimlessly around the area.{{Verify|verified}}
