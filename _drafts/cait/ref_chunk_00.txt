'''Cait''' is a cage fighter working at the [[Combat Zone]] in [[the Commonwealth]] {{in|FO4}}. She is a possible [[Fallout 4 companions|companion]] of the [[Sole Survivor]]. 

==Background==
Born into an emotionally distant and abusive family of [[Ireland|Irish]] descent<ref name="ToughIrishGal"/> around [[Timeline#2260|2260]] or [[Timeline#2261|2261]], Cait remembers that her parents would routinely abuse her both physically and verbally for the first 18 years of her life. She attempted to run away twice; they locked her in a shed outside of the house the first time she did it, and the second time they broke one of her legs.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Why didn't you run away?"''<br />Cait: ''"{{Tooltip|I tried. Twice. The first time I did it, they locked me in a shed outside of the house we lived in. The second time, they broke one of me legs.|Somber}}"''<br />([[Cait's dialogue]])</ref> The abuse eventually led to Cait becoming convinced she was a mistake. Still, she deluded herself into thinking they loved her enough not to kick her out of the house. This illusion was shattered once her eighteenth birthday came; her parents slapped a [[Slave collar|shock collar]] around her neck, and sold her into [[slavery]], all without a hint of regret.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"I appreciate your trust."''<br />Cait: ''"{{Tooltip|Well, you're the first to earn it.|Friendly}} {{Tooltip|It all starts with two wastes of humanity I suppose you could call me parents.|Disgust}} {{Tooltip|I'm convinced I was a mistake, because I can't remember a single moment that they treated me like their daughter.|Irritated}} {{Tooltip|I was yelled at and beaten. Everythin' I did was wrong. Nothin' but a nuisance in their eyes.|Angry}} {{Tooltip|The whole time I was tellin' meself that they had to love me, even if it was just the tiniest bit, because they never kicked me out.|Irritated}} {{Tooltip|Then me eighteenth birthday arrived, and I found out why they kept me around.|Somber}} {{Tooltip|They slapped a shock collar around me neck and sold me to slavers. They didn't even care enough about me to say goodbye.|Somber}} {{Tooltip|Eighteen years of sufferin' through that shite and all I was worth to them was a pocketful of caps.|Angry}}"''<br />([[Cait's dialogue]])</ref><ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"How could you kill your own parents?"''<br />Cait: ''"{{Tooltip|How can you call them parents? They were opportunists who were takin' advantage of a human bein' just to make a few caps! If I had come out of that bitch of a mother deformed, they would have drowned me in the river and started again.|Angry}} {{Tooltip|They didn't give a shite about me. So I didn't give a shite about them. End of story.|re-record / Angry}}"''<br />([[Cait's dialogue]])</ref>

For the next five years, the young adult Cait was used as entertainment for the slavers, which she refuses to detail. Her time with the slavers wrecked her emotionally and warped her personality. Above all, Cait developed significant resentment towards her captors, but endured it while learning some tricks of their trade. After stealing caps from sleeping men after they used her, she eventually managed to buy her freedom. With 23 years worth of pent-up fury, the first thing she did with her newfound freedom was track down her parents so she could empty both barrels of her [[Double-barrel shotgun (Fallout 4)|shotgun]] into them.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"My god. I'm so sorry."''<br />Cait: ''"{{Tooltip|Thanks, but there's more to the story.|Friendly}} {{Tooltip|It would be easy to blame me charmin' personality on me parents. However, even with her freedom and her revenge against her parents, Cait did not feel fulfilled. To cope she took to alcohol and chems to help forget her past. At some point around [[Timeline|2284]], Cait found her way to [[Tommy Lonegan]] and his setup at the [[Combat Zone]] where she joined up as a cage fighter and fought to provide her with the funds to buy everything she needed to forget her past. But Cait knew the point of the fighting wasn't to provide for herself monetarily. She hated everyone, herself the most, and the violence, pain, wounds, and getting herself addicted to [[Psycho (Fallout 4)|Psycho]] were ways to punish herself and hopefully to one day meet the bloody death she had felt she deserved.<ref name="DeathSeeking">The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"I always have time to listen to you, Cait."''<br />Cait: ''"{{Tooltip|Good, because this isn't easy for me to say, and I want to get it right.|Friendly}} {{Tooltip|Sigh. Where do I begin?|to self aloud, getting ready for heart-to-heart / Concerned}} {{Tooltip|Did you know I spent three years fightin' at the Combat Zone?|Question}} {{Tooltip|Three years of gettin' beaten to hell by a bunch of losers and lunatics. I hated the crowds, I hated the other fighters and I hated meself.|Irritated}} {{Tooltip|I never understood why I put myself through all of that. Until now. It's because I was alone. And I think deep down, I wanted to die... I wanted one of me opponents to crush the life out of me. The easy way out.|Somber}}"''<br />([[Cait's dialogue]])</ref>

Against her dark hopes, Cait proved tough to beat, even after [[Raiders (Fallout 4)|raiders]] moved in and took control of the Combat Zone, resulting in the bloody sport getting extremely lethal. With the raiders in charge, those who didn't keep their head on a swivel were liable to be sucker-punched and robbed in the best of circumstances. Cait herself learned that short of buying herself some friends, she would have been a victim waiting to be preyed upon. The teacher of that vital lesson was a raider named [[Stratton (Fallout 4)|Stratton]], who abandoned her to be beaten by other raiders after she refused to sleep with him.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Why are you so paranoid about debts?"''<br />Cait: ''"{{Tooltip|It was kind of my fault. By [[Timeline#2287|2287]], Cait had been a cage fighter in the Combat Zone for three years, two of them under raider management, all with hundreds of victories to her name.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"What exactly do you think I'm expecting in return?"''<br />Cait: ''"{{Tooltip|Who knows? ==Personality==
Cait puts up a deceptively convincing tough Irish gal act, but beneath this exterior is a broken woman with a lifetime of misery behind her.<ref name="ToughIrishGal">The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Sounds like justice to me."''<br />Cait: ''"{{Tooltip|Was it justice or was it murder?|Somber}} {{Tooltip|When I close me eyes, all I can see is their faces twisted with fear.|toughest part of the story - somber mood / Somber}} {{Tooltip|And then me mind starts wanderin' and I start judgin' myself. And it's rippin' me the fuck apart. You think I inject myself with all that shite and drink myself drunk because I'm a 'tough Irish gal?' I do it so I can forget and move on with me miserable life. So there you are. The entire flawed package known as Cait, stripped bare for your perusal.|Somber}}"''<br />([[Cait's dialogue]])</ref>  Cait is deeply paranoid about being emotionally vulnerable, as everyone she has known intimately has betrayed her in some way. This has in turn caused an inherent mistrust in people in general, especially those making selfless offers of kindness. The only relationship she fully trusts is trade relationships, involving either the offer of caps or other services.<ref>Cait: ''"{{Tooltip|Have a minute? Got somethin' on my mind.|Friendly}}"''<br />The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Of course, anything you need."''<br />Cait: ''"{{Tooltip|Anythin' I need, huh? I might take you up on that one day.|Friendly}} {{Tooltip|After Tommy stuck me with you, I was expectin' to hate your guts.|re-record / Irritated}} {{Tooltip|Not only because you agreed to pick up me contract, but because I was waitin' for you to order me around like hired help.|Irritated}} {{Tooltip|Now so far, you've been treatin' me like a friend. Hell, you've been damn near NICE to me.|Impressed}} {{Tooltip|Now I don't mean to sound ungrateful, but your kindness is startin' to make me wonder.|Suspicious}} {{Tooltip|If there's anythin' I learned at the Combat Zone, it was that nobody does things for other people without expectin' somethin' in return.|Suspicious}}"''<br />([[Cait's dialogue]])</ref> Cait is also deeply regretful of many of the choices she has made up to the present, questioning if the brutality she has known all her life is truly the only way to settle things.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Why judge yourself?"''<br />Cait: ''"{{Tooltip|Me parents sold me into slavery, but did they deserve to die for it?|Question}} {{Tooltip|Yeah, I took some hard knocks as I was growin' up, but they fed me and kept me from glowin' with rads. This is the kind of crap that starts me mind wanderin' and drives me literally to drink.|Irritated}} {{Tooltip|I guess I'm just waitin' to hear what you think of me now.|Concerned}}"''<br />([[Cait's dialogue]])</ref>
