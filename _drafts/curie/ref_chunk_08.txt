==Behind the scenes==
* Curie was written by quest designer [[Ferret Baudoin]].<ref group="Non-game">[https://youtu.be/-vS0lrmXjo4?t=120 Bethesda Fallout 76 Interview ~ QuakeCon At Home 2020]:<br />Ferret Baudoin: ''"I was a designer on Fallout 4, I did the Railroad, I did Deacon, I did Curie, I did all sorts of fun quests there. And I was the lead quest designer at launch for all the content for Fallout 76, and then post-launch, I was lead designer."''</ref>
* Curie is voiced by multilingual actress [[Sophie Cortina]], who also voiced other Miss Nanny robots, including [[Miss Edna]].
** In localized versions of the game, Curie's voice is dubbed by Ana Esther Alborg (Spanish), Naoko Sakakibara (Japanese) and Francesca Perilli (Italian).
* Curie is named after [[wikipedia:Marie Curie|Marie Skłodowska Curie]], the physicist and chemist who conducted pioneering research on radioactivity, a fact that Curie will acknowledge in-game. 
** The [[wikipedia:Curie (unit)|curie]] is also the name of the unit of radioactivity (as in the explicit number of nuclear disintegrations per second, distinct from the unit of absorbed dose, the [[wikipedia:Rad (unit)|rad]]), and though it was officially named for Marie Curie's husband [[wikipedia:Pierre Curie|Pierre]] in 1910, it is widely accepted now that the contributions they made to science as a team are indivisible.
** Her name may also be a pun on the word "cure" in reference to her original purpose.

==Bugs==
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} As a Miss Nanny, she is able to equip the [[Bowler hat (Fallout 4)|bowler hat]] and [[Triggerman bowler]], just like Codsworth. This has no effect on her total health, despite the item's +1 Endurance bonus. {{verify|Verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} Curie in her synth body does not have any sneak ability which causes constant detection, all other companions have at least Sneak level 4 by default.{{Verify|verified}} 
** {{platforms|pc}} Workaround: each level of sneak can be added with the console: {{console|00102249.addperk 0004c935}} then {{console|00102249.addperk 000b9882}} then {{console|00102249.addperk 000b9883}} then {{console|00102249.addperk 000b9884}}
* {{Platforms|pc}} Sometimes (only confirmed in synth body) no other dialogue options will be available outside of the ones in the Talk menu. {{verify|verified}} 
** {{Platforms|pc}} This can be fixed by shooting Curie with a weapon. {{verify|verified}}
;Transfer-related
* {{platforms|pc}} Curie's robot body may disappear following the transfer, thus potentially losing any items still stored in her inventory before the transfer. To relocate it, type {{console|prid 865d1}} into the console, followed by {{console|moveto player}}, which will bring back Curie's old body to the player character.
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} On occasion Curie, if she has been turned into a synth already, will still be accessible in the [[robot workbench]]. If selected, this will revert her to her Miss Nanny body.{{Verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc}} If Curie is modified using the robot workbench and the player character does the quest "Emergent Behavior" Curie will not talk during the entirety of the player character's conversation with Doctor Amari. If the player character sits on the bench and waits for a day, she will revert to her Miss Nanny self and all modifications will be lost. {{Verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} [[Diamond City security]] and other NPCs will continue to comment on having Curie as a companion as if she were a robot even if she has been moved into the body of a synth.{{Verify|verified}}
;Behavior
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} Curie may unequip her base weapon and start collecting any and all dropped weapons to use instead. Even when given more powerful weapons, she will collect things like pipe pistols or swords as the player character defeats enemies. She also tends to loot ammo from dead enemies; even when possessing a weapon that uses that ammo type she will not use the weapon.{{Verify|verified}} 
** {{platforms|pc}} To lock Curie to using her base laser rifle and stop picking things up type {{console|00102249.equipitem 0022cc13 1}} into the console.
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} When Curie is transferred into her new body, she will still have her old battle AI, meaning for long-range attacks, she will use her default laser rifle. In close range, she will not have the cutting saw arm and will use her fists unless she has found or has been given a melee weapon. This can be fixed by recruiting a different companion, then going back to Curie. She will then behave like a normal humanoid companion.{{Verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} If Curie is given clothing or armor, or if she steps into power armor, her default outfit will disappear; once she steps out of the armor, or the clothing item is removed from her inventory, she will be naked.{{Verify|verified}} 
** {{platforms|pc}} To fix this issue with her clothing and inventory, her default outfit can be forcibly equipped and made unremovable by typing in this command {{console|00102249.equipitem 00117EB9 1}} in the console.
;Dismissal and travel
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} Sometimes Curie will not go to the location specified when dismissed and will not return to the original location, known locations, settlements, or any other feasible location, and as such simply disappears, even after sleep/resting for several in-game days.{{verify|verified}} 
** Possible fix: Track her with the [[Vault-Tec Population Management System]].
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} Upon dismissal Curie will reappear outside the medical room in Vault 81, regardless of where she is dismissed, even if dismissed to Sanctuary while in Sanctuary, she will immediately sprint toward Vault 81. {{Verify|verified}} 
** Workaround: Reload an earlier save file and don't give the cure to Dr. Forsythe at the end of the "Hole in the Wall" quest until after speaking to Curie inside the doctor's clinic. She won't actually enter the room herself, so the player character has to get behind Curie and push her into the clinic. She will then initiate the proper dialogue to become a companion and will stay at the player character's chosen settlement. Alternatively, {{console|resetquest comcurie}} followed by {{console|setstage comcurie 83}} will trigger the join companion dialogue, after which Curie can be moved as required.
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4}} Sometimes, if she is transferred into synth body, she will have a smoking animation while idle with no weapon equipped. The animation may be normal and may normally stop after she equips the weapon, but is still out of character, though technically synths can smoke.{{Verify|verified}} 
** Workaround: Take her to the end of the catacombs in [[Old North Church]], where the Sole Survivor first meets [[Desdemona]]. She'll stand on the platform and finish her cigarette.
;Affinity
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4}} Curie will dislike the player character for entering [[Covenant]] for the first time, regardless of whatever the player character says during the SAFE test ''or'' the fact that she will comment on how cute it is and suggest going inside.{{Verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} Curie will not have any dialogue when asked how the relationship with the player character is going if the player character did not romance her.{{Verify|verified}} 
** {{platforms|pc}} Workaround: To fix this, open the console and click on Curie. In the console, type: {{console|setav CA_WantsToTalkRomanceRetry 1}}
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} Curie's arm will sometimes get stuck in a pointing downwards position through the floor, resulting in her shooting her laser into the ground in combat. The only fix appears to be traveling to a different location. {{Verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc}} Curie's [[Perception (Fallout 4)|Perception]] and [[Intelligence (Fallout 4)|Intelligence]] stats will each be 1 higher than the value that they are set to. In-game she has 5 Perception; if setting the value to 8, she will have 9 Perception.{{Verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} Curie is missing the form id for Inspirational perks to work with her as a companion.{{Verify|verified}} 
** {{platforms|pc}} Workaround: add the companion inspirational perk with the console {{console|00102249.addperk 001d33d7}}
* {{platforms|pc}} After reaching the highest level of affinity with her, there is a conversation bug when you go to -> Talk -> press on Relationship and nothing happens. Just jumps back to Talk section. To fix this use console commands {{console|prid 102249}}, {{console|getav ca_affinity}} and {{console|getav CA_WantsToTalkRomanceRetry}} and try again. If she does not respond {{console|setav CA_WantsToTalkRomanceRetry 1}}. {{verify|verified}}
