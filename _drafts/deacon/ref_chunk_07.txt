* {{Note|(Note)}} While an active [[Fallout 4 companions|companion]], Deacon may reference a time in the past that he got a face change and "was a girl for a few months," which apparently elicited strong reactions from other Railroad members at HQ. Given Deacon's tendency to stretch the truth and don a number of disguises, this may or may not be taken as an interpretation of a genuine past identity.<ref>Deacon: ''"One time, I got a face change and was a girl for a few months. ==Behind the scenes==
* Deacon was written by quest designer [[Ferret Baudoin]].<ref group="Non-game">[https://youtu.be/-vS0lrmXjo4?t=120 Bethesda Fallout 76 Interview ~ QuakeCon At Home 2020]:<br />Ferret Baudoin: ''"I was a designer on Fallout 4, I did the Railroad, I did Deacon, I did Curie, I did all sorts of fun quests there. And I was the lead quest designer at launch for all the content for Fallout 76, and then post-launch, I was lead designer."''</ref>
* In localized versions of the game, Deacon's voice is dubbed by Antonio Domínguez (Spanish), Jiro Saito (Japanese) and Alessandro Capra (Italian).
* [[Ryan Alosio]], Deacon's voice actor, has expressed interest in reprising his role as Deacon in future installments, and often shares fan-created content related to Deacon on his social media.<ref name="Gamerant interview" group="Non-game">[https://web.archive.org/web/20200526130748/https://gamerant.com/fallout-5-pre-production-rumor-257/ Ryan Alosio interview on Gamerant]</ref>
* Deacon's dialogue contains nods to previous ''Fallout'' games:
** Deacon's mentions of the Capital Wasteland and its drinkable water allude to the main plot of ''Fallout 3''.
** Deacon mentions "Watts" when claiming he helped found the Railroad, referencing [[Victoria Watts]] from ''Fallout 3''.<ref name=leader /> Watts is also mentioned elsewhere on a Railroad terminal.
** Deacon suggests "Code Violet" as a team name, which was part of [[Harkness]]' override code during [[The Replicated Man]] in ''Fallout 3''.<ref>Deacon: ''"Since we're a team, you think we could use a code name? Red Orchard? Or... Code Violet? Or... the Death Bunnies. That'll confuse them."''<br />([[CompanionDeacon.txt/COM#1033|Deacon's dialogue]])</ref><ref>[[Horace Pinkerton]]: ''"Just say to him, "Activate A3-21 Recall Code Violet" and that will activate the hidden subroutines."''<br />([[Pinkerton.txt#64|Horace Pinkerton's dialogue]])</ref>
** He jokes about convincing a new Railroad member that he is actually [[John Henry Eden|President John Henry Eden]].<ref>Deacon: ''"I'm convincing the next raw recruit I'm actually President Eden. Think I can pull it off?"''<br />([[CompanionDeacon.txt/COM#1081|Deacon's dialogue]])</ref>
** He may attempt to use what he says are [[Robert House]]'s personal override codes on [[Deezer]] (which he supposedly won in a [[Gambling|poker game]]).<ref>Deacon: ''"Command override. Vocal audio House, Robert. "Access core programming.""''<br />[[Deezer]]: ''"You're really missing out. Enjoy a longer, healthier life with: Deezer's Lemonade."''<br />Deacon: ''"Ah, too bad. I won those codes in a poker game once. Hasn't worked yet."''<br />([[CompanionDeacon.txt#201|Deacon's dialogue]])</ref>
