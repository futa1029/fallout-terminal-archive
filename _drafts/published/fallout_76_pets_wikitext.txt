'''Pets''', also known as '''CAMP pets''', are a feature in ''[[Fallout 76]]'', released with the ''[[Gleaming Depths (update)|Gleaming Depths]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] which brought in pet [[dog]]s and [[cat]]s. Pets inhabit a player's [[CAMP]] in a manner similar to the [[Fallout 76 allies|allies]] feature, and their respective CAMP objects are built under the ''Dwellers'' (formerly known as ''Ally'') section. Pets can be customized and interacted with in a number of ways, including giving them their own names.<ref name="TGS">[[:File:Fallout 76- Camp Pets - Developer Announce TGS 2024|Xbox Tokyo Game Show 2024 preview]]</ref>

==Interactions==
There are three options to interact with a pet:
* Interact - This opens a wheel menu with three options: pet, speak and sit.
* Customize - Specific apparel items (crafted at an [[Armor workbench (Fallout 76)|armor workbench]]) can be equipped by pets. The effects are aesthetic only.
* Edit Name - The pet can be named whatever the player wants. Other players do not see the pet's name when visiting a CAMP.

==Notes==
[[File:FO76 Cardinal bird cage in-game.png|thumb|200px|The cardinal]]

* A player can have a pet and an ally at a CAMP at the same time, but they cannot have more than one of each.
* CAMP pets do not follow the player into the world, but do follow the player around the vicinity of their CAMP.
* Like allies, CAMP pets are not available in [[Fallout 76 shelters|Shelters]].
* There are a variety of other animals that can be placed at a CAMP, although they have little interactivity. These include birds (including a caged cardinal and a caged [[owl]]), the raven perch, the turkey buzzard, fish (in either the Atlantic City aquarium, the mutated goldfish, or the TV aquarium object), a pet lizard, butterflies, rabbit hutch, and ants in a giant ant farm. A mole rat generator exists. There is also a strange creature in a jar called Blinky that was a [[Pioneer_Scouts:_Skyline_Valley#Page_4_(Rank_21)|season reward]].

==Development==
[[Creature taming]] has existed as a gameplay mechanic in ''Fallout 76'' since its release, but a separate system for CAMP pets has a long development history.

In April 2020, shortly after the release of the ''[[Wastelanders]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]], then-project lead [[Jeff Gardiner]] stated in a Reddit AMA that the team was working on CAMP pets as a follow-up to the ally system.<ref>[https://www.eurogamer.net/fallout-76-to-add-pets Fallout 76 to add pets]</ref> In March 2021, the yearly roadmap for 2021 was released, teasing CAMP pets for release in the Winter 2021 update (then known as ''Tales from the Stars'', later changed to ''[[Night of the Moth]]'').<ref>[[:File:Fallout76 2021 Original Roadmap Full-EN.jpg|''Fallout 76'' original 2021 roadmap (March 2021)]]</ref> A few days later, then-design director [[Mark Tucker]] confirmed in another Reddit AMA that the team was still working on CAMP pets as a mechanic, and that they intended to start with various types of dogs, expand to cats, and then "go from there."<ref>[https://www.pcgamesn.com/fallout-76/camp-pets Fallout 76 CAMP pets will include dogs then cats - and potentially fan requests]</ref> Several [[Fallout 76 consumables|consumable items]] intended for pets (with the [[editor ID]] prefix "PETS_") were datamined around this time, including [[burger bits]], [[forrage porridge]], [[meat treats]], [[seed feed]], [[steak sticks]] and [[veggie vittles]]. However, CAMP pets were removed from the roadmap in September 2021.<ref>[[:File:Fallout76 2021 Roadmap Full-EN.jpg|''Fallout 76'' updated 2021 roadmap (September 2021)]]</ref> A Reddit comment from a [[Bethesda Softworks|Bethesda]] community manager announced that CAMP pets had been delayed to 2022.<ref>[https://archive.ph/Oq8vG "2021 Roadmap silently updated (we have receipts)" on /r/fo76 (archived)]<br />Valseek: ''"Hi! As a few others have pointed out in the thread, we are indeed pushing CAMP Pets and the Invaders from Beyond event into 2022. This is because we need some more time for development and internal testing before we're ready to bring them to the game."''</ref> However, CAMP pets were not included on the 2022 roadmap, and no news was given on their status after that point.<ref>[[:File:F76 2022 Roadmap Horizontal-EN-FULL.jpg|''Fallout 76'' 2022 roadmap (February 2022)]]</ref>

In July 2024, datamines of the [[Public Test Server]] found that a tutorial menu for a pets mechanic had been added to the game's files.<ref>[https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/it-looks-like-fallout-76-is-finally-getting-one-of-its-most-requested-features It looks like Fallout 76 is FINALLY getting one of its most requested features]</ref> In September 2024, in a video segment at the Xbox Tokyo Game Show, director [[Jonathan Rush]] officially confirmed that pets would be coming to ''Fallout 76'' at the end of 2024.<ref name="TGS" /> The feature was added to the [[Public Test Server]] on September 30, 2024.

More previews of the CAMP pets feature were shown during the [[Fallout Day Broadcast]] in October 2024. It confirmed that the [[Cat (Fallout 76)|tabby cat]] and [[Dog (Fallout 76)|German shepherd]] would be the first two pets to release, with more to come in the future, teasing creatures with and without fur.<ref>[[:File:Fallout Day Broadcast 2024|Fallout Day Broadcast 2024]]</ref> CAMP pets were introduced as part of the ''[[Gleaming Depths (update)|Gleaming Depths]]'' update in December 2024.

==Behind the scenes==
* According to [[Jonathan Rush]], systems and monetization designer [[Matthew Dondelinger]] was the primary designer on the CAMP pets feature.<ref>[https://archive.ph/R7cve Jonathan Rush on Twitter]: ''"Matt has been the primary Designer for this long awaited feature!<br />Awesome work, Donde!<br />#Fallout #Fallout76 @Fallout #DondeForPresident"''</ref>
* In early versions of the [[Public Test Server]], the petting interaction only worked in first-person. The third-person animation was updated in the PTS on November 1, 2024.<ref>[https://archive.is/tfbki Inside the Vault - Gleaming Depths Public Test Server Part 2]</ref>
