'''Brother Moncrief''' was a member of the [[Cult of the Mothman]]. His voice can be heard on a recording during [[The Path to Enlightenment|Event: The Path to Enlightenment]].

==Background==
Brother Moncrief was a [[Cultist (Fallout 76)|cultist]] who vocally theorized about some sort of ''"physical light of the Mothman."''<ref name=":0">''"I wonder if Brother Moncrief was able to find the physical light of the Mothman that he theorized about."'' ([[Steven Scarberry/Dialogue|Steven Scarberry's dialogue]])</ref>

He wrote a note to the High Priestess of his cult while taking shelter at [[Landview Lighthouse]]. There, he observed [[Firefly|fireflies]] while they were mating and collected their [[bioluminescent fluid]] to light the lamp, hoping to summon the [[Mothman]].<ref>[[Brother Moncrief's note]]</ref>

After calling in vain to nearby cultists for aid in a ritual to summon the [[Mothman|wise and beneficent Mothman]],<ref>[[The Path to Enlightenment|Event: The Path to Enlightenment]] voice-over: ''"Gather now, faithful followers of the most wise and beneficent Mothman. Let us light his holy beacon with the radiance of the fireflies."''</ref> Moncrief decided to perform the ritual by himself. While gathering their bioluminescent fluid, aggressive fireflies attacked the cultist, inflicting fatal wounds which forced him to abandon his quest.<ref>[[The Path to Enlightenment|Event: The Path to Enlightenment]] voice-over: ''"A lone cultist tried to summon the Wise Mothman by lighting the Old Lighthouse lamp. His plan to use the bioluminescent fluid from the fireflies might have succeeded had he not succumbed to his wounds."''</ref>

His [[Skeleton (Fallout 76)|skeleton]] can be found lying on a [[Sleeping bag (Fallout 76)|sleeping bag]] on the top floor of the lighthouse.

He is remembered positively by [[Steven Scarberry]],<ref name=":0" /> so he was probably a member of the [[Cult of the Mothman|Followers of the Winged One]] sect.

==Notes==
* Moncrief's relationship to the cults is somewhat unclear.
** He referred to his would-be savior as "the wise and beneficent Mothman." It is unclear if he sought to summon [[Mothman|any Mothman]] or the [[Wise Mothman]] specifically.
*** He hoped to "commune with him and partake of his wisdom."<ref>[[The Path to Enlightenment|Event: The Path to Enlightenment]] voice-over: ''"Let our beacon pierce the darkness and summon forth his divine presence, that we may commune with him and partake of his wisdom."''</ref>
** His note is addressed to a High Priestess.
** Whenever [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] lit the lighthouse beginning in 2102, the [[Wise Mothman]] would visit them. 
*** The larger sect, the [[Cult of the Mothman|Followers of the Winged One]], hold the Wise One to be an agent of false prophecy.<ref name=":2">[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"Interpreter Clarence walked me through the steps of the ritual."''<br />[[Steven Scarberry]]: ''"{{Tooltip|Oh, my apologies, I didn't realize that we were discussing the false prophet.|Interpreter Clarence walked me through the steps of the ritual.}} When people say 'Mothman' I assume they're talking about the 'Holy Mothman', and not the purple liar. {{Tooltip|But please, do go on. Tell me of the the stories of their glorified butterfly.|Sarcastic and a bit rude towards the Enlightened. Insulting the Rival cult and their beliefs}}"''<br />([[Steven Scarberry/Dialogue|Steven Scarberry's dialogue]])</ref> Their beliefs were shaped in the post-War period by a figure known as the [[First Priestess of the Wood]].<ref>[[His Priestess]]</ref> They have been hostile to the Wise Mothman since returning to the region in 2103.
*** The opposing sect, [[the Enlightened]], revere only the Wise Mothman, which they believe to be an individual mortal being with [[Psychic|profound metaphysical capabilities]]. However, they are not known to have a High Priestess, or anything of the sort. They posture in opposition to religious behavior, and style themselves with secular titles such as Observer and Interpreter.
**** The event itself is called "[[The Path to Enlightenment]]," possibly alluding to [[the Enlightened]], who were not revealed until the ''[[Night of the Moth]]'' update.
