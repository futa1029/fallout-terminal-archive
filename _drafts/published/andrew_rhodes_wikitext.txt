'''Andrew Rhodes''' was a member of the original [[Responders]] in [[Appalachia]], seeking to join the elite [[Fire Breathers]] to combat the surge of the [[Scorched Plague]]. His remains are found {{In|FO76}}.

==Background==
Sometime prior to [[Timeline#2095|2095]], the [[Scorchslayers]] squad were sent to the [[Belching Betty]] mine to demonstrate their training against the [[Scorched]] inside in order to complete their final exam for membership in the Fire Breathers division of the Responders.<ref>[[Scorchslayer's journal - Part 1]]</ref> Consisting of squad leader [[Maxine Ballard]] along with members Rhodes, [[Rita Wilcox]], [[Timothy Wolfe]], and [[Sylvester Tate]], the group encountered heavy offensives from the Scorched while trying to find [[Melody Larkin]]'s beacon as part of the exam, leading to Rhodes and Wolfe dying when a fear-stricken Tate failed to cover their flank.<ref>[[Scorchslayer's journal - Part 2]]</ref> The remaining members of the squad would perish a short time later as well, with all of their remains left to rot in the Scorched-filled mine.<ref>[[Scorchslayer's journal - Part 3]]</ref>
