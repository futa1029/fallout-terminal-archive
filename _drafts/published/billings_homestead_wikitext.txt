The '''Billings homestead''' is a [[Fallout 76 locations|location]] in [[the Forest]] region of [[Appalachia]]. It is a [[Workshop (Fallout 76)|public workshop]] with [[Claim Workshop|claim]] and [[Defend Workshop|defend]] [[Fallout 76 events|events]].

==Layout==
There is a simple two-story farmhouse with no basement. There is random loot inside and a locked ([[Picklock]] 2) safe on the second floor. There are two [[ash rose]]s in the backyard with two [[blackberry]] bushes. There are more than 40 [[Razorgrain (Fallout 76)|razorgrain]] plants in the field to the south. 

The workshop is outside against the south wall of the house along with an [[Armor workbench (Fallout 76)|armor workbench]]. The suitcase beside the armor workbench provides access to the player character's [[My stash box|stash box]]. There is a [[Poseidon power box]] near the workshop which provides 10 units of power, unless the [[Poseidon Energy Plant WV-06]] was restored to operation earlier during the [[Powering Up (Fallout 76)|Powering Up]] event, then the box will provide 100 units of power. More than enough to cover all the extractors that can be placed in the workshop build area, as the resource deposits are scattered all across the field where the crops were grown.

North of this location and south of the neighboring [[Silva homestead]], there is a single silo with a large semi-circular shed immediately adjacent to it. While it is equally close to both, it appears to actually belong to Silva - the door faces Silva and the land between the barn and Silva's farmhouse is tilled. Inside the shed, one has a chance to find [[Power armor (Fallout 76)|power armor]] at the [[Power armor station (Fallout 76)|power armor station]].

==Notable loot==
* Over 40 [[Razorgrain (Fallout 76)|razorgrain]] plants in the fields.
* 16 [[Tato flower (Fallout 76)|wild tato blossoms]] in the fields near a derelict truck.

The Billings homestead appears only {{In|FO76}}.
