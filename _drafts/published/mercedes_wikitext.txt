'''Mercedes "Mercy" Stern''' is a [[Secret Service (Fallout 76)|Secret Service]] agent in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Mercedes is one of the Secret Service agents located in [[Vault 79]] who became trapped inside it over a decade ago. She runs security and was a three-time marksman champion.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What are you in charge of?"''<br />Mercedes Stern: ''"Security. I'm the muscle. I do whatever it takes to keep visitors like you in line. I was a three time marksman champion and held the course record for simulation kills in hostage drills. Never got to actually use in the field before the [[Great War|bombs dropped]]."''<br />([[Mercedes Stern/Dialogue#109|Mercedes Stern's dialogue]])</ref> In her free time, she practices martial arts.<ref>[[Reginald Stone's journal]]</ref>

After being in the vault for nearly fifteen years, she became pregnant by one of the men. It was not a planned pregnancy according to her [[Mercedes Stern's journal|journal]] in which she was in disbelief and expressed feelings of horror, thinking she and her baby would die in the vault. She was taken care of by [[Cole Carver]] during this time. Although Mercy has not revealed who the father is and none of them claimed the baby, the agents took fatherly roles and decided to help raise [[Maggie Stern|Maggie]] together.<ref>[[Chase Terrier's journal]]</ref>

Mercy is also very protective of her daughter, Maggie, and is willing to put down any potential threats to her safety without a second thought.<ref>Mercedes Stern: ''"[[Chase Terrier|Digger]] is a ghoul now. What if he does something to my daughter? We can't take that chance."''<br />([[Mercedes Stern/Dialogue|Mercedes Stern's dialogue]])</ref> According to Maggie, Mercy taught her how to shoot a gun when she was only four years old.

She survived [[Shorty (Wastelanders)|Shorty]]'s mutiny years earlier as well as the more recent reactor malfunction. She and a few other agents as well as her daughter locked themselves in a small part of the vault to protect themselves from [[Feral ghoul (Fallout 76)|feral ghouls]] and rogue security systems. Now, they are running out of supplies and will need rescue.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What happened down here?"''<br /> [[Derek Garrison]]: ''"We were doing routine work in this part of the vault when the reactor went. Next thing we knew half our people were turned into monsters trying to kill us. Worse, the fighting activated the security systems and robots. It was either lock ourselves in here or die. That was almost three months ago. We're running out of food and water, so it's a good thing you showed up."''<br />([[Derek Garrison/Dialogue#249|Derek Garrison's dialogue]])</ref>
