'''Blackeye''', formerly known as '''Heather''',<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"And you guys don't mind? You like the name Blackeye?"''<br />Blackeye: ''"Bones doesn't let anything go. It can get old fast but we're used to it. Blackeye sure beats the hell out of Heather though."''<br />([[Blackeye/Dialogue#85|Blackeye's dialogue]])</ref> is a member of the self-proclaimed "[[Blackwater Bad Boys]]" [[Crater Raiders]] group at [[Ohio River Adventures]].

==Background==
Blackeye worked as a first grade teacher before the [[Great War]], though she says that she was never one for "soft living."<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What was your life like before the war?"''<br />Blackeye: ''"Would you believe it if I told you I was a school teacher? Don't get the wrong idea though. I was never one for soft living."''<br />([[Blackeye/Dialogue#95|Blackeye's dialogue]])</ref> She prefers robbing [[Foundation]] [[Settlers (Wastelanders)|settlers]] to disciplining her class over things like turning in spelling homework,<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"The life of a school teacher was a hard one, huh?"''<br />Blackeye: ''"You ever try to herd a horde of seven year olds, much less enforce turning in their spelling homework? Yeah, I didn't think so."''<br />([[Blackeye/Dialogue#86|Blackeye's dialogue]])</ref> especially on a Monday.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Disciplining children... are those skills that transfer over to being a raider?"''<br />Blackeye: ''"You joking? I'll take holding up some Foundation chumps over my old 1st grade class any day of the week. Especially on a Monday."''<br />([[Blackeye/Dialogue#87|Blackeye's dialogue]])</ref> After joining the Raiders, she lived in [[the Crater]] for a time before becoming part of the group sent to establish an outpost at Ohio River Adventures with [[Fishbones]] and [[Gills]], where they could coordinate the trade of [[Mirelurk (Fallout 76)|mirelurk]] products such as [[Mirelurk egg (Fallout 76)|eggs]] and [[Softshell mirelurk meat (Fallout 76)|softshell meat]].

Although Gills was later replaced by [[Bruiser (Wastelanders)|Bruiser]], the group successfully built up the location. Fisheyes, wanting to keep up morale, gave Blackeye her nickname in reference to an incident where she accidentally hit herself in the face with a plank of wood on her first day.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Blackeye, huh? What's her deal?"''<br />[[Fishbones]]: ''"Really pulls her weight around here. She's a sharp one and deadly too, but a bit of a klutz. Earned herself a little pet name too. First day workin' out here, tripped over a loose board and smashed her face up. Been callin' her Blackeye ever since. Pretty sure she loves it."''<br />([[Fishbones/Dialogue#66|Fishbones' dialogue]])</ref> Despite being a talented sharpshooter, she is described as a klutz by Fishbones. Blackeye, Bones and Bruiser now lead the outpost, though they have recently been having troubles with mirelurks attacking the docks.

===Other interactions===
Blackeye can be sold [[Mirelurk (Fallout 76)|mirelurk]] meat as way to increase [[Wastelanders reputations|reputation]] with the Raiders, but only one product per day. [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] will need to wait a day before selling new product to her, especially collecting it from helping [[Fishbones]] defend the water purifier from mirelurks. Blackeye requires 15 [[Mirelurk egg (Fallout 76)|mirelurk eggs]], 10 [[Mirelurk meat (Fallout 76)|mirelurk meat]], 5 [[Softshell mirelurk meat (Fallout 76)|softshell mirelurk meat]], or 1 [[Queen mirelurk meat (Fallout 76)|queen mirelurk meat]]. Giving her a piece of mirelurk queen meat earns more reputation than regular or softshell mirelurk meat. Occasionally, when delivering the meat to Blackeye, she'll possibly reward Dwellers with some random items, such as weapons or mirelurk products.
