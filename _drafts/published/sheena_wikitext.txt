'''Sheena''' is a member of the [[Crater Raiders]]' [[War Party]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
As a child, Sheena would always win at the [[Blast Radius board game (Fallout 76)|Blast Radius board game]] due to her strong tactical intellect.

In 2103, Sheena is the tactician of the Crater [[War Party]] and plans out their operations against the Brotherhood under the orders of [[Meg Groberg]]. Sheena is one of the three raiders that inhabit [[the Crater war room]] along with [[Burke (Steel Dawn)|Burke]] and [[Pierce (Steel Dawn)|Pierce]]. She has known the two from back when she helped an imprisoned Pierce escape with Burke.<ref>[[Burke (Steel Dawn)|Burke]]: ''"My employer was holding Pierce prisoner. I was his guard. Even in that situation, Pierce treated me like a person. Not as a tool. He saw me. When Sheena arrived, we all escaped together. The rest is history."''<br />([[Burke (Steel Dawn)/Dialogue#206|Burke's dialogue]])</ref> When [[Marcia Leone]] joins the gang, the young women become fast friends.

Sheena is rather misanthropic, saying she does not give two craps about humanity, saying the species has done nothing but abuse and betray each other, also pointing out their world has been destroyed by nuclear warfare. She mentions she will only look out for her and her own, saying the rest do not matter.<ref>Sheena: ''"I don't give two craps about humanity. As a species, we've done nothing but abuse and betray each other. And beyond that, look at what we've done to this Earth. So I look out for me and my own. The rest don't matter."''<br />([[Sheena/Dialogue#177|Sheena's dialogue]])</ref>

In 2104, Sheena and Burke become imprisoned by the [[Hellcat Company]] in the basement laboratory of the [[AMS corporate headquarters]].

===Quests===
* [[Property Rights]]: After the confrontation with Pierce in the [[makeshift vault]], the Vault Dweller meets Sheena when Pierce refuses to cooperate. Sheena strikes a deal to trade intel for intel; she will reveal the source of where the Raiders found their weapons if the Vault Dweller returns her [[Sheena's holotape|holotape]] once it has been downloaded with Brotherhood information. The Dweller is free to refuse.
* [[Missing Persons]]: Sheena and Burke go to the [[AMS corporate headquarters]], but there, they become captured and imprisoned in the basement by [[Kit]] and the [[Hellcat Company]]. The Vault Dweller and Marcia set out to rescue them.

===Other interactions===
* If one calls Burke a freak in conversation with them, Sheena will come to their defense, though Burke will remark that they are not surprised by such remarks anymore.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What a freak."''<br />[[Burke (Steel Dawn)|Burke]]: ''"..."''<br />Sheena: ''"Hey! ==Appearances==
Sheena appears only in ''[[Fallout 76]]'', introduced in the ''[[Steel Dawn]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] and expanded upon in ''[[Steel Reign]]''.

==Behind the scenes==
Sheena is voiced by [[Bennett Abara]], previously known as and listed in the in-game credits as Amanda C. Miller. In localized versions of the game, Sheena's voice is dubbed by Elisa Pape in German.
