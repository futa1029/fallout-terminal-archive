'''Cole''' was a survivor from [[Harpers Ferry]] who, along with [[Billy (Harpers Ferry)|Billy]], [[Lucas (Nuclear Winter)|Lucas]] and [[Red (Harpers Ferry)|Red]], was sent to [[Vault 94]] by the mayor of Harpers Ferry, [[Miranda Vox]].

==Background==
Upon arrival, the group was suspicious of the vault dwellers, thinking that their altruism was a cover for something sinister. They barged into a community meeting of the dwellers and demanded that the vault's leader, Pastor [[Gabriella Salavar]], tell them what the dweller's true game was. When the genuinely altruistic dwellers informed him there was no game, he lost his patience and told his men to open fire.<ref>[[Vault 94 Community Council recording]]</ref>

After they realized that the vault didn't have weapons, they massacred the residents with the weapons in their possession, including at least one [[Minigun (Fallout 76)|minigun]] which was used by Red. Once in the vault, they found the security field surrounding a [[G.E.C.K. (Fallout 76)|Garden of Creation Kit]] and forced their way through it. Not realizing what the G.E.C.K. was, Cole assumed it was some kind of mind control device and ordered Red to shoot it with the minigun, destroying it and causing an explosion which blanketed [[the Mire]] with a cloud of mutagenic gas.<ref>[[Vault 94 G.E.C.K. recording]]</ref><ref>[[Harpers Ferry terminal entries]]</ref> He and the rest of his team can be found as feral ghouls within the ruined vault.

==Appearances==
Cole was added to the game {{In|FO76NW}}, however he was removed from the game when vault raids were removed.
