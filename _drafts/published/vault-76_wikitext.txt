'''Vault 76''' is a [[Fallout 76 locations|location]] {{in|FO76}}. It is the first location discovered upon starting a new game and completing the prologue.

One of [[Vault-Tec Corporation|Vault-Tec's]] many [[Vault]]s across [[United States of America|America]], Vault 76 was constructed north of [[Flatwoods]], [[Appalachia|West Virginia]], in [[the Forest]] region before the [[Great War]] and was designated as the "Official Vault of the Tricentennial" by the corporation, under the tagline "Vault-Tec Salutes America."<ref group="Supplement">Vault 76 promotional materials{{Citation needed}}</ref><ref group="Supplement">''[[Fallout 76 Vault Dweller's Survival Guide]]'' p.286: ''"01. VAULT 76 (VAULT)''<br />''Welcome to Vault 76! We hope you had a wonderful Reclamation Day shindig, but it's now time to leave. Follow the onscreen instructions, visiting each of the locations on the map, before heading out into the Appalachian wilderness. Remember you can return to the (relative) protection of the Vault entrance when using Fast-Travel without having to spend any Caps. Usually, though, you should gather all items you can before leaving."''<br />([[Fallout 76 Vault Dweller's Survival Guide Atlas of Appalachia]])</ref>

==Background==
===Construction===
{{Quotation|Since debuting Vault 76 last year, in honor of America's tercentenary, Vault-Tec continues to expand with plans for well over 100 Vaults around the country.|[[Newscaster]]|sound=Newscaster-Vault76-Fallout4.ogg}}
Vault 76 was one of 17 control Vaults, part of the overarching [[Vault#True purpose|Vault experiment]]. Construction began in February 2065 and ended in October 2069. The maximum capacity was stated to be 500 occupants, and construction blueprints record a capacity of 88.<ref name="CitadelTerminal">[[Citadel terminal entries#Vault 76|Citadel terminal entries; Vault-Tec terminal, Vault 76]]</ref><ref>[[:File:F76 Vault 76 Construction Plans.png|Construction plans]]</ref> The Vault was slated to open to the outside world 25 years after nuclear war so that the individuals within could emerge to recolonize [[Appalachia]]. This was so that Vault-Tec could study their recolonization efforts in comparison to the experiments within other Vaults.<ref name="CitadelTerminal" /><ref name="V76 report">[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Vault-Tec Report|Vault 76 terminal entries; overseer's terminal, Vault-Tec Report]]</ref>{{Ref|(Note 1)}} The day that the Vault reopened to the outside would be known as [[Reclamation Day (event)|Reclamation Day]] (distinct from a different version of Reclamation Day used internally by Vault-Tec, which was intended to occur at a much later time after nuclear war).<ref>{{FOTV|2|2}}</ref>

Vault 76 was designed with [[LightLife]] Geo-Thermal for its primary power supply, and [[General Atomics International|General Atomics]] [[Generator (Fallout 76)|nuclear generator]] as a secondary power supply,<ref name="CitadelTerminal" /> but was provided with a pair of [[super reactor]]s in the end.<ref name="SupRec" /> Its computer control system was [[Brainpower]] 4.<ref name="CitadelTerminal" /> Furthermore, the Vault also came with exterior sensors, intended to allow its occupants to gauge the status of the outside world after the bombs.<ref name="sensors">[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"Do we know anything about the world outside?"''<br />[[Crutchley]]: ''"Nothing at all. The exterior sensors were taken out of commission after that unfortunate business with the Nuclear Bombs. With so many of your friends having been gone so long, I cannot begin to speculate how they've transformed Appalachia already."''<br />([[Crutchley/Dialogue|Crutchley's dialogue]])</ref>

The overseer was instructed to ignore standard operating procedures, as Vault 76 was deemed a special project essential to rebuilding the United States in the wake of nuclear war.<ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Vault 76 Parameters|Vault 76 terminal entries; overseer's terminal, Vault 76 Parameters]]</ref> The occupants were some of America's finest, including students from [[Vault-Tec University]], military members, and a former [[White House]] Chief of Staff. Occupants were provided fully furnished apartments, including a bedroom, living room, bathroom and color television.<ref>Interior of the Vault apartment during [[Reclamation Day]]</ref>

At some point, Vault-Tec Assistant Chief Executive Officer [[Giles Wolstencroft]] visited to inspect the construction site, and was abducted by the [[Zetan]] aliens and taken aboard [[Zeta|Mothership ''Zeta'']].<ref>[[Alien captive recorded log#Alien captive recorded log 13|Alien captive recorded log 13]]</ref>

===Debut===
[[File:Fo76 intro slide 6.png|thumb|250px|The opening ceremony of Vault 76 on July 4, 2076]]

Vault 76 debuted on July 4, 2076,<ref>[[Newscaster|Speaker]]: ''"War. War never changes. In 1776 this great nation accepted that armed conflict was the only way to preserve our rights to life, to liberty, and the pursuit of happiness. If our founding fathers could only see us now... From Antietam to Amiens, Okinawa to Anchorage, we have fought. And now, the Red Menace is at our doorstep. Democracy itself is under threat of annihilation. That is why today, July 4th 2076, 300 hundred years after our great nation began, we gather together to honor the completion of Vault 76. This sprawling underground shelter may have been engineered by Vault-Tec, but it was built you, the good people of America. So that if the bombs do come, if the end does come, our way of life will endure. But not everyone will be saved. Those chosen to enter this Vault must be our best and our brightest. For when the fighting has stopped, and the fallout has settled, you must rebuild. Not just walls, not just buildings, but hearts and minds. And ultimately America itself. So as we stand here today, we pray that the world will know peace. But, if that is not our destiny, if war must come, we stand together knowing that here in Vault 76, our future begins."''<br />([[Fallout 76 intro|''Fallout 76'' intro]])</ref> seven years after its construction ended, in honor of the [[United States of America|United States]]' Tercentenary/Tricentennial of its founding in 1776.<ref name="newscaster">[[Newscaster]]: ''"Since debuting Vault 76 last year, in honor of America's Tercentenary, Vault-Tec continues to expand with plans for well over 100 Vaults around the country."''<br />([[MQ101TVAnchorman VoiceOnly.txt|Newscaster's dialogue]])</ref> An opening ceremony, which was broadcasted on television, was held at the entrance and its [[Vault 76 overseer|overseer]] attended. However, in order to become the overseer, she had to break off her engagement with her fiancé, [[Evan (Fallout 76)|Evan]], or be transferred to another Vault to have him join her in it.

===Great War===
After the [[Great War]] broke out, Vault 76 was successfully sealed on October 23, 2077, when the air raid sirens sounded for the last time as Chinese nukes headed for American soil. The Vault-Tec security team ensured compliance and defended the individuals as they entered the Vault, authorized to use lethal force to ensure only those authorized would enter. Everyone else was considered an enemy combatant.<ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#VAULT 76 SECURITY INSTRUCTIONS|Vault 76 terminal entries; security terminal, VAULT 76 SECURITY INSTRUCTIONS]]</ref> They succeeded in their mission, and the Vault door was sealed, to be opened only 25 years later, once the all-clear was given after outside radiation levels had subsided to a safe threshold.

[[File:Fo76 intro slide 12.png|thumb|right|250px|The [[Vault 76 overseer]] leading a Vault-Tec course about Pip-Boys to Vault 76 residents]]

This was only the beginning of the project. The occupants were a diverse group selected from every walk of life, and apart from the Vault staff, all were highly accomplished before the war and possessed competitive personalities. As such, Vault-Tec expected the Vault dwellers to present unique challenges in maintaining order. As a last resort, the overseer was granted the ability to lock down each domicile and imprison dissenters for any duration deemed appropriate, with the note to account for the fact that enforced solitary confinement could lead to psychological issues. Furthermore, the security team was provided with less-than-lethal devices to ensure compliance. Firearms and other munitions were stowed after the amended entry period.<ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Day to Day Duties|Vault 76 terminal entries; overseer's terminal, Day to Day Duties]]</ref>

===Ongoing operations===
[[file:Vault76PlayerRoom.png|right|250px|thumb|A room belonging to one of the dwellers]]
For the next 25 years, Vault 76 continued to operate as planned. The overseer found order easy to maintain, though the impressive pedigree of those the Vault sheltered and their competence led to the aforementioned unique challenges, most often challenging her authority. To provide them with an outlet for their competitiveness, the overseer instituted a number of awards for the Vault dwellers to obtain, as award ceremonies were a great way to placate even the most hardened egos (altercations aside; one lost a tooth fighting over the Best Dental Hygiene award). The high point of the program was the Vault 76 World Cup, even if it nearly broke the jukebox. The overseer credited the success to the "exemplary" service of her "loyal and dependable" senior staff.<ref name="2100rev">[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Annual Review: 2100|Vault 76 terminal entries; overseer's terminal, Annual Review: 2100]]</ref>

Meanwhile, outside of Vault 76, the survivors of the Great War were aware of the Vault's existence and wondered why it was still closed years later. In particular, the [[Responders]] wanted to know if they could open the Vault somehow.<ref>[[:File:FO76 Responders map.png|Responders map]]</ref> The Vault's sealing led to the perception from outsiders that the residents of Vault 76 are cushy and privileged, while the rest of the world adjusted to [[wasteland]] life.<ref>[[Sargento]]: ''"{{Tooltip|Word is they were holed up, living in a cushy vault for twenty-five years while the rest of us adjusted to Wasteland life.|High energy, disapproving of the player, trying to elicit booing from the audience}} {{Tooltip|Now being forced to fight for survival like an animal. How's that for a cold dose of reality!|High Energy, trying to elicit cheering}}"''<br />([[W05 Raider Sargento.txt|Sargento's dialogue]]) {{Small|Note: This line is spoken over the loudspeaker at the [[Watoga Civic Center]] during [[The Ol' Weston Shuffle]], when the player character is introduced as a fighter.}}</ref>

However, Vault 76 also had another objective: the Vault's overseer was ordered to find and secure [[Site Alpha|three]] [[Site Bravo|nuclear]] [[Site Charlie|silos]] all on her own after its opening. If these sites were still nuclear-capable, she was to ensure no one except Vault-Tec could access or launch nuclear ordnance. Any other authorities, government, militia or otherwise, were to be ignored, to ensure that Vault-Tec alone controlled these silos and could deny their use to any third parties, even if it meant going against any surviving [[United States Armed Forces]] personnel that would assert control of the silos themselves as part of the [[Department of Defense|U.S. Department of Defense]].<ref name="V76 report" /><ref name="ReclamationDay">[[Reclamation Day]]</ref> However, Vault-Tec may have known that the task was impossible for her to accomplish on her own, especially since they purposefully picked someone young and inexperienced for an overseer.<ref>[[Overseer's log - Nuke launch]]: ''"I had a directive from Vault-Tec: secure the region's three nuclear missile silos. I... WE... had to make sure they didn't fall into the wrong hands. I knew I couldn't do it alone, so I asked you -- my Vault 76 family -- for help.''"</ref>

As the Vault neared the end of its duration and approached Reclamation Day, the situation started to slowly deteriorate. In 2100, the Vault was over capacity, with hydroponics struggling to keep up with the demand and mandatory rationing instituted by the overseer. Six individuals had to be placed under disciplinary lockdown (up from four at the end of the previous year), with the morale officer tasked with pacifying rumors about Appalachia around the clock. Nobody wanted to emerge into Appalachia to fight cannibal mutants and brave burning rain.

Regardless, tensions were running high,<ref name="2100rev" /> especially with some people being difficult to handle. [[Jake (Vault 76)|Jake]], an arrogant pre-War lawyer, was detested by the security team so much that only one officer could be trusted to be sent down and not strangle him.<ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Assault Investigated|Vault 76 terminal entries; security terminal, Assault Investigated]]</ref><ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Theft Investigated|Vault 76 terminal entries; security terminal, Theft Investigated]]</ref> Furthermore, the "Ressies," as the security team called them, started hacking into terminals to learn more about the Reclamation and the Activation Notices. Although the security team made a token effort to curtail unauthorized access, they all knew trying to keep geniuses out of the files was a lost cause.<ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Unauthorized Access Investigation|Vault 76 terminal entries; security terminal, Unauthorized Access Investigation]]</ref>

===Preparations===
As a result of these mounting concerns, 2102 and Reclamation Day was met with relief from all parties; some were so relieved they even broke into the stores to enjoy it early.<ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Drunk and Disorderly|Vault 76 terminal entries; security terminal, Drunk and Disorderly]]</ref> The overseer mandated a Reclamation Day party the day before the Activation Notice, opening up restricted alcohol stashes and the remaining food supplies. The happiness and cheering in the atrium as the overseer counted down to midnight were second only to the time when the first Vault child was born. The party continued well into the night when the overseer called lights out and had security help stragglers into their beds. Then, she and her team went through the final checklists. Standees and supplies were placed along the exit route to the Vault door to provide a last refresher course for the Ressies,<ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Reclamation Day Has Arrived|Vault 76 terminal entries; overseer's terminal, Reclamation Day Has Arrived]]</ref> while the security team went through their duties with record speed: Wiping logs, securing non-essential gear and acquiring Reclamation Packages for themselves.<ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Rundown Complete|Vault 76 terminal entries; security terminal, Rundown Complete]]</ref>

The Vault 76 dwellers ultimately had no other choice, as the Vault itself was claimed to be designed to cease function and be rendered inhospitable 24 hours after opening, a feature intended to force those within to leave. Studies showed that dependency on the Vault's amenities was common, so the killswitch was established and set to kick in after the All-Clear message was received after the aforementioned delay. Air circulation would shut down last, giving everyone enough time to leave the Vault, but rendering it hazardous for habitation. Only robotic stewards would remain to preserve the remains, while the former inhabitants focused on rebuilding America.<ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Mandatory Shut Down|Vault 76 terminal entries; overseer's terminal, Mandatory Shut Down]]</ref>

===Reclamation Day===
[[File:Fo76 intro slide 19.png|right|250px|thumb|The [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] leaving Vault 76]]

Vault 76 successfully opened on October 23, [[Timeline (2100-2149)#2102|2102]], exactly 25 years after the Great War, in a much-celebrated event known as Reclamation Day.<ref name="ReclamationDay" /> The Vault opened and the inhabitants emerged into an unknown world, transformed by nuclear war and the ensuing chaos and decay that followed. Nobody knew what to expect, as the bombs knocked out all of Vault 76's external sensors except for the Geiger counters.<ref name="sensors" /> Additionally, the overseer had left before everyone else, leaving behind an announcement vaguely claiming she had her own mission to perform in Appalachia; in reality, she was instructed by Vault-Tec to secure three nuclear missile silos in the region.

[[file:Vault76PopUp5.png|right|250px|thumb|One of the pop-ups introducing survival concepts to the emerging Vault Dwellers.]]

Still, some on the security team were more concerned with internal threats than external ones, as rumors were flying high of who would team up with who after leaving the Vault. The competitive inhabitants were already making alliances and pacts before exiting, and anything could happen without some sort of guiding force.<ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Keeping a lid on it|Vault 76 terminal entries; security terminal, Keeping a lid on it]]</ref> The team feared that the Ressies could go off the rails, breaking the respectable record of 25 years without casualties. Although some argued for arming everyone before they leave the Vault, using the surplus stocks, the overseer denied the request.<ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Final entry|Vault 76 terminal entries; security terminal, Final entry]]</ref> The dwellers received the standard survival packages and were told to run fast and far away from Vault 76 to complete their mission.<ref>[[Vault 76 terminal entries#Survival First|Vault 76 terminal entries; overseer's terminal, Survival First]]</ref>

===Last holdouts===
Even after Reclamation Day and the claimed shutdown looming over Vault 76, a few of the dwellers still refused to leave the Vault. The Vault's claimed shutdown one day after its opening evidently did not occur, enabling these last holdout dwellers to continue living inside the Vault for months and even years,{{Ref|(Note 2)}} persisting off of the Vault's remaining supplies. The Mister Handy attendants kept to their duty of maintaining the Vault and manning the survival preparation stations for dwellers on their way out of the Vault; over time, they also grew tired of their roles, but could not stop with dwellers still in the Vault.

The Mister Handies' duties did not extend to cleaning up the Vault's atrium, which was still littered with the trash and decorations from the party for Reclamation Day, or individual dwellers' apartments. The overseer's prerecorded message announcing the arrival of Reclamation Day also continuously played on loop, set to repeat once every morning, though the system was starting to break down after months of repetitions. Even with these inconveniences, the last dwellers continued to stay in Vault 76 until its food supplies finally ran dry and they had no other option but to leave.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"This is my home. I don't want to leave."''<br />[[Crutchley]]: ''"It is endearing to hear how wonderful a job myself and the others have done maintaining the Vault for these many months. But the food's all run out and it's time to take what you've learned and use it."''<br />([[Crutchley/Dialogue|Crutchley's dialogue]])</ref>

==Layout==
===Interior===
Vault 76 is the game's starting and tutorial area, providing a direct route to the exit with few branches. The player character awakes in their apartment and has the opportunity to explore their surroundings and collect the [[Nuka Tapper]] game holotape from their personal terminal. They are only allowed to exit the apartment once they collect the [[Pip-Boy 2000 Mark VI]] from its storage rack next to the door.

Upon exiting, they enter an atrium area with detritus from the previous evening's party. The atrium is large, complete with tables, a diner and a soccer field in the middle. There is a note that cannot be taken sitting on one of the tables in the diner on the upper level, called the [[love note]]. One of the rooms on the way to the exit features a projector showing a [[Vault 76 slideshow|slideshow]] with tips on living outside the Vault. Side routes are inaccessible but the player character can access the personnel files in the projector room, the survival displays, and the overseer's office.

Several Mister Handies are positioned throughout the Vault on the route to the exit. The first one is [[Crutchley]], who is found in the hallway on the other end of the atrium, and can be briefly spoken to. In the hallway and rooms after him, there are several stations manned by other Mister Handies at which the player character can acquire their [[CAMP|C.A.M.P.]] and other basic necessities as they prepare to leave. (One of the stations, which includes a backpack of building supplies, also gives several additional consumable items, which is not mentioned in the station.)

The overseer's office can be found at the end of the hallway between [[Merriman]]'s and [[Crowley (Fallout 76)|Crowley]]'s stations, past the wooden barricade. Inside the office, the overseer's terminal can be read and has the [[Overseer's log - Vault 76]] holotape.

In the room with [[Poole]]'s station, there is another terminal for the Vault's personnel manager, which includes entries mentioning a few of the Vault's former dwellers. Past this, it is a linear route to the Vault's entrance, though the security room is open off to the side and can optionally be explored; it only has a security terminal which can be hacked (level 0).

The Vault door will automatically open upon approaching it. [[Alderton]] is nearby and will offer some final floating dialogue. After leaving, no one can return to the interior, but newly-created characters will always start in the Vault.

===Exterior===
Outside, the Vault entrance terminates in a large terrace still decorated to mark the tricentennial celebrations. Heading south from there will lead down to another set of two successively lower terraces; from there, a marked dirt trail heads down towards the [[overseer's camp]]. Heading west instead leads down to a passenger drop-off road loop overlooking a parking lot; following the road out of the parking lot will eventually lead to the [[Tyler County Fairgrounds]]. There are also unmarked dirt paths leading off north and east into the woods.

As of the ''[[Burning Springs (update)|Burning Springs]]'' update, the most prominent feature just outside the Vault is [[the Ghoul]], who can be found standing on a [[raider corpse]]; speaking to him will give an objective to meet him later in [[Highway Town]]. There is also a sign advertising [[the Wayward]] found just opposite the stairs down to the middle terrace.

The Mister Handy [[Pennington]] is found on the terrace below, and he is spoken to as part of [[Wayward Souls]]. Across from him, there is also a [[donations box]] where veteran players can leave items for new arrivals to collect, which has the note [[Welcome to the Wasteland]] pinned to it. Next to the box is a barrel with the note [[Anglers wanted]] attached; reading the note will start [[Casting Off]] and mark [[Fisherman's Rest]] on the map.

Heading down the stairs from Pennington leads to the lowest terrace. [[Lacey Drummond]] and [[Isela Mejia]] are standing here at the foot of the stairs, and speaking to them will point the player character to the Wayward. The duo's campsite can be found just nearby, consisting of a campfire, two [[Sleeping bag (Fallout 76)|sleeping bags]], and some assorted junk items. From there, the aforementioned dirt trail leads down to the southwest. The trail is occupied by [[Feral ghoul (Fallout 76)|feral ghouls]]; not far down the way is a dead [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Responders protectron]], while there is also an unmarked lookout tower a little further down.

Heading west from the Vault entrance or any of the terraces leads to the parking lot or the passenger drop-off overlooking it. These sections are also infested with feral ghouls. On the top terrace (the Vault entrance area), there is a [[Responder corpse]] on the stairs leading down to the passenger drop-off. The road from there down to the parking lot contains a wrecked military [[Off-road vehicle|jeep]] with an [[explosives crate]]. At the bottom, there is an [[Armored personnel carrier (Fallout 76)|APC]] with an [[Ammo box (Fallout 76)|ammo box]] and a [[Duffle bag (Fallout 76)|duffle bag]] inside. A [[campaign hat]] is on the ground outside the carrier.

The parking lot itself also has a few more loot containers scattered around the various ruined vehicles, which include a [[Grafton Steel (company)|Grafton Steel]] van. The road west is blocked by a military barricade, though it can be easily jumped over. There is another sign advertising the Wayward found here.

==Notes==
;Clarifications
* {{Note|(Note 1)}} In ''[[Fallout 3]]'', [[the Citadel]]'s [[Citadel terminal entries#Vault 76|Vault-Tec terminal]] includes a listing of Vault 76 which says the Vault is intended to open 20 years after its sealing.<ref name="CitadelTerminal" /> In ''[[Fallout 76]]'', the [[Vault 76 terminal entries#Vault-Tec Report|overseer's terminal]] in Vault 76 instead says that the Vault is intended to open '''25''' years after its sealing.<ref name="V76 report" /> This latter date is the one used in ''Fallout 76'', since Reclamation Day officially occurred in 2102.
* {{Note|(Note 2)}} In gameplay, prior to the ''[[Wastelanders]]'' update, new player characters woke up in Vault 76 on the morning of October 23, 2102, just hours after the Vault's opening. With ''Wastelanders'', the in-game year was moved forward to 2103; to account for this, details in the opening quest and related dialogue were changed to present new player characters as the last few dwellers who were still refusing to leave the Vault even months after Reclamation Day.
** Successive updates have since incremented the game year multiple times. However, the opening quest and relevant dialogue in Vault 76 still say that the dwellers have only been inside for ''months'' after Reclamation Day rather than ''years'', as they have not been updated since ''Wastelanders''.
;Trivia
* Vault 76 can not be re-entered after leaving. Players must create a new character to explore it again.
* Vault 76 is actually not a single player instance in a public server, as players can encounter each other which is why they spawn in different but identical-looking rooms in the beginning. It is rare for players to encounter other players in Vault 76, however.
* In ''[[Fallout 4]]'', a [[newscaster]] reporting on [[Boston]] area news on the morning of the Great War mentions the debut of Vault 76 the previous year, in honor of America's Tercentenary.<ref name="newscaster" /> The same newscaster is the speaker seen in the [[Fallout 76 intro|''Fallout 76'' intro]] presenting at the opening of the Vault.
* [[Reuben Gill]] and [[ZAX 1.3c]] within [[Vault 51]] discuss the opening of the Vault on Reclamation Day, where ZAX refuses Gill's command of opening their Vault so that he could travel to Vault 76.<ref>[[Reuben Gill - 21021023]]</ref>
* Vault 76 cannot be nuked in the quest [[I Am Become Death]].
* The door and surrounding decoration of Vault 76's entrance are designed to resemble the Vault-Tec logo.
* Many objects inside the Vault are interactable (mainly either playing a sound effect or showing a visual effect). This includes props inside the player character's apartment, such as the sink, toilet, washer, dryer, alarm clock, and more; as well as items outside, including the mailboxes and garden gnomes outside the apartments, and the coffee machine and stoves in the cafeteria.
;Update history
* Prior to [[Fallout 76 patch 1.7.6.7|update 1.0.85.0]], the enemies outside the Vault were [[Liberator]]s rather than feral ghouls, and they were only in the parking lot area. This patch was the same one that added the donations box outside the Vault.
;Cut content
{{FO76CC}}
* [[Welcoming Committee]] is a quest cut from the location.
* With the ''[[Expeditions: Atlantic City]]'' part one update ''[[Boardwalk Paradise]]'', content was added to the game files involving a cut, updated version of the opening quest for ''Fallout 76''. This quest would have updated the explanation for why a new player character was still in Vault 76 even years after Reclamation Day; rather than simply being a holdout who refused to leave, the player character would be a dweller who was unable to leave because of a medical issue and was subsequently being attended to by [[Dayton|Professor Dayton]], a previously mentioned-only dweller who would have made a full, in-person appearance in the quest. 
{{FO76CC|end}}

==Appearances==
Vault 76 appears only in ''[[Fallout 76]]'' and is mentioned in ''[[Fallout 3]]'', its [[Fallout 3 add-ons|add-on]] ''[[Mothership Zeta (add-on)|Mothership Zeta]]'', and ''[[Fallout 4]]''.

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:FO76 5th Ani 1.png|thumb|250px|Vault 76 decorated for the 5th Anniversary]]
* In the quarters that the player character wakes up in at the start of the game, there is a sticky note on the mirror in that bathroom, bearing the message "Don't put TP on backwards!" This is a reference to viral debates about [[wikipedia:Toilet paper orientation|toilet paper orientation]]. The debate extended to ''[[Fallout 4]]'' after its release, as players argued about the "under" orientation of the toilet paper in the [[Sole Survivor's house]]. In 2025, the twentieth [[Fallout 76 seasons|in-game season]] for ''Fallout 76'', ''[[Glow of the Ghoul (season)|Glow of the Ghoul]]'', included two CAMP items that further reference this debate: the toilet paper holder ("under" orientation) and the "correct" toilet paper holder ("over" orientation). The bathroom in Vault 76 uses the "over" orientation.
* When reaching the first checkpoint, the "celebration" music from ''[[Fallout Shelter]]'' plays.
* In the Japanese version of ''Fallout 76'', Reclamation Day is known as "Saisei no hi" (再生の日). "Saisei" (再生) has the theme of "life" and "biology" in its name, thus the term could be translated as Rebirth Day, Revival Day, Regeneration Day or Regrowth Day. "Seisei" also has the meaning of coming back from either death or severe injury, which would be the Great War in this case.
* Vault 76 was featured in an official [[Bethesda Softworks|Bethesda]] livestream for the [[Fallout 76 5th Anniversary]] event, in which the Vault's exterior was decorated and the Vault 76 overseer delivered a three minute speech to begin the parade.
* A [https://youtu.be/Vo_FX7bSwDI trailer] for ''Fallout 76'' erroneously claims Vault 76 was the first to be opened after the Earth was destroyed by the Great War. For more information, see [[Vault#Behind the scenes|here]].
