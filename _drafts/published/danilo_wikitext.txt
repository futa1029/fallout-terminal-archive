'''Danilo''' is a resident of [[The Pitt (Fallout 76)|the Pitt]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' update ''[[Expeditions: The Pitt]]''.

==Background==
Danilo's history is mysterious as he does not want to talk about his past much, though it is known he is a former member of the [[Fanatics]]. He originally aligned with them because he thought being with the raider group was justified for his own survival, though he is now aware he actually felt like being with them was simply the easier route.

With a reevaluated sense of his own morality, Danilo abandoned the raider group and now leads efforts to raid [[the Sanctum]] and free captive [[laborer]]s. Although he is doing work for the [[Pittsburgh Union|Union]], he refers to himself as an independent party not belonging to the organization. He is also acquainted with [[Ava Rose]] and views her as a sentimental woman. To help fight against the [[Trog (Fallout 76)|trogs]], Danilo has also invented an explosive concoction called Troglocide and has hidden it around the tunnels due to their heavy weight.
