'''Doctor Dias''' is a pre-[[Great War|War]] scientist in the [[RobCo Research Center]], whose still-living brain remains preserved there.

==Background==
At some point before the war, Doctor Dias devised the Encephalon Extraction Program, a part of RobCo Industries' Semi-Autonomous Immobile Weapon Platform Research. The project ensured that the entire research team of the facility would have their brains scooped and suspended in a biomedical solution which would keep their cognitive functions intact and ready to be used for several lifetimes.<ref>[[RobCo Research Center terminal entries#Good news, everyone!]]</ref> The project was approved by [[RobCo Industries|RobCo]], despite the ethics department and the other scientists objecting to the idea.

Her [[Doctor Dias's brain|brain]] was one of the ones that were removed and stored in preservation vials, but because of the Great War, it was never installed into a [[robobrain]]. The player character can choose between hers and two more brains to install into a robobrain. She is incredibly proud and arrogant, and despite being the leader of the RobCo team which designed the [[Automated turret (Fallout 76)|automated turrets]] used in [[Vault 79]], she relishes the opportunity to one up her original work on the turrets by dismantling them.
