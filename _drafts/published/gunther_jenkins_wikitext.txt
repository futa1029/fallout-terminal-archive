'''Gunther Jenkins'''<ref>[[Nuka-World on Tour terminal entries#Staff Jobs List|Nuka-World on Tour terminal entries; manager's terminal, Staff Jobs List]]</ref> is the host of the Wild West Town attraction at [[Nuka-World on Tour (location)|Nuka-World on Tour]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
He grew up in an orphanage but was expelled from it in around 2047; it is hinted he got into a physical fight with another boy over someone named Tabitha. He lived in the [[Boston]] area.

By 2056, he was hired by [[Nuka-World (location)|Nuka-World]] due to his cowboy impressions. He looked forward to eventually seeing [[Texas]].

During the [[Great War]] in 2077, he was in a location waiting for all the rumbling to stop. He had [[Patricia Myers|Pat]] and [[Pete Myers]] with him who were children at the time. Once the bombs stopped, he went outside to explore which turned him into a ghoul due to the lingering radiation, which he would come to regret.

He has been with the traveling carnival since the beginning. Despite the post-apocalyptic lifestyle, he still finds things to enjoy about living, though admits he is lucky because of that. Pat and Pete give him a reason to live and he has viewed the siblings as if they were his own children. He has also taught Pete some basic repair skills.
