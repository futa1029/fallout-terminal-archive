'''Observer Johanna''' is a member of [[the Enlightened]] in [[Appalachia]]. Alongside fellow Observers [[Errol]] and [[Marlon]], she accompanies [[Clarence (Fallout 76)|Interpreter Clarence]] each [[The Mothman Equinox|Mothman Equinox]] in a seasonal pilgrimage from [[the Lantern]] to [[Point Pleasant]].<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Wait, there's more of you?"''<br />[[Clarence (Fallout 76)|Interpreter Clarence]]: ''"{{Tooltip|Yes. I was accompanied by three Observers: Errol, Marlon, and Johanna.|"Yohanna"}} You will find them tending to the ritual pyres in the far reaches of this sacred site. {{Tooltip|If you speak, do not be offended by the absence of their minds. They are but humble witnesses to his wisdom, and have much left to learn from him.|Somewhat sympathetic to them}}"''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D914--></ref> Together, they reclaim the homeland of the [[Wise Mothman]].<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Where do you come from?"''<br />[[Clarence (Fallout 76)|Interpreter Clarence]]: ''"We have ventured from our place of study, the Lantern, far from here. During the time of the Equinox, myself and a group of Observers make the perilous trip to the Wise One's homeland."''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D91C--></ref> Johanna seeks the company of the Wise One, whom she calls "Poppa,"<ref name=":0" /> and strives to bring him joy.<ref>Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|You seek his wisdom, yes? I seek his company.|Emphasis on I}}"''<br />([[Fallout 76 characters|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Observer Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|We have traveled so very far to be here. I know he is happy to see our little faces, so it is all worth it.|Excited}}"''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!--0062DBD4--></ref>

==Background==
Johanna is a member of the Enlightened who lives at the Lantern. Her brother [[Jelle]] once served the cult alongside her, but he was eaten by "[[Mothman#Mothman hatchling|little ones]]" after he foolishly attempted to clone the Wise Mothman.<ref>Observer Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|My brother Jelle, he tried to clone him, you know? So foolish. His wisdom was not his at birth. The little ones, they eat poor Jelle.|Jelle: Yell-uh}}"''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!--0062DBD8--></ref> Her strong accent and unusual speech patterns suggest her first language may be Romanian.

Her favorite equinox is [[the Mothman Equinox]],<ref>Observer Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|You know, the Mothman Equinox is my favorite Equinox. What's your favorite Equinox?|Playful}}"''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!--0062DBD2--></ref> and her nose feels "very tingly" when the planets align.<ref>Observer Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|Do you also feel the tingle in your nose when the planets align? It is very tingly.|Curious}}"''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!--0062DBD9--></ref> She ponders over how many equinoxes she might see in her lifetime,<ref>Observer Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|How many Equinoxes will I live to see I wonder? Could this be my last? One can only hope, yes?|Death does not scare her}}"''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!--0062DBDA--></ref> as Observer Marlon informed her about the risks of prolonged inhalation of the [[neurotoxic dust|dust]] during the ritual.<ref>Observer Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|They say the dust may harm us if we breathe it too long. Who said so? Observer Marlon. Well, THEY told him. They told him, he told me.|Referring to the voices in marlon's head}}"''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!--0062DBDB--></ref> However, this does not keep her from embracing the dust.<ref>Observer Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|Mm! The dust! How I missed the dust! Enter into my lungs, pretty dust!|Gleeful}}"''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!-- 0062DC0F--></ref> She also has family. Her mother wanted to come to Point Pleasant, but never saw "him" again.<ref>Observer Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|My Mother, she wanted to be here. She never saw him again. Oh, Poppa...|Feels bad for her mother}}"''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!--0062DBD7--></ref> She misses her "Poppa."<ref name=":0">Observer Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|I miss him so whenever he is not here with me, my Poppa...|Sad}}"''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!--0062DBDC--></ref> 

During the equinox, Johanna is tending to the waterside pyre on the western side of town. She is anxious to see the Wise Mothman again,<ref>Observer Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|I can't wait to see him. It's been so long. He's missed so many of my birthdays, you know.|Talking as if a kid has been naughty}}"''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!--0062DBD3--></ref> and will be overjoyed if the summoning ritual succeeds.<ref>Observer Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|I'm so happy to see him again. I wish to cry. I'm so happy!|Ecstatic}}"''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!--0062DC13--></ref><ref>Observer Johanna: ''"{{Tooltip|Mother would be so glad. It is so good to see him.|Ecstatic}}"''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!--0062DC14--></ref>
