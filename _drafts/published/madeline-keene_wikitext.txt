'''Madeline Keene''' is a [[Scavenger (Wastelanders)|scavenger]] and member of [[Marion Copeland]]'s group at the [[Emmett Mountain disposal site]] in [[Appalachia]] {{in|FO76GW}}.

==Background==
Madeline works alongside a group of scavengers extracting [[Radiation|radioactive]] ore from the [[Emmett Mountain disposal site]], led by [[Marion Copeland]].<ref>Events of [[Radiation Rumble|Event: Radiation Rumble]].</ref><ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"Tell me about your group."''<br />[[Marion Copeland]]: ''"We're scavengers. Most survivors are scavengers at some level, nowadays, but we like to take on the tough jobs. We go to the dangerous places and pick them clean. Then we sell what we take to the highest bidder."''<br />([[Marion Copeland/Dialogue|Marion Copeland's dialogue]])</ref> Madeline herself is a friendly woman who wants to stay out of trouble, and describes herself as just a "grunt" within the scavenging operation.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"I'm not on the team, but I am here for irradiated ore."''<br />Madeline Keene: ''"I see. Well... I don't want any trouble. I'm just a grunt. Came in to grab some tools. I'll just say that Marion hasn't been kind to other scavengers digging around on her turf. Not without joining her crew first, anyway."''<br />([[Madeline Keene/Dialogue|Madeline Keene's dialogue]])</ref>

===Quests===
[[File:FO76GW Hostage.png|thumb|right|Madeline being threatened]]
[[Leap of Faith]]: Madeline is encountered at the [[Emmett Mountain side tunnel]] during this quest. While [[Kevan Asherton]] is extracting ore from the tunnel, Madeline comes in and converses with the player, who is standing guard nearby. When Kevan returns, Madeline panics, while Kevan believes it is necessary to kill her to protect the Revenants' secrecy. The player can agree with Kevan and let him kill her, convince him to let her live with [[Strength (Fallout 76)|Strength]] or [[Charisma (Fallout 76)|Charisma]] of 8+, or fail to change Kevan's mind altogether.
