'''Greg Goldstein''' is a pre-[[Great War|War]] employee of the [[RobCo Research Center]] whose still-living brain remains preserved there in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Greg is described as a "truly unremarkable" and "perfectly normal mind" to talk to, as long as the extraction of [[Greg's brain|his brain]] didn't traumatize him.<ref>[[Greg's brain]] message description: ''"This is a truly unremarkable brain, in the best possible sense. Assuming the extraction process didn't traumatize him, Greg Goldstein should be a perfectly normal mind to talk to."''</ref>

In fact, Greg's pre-War job in [[RobCo Industries]]' Immobile Weapons Research team has left him extremely neurotic, unstable and fearful of his boss Dr. [[Dorothea Dias]], who dictated the designers' testing to her exact specifications and changed them every day. His brain extraction was scheduled for October 23, 2077, the day of the Great War, but he obeyed even with the bombs falling because he feared Dr. Dias more than the apocalypse.<ref>Greg Goldstein: ''"I came in to the office when the bombs were falling because I was scheduled and I feared her more than the Great War."''<br />([[Greg Goldstein/Dialogue#125|Greg Goldstein's dialogue]])</ref>

As part of the RobCo team which designed the [[Automated turret (Fallout 76)|automated turrets]] used in [[Vault 79]], Greg is highly familiar with the technology and can easily create tools to disarm them. His experiences at work have left him with little goodwill for his boss and his workplace, and if installed into a [[Robobrain (Fallout 76)|Robobrain]], Greg intends to seize the opportunity to take his revenge on both.<ref>Greg Goldstein: ''"I bet her brain is stored somewhere. Might as well go destroy it or maybe... hmm... experiment on it! Yeah!"''<br />([[Greg Goldstein/Dialogue#156|Greg Goldstein's dialogue]])</ref>
