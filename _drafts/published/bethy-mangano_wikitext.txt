==Background==
Prior to the [[Great War]], Dr. Mangano was a professor of soil sciences. Her mother always told her to thank people who compliment her.<ref name="CharPass">A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"[Charisma 12+] Do you have a map? I've gotten lost in your eyes."''<br />Bethy Mangano: ''"{{Tooltip|Well, aren't you a charmer? My mama always said I should thank those that compliment me. So, thank you. But, I know I'm not much to look at now.|Preface line with a giggle/snort. She's flattered but knows she's hideous looking now and a little sad about that.}} {{Tooltip|Your words are a kindness. I try not to be sensitive to my ... situation but ... well, it's hard on the self esteem. It's better than being dead.|A bit subdued but she means what she says, gets a bit sad in the middle, then resolute and determined to make the best of things.}} {{Tooltip|Plus, there are some advantages. I can go into really toxic places without fear of radiation. So, that's nice.|Bucking up, trying to be cheerful and assertive that it's not all bad.}}"''<br />[[Bethy Mangano/Dialogue|Bethy Mangano's dialogue]])</ref> She can recite the Friedrich Nietzsche quote, "Wake and listen! Verily, the earth shall yet be a source of recovery. Remain faithful to the earth, with the power of your virtue."<ref name="IntPassBoth">A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"[Intelligence 8+] Looks like you're taking some soil samples. Is your interest edaphological or pedological?"''<br />Bethy Mangano: ''"{{Tooltip|Both! But right now, my focus has to be edaphology. We need to be able to reclaim these lands and grow crops even in the most toxic of environments.|Surprised and very happy that the player knows these terms. Warming up to an enthusiastic conversation about dirt.}}"''<br />A Vault Dweller: ''"[Intelligence 12+] To quote Blake, 'Ah, to see a world in a grain of sand and a heaven in a wildflower.'"''<br />Bethy Mangano: ''"I couldn't agree more. The structure and complexity is a source of never-ending wonder for those with the capacity to see it. {{Tooltip|'Wake and listen! Verily, the earth shall yet be a source of recovery. Remain faithful to the earth, with the power of your virtue.' Nietzsche saw it.|Reverant. This quote is really meaningful to her. It inspires her work.}} {{Tooltip|And you see it too, I can tell.|Satisfied and grateful to have found someone that understands her passion.}}"''<br />A Vault Dweller: ''"Yes, I do. What a beautiful sentiment."''<br />Bethy Mangano: ''"{{Tooltip|Yes. Even in my darkest moments, when the despair that the War has wrought creeps into my mind and tries to overwhelm me, I remember those words.|Revealing something deeply personal and sad.}} {{Tooltip|I can make a difference. All business at the end.}}"''<br />[[Bethy Mangano/Dialogue|Bethy Mangano's dialogue]])</ref>

When the War struck, she was exposed to massive amounts of radiation and became a ghoul, but was otherwise physically unharmed. Being a ghoul hurt her self-esteem in regards to her beauty, but still feels it is better than being dead, and likes how she can enter toxic places without fear of radiation. She tries to remain optimistic and polite,<ref>Bethy Mangano: ''"{{Tooltip|Good to see you again. Not terribly interested in meeting other people. She's busy.}}"''<br />[[Bethy Mangano/Dialogue|Bethy Mangano's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Bethy Mangano: ''"{{Tooltip|HELLO THERE! I hope you can hear me through that helmet.|Speaking loudly initially to a player in a power armor helmet. Bethy has recently devoted herself to studying the soils of toxic environments such as the [[Toxic Valley]] to determine how to reclaim them for farming.<ref name="IntFail">A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"[Intelligence 7-] Why are you mucking around with dirt?"''<br />Bethy Mangano: ''"{{Tooltip|The simple answer is that I specialize in the study of soil with an emphasis on how to encourage plant growth.|Cheerful, but a bit disappointed with the question. ===Effects of player's actions===
* If the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]] passes two [[Intelligence (Fallout 76)|Intelligence]] 8+ checks with her, she will offer some more information about soil science as well as hand over one of two unique junk items at random, the [[Medium Int rock]] or the [[High Int rock]].<ref name="IntPassBoth"/>
* If the Vault Dweller fails the first Intelligence check (7-) she will gift them a copy of ''[[Tesla Science Magazine (Fallout 76)|Tesla Science Magazine]]''.<ref name="IntFail"/>
* If the Vault Dweller passes a [[Charisma (Fallout 76)|Charisma]] 12+ check with her, she will be flattered and open up about how becoming a ghoul has affected her self-esteem, but also the benefits it has for her work.<ref name="CharPass"/>

==Appearances==
Bethy Mangano appears only in ''[[Fallout 76]]'', introduced in the ''[[Wastelanders]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]].

==Behind the scenes==
During voice recording for Bethy, half of the recording session went by before the team realized that Bethy was supposed to be a ghoul, when they had previously recorded her with a normal, serious human voice. This required voice actress [[Chris Anthony Lansdowne]] to rerecord the dialogue with a ghoul's inflection.<ref group="Non-game">[https://talknvoiceovers.buzzsprout.com/880678/6828640-s2-e7-chris-anthony-landsdowne-adventures-in-odyssey-talking-barbie-and-more S2:E7 Chris Anthony Landsdowne - Adventures in Odyssey, Talking Barbie and more...] (48:00)</ref>
