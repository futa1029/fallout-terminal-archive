'''Maddox Mullen''' is a [[ghoul]] who was a co-founder of the [[Revenants]] and serves as the [[Fallout 76 factions|faction's]] ''de facto'' leader at [[Radiant Hills]] in [[Appalachia]] {{in|FO76GW}}.

==Background==
Maddox was [[Birthdates|born]] on October 23, [[Timeline#2059|2059]]. During his childhood, he bounced around from foster home to foster home, and looked forward to going out on his own in the future as an adult. However, the [[Great War]] occurred on the day of his eighteenth birthday, and he became [[ghoul]]ified from the resulting [[radiation]].<ref name="dob">[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"So, what's your story?"''<br />Maddox Mullen: ''"Nothing's more important than what we're doing here and now. But our pasts shape us, lead us to the present. I know mine did. Growing up, I was tossed from foster home to foster home. Never knew my parents. Never needed to. I just wanted to get out on my own. Eighteen rolled around. I could have done with a regular birthday candle, but I got a little more than that. One big mushroom cloud on the horizon. The Great War. I was out on the cliffside watching it. Seeing the new world created right before my eyes. Not knowing how it would change me."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> Maddox describes this as "twisted karmic poetry," but he nevertheless saw it as a liberating experience.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"Quite a tragic backstory you've got there. How long did it take you to come up with it?"''<br />Maddox Mullen: ''"You think I'm fishing for pity? There's nothing that could be more useless to me. I don't mind that any of that happened. The bombs dropping on my birthday was some kind of twisted karmic poetry. Watching that happen, for the first time I felt... unburdened."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> [[Nuclear weapons|Bombs]] dropping on the world put people on equal footing, which was therapeutic for Maddox, someone who had next to nothing to live for in the pre-War world.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"That must have been difficult."''<br />Maddox Mullen: ''"On the contrary. It was therapeutic. I had nothing. Nothing to go back to. Nothing to call my own. While everyone around me had homes, families, careers... The War wiped the slate clean. I could start my life on equal footing. All of us could."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> Maddox recognizes that his thoughts on the Great War could be seen as callous, but also does not shy from admitting that he has no love for the pre-War world that rejected him, even if life as a ghoul is not much better or is even worse.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"That's pretty messed up. A lot of people died, you know."''<br />Maddox Mullen: ''"Yeah, I get that. But I'm not going to lie and pretend that I shed any tears for a world that never wanted me. The kicker is that it still doesn't want me. Doesn't want any of us."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref>

After turning into a ghoul, Maddox fell in with [[Lucy Harwick]]'s group of survivors, who were trying to create a place for ghouls to live at [[Valley Galleria]]. After the ghouls began being hunted down by [[Duncan McKann]], a trio including Maddox, [[Elmira]] and [[Kevan Asherton]] split off from the main group and headed east, instead of going to [[Dyer Chemical]].<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dwellers]]: ''"How did this sanctuary for ghouls come to be?"''<br />Maddox Mullen: ''"It's not the happiest story, but it's a part of who we are. I'll start at the beginning. Valley Galleria, right here in Appalachia. A group of ghouls lived there openly. Others didn't like that so much. A hunter named Duncan McKann was planning to wipe us out. Lucy, who led the survivors there, decided to move everyone out before the attack. Duncan tracked them, of course. If you go to Dyer Chemical, well... you'll see what happened. Me, Asher, and Elmira had broken off from the group. Instead of fleeing north to Dyer, we went east. Covered our tracks well. The idea was to find a place we could start fresh, establish a new identity. One that people wouldn't mess with. You've heard about how we do things."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> This proved to be the right move, as Lucy's group were eventually killed, while Maddox, Elmira and Kevan covered their tracks and escaped.<ref>[[Lucy's holotape]]</ref>

The three engaged in raiding to try and survive, as well as gather supplies to make their escape from the area. For their final raid on a camp, they intended to keep things quiet and with minimal damage before anyone knew they had been there. However, the group was not used to such tactics at the time and things went awry. The residents of the camp were alerted to the ghouls' presence, and Elmira chose to make a distraction. She ripped off her mask and thrust herself into the camp's spotlights, and the wastelanders there assumed she was feral. Elmira was killed by the wastelanders, and her sacrifice bought the others, including Maddox, enough time to escape without more casualties.<ref name="Elmira">[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"You needed a place to hide. So you came here."''<br />Maddox Mullen: ''"We were lucky to find this place. Unfortunately, not all of us made it here. We needed to save up enough resources to make our escape from the area, so we planned one final raid. It was supposed to stay quiet - in and out before anyone knew we were there. But at the time, we weren't used to working that way. The camp got alerted. Elmira... decided to cause a distraction. She tore off her mask and ran in front of the spotlights. They thought she was feral. Bought us enough time to get out while they scanned the entrance for more of "her kind". All we can do now is try to honor her sacrifice. Live on the way she would have wanted us to."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref>

The survivors eventually came across an abandoned, inactive missile silo far to the north, in the [[Savage Divide]]. The settlement became known as [[Radiant Hills]]. It was heavily irradiated, perfect for a community of ghouls, and had a non-functional, leaking nuclear warhead inside. The faction has since grown to include more ghouls seeking refuge, and its co-founders, such as Maddox, honor Elmira's sacrifice.<ref name="Elmira" /><ref>[[Elmira's dream]]</ref> Maddox serves as the faction's ''de facto'' leader by [[Timeline#2105|2105]],<ref>[[Revenant (character)|Revenant]]: ''"Maddox says this is home. But how long until it's taken from us?"''<br />([[Revenant (character)/Dialogue|Revenants' dialogue]])</ref><ref name="train">[[Asher's training notes]]</ref><ref>[[Eugenie's response]]</ref> and has plans for expansion,<ref>Maddox Mullen: ''"This place will grow. A sanctuary for all ghoulkind. True sanctuary."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> perhaps by recruiting struggling ghouls at [[Foundation]], though he worries that it may be too early and could compromise the Revenants' safety.<ref>Maddox Mullen: ''"Need a plan for what's next. Too early for expansion. Could be ghouls struggling at Foundation..."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> Maddox continues to mentor the Revenants,<ref name="train" /> and oversees acquiring supplies for individual members.<ref>Maddox Mullen: ''"Fertilizer for Ernie, cloth for Jaye, oil for Asher... and I don't even know where to start with Parthenia's list."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref>

===Ideology and personality===
Maddox is committed to protecting the ghouls in the Revenants at any costs. He sees ghoulification as akin to a superpower,<ref>Maddox Mullen: ''"Wasn't a dream. Not to fear, though. The radiation messed a lot of things up. And it made us into superheroes."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> and believes that ghouls should lift each other up with reciprocal aid and look out for each other when the world and, in particular, unaffected [[human]]s beat them down.<ref>Maddox Mullen: ''"No more infected wounds. No more worrying about radiation. You're free, now. Unstoppable. And you're going to carry our cause: the dream of the ghouls. See, people will try to beat us down, because we have what they don't. And they're afraid of that. So we answer by living our lives to the fullest. Grasping everything we ever wanted in life, no matter who wants to keep us from it. Holding on tight. Protecting each other, and lifting up ghoulkind. It doesn't matter who you were before. You're one of us now. And we're gonna count on you, just as you can count on us."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Maddox Mullen: ''"Remember to look out for each other. You can be damn sure no one else will."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> Maddox is a realist who sees the harsh world of the [[wasteland]] for what it is, and he believes that those who want something should simply step up, act and take it.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"I understand what you mean. It's not fair to be stuck on the back foot through no fault of your own."''<br />Maddox Mullen: ''"Yet, so many of us are. {{Tooltip|It took me a long time, but I finally realized it just doesn't matter what's fair or not. Because it is what it is, regardless.|A harsh truth. He's accepting of this, but that doesn't mean he likes it.}} No amount of philosophizing about what should be makes that the reality. If you want something, you just have to take it yourself. That's what we're doing here. Carving out a piece of the world where we can be safe and free."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Maddox Mullen: ''"Anything you've been mulling over? Here's your sign. Stop mulling and act."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref>

Maddox is aware that others may see the Revenants as nothing more than simple [[raiders]], but he believes that life is different enough for ghouls that it creates a different set of needs and circumstances for them.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dwellers]]: ''"Your story isn't that unique. You just sound like another group of raiders to me."''<br />Maddox Mullen: ''"Well, maybe you've been lucky so far. But I think you'll understand soon enough. What makes life different for ghouls. I'll be there when you do."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> He is firm in his choice of prioritizing protection of the Revenants over outsiders' safety; he claims to avoid violence when possible, but does not pretend to be a saint.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"Let's talk about your methods for acquiring resources."''<br />Maddox Mullen: ''"You mean our raids. Like the one dear Leamon found himself in the middle of. We don't pretend to be saints. If we can avoid violence, we do. But, if it's between our safety and theirs, we protect ourselves. {{Tooltip|I hope you can understand something as simple and necessary as that.|A little prickly. Not interested in disagreement on this point}}"''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> However, he also declares that the Revenants should have "no mercy" for outsiders,<ref>Maddox Mullen: ''"No mercy for outsiders. Not anymore."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> and is willing to "take the fight to them" before disappearing without any outsider knowing what had happened.<ref>Maddox Mullen: ''"Take the fight to them. Then... disappear before they ever know it happened."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> Within Radiant Hills itself, however, Maddox is empathetic to fellow ghouls and believes that they deserve more than their current situation.<ref>Maddox Mullen: ''"Still not enough. They deserve more than this."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref> He wants to make sure that the Revenants are never found or disturbed by the outside world, and that Radiant Hills remains as a sanctuary for them, even if it is at a potentially fatal expense for outsiders.<ref>Maddox Mullen: ''"If they find us... We'll make sure they never return home."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Maddox Mullen: ''"This is our sanctuary. No one's going to take it from us."''<br />([[Maddox Mullen/Dialogue|Maddox Mullen's dialogue]])</ref>
