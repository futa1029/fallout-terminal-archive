'''Maxine Ballard''' is a deceased [[Fire Breathers]] recruit  found inside [[Belching Betty]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Maxine Ballard was the squad leader of the [[Scorchslayers]], a squad of Fire Breather recruits.<ref>[[Scorchslayer's journal - Part 1]]</ref> During the squad's final exam in [[Belching Betty]], the whole squad would be wiped out. [[Timothy Wolfe]] and [[Andrew Rhodes]] were killed by [[Scorched]] during the exam and [[Sylvester Tate]] deserted,<ref>[[Scorchslayer's journal - Part 2]]</ref> leaving only herself and [[Rita Wilcox]] to reach the end of the mine. At the bottom of the mine, they activated a beacon which they believed would give them further instructions on how to proceed with the exam. However, this beacon instead triggered an alarm which attracted a horde of Scorched to their location. Both Maxine and Rita were overwhelmed by the Scorched, dying before they were able to complete the exam.<ref>[[Scorchslayer's journal - Part 3]]</ref>
