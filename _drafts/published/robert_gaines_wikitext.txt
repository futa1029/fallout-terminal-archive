'''Doctor Robert Gaines''' was a scientist and member of the [[Enclave (Fallout 76)|Enclave]] in [[Appalachia]], working at a research facility in what became known as the [[Gleaming Depths (location)|Gleaming Depths]].

==Background==
Dr. Robert Gaines was the lead scientist of [[Project Vulcan (Enclave)|Project Vulcan]], a research project focused on studying the properties of "ultragenetic resonance," or more specifically, sound-regulating [[ultracite]] crystals.<ref>[[Gleaming Depths terminal entries#Ultragenetic Resonance|Gleaming Depths terminal entries; Vulcan lab terminal, Ultragenetic Resonance]]</ref> Initially, Gaines advocated for using [[radiation]] treatment in the experiments, while [[Esther Wright|Dr. Esther Wright]] stood for using sonic wave manipulation.<ref>[[Project Vulcan log 02: Direction]]</ref> Dr. Wright's methods proved to be successful, and she believed that Dr. Gaines was "finished" professionally. Furthermore, she made ominous allusions towards lessening his influence over the project.<ref>[[Project Vulcan log 03: Breakthrough]]</ref>

On July 31, [[Timeline#2080|2080]],<ref name="IR">[[Gleaming Depths terminal entries#Incident Reports|Gleaming Depths terminal entries; Vulcan security terminal, Incident Reports]]</ref> Dr. Gaines died during one of his experiments. One of the creature test subjects became so enraged that it broke free and mauled several staff members, and killed Dr. Gaines.<ref>[[Gleaming Depths terminal entries#Animal Testing|Gleaming Depths terminal entries; Vulcan lab terminal, Animal Testing]]</ref> Security chief [[John Holloway|Captain John Holloway]] lamented Dr. Gaines' death, saying that he liked Gaines the most out of all the "eggheads."<ref>[[Vulcan security log 02]]</ref> After his death, Dr. Esther Wright took over the project.<ref name="IR" />

Even after his death, Dr. Gaines' influence still hung over the experiments in the Gleaming Depths' research facility. Dr. Wright built on Dr. Gaines' previous research, seeing it as ironic given their rivalry,<ref>[[Project Vulcan log 04: Ultragenesis]]</ref> and continued to blame him as the facility descended into chaos.<ref>[[Project Vulcan log 05: Disaster]]</ref> His pet snake, Butch, eventually transformed into the gigantic [[Ultracite Terror]].<ref>[[Butch (note)]]</ref>
