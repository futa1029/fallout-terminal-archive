'''Observer Marlon''' is a member of [[the Enlightened]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Marlon, alongside fellow Observers [[Errol]] and [[Johanna]], was part of a group led by [[Clarence (Fallout 76)|Interpreter Clarence]].<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Wait, there's more of you?"''<br />[[Clarence (Fallout 76)|Interpreter Clarence]]: ''"Yes. I was accompanied by three Observers: Errol, Marlon, and Johanna. You will find them tending to the ritual pyres in the far reaches of this sacred site. If you speak, do not be offended by the absence of their minds. They are but humble witnesses to his wisdom, and have much left to learn from him."''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D914--></ref> Together, they undertook a long trip from [[the Lantern]] to [[Point Pleasant]], in order to see the home of the [[Wise Mothman]].<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Where do you come from?"''<br />[[Clarence (Fallout 76)|Interpreter Clarence]]: ''"We have ventured from our place of study, the Lantern, far from here. During the time of the Equinox, myself and a group of Observers make the perilous trip to the Wise One's homeland."''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D91C--></ref>

He mostly talks to himself and the voices that only he can hear,<ref>Observer Marlon: ''"{{Tooltip|Did you say something? No? Oh, it was you. I thought I told you we weren't talking.|Talking to two different voices. The second one he is upset with}}"''<br />([[Marlon/Dialogue|Observer Marlon's dialogue]])<!--0062DBCB--></ref><ref>Observer Marlon: ''"{{Tooltip|I can't! I won't! You cannot make me... No, you can't! No... Fine.|To voices. Stubborn then giving up}}"''<br />([[Marlon/Dialogue|Observer Marlon's dialogue]])<!--0062DBCF--></ref><ref>Observer Marlon: ''"{{Tooltip|Keep it down will you?|To a voice}}"''<br />([[Marlon/Dialogue|Observer Marlon's dialogue]])<!--0062DBCD--></ref><ref>Observer Marlon: ''"{{Tooltip|I'm not going to say that. No. Well, if you want them to know so bad, why don't you just tell them yourself?|Talking to voices}}"''<br />([[Marlon/Dialogue|Observer Marlon's dialogue]])<!--0062DBC6--></ref> going as far as arguing with them,<ref>Observer Marlon: ''"{{Tooltip|Stop it. I didn't-- no, you stop that!|Almost like bickering between kids}}"''<br />([[Marlon/Dialogue|Observer Marlon's dialogue]])<!--0062DBCA--></ref><ref>Observer Marlon: ''"{{Tooltip|No... Yes... Well, no... Yes... I see. Fine.|Talking to voices. A bit impatient}}"''<br />([[Marlon/Dialogue|Observer Marlon's dialogue]])<!--0062DBC7--></ref> or having them telling jokes to him.<ref>Observer Marlon: ''"{{Tooltip|I've heard that one before... No, that's a new one. Okay, who's there? ... Okay, Vault Dweller who? ... (chuckle) Oh, grow up.|Listening to his voices tell jokes}}"''<br />([[Marlon/Dialogue|Observer Marlon's dialogue]])<!--0062DBCC--></ref> While he is in this state of mind, he gets irritated easily when interacting with an actual person.<ref>Observer Marlon: ''"{{Tooltip|Who is this? I don't know, do you know? Yes, you! Do you know? Who are you?|Starts talking to player, then to self, then back to player}}"''<br />([[Marlon/Dialogue|Observer Marlon's dialogue]])<!--0062DBC5--></ref><ref>Observer Marlon: ''"{{Tooltip|Why are they still here? Not you, them. I wish I could be rid of you... I'm sorry, you know I don't mean that.|At first to player, then to voices}}"''<br />([[Marlon/Dialogue|Observer Marlon's dialogue]])<!--0062DBC8--></ref> Despite all of this, Marlon is aware that the voices will leave him temporarily and speak to the Wise Mothman instead, if the latter is summoned successfully during the [[The Mothman Equinox|equinox]].<ref>Observer Marlon: ''"{{Tooltip|He will be here soon, then I can finally enjoy some peace again... Yes, yes, I know.|To self, then to voices}}"''<br />([[Marlon/Dialogue|Observer Marlon's dialogue]])<!--0062DBC9--></ref> During these brief moments, he possesses a clarity that he himself enjoys.<ref>Observer Marlon: ''"{{Tooltip|Ah, a brief respite. I hope I did not alarm you. They are speaking to him now, so at last I can hear more clearly.|Finally hearing things clearly again}}"''<br />([[Marlon/Dialogue|Observer Marlon's dialogue]])<!--0062DC08--></ref> The voices say more pleasant things to Marlon, after they have spoken with the Wise Mothman.<ref>Observer Marlon: ''"{{Tooltip|I wonder what news they will bring back to me? They are always more pleasant after speaking with him.|Hearing clearly, but misses the voices}}"''<br />([[Marlon/Dialogue|Observer Marlon's dialogue]])<!--0062DC09--></ref> They also told him about the dangers of inhaling [[Neurotoxic dust|dust]] for prolonged periods of time during the ritual.<ref>[[Johanna|Observer Johanna]]: ''"They say the dust may harm us if we breathe it too long. Who said so? Observer Marlon. Well, THEY told him. They told him, he told me."''<br />([[Johanna/Dialogue|Observer Johanna's dialogue]])<!--0062DBDB--></ref>

During the equinox, Marlon is tending to the pyre underneath the [[Silver Bridge]] in Point Pleasant.
