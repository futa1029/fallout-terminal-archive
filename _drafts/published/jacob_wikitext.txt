'''Jacob Lerner''' was a member of the [[Free States]] sent by [[Ella Ames]] to retrieve samples from [[Valley Galleria]]. 

==Background==
After the [[Great War]], Jacob helped with the rebuilding of [[Harpers Ferry]], assisting [[Ella Ames]] with her medical endeavors despite his lack of training.<ref>[[Harpers Ferry terminal entries#Jacob|Harpers Ferry terminal entries; Ella's terminal, Jacob]]</ref> He was one of the Free Staters to attend the survival training course run by [[Eddie Hayes]] at [[Camp Venture]].<ref>[[Camp Venture terminal entries#March Roster|Camp Venture terminal entries; terminal, March Roster]]</ref>

In an attempt to impress Ella and outdo [[Charlie Walker]], with whom he was vying for her affections, Jacob agreed to go to the [[Valley Galleria]] to retrieve flowers. The Galleria had been used as a sanctuary for [[ghoul]]s, however most had gone [[Feral ghoul (Fallout 76)|feral]] by the time Jacob arrived. He became trapped, having used all his ammo and lost his [[Machete (Fallout 76)|machete]]. After managing to survive on junk food and [[Nuka-Cola (Fallout 76)|Nuka-Cola]] for several days, he sneaked past the ghouls with the flowers. He was then shot in the back by Charlie, who returned the flowers to Ella.<ref>[[Jacob's holotapes]]</ref> His body can be found in the parking lot outside.
