'''Knight Esposito''' was a member of the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Brotherhood of Steel]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Prior to the [[Great War]], Esposito was a private and rifleman in the elite [[Taggerdy's Thunder]] unit. After the War, she opted to remain with the squad and became a member of the new Appalachian chapter of the Brotherhood of Steel with them.

As a member of the Brotherhood, she replaced [[Johnny Moreno|Knight Moreno]] as the commander for [[Grafton Dam]] when problems with the super mutants began to escalate in the Savage Divide.<ref>[[Esposito diary - page 12]]</ref> When fighting the scorched became the Brotherhood's first priority, Grafton Dam was mothballed and she was reassigned to [[Camp Venture]] as a training officer under the command of [[Ted Wilson|Senior Knight Wilson]].<ref>[[Fort Defiance terminal entries#Satellite is Down]]</ref> At Camp Venture, she proved to be a tough but fair training officer, taking responsibility for any lapses in her squires' training.<ref name="Belmonte">[[Belmonte incident]]</ref>

Esposito died along with the rest of the Thunder when the battle with the [[Scorched]] and the [[scorchbeast]]s escalated. She was killed when she was attacked by three scorchbeasts.<ref>[[Last of the Thunder]]</ref>
