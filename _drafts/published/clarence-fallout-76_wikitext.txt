'''Interpreter Clarence''' is a member of the [[the Enlightened]] in [[Appalachia]] {{in|FO76NM}}.

==Background==
Clarence was part of a group of cult members, whose other constituents were Observers [[Errol]], [[Johanna]], and [[Marlon]].<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Wait, there's more of you?"''<br />Interpreter Clarence: ''"Yes. I was accompanied by three Observers: Errol, Marlon, and Johanna. You will find them tending to the ritual pyres in the far reaches of this sacred site. If you speak, do not be offended by the absence of their minds. They are but humble witnesses to his wisdom, and have much left to learn from him."''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D914--></ref> Together, they undertook a long trip from [[the Lantern]] to [[Point Pleasant]], in order to see the home of the [[Wise Mothman]],<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Where do you come from?"''<br />Interpreter Clarence: ''"We have ventured from our place of study, the Lantern, far from here. During the time of the Equinox, myself and a group of Observers make the perilous trip to the Wise One's homeland."''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D91C--></ref> and to commune with him.<ref>Interpreter Clarence: ''"Weary we are, for far we have traveled... All for the sake of his voice. All for the sake of his wisdom..."''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D8F9--></ref>

Clarence possesses an open posture and friendly demeanor towards visitors,<ref>Interpreter Clarence: ''"A child approaches. Do they seek wisdom?"''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D90B--></ref><ref>Interpreter Clarence: ''"I am Interpreter Clarence, of the Wise Mothman's Enlightened. How may I be of service to you, child?"''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D926--></ref><ref>Interpreter Clarence: ''"I must thank you, child! We witness glory thanks to your efforts!"''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D928--></ref> as long as they do not belong to the "Dim Ones" sect of the [[Cult of the Mothman]] who worship the red-eyed Mothman.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"I've been attacked by Mothman Cultists before. Why are you different?"''<br />Interpreter Clarence: ''"Do not mistake us for the Dim Ones. Followers of the red-eyed pretender. Fools, who flee from his wisdom, and cling to a false god -- a flawed, vision-less 'holy' Mothman. They seek violence because facing a 'worthy' death is far less frightening than facing the Truth."''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D91D--></ref> He is always in pursuit of knowledge,<ref>Interpreter Clarence: ''"The light has drawn you to us. Speak. There is much to learn."''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D90C--></ref> and has already made significant progress towards enlightenment, as he acts as an interpreter of the Enlightened.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Observers? Is that some kind of rank?"''<br />Interpreter Clarence: ''"Those of us who endeavor to find the Truth in the Wise One's words go through several stages of learning. The Observer is the larva. One who knows their place, and knows there is much more to come. For the knowledge of ignorance, of our place, is an essential first step. A bug must first know it's a bug. I, as an Interpreter, am a pupa, surrounding myself in a cocoon of his words; absorbing... Casting form from them. The venerable Wise Charles, who graces us with his company, has broken from his cocoon. He, and very few like him, may flutter and dance in the light of his infinite wisdom for the remainder of his bright but fleeting existence."''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Interpreter Clarence's dialogue]])<!--0062D910--></ref> 

During the [[The Mothman Equinox|equinox]], he can be found on the roof of the [[Mothman Museum]] in Point Pleasant, accompanying [[Charles (Cult of the Mothman)|Wise Charles the Forewarned]]. The festival's hourly rituals are conducted by the Observers [[Marlon]], [[Johanna]], and [[Errol]]. Regarding their various eccentricities, Clarence apologizes for "the absence of their minds."<ref>Interpreter Clarence: ''"If you speak, do not be offended by the absence of their minds. They are but humble witnesses to his wisdom, and have much left to learn from him."''<br />([[Clarence (Fallout 76)/Dialogue|Clarence's dialogue]])</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
* Clarence's English voice actor has not been credited or announced anywhere; voiceover credits were not published in-game for ''Night of the Moth''.
* In the game files, the entries for his voice type and his outfit refer to him as "Beholder Clarence."
* The [[Fallout 76 voice types|voice type]] actually labeled as "Interpreter Clarence" is instead assigned to Wise Charles the Forewarned.
