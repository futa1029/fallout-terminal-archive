'''Kerry''' was a member of the [[Cutthroats]] [[Raiders (Fallout 76)|raider]] gang in [[Appalachia]]. His corpse can be found on the rooftop of the Serenity Road Art Gallery in [[Lewisburg]].

==Background==
Kerry lived in the Snowdrift Lodge at the [[Pleasant Valley Ski Resort]], sharing a room with [[Brick (Fallout 76)|Brick]], [[Derrik]] and [[Mark Jackson]]. After Kerry's death, Mark sent out a public memo asking for a new roommate, sharing rent between the roommates at 55 [[Cap (Fallout 76)|caps]] per month.<ref>[[Pleasant Valley cabins terminal entries#Roommate Wanted|Pleasant Valley cabins terminal entries; Common room terminal, Roommate Wanted]]</ref>

At some point, Kerry went to Lewisburg after receiving intel from [[Brody Torrance]] that a member of the [[Order of Mysteries]] would be there.<ref>[[Kerry's orders]]</ref> Once there, he staked out the area, and it was decided that an ambush would be carried out from the rooftops during the Spring Garden Festival.<ref>[[Easy pickins]]</ref> However, the ambush failed, resulting in the death of Kerry and several other raiders.<ref>Kerry's corpse, along with other [[raider corpse]]s on the Lewisburg rooftops.</ref>
