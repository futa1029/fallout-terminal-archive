'''Gail''' is a [[Super mutant (Fallout 76)|super mutant]] who came to [[Appalachia]] in [[Timeline#2103|2103]]. She is loyal to the [[Crater Raiders]].

==Background==
Gail left her home sometime long before 2103 and, in the following years, found herself alone with nobody to take care of her. During this time, she found [[Ra-Ra]], who had been abandoned by her own companions, and they quickly bonded because of their solitude.<ref>The [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Who is Ra-Ra?"''<br />Gail: ''"{{Tooltip|Ra-Ra is precious little human girl. Gail met Ra-Ra years ago.|She had fondness for Ra-Ra}} Ra-Ra was alone. Gail was alone. Now Ra-Ra and Gail are together. Ra-Ra take care of Gail. Gail take care of Ra-Ra. Ra-Ra is what matters most to Gail."''<br />([[Gail/Dialogue|Gail's dialogue]])</ref> Since then, they have protected and watched out for each other, with Gail becoming a surrogate mother to Ra-Ra.

Gail and Ra-Ra came to Appalachia in 2103, and she was introduced to [[Meg Groberg]] by Ra-Ra, who had found Meg and brought her to help them out when Gail had needed aid. Impressed by Meg's acceptance of her being a super mutant, and by Meg's additional assistance in caring for Ra-Ra, they chose to join the raiders at [[the Crater]].<ref>The Vault Dweller: ''"How did you two end up with Meg?"''<br />Gail: ''"Meg good human. Ra-Ra found Meg when Gail needed help. Meg didn't care that Gail was Super Mutant. She help Gail anyway. Meg help raise Ra-Ra and protect her. She take care of us, and we help her in return."''<br />([[Gail/Dialogue|Gail's dialogue]])</ref>

However, her relationship with the other raiders such as [[Lev]] and [[Surge]] is shakier, especially because they find her unnatural and freakish. Gail feels frustrated by the fact that the rest of the raiders make up excuses to not help her when she needs it, claiming her temperament is too poor to offer assistance. She insists that if she is angry, it is precisely due to the lack of help. She also has a disparaging opinion of other super mutants but believes that they would benefit from the same type of friendship she has found with Ra-Ra.

===Quests===
* [[Fun and Games]]: [[Meg Groberg]] tasks the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]] of [[Vault 76]] with recruiting Gail and [[Ra-Ra]] for the [[Vault 79]] heist. Gail agrees to join, so long as the Vault Dweller first helps her find Ra-Ra and ensure her safety.
* [[Buried Treasure]]: Gail is part of the Vault 79 heist team, acting as the crew's "muscle," and follows the Vault Dweller as a temporary companion for the majority of the heist. When disabling the security system, she will be forced to use a biometric scanner due to [[Johnny Weston]]'s hacking attempt failing, and the Vault Dweller must talk her through the scanning process.

===Other interactions===
After the Vault 79 heist is over, Gail and Ra-Ra will return to their room in the Crater Core. Gail can be spoken to, and replying positively to one of her dialogue options will result in a small gain in [[Wastelanders reputations|Raider reputation]].
