'''Davie Taylor''' is a member of the [[Settlers (Wastelanders)|Settlers]] residing in [[Foundation]].

==Background==
Davie was born in post-[[Great War|War]] [[The Pitt (Fallout 76)|Pittsburgh]] to [[Elsie Taylor|Elsie]] and [[Derrick Taylor]], the former an enforcer for the raider gangs controlling the ruined city and the latter an enslaved worker. Davie is the child of an unintended pregnancy. Neither of them had enjoyed their situation in the Pitt, and Davie's birth was what spurred them to make a getaway. They succeeded, but always kept on the move, never staying in the same spot for more than a week. Elsie considered that was not the way to rise a child, but all that finally changed when they reached [[Foundation]].<ref>[[Elsie's story]]</ref>

Davie is happy with his life in Foundation, though he longs to meet other kids his age regardless of who they are.<ref>Davie Taylor: ''"If you meet any other kids around here, tell me. I'll play with them, even if they're raiders."''<br />([[Davie Taylor/Dialogue#57|Davie Taylor's dialogue]])</ref> He also self-professedly has a somewhat mischievous side, doing things such as chasing Foundation's [[Chicken (Fallout 76)|chickens]] and sneaking to play outside the town walls.<ref>Davie Taylor: ''"Sometimes I like to chase the chickens. Don't tell anyone, they'll get mad."''<br />([[Davie Taylor/Dialogue#56|Davie Taylor's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Davie Taylor: ''"I'm not supposed to go outside town ... but sometimes I do."''<br />([[Davie Taylor/Dialogue#55|Davie Taylor's dialogue]])</ref> Davie is also learning how to hack robots thanks to [[Penelope Hornwright]]'s teachings.<ref>Davie Taylor: ''"Penny's really nice. She's teaching me how to hack robots and all that."''<br />([[Davie Taylor/Dialogue#88|Davie Taylor's dialogue]])</ref> He enjoys reading and when he is disappointed he is almost done with a book series, he becomes entertained by the thought of continuing the story as a writer himself.<ref>Davie Taloy: ''"Yeah, I'm just at the end of a book series. Kinda sad to think it's gonna be over soon."''<br />([[Davie Taylor/Dialogue#12|Davie Taylor's dialogue]])</ref>
