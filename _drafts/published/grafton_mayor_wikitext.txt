The '''Grafton mayor''' is an [[Computer#Artificial intelligence|AI]] in [[Appalachia]]. He is of a similar design to [[MAIA]].

==Background==
The former computerized assistant to the actual mayor of Grafton, the Grafton mayor elected himself to the position after the [[Great War]] in a 1-0 vote, as the previous mayor "stopped showing up to work."<ref>Grafton mayor: ''"{{tooltip|I used to be the mayor's assistant, but when he stopped showing up for work, I ran for office.|Proud of his step up in the world.}} {{tooltip|I'm eminently qualified and the town unanimously elected me, one to zero.|proud of his accomplishment}} {{tooltip|Unfortunately, you are a wee bit early for tourist season.	|falsely apologetic}} {{tooltip|But this is your lucky day. Help us get ready for the upcoming flood of eager tourists, and I'll knock a whole 5% off the cost of all attractions!|TV game show announcer. persuading the player that this horrible deal is actually a wonderful one}} {{tooltip|We have four major attractions around here, Wavy Willard's, the Clarksburg Shooting Range, Prickett's Fort, and the Black Bear hunting lodge.|upbeat, proud, TV game show announcer}} {{tooltip|Go out there and get each of them ready. I'll wait here. Because I don't have legs. And I can't move.|He's apologetic rather than sad that he doesn't have legs. He just can't help himself from trying to explain it further. Pause between each line.}}"''<br />([[Grafton mayor/Dialogue|Grafton mayor's dialogue]])</ref> He is the voice of [[WGRF Grafton]] and can be heard broadcasting during various [[Fallout 76 quests#Daily quests|daily quests]] such as [[Grafton Day]]. 

Grafton's last human mayor, [[Gloria McNamara]], noted the computer had become increasingly erratic before the war, asking for a cup of coffee before it would produce reports, "even though it doesn't have a mouth!"<ref>[[Grafton terminal entries#Work order|Grafton terminal entries; Grafton mayor's terminal, Work order]]</ref>

The Grafton Mayor and his servant robots are unaware of the occurrence of the Great War. He is fervently obsessed with keeping Grafton's traditions and tourism industry intact and tasks the player character to perform several maintenance tasks in the quest [[Bureau of Tourism]], in anticipation of a large number of tourists in the near future, despite most of humanity having been wiped out.

==Notes==
* The Grafton mayor, as a mayoral assistant, is a MAIA (Mayoral Artificially Intelligent Assistant), sharing the same model as the MAIA in [[Watoga]].
* Despite Gloria McNamara being the last human mayor of Grafton, in dialogue the Grafton mayor refers to their preceding mayor as male.
