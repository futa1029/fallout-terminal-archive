'''Crutchley''' is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] in [[Vault 76]].

==Background==
Crutchley is one of the many Mister Handies that serve Vault 76. It can be found roaming around the exit on the atrium's lower level on [[Reclamation Day]], saying farewell to the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] as they leave to the outside world and instructing them to pick up their supplies at the various stations on the way out.

By [[Timeline#2103|2103]], it is still waiting on the individuals who have remained in the Vault living off the remaining provisions supplied by Vault-Tec until they finally decide to leave to explore Appalachia.
