'''Rita Wilcox''' is a deceased [[Fire Breathers]] recruit found inside [[Belching Betty]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Rita Wilcox was a member of the [[Scorchslayers]], a squad of Fire Breathers recruits who made it to their final exam. During the exam, she accused fellow recruit [[Sylvester Tate]] of causing the deaths of [[Timothy Wolfe]] and [[Andrew Rhodes]].<ref>[[Scorchslayer's journal - Part 2]]</ref> She died alongside her squad leader [[Maxine Ballard]], fighting off waves of [[Scorched]] in Belching Betty after triggering the mine alarm as part of the final stage of the exam.

Prior to beginning training to join the Fire Breathers, Wilcox was a member of the [[Responders]]. She was also romantically involved with fellow Responder [[Buck (Fallout 76)|Buck]], and the pair had one last night together at the [[Alpine River Cabins]] before Wilcox went to the [[Charleston Fire Department]] for training.<ref>[[Rita - The Fire Breather]]</ref>
