{{Quotation|I'm the Union's inventor, quartermaster, and coordinator. I'm the brain guy. I use the know-how in this dome here of mine to help the kids in the field in any way I can.|sound=Skippy Roerich 00639f3e_1.ogg}}

'''Skippy Roerich''' is a member as well as the inventor, quartermaster, and coordinator for the [[Pittsburgh Union]] from [[the Pitt]]. By 2104, he serves as their representative to the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dwellers]] in [[Appalachia]] and resides at [[The Whitespring Resort|the Whitespring Refuge]]. He [[Fallout 76 characters|appears]] {{In|FO76TP}}.

==Background==
===Union boy===
Born in [[The Pitt|Pittsburgh]], [[State#Pennsylvania|Pennsylvania]] before the [[Great War]],<ref>Skippy Roerich: ''"Because it's home, damn it! Mine, ours, all of us who were born and lived there, before and after the bombs! Ain't that enough? If the Fanatics are out of the picture, we can rebuild. Make something out of it again. It doesn't have to stay like it is. If that ain't enough for you, picture this: a horde of Fanatics, hundreds, thousands strong, setting their sights here. Sound like something you want?"''<br />([[Skippy Roerich/Dialogue|Skippy Roerich's dialogue]])</ref> Skippy's father supported his family as a worker in the city's steel mills, while Skippy himself decided to become a tinkerer for an armaments firm instead. Although he lived a stable life, he says he found it quite boring.<ref name=":0">Skippy Roerich: ''"Well, I'm a Pittsburgh man, through and through. Daddy worked the steel mills hard, but I took a different route, tinkering for an armaments firm. This may surprise you, but I did everything by the book back then. Everything done to spec, if it ain't broke, don't fix it. Life was stable, but boring as hell. I didn't really start living until after the bombs. They woke me up good."''<br />([[Skippy Roerich/Dialogue|Skippy Roerich's dialogue]])</ref>

After nuclear warheads devastated Pittsburgh, Skippy remarks that he felt like he truly "woke up" and became far more aware of the world and his role in it.<ref name=":0" /> He was able to survive the bombs due to residing in a strongly constructed factory at the time.<ref>Skippy Roerich: ''"One good thing about making weapons of war for Uncle Sam is that they build those manufactories to last. The plant I worked in may as well have been a bunker, like one of those Vault-Tec vaults the richies bought themselves. When the bombs came down, enough stood standing to keep us lucky few alive. What came after -- the rads, the ferals, the Fanatics -- that was the real test."''<br />([[Skippy Roerich/Dialogue|Skippy Roerich's dialogue]])</ref> In the following decades, he put his tinkering skills to use helping out the [[Pittsburgh Union]], formed from the conglomeration of former steelworkers, in their goal to rebuild the city back to its former glory.

===Help from Vault 76===
By [[Timeline#2104|2104]], however, the Union needed outside help, especially the Local 42 chapter led by [[Hex (Expeditions: The Pitt)|Hex]], which received the brunt of the attacks from the raider gang known as the [[Fanatics]] in their bid to take over the city now known as "the Pitt."<ref>Skippy Roerich: ''"Well, we got two different Locals I know are in need of help. Locals are what we call Union groups that act independently. Local 42's in the Industrial District. Hex, their leader, is struggling to keep her people alive and retake a foundry from the Fanatics. We got another over in the Trench, working against a Fanatic forced labor camp. Danilo is your contact there. Haven't heard from other Locals recently, but I'm sure we'll be hearing from them if they got trouble, too. It's up to you who you want to help right now. Maybe you'll consider going back again later for the other."''<br />([[Skippy Roerich/Dialogue|Skippy Roerich's dialogue]])</ref> Sometime in October of that year, against the wishes of Hex,<ref>[[Industrial District terminal entries#Help is on its way|Industrial District terminal entries; Hex's terminal, Help is on its way]]</ref> Skippy left the Pitt and traveled south, to [[Appalachia]], where he met with [[Orlando]] at [[The Whitespring Resort|the Whitespring Refuge]] to spread word of his call for help and took up residence there in the meantime. Acting as a representative for the Union, he requested the support of the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|inhabitants]] of [[Vault 76]] to fight against the Fanatics and stop them from taking over the Pitt.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"Who are these allies?"''<br />[[Orlando]]: ''"They call themselves the Union. Something of a resistance movement, I understand, against more aggressive forces. One of their representatives is currently staying with us, a Mr. Skippy Roerich. Delightful man."''<br />([[Orlando/Dialogue|Orlando's dialogue]])</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
* Skippy's voice actor has never been credited or announced anywhere. In localized versions of the game, his voice is dubbed by Antonio Villar (Spanish).
* Concept art for Skippy's character design was created by [[Art Bully Productions]] artist [[Stevan Loncarevic]].<ref group="Non-game">[https://www.artstation.com/artwork/WmylLG ArtStation]</ref> This artwork was later repurposed into a player icon used as a reward for ''[[The City of Steel]]'', the tenth [[Fallout 76 seasons|in-game season]].
{{FO76CC}}
* In the game's files, there is an unused [[Fallout 76 voice types|voice type]] assigned to a character named "Freddy Roerich," although no dialogue exists for them. This implies that a relative or family member of Skippy was intended to appear in the update but was cut from the final release.
{{FO76CC|end}}
