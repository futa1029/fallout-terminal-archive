'''Elsie Taylor''' is a member of the [[Settlers (Wastelanders)|Settlers]] residing in [[Foundation]].

==Background==
A former enforcer from [[The Pitt (Fallout 76)|the Pitt]], Elsie was unhappy with life as a raider and longed for a change. Falling in love with then-enslaved [[Derrick Taylor]] and having a [[Davie Taylor|son]] with him only solidified her desire to leave, although it was an unintentional pregnancy. They eventually staged a bloody escape in which she killed eight raiders on the way out. She suffered a cracked rib and a bullet grazed her scalp, but managed to keep her husband completely unharmed. The raiders stopped looking for them after she ambushed the first hunting party they sent.

During the next years, Elsie and her family always kept moving, never staying on the same spot for more than a week. Elsie considered that was not the way to raise a child, but all that finally changed when they arrived at Foundation.<ref>[[Elsie's story]]</ref>

She is known around Foundation for her skilled marksmanship, making her one of the few skilled lookouts for the settlement.<ref name="Watch">Settler: ''"Aren't you tired of being on watch, Elsie?"''<br />''Elsie Taylor: ''"Nah, I am an angel of death with this bad boy. What more could I want?"''<br />Settler: ''"Hmm... dunno. Maybe some bourbon?"''<br />''Elsie Taylor: ''"Thank you, ma'am. I will definitely take you up on that, next break I take."''<br />([[Settlers (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Settler]] and [[Elsie Taylor/Dialogue|Elsie Taylor's dialogue]])</ref> Additionally, Elsie also helps with the Settlers' construction efforts.

Elsie claims family is the only thing that matters to her. She is training her son Davie how to shoot so that he can protect himself, but hopes that they can build up Foundation enough that he won't have to ever use a gun. Elsie is contemplating to get a [[Dog (Fallout 76)|dog]] to guard her house if the settlement keeps prospering.<ref>Elsie: ''"Maybe if this place keeps going, I should get a dog to help me guard the house."''<br />([[Elsie Taylor/Dialogue|Elsie Taylor's dialogue]])</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Elsie is voiced by [[Mara Junot]], who also voiced [[Meg Groberg]] and [[Lacey Drummond]]. In localized versions of the game, her voice is dubbed by Chieko Miyazaki (Japanese) and Nieves "Nikki" García (Spanish). Elsie's English and Japanese voice actresses share the same roles, but this is not the case for the Spanish dub.
