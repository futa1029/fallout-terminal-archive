'''Barb''' is a member of the [[Crater Raiders]] located inside the [[Crater Core]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
After her parents died, Barb and her older brother [[Creed]] survived together in the wasteland keeping each other alive. Eventually, both of them joined the raiders sometime prior to the group's return to Appalachia, after hearing they could make their own fortune and that the raiders didn't care where they came from.

Despite depending on her brother in the past, she now views him as a coward because he does not want to engage in raiding and she feels he gets her into trouble. She feels he has always been a loser.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What can you tell me about Creed?"''<br />Barb: ''"You want to know about my brother? Creed's a coward, always has been."''<br />([[Barb (Wastelanders)/Dialogue#142|Barb's dialogue]])</ref> She is currently dating her brother's best friend, [[Axel (Wastelanders)|Axel]], whom she thinks is just like a puppy dog that is not as tough as he thinks.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What do you know about Axel?"''<br />Barb: ''"A lot. Me and Axel are shacking up. He's like a puppy dog who likes to think he can protect everybody, but he's not as tough as he thinks he is."''<br />([[Barb (Wastelanders)/Dialogue#143|Barb's dialogue]])</ref>

[[Meg Groberg]] likes Barb, but is aware how she has a self-preserving attitude and is willing to lie to keep up her reputation.<ref>[[Meg Groberg]]: ''"I like Barb, but she's all about self-preservation. She's quick to lie to save her own ass, but loves seeing someone else fall from grace. She'll snitch if she knows someone else is guilty."''<br />([[Meg Groberg/Dialogue#177|Meg Groberg's dialogue]])</ref> She is described by her brother as being a troublemaker who lies to cover herself up; she once killed [[Wren (raider)|Wren]]'s dog because the dog would not stop barking and got away with it because Wren believed her alibi.

Barb is said to dislike children, once not deciding to cover for [[Ra-Ra]] after she stole some of Meg's snacks, having told Meg the truth.

===Quests===
* [[From Russia With Lev]]: Barb is the snitch and must be made to tell [[Lev]]'s location. Depending on the player's actions, the player may threaten her not to deal with Lev again, or choose to kill her.
**If Barb is killed, she will say "screw you!" and not go down without a fight. The raiders underground will not react to Barb attacking the Vault Dweller, and Barb apparently can not go upstairs.
***Outside, Axel and Creed will somehow already know Barb is dead in an omniscient manner. Axel may say, ''"I can't believe Barb's dead! I mean, that's what happens to traitors, but... oh Barb."'' and Creed may say, ''"Barb's really dead. She was my little sister. I mean, it had to be done, for everyone's safety, but... man.... I don't know what to do now."'' These lines are part of their idle dialogue as the player can not have a conversation about it.
***Meg can be informed later; she replies she figured Barb was the mole on account of the Vault Dweller killing her. Meg brushes it off, saying she isn't mad since she said earlier to take care of the problem by any means necessary. Meg says she would only be upset if she said not to kill Barb and this order was defied. Meg finishes up the conversation by saying she can live with sending a not-so-subtle message to everyone else if people decide to become traitors. There will be a small gain of Raider reputation.
**If Barb is alive, the player may tell Meg that Barb was the mole which will result in a small gain of Raider reputation. Meg will spare Barb but keep her in line.
