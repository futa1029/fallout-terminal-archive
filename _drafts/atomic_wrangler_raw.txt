The '''Atomic Wrangler Bar & Casino''', typically shortened to just the '''Atomic Wrangler''', is an [[Fallout: New Vegas locations#Freeside|unmarked location]] within [[Freeside]] in the [[Mojave Wasteland]] {{In|FNV}}. It also appears in [[Fallout TV series season 2|season 2]] of {{in|FOTV|noin}}.<ref name="FOTV0205">{{FOTV|2|5}}</ref>

==Background==
{{Quotation|It's not much to look at, but if you can't afford to get into The Strip, the Atomic Wrangler's the best place to gamble in Freeside.|[[Fallout: New Vegas loading screens|''Fallout: New Vegas'' loading screen]]}}
The Atomic Wrangler Bar & Casino was a pre-[[Great War|War]] nightclub located on [[Fremont Street]] in [[New Vegas|Las Vegas]], [[Nevada]], advertising itself as "the West's finest watering hole."<ref name="billboard">[[:File:AtomicWranglerAdv.png|Atomic Wrangler billboard]]</ref> In 2077, it played host to a meeting between [[The Ghoul|Cooper Howard]] and [[Diane Welch|Congresswoman Diane Welch]], who arranged for Howard to secretly meet with the [[Last President of the United States|U.S. president]] in order to give him the [[Cold fusion|cold fusion diode]] Howard had stolen from [[Vault-Tec Corporation|Vault-Tec]].<ref>{{FOTV|2|7}}</ref>

By [[Timeline#2281|2281]], the Atomic Wrangler is the only active [[Fallout: New Vegas casinos|casino]] in the slum community of Freeside,<ref>[[:File:FNV Atomic Wrangler card.png|Vault playing card]]</ref> also doubling as a flophouse, brothel, and prominent source of the [[chems]] craze in the area. Its owners and operators are the Garret twins, [[James Garret|James]] and [[Francine Garret|Francine]], who keep a staff of armed thugs for protection and provide service even to members of the [[Van Graffs]] in the nearby [[Silver Rush]] and the [[Kings]] from the down-the-street [[King's School of Impersonation]]. Many caravaneers and poor (but not impoverished) [[New California Republic|NCR]] folks can also be found here when they lack deep enough pockets to make it to the [[New Vegas Strip|Strip]].<ref group="Supplement">[[Fallout: New Vegas Official Game Guide Collector's Edition Tour of the Mojave Wasteland#2C.06 Atomic Wrangler Casino|''Fallout: New Vegas Official Game Guide Collector's Edition'' Tour of the Mojave Wasteland; 2C.06 Atomic Wrangler Casino]]</ref>

As of 2296, the Atomic Wrangler continues to operate in Freeside, though by then, the barkeep is a man named [[Shotgun Jeff]] and it is unclear whether the Garret twins are still involved with the establishment.<ref name="FOTV0205"/>

==Layout==
===''Fallout: New Vegas''===
Situated in the south end of Freeside, the Wrangler can be seen past the patched-together "Freeside" sign on the west side with itself featuring a neon sign of a futuristic cowboy riding a stylized atom.<ref name="billboard" />

====Bottom level====
The main room has a vaulted ceiling, a stage, and a bar. The room directly opposite the entrance features the casino's [[Atomic Wrangler cashier|cashier]] and casino games. The cashier's room contains some [[Pre-War money (Fallout: New Vegas)|pre-War money]] and [[Fallout: New Vegas consumables|chems]] in the safes. To the right are the bathrooms and a staircase to the second story balconies.

Behind the bar is a kitchen and storeroom, accessible via a door around the corner near the bathroom with a Very Hard lock. Both of the Garrets have the [[Fallout: New Vegas keys#Atomic Wrangler upstairs key|key]] to the room, but it will eventually become unlocked as soon as one of the Garrets head upstairs to their room, as they neglect to relock it.

The storage room/kitchen area behind the bar contains a significant amount of common items of moderate value, such as various alcoholic beverages. The Courier can also find a large number of [[Fresh apple (Fallout: New Vegas)|fresh apples]], [[Fresh pear (Fallout: New Vegas)|pears]], and [[Fresh carrot (Fallout: New Vegas)|carrots]] (at least a half-dozen each), as well as a weapons locker and medicine locker, both containing many useful items.

====Second level====
This level has a balcony that oversees the stage and bar. Off of the balcony are four rooms. A rentable room is located in the eastern corner of the second floor. Stairways lead back down or up to the third level.

====Corner room====
{{main|Atomic Wrangler corner room}}
On the second level, there is a door that leads to the corner room, which can be gained as [[Fallout: New Vegas player character housing|player housing]] after completing [[Debt Collector]] for Francine.

====Third level====
This level is where the employees, including the Garret twins, live. In the employee living area, four guards can be found either milling around or sleeping. The door to this level is secured with an Average lock or opened with the key held by the Garrets. Guards here will not be hostile initially, instead following the Courier around, repeating a  warning to leave. Rooms include a large bedroom the Garret twins take turns sleeping in, two general sleeping areas for employees (one containing 8 beds and one with 6 beds), two bathrooms and a storage room. Guard sleeping areas have [[Footlocker (Fallout: New Vegas)|footlockers]] with minor loot. The two bathrooms contain [[First aid box (Fallout: New Vegas)|first aid box]]es. The storage room, located to the right of the sleeping area, is filled with file cabinets, metal crates, and a gun cabinet.

===''Fallout'' TV series===
The Atomic Wrangler, as depicted in the TV series, has both similarities to and differences from its portrayal in ''Fallout: New Vegas''. The casino storefront is still located across from the [[Silver Rush]], except down at the end of a main street with multiple other stores including [[Sonny's Sundries]].

The bottom floor is widely different from its previous appearance, with glass double doors instead of one door, no visible evidence of a performance stage, and the second floor balcony extending above the bar. The staircase itself is also relocated into a curved form opposite from the bar instead of behind it.

What's shown of the upper floors consists mostly of a single hotel room which has an attached bathroom and open windows looking out into Freeside.

==Gambling==
[[File:TheAtomicWrangler.jpg|thumb|220px|The gambling tables]]
; Games
* The Atomic Wrangler offers [[Blackjack]], [[Roulette]], and [[Slots]].
* The Atomic Wrangler pays 6:5 on blackjack, and the dealer must draw to 16.

; Rewards and Ban
As the player earns chips, the casino offers increasingly valuable gifts:
* 1,250+ chips: [[Beer (Fallout: New Vegas)|beer]]
* 2,500+ chips: [[Whiskey (Fallout: New Vegas)|whiskey]] and dapper gambler hat
* 3,750+ chips: [[absinthe]], [[Rum & Nuka]] & [[dapper gambler suit]]
* 5000+ chips: banned from gambling.

After the player character earns 5,000+ chips, they will be banned from playing games in the Atomic Wrangler Casino. They can still use its other functions, such as the bar and services of the prostitutes (if [[Wang Dang Atomic Tango]] has been completed), but they cannot win any more money, permanently. If the player character breaks the house limit before getting any comp gifts (by getting a large win) they will not receive any comps they have missed. There is no floor manager at this casino, so the comp gifts are awarded directly to the player character upon reaching each winning milestone. Play is still interrupted by the player character being forced out of whichever game they were playing to receive the message rewarding their earned comps.

==Notes==
* The casino's [[Atomic Wrangler crier|crier]] can be found outside on the street corner below the "Freeside" sign, advertising the casino to the public.
* Despite the Wrangler's reputation as a free-swinging brothel, dialogue options with Francine Garret that would suggest the same, and sex noises coming through the walls of most rooms, there are no prostitutes in the casino beyond ones the player character recruits during Wang Dang Atomic Tango. After finishing the quest, one can then have sex with either ghoul cowgirl/dominatrix Beatrix Russell, Santiago/Old Ben (depending on decisions during the quest), or the sex-bot Fisto.
* Like all other casinos in the game, the amount of money kept in the cashier's office is small. Robbing the cashier nets at random a few dozen bundles of pre-War money and approximately 100 [[Bottle cap (Fallout: New Vegas)|caps]]. The [[Fallout: New Vegas keys#Atomic Wrangler bank key|Atomic Wrangler bank key]] is carried by both Garret twins.
* Exiting from dialogue with Fisto will give 10 [[Bottle cap (Fallout: New Vegas)|caps]], which can be repeated ad infinitum.
* Being banned from the casino by gambling does not affect the progress of completing the [[The Courier Who Broke the Bank]] achievement/trophy.
* The casino also appears in ''[[Fallout 76]]'' as a [[Fallout 76 shelters|shelter]] purchasable in the [[Atomic Shop]]. In-game it has a similar layout to its initial appearance though is larger than it appeared in ''Fallout: New Vegas''.

==Behind the scenes==
;''Fallout: New Vegas''
* The in-game Atomic Wrangler Casino is based on the real-world Atomic Liquors store, located on [[wikipedia:Fremont Street|Fremont Street]] in downtown Las Vegas. 
* The logo of the Atomic Wrangler is a reference to a scene in the film ''[[Wikipedia:Dr. Strangelove|Dr. Strangelove]]'' in which Major T. J. "King" Kong rides a falling nuclear bomb like a rodeo cowboy.
* Area designer [[Robert Lee]], who was one of three designers on Freeside, wrote for characters within this location and designed the quests [[Wang Dang Atomic Tango]] and [[Debt Collector]].<ref group="Meta">[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E4zN5Qy3Izo Freeside's Cut Content]</ref>
;''Fallout'' TV series
* In season 2 of the TV series, Freeside and the exterior of the Atomic Wrangler were filmed at [[wikipedia:Movie ranch#Monogram Ranch/Melody Ranch|Melody Ranch Motion Picture Studio]] in [[wikipedia:Santa Clarita, California|Santa Clarita, California]]. Melody Ranch has previously been used to film prominent Westerns as well as TV series like ''[[wikipedia:Westworld (TV series)|Westworld]]'', also produced by [[Jonathan Nolan]] and [[Lisa Joy]].<ref name="latimes" group="Meta">[https://archive.ph/HTk7h Los Angeles Times: "‘Fallout’ fans know New Vegas. How the show brought the video game location to life"]</ref><ref group="Meta">[[:File:FOTV season 2 Johnny Pemberton Freeside tour.mp4|Freeside film set behind-the-scenes tour]] by [[Johnny Pemberton]]</ref>
{{Quotation|The Atomic Wrangler was so specific in the game. It has specific architecture and has this terrific neon sign that I love with the cowboy ... There's no way to take [a building that] already existed [on set] and have it look like the Atomic Wrangler ... so I put a facade in front of a facade.|[[Howard Cummings]], quoted by the L.A. Times}}
* The interior of the Atomic Wrangler was also shot at Melody Ranch, but in a different building across the street from the one used for the exterior. The building used for the interior was the one used to film the [[w:c:westworld:Mariposa Saloon|Mariposa Saloon]] in ''Westworld'', with some features such as the stage moved to a different side.<ref name="latimes" group="Meta" />

* {{Platforms|ps3}} Once a player attempts to go to the second floor, or perhaps attempts to lockpick the offices/rooms, a bug may occur that interferes with interactions with non-player characters inside of the Atomic Wrangler. Speaking with Francine Garret or the cashier may result in a locked dialogue, and the only action that can be made is dropping inventory items from your apparel (by hitting square, as the Pip-Boy doesn't respond). Once your apparel inventory is empty (not including what is equipped) the dialogue will unlock and allow you to finish the conversation. This may also interfere with any dialogue with James Garret, as it appears that he will have no dialogue options beyond just greeting you. A simple solution to the problem with the dialogue lock/inventory dump is to approach the non-player characters that the bug affects with your apparel inventory empty, except for what you want to have equipped. This allows uninterrupted dialogue.{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc,ps3}} Once you enter the Atomic Wrangler and subsequently leave you will still hear the sounds of the casino's patrons in the background.{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc}} Once you enter the Atomic Wrangler you may encounter a large framerate drop. If this happens turning around and facing the door has been known to stop it, however, running around backward can get quite irritating.{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc,xbox360}} A bug can occur where you will not be able to enter the Atomic Wrangler, clearing cache does not work. The black screen permanent crash can be fixed if you wear Caleb McCaffery's hat before entering the casino.{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|xbox360}} After completing the quest Wang Dang Atomic Tango, if you acquired Santiago and Old Ben, both will show up at the casino. You can only solicit Santiago's services, Ben will just sit at the counter. If you open a dialogue with Ben, you cannot ask for his services, but you can go through the initial quest dialogue with him. You have the option of telling him that James Garret is looking for someone to work for him.{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|ps3,xbox360}} Sometimes you may not receive the 'prize' for winning chips from the slot machine, even after exceeding the limit (i.e. 1250 chips). {{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc,ps3}} If you're given the free room in the casino, prostitutes will not visit the player there, and you cannot purchase a room for them to visit. (Santiago and Fisto work in 1.2.0.285. This bug seems specific to Beatrix Russell. Additionally, Old Ben can never be hired).{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|ps3,xbox360}} Once the player enters the Atomic Wrangler, all non-player characters within the building may automatically become hostile.{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|xbox360}} Entering the Garrets' room upstairs can cause the game to freeze on the loading screen.{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|ps3,xbox360}} Once you recruited Fisto to work with the Garrets, you can talk with him and he will offer you his services for 10 caps. If you refuse, you will be awarded 10 caps. This can be repeated (confirmed on the patch 1.2.0.315).{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc,ps3,xbox360}} Entering a room upstairs, you might find yourself locked in the room. You cannot pick the lock, as when attempting to open the door, it says it cannot be lock picked and you must use a key. No patch is known yet to fix this, so every time you enter the Atomic Wrangler, it is highly suggested you save your game upon entering.{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|ps3}} If Beatrix Russell is hired, she will never show up at your room and you cannot rehire her again.{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc}} Most of the guards in the Atomic Wrangler don't seem to "see" the player. The player will often remain "hidden", even while in plain sight of the guards. This seems especially consistent with the guards that run up when you are in an area that you shouldn't be.{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|pc}} All of the gamblers, the card dealer, and the roulette dealer in the blackjack and roulette area can be killed without causing surrounding non-player characters to become hostile, and without any faction penalty, as long as they are killed with a silenced weapon from behind and the player is crouching. This is the case even if the guards and cashier are all watching, and regardless of if the player is "hidden". A Karma penalty still applies.{{verify|verified}}
* {{Platforms|xbox360}} The game might crash whenever you win any leave either the blackjack table or the slots with a large number of caps earned. This can be avoided by briefly leaving either game after each progressive win (roughly 200 caps).{{verify|verified}}
