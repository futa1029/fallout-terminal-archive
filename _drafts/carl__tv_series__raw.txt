'''DJ Carl''' is a radio station host {{In|FOTV}}.

==Background==
DJ Carl operates [[KPSS radio station|KPSS]] in the [[Hollywood Hills]], playing music only he has a preference for, [[Wikipedia:Fiddle|fiddling]] in particular. However, he has booby-trapped his radio station extensively to protect himself from the numerous "[[critic]]s" who attempt to silence his broadcasts.<ref>[[Thaddeus]]: ''"Great job on these booby traps, by the way. People just, uh, can’t be nice."''<br />({{FOTV|1|7}})</ref>

==''Fallout'' TV series==
===[[The Radio]]===
As [[Thaddeus]] carrying [[Siggi Wilzig|Wilzig's]] head trudges through the wasteland with a broken foot, DJ Carl's voice could be heard via broadcast and notes that, like all weeks, it's fiddle music week. He reminds listeners that if someone doesn't like his fiddle music, he doesn't allow visitors at his station and recommends a simple postcard instead.

Eventually, Thaddeus reaches DJ Carl's station and sits and waits for his ride from his [[Brotherhood of Steel]] comrades. He thanks DJ Carl for allowing him to use his radio.

When Thaddeus asks DJ Carl if people don't enjoy his fiddle music, he responds that people get really mad and that they don't realize that the music he plays is original recordings. He explains the warm sound and frequency ranges that the recordings have to Thaddeus, who tries to understand and agree.

Thaddeus changes the subject to the booby traps that DJ Carl had placed around his station to deter what he calls critics. DJ Carl then spots [[Lucy MacLean|Lucy]] and [[Maximus]] walking toward the station and asked Thaddeus if they were his ride. However, when Thaddeus starts firing at them, he realizes that's not the case and closes up his station.

==Personality==
DJ Carl is a soft-spoken individual who enjoys listening to and playing fiddle music. Despite his cordial demeanor, he detests what he calls critics and has armed his radio station with various booby traps to deter or eliminate them.
