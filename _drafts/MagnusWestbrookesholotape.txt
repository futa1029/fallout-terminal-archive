'''Magnus Westbrooke's holotape''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] {{In|FO76}}. 
__TOC__
==Location==
The holotape can be found in [[Bolton Greens]] at the front reception desk, next to the facility director's terminal.

==Behind the scenes==
The contents of this holotape appear to broadly [[Fallout 76 cultural references#A Streetcar Named Desire|reference]] the 1947 play ''[[wikipedia:A Streetcar Named Desire|A Streetcar Named Desire]]'', famously adapted into a 1951 [[wikipedia:A Streetcar Named Desire (1951 film)|film version]]. The story concerns a once-prosperous Southern belle who must move into a run-down apartment building. The story of the holotape shares a similar premise, with a privileged young woman forced into circumstances she is uncomfortable with. However, Magnus' situation is different in that Bolton Greens is already a wealthy area, but a millionaires' retreat seems squalid to the even more ultra-rich Westbrookes. The character DuBois, mentioned in the holotape, shares a surname with the main character of ''A Streetcar Named Desire'', [[wikipedia:Blanche DuBois|Blanche DuBois]].
