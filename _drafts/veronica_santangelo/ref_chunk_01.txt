While Veronica's absence from the bunker did keep her out of command's hair, her exposure to the outside world only made her more aware of the Brotherhood's failure. Watching the influx of NCR troops, merchants, and citizens to the Mojave, among other groups, made her realize how the Brotherhood had failed to adapt.
