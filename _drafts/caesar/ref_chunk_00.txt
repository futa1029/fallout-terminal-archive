'''Caesar''', born '''Edward Sallow''',<ref name="FNVHH">The [[Courier]]: ''"Were you always with the New Canaanites?"''<br />[[Joshua Graham]]: ''"I was born in Ogden, what people came to call New Canaan. Things were more peaceful when I was growing up. When I was a young man, I went out into the world to do missionary work as all New Canaanites do. I traveled along the Long 15 and followed 89 south into Arizona. Along the way, I met two men from a group called the Followers of the Apocalypse. Edward Sallow and Bill Calhoun. They came to teach the tribes. Calhoun was a good man. Edward was the one who got us into trouble down the road."''<br />([[NVDLC02Joshua.txt|Joshua Graham's dialogue]])</ref> is the leader, dictator and co-founder of [[Caesar's Legion]] in [[Timeline (2250-2300)#2281|2281]]. Under his command the Legion has been building up a presence in the [[Mojave Wasteland]], fighting a prolonged war with the [[New California Republic]] for control of the region. He is a central character {{In|FNV}}.

By the events of the [[Fallout (TV series)|''Fallout'' TV series]] in [[Timeline (2250-2300)#2296|2296]], Caesar has been dead for some time, and his demise has led to a [[Legion civil war|civil war within the Legion]] over his succession.

==Background==
Originally educated as an anthropologist, the man known as Caesar is feared as the most immediate existential threat facing the [[New California Republic]]. Inside the Legion, he is respected for his charisma, strategic mind, and unforgiving brutality. These traits are the main reason such a large, sprawling organization hasn't collapsed into a slurry of small, infighting factions.

But despite the grand persona Caesar has created for himself, he also has a less graceful side. If confronted with his past mistakes, or if he feels that he is not in complete control of a situation, Caesar will reveal his true nature as that of a prideful, megalomaniacal bully with a fragile ego and a hair-trigger temper, and act out in petulant denial and anger or childish spite.

Now in 2281, Caesar looms in his field base/headquarters at [[The Fort|Fortification Hill]], waiting to cross the Colorado River to avenge his humiliation at the [[First Battle of Hoover Dam]] and finally annex [[New Vegas]] as the capital he feels his empire truly deserves. Caesar is playing his cards more cautiously this time, and will not give the order for [[Lanius]] to attack the Dam until he can unearth the contents of the [[Securitron vault|vault]] sealed beneath his base in the Mojave. Caesar also needs to neutralize House, attempt to forge an alliance with the [[Boomers]], [[Great Khans]], and the [[White Glove Society]], as well as destroy the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Mojave chapter)|Mojave Brotherhood of Steel]], and attempt to assassinate [[Aaron Kimball|President Kimball]]. Two factors have halted his progress: firstly, a [[Et Tumor, Brute?|debilitating brain tumor]] that prevents him from fully administering to the day-to-day operations of the Legion, and secondly, his interest in reports of a resourceful [[Courier]] whose ambiguous allegiance could either be a problem or, potentially, a boon to his efforts.

===Early life===
Born in [[Timeline (2200-2249)#2226|2226]]<ref group="Non-game">''[[Fallout: New Vegas Official Game Guide]] Collector's Edition'' p. 461: ''"'''Important Dates'''"''<br />"2226 ''The boy who would become Caesar is born."''<br />([[Behind the Bright Lights & Big City]])</ref> near the [[Boneyard]] as Edward Sallow, he was once a citizen of the [[New California Republic]]. Following the death of his father at the hands of raiders in [[Timeline (2200-2249)#2228|2228]], his mother sought the protection of the [[Followers of the Apocalypse]]. While she worked for the Followers, cooking and cleaning in their Library, the young Edward learned how to read and started taking courses, provided by the organization free of charge.<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"How did you rise to power?"''<br />Caesar: ''"Ironically, I was born a Profligate myself, a citizen of the NCR. My family lived not far from the great Boneyard. After Raiders killed my father, my mother sought the Followers' protection. I was two years old. She found work at their Library, cooking and cleaning. I learned how to read and soon I was taking courses, free of charge."''<br />([[FortCaesar.txt|Caesar's dialogue]])</ref> Taught to bring the torch of knowledge to the wastes, Sallow was a student of uneven quality. Though he was highly intelligent, his success in scientific pursuits was only proportional to his interest in the given subject, nor was he particularly popular among his peers, due to his bad temper and narcissistic attitude. For Sallow, the Followers were never an inspiring example, their devotion to scholarship too stifling, their mission of enlightenment too naïve.<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"You were a Follower of the Apocalypse?"''<br />Caesar: ''"Oh yes, raised in that tradition.
