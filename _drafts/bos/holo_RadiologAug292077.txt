==Notes==
* {{Note|(Note 1)}} The title of the holotape presents a lore contradiction - the contents (''"America's fallen"'') indicate that it was recorded in the aftermath of the [[Great War]], while the tape's date precedes that date by nearly two months. This is further reinforced in Taggerdy's [[Taggerdy's journal: OCT 21 2077|journal entry]] dated October 21, 2077, which states that Taggerdy's Thunder had not been in America, much less Appalachia, until that date. Maxson also was not aware of his unconscionable orders until October 13, 2077<ref>[[Captain Maxson's diary#Oct.13_2077|Captain Maxson's diary; Oct. 13 2077]]</ref> and would not have been considered a traitor until October 20, 2077.<ref>[[Captain Maxson's diary#Oct.20_2077|Captain Maxson's diary; Oct. 20 2077]]</ref>
* {{Note|(Note 2)}} The subtitles here differ from the spoken line. In the voiceline, Maxson says "Lizzy, look around you."
