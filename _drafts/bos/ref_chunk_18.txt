
===Mercenary groups===
During it's history the Brotherhood has consistently clashed with large mercenary groups. These groups are often motivated only by monetary compensation and are willing to commit heinous acts such as, kidnapping, human trafficking and mass murder. Because of their willingness to accept such contracts and commit these crimes, they are often drawn into conflict with the Brotherhood of Steel.<ref name= "Steel Reign" >Events of ''[[Steel Reign]]''</ref><ref name="qcy">[[Quincy Massacre]]</ref> These groups might also be in possession of advanced weaponry and technologies, and thus they may be viewed as competitors or a threat by the organization. In 2104, the Appalachian chapter of the Brotherhood would all but annihilate what remained of the [[Hellcat Company]] during their assaults on [[Vault 96]] and [[West Tek research center (Fallout 76)|West Tek]]. The mercenary group had been assisting [[Edgar Blackburn]] in abducting innocent civilians for his inhumane experiments with [[FEV]], and were responsible for countless deaths in the region.<ref name= "Steel Reign" /> 

[[File:Ft Bannister BOS aqua pura security after Broken Steel.jpg|thumb|Alpha Squad launching its assault on [[Talon Company]] at Fort Bannister]]
In 2277, the Capital Wasteland chapter of the Brotherhood would come into conflict with the mercenaries of [[Talon Company]]. This occurred because they were attacking [[Water Distribution Network|Brotherhood water caravans]] that were carrying clean water meant for the settlements of the Capital Wasteland. These raids resulted in the Brotherhood launching an [[Battle of Fort Bannister|assault]] on [[Fort Bannister]].<ref>[[Jensen (Broken Steel)|Paladin Jensen]]: ''"The Brotherhood is getting ready for a little payback. We're tired of Talon Company ambushing our water caravans."''<br />([[DLC03EncounterFBBrotherhoodA04.txt|Paladin Jensen's dialogue]])</ref> Alpha Squad led by [[Jensen (Broken Steel)|Paladin Jensen]], assisted by a knight, the [[Lone Wanderer]], would enter the base. Once inside they killed all Talon Company mercs in their path, including their leader [[Commander Jabsco]], effectively crippling the group. Following this, the Brotherhood would occupy Fort Bannister to use as an outpost.<ref name="fo3bs" />

Even prior to this incident, the Brotherhood was hostile towards Talon Company, with both groups sporadically clashing. This was due to their cruel reputation, their willingness to accept any contract was entirely motivated by profit,<ref>[[Galaxy News Radio (radio)#News and drama|Galaxy News Radio]]: ''"I've been getting more and more reports of these mercenary maniacs from Talon Company, especially in the downtown D.C. area. ===Relations with NCR===
{{Main|NCR-Brotherhood War}}

Prior to the late 2240s, the Brotherhood had moderately good relations with the quickly developing New California Republic, to the point that one of the provinces of the fledgling nation state was named after the founder of the Brotherhood: Maxson. It was also the location of the Brotherhood's subterranean headquarters, Lost Hills, but it was never incorporated into the NCR. The two factions were involved in trading and the sharing of some technologies, the Brotherhood even possessed an outpost in the NCR's capital of [[Shady Sands]].<ref>[[NCR Brotherhood outpost]]</ref><ref group="Non-game">Question: ''"How was the Brotherhood of Steel involved with NCR after the destruction of the Enclave?"''<br /> [[Chris Avellone]]: ''"Unknown. Presumably, they'd already established some level of co-existence with NCR even before the events of F1, judging by one of the states of NCR being dubbed 'Maxson' (more on that in a future update except to say that the Lost Hills Bunker was NOT turned into a town in NCR) and considering their pre-existing ties to the Hub, which became a state by the time of F2. I've always imagined that NCR and BOS have maintained an uneasy truce, with barter and (some) technology sharing between the two groups."''<br /> [[Chris Avellone]] Q&A</ref> These relations would eventually sour due to the introduction of reactionary policies by the Brotherhood in the 2250s. They sought to limit and control the access to dangerous technologies within the region. This brought them into open conflict with the NCR, from which a bloody struggle ensued for decades. Despite past differences and the [[NCR-Brotherhood War|current conflict]], the Brotherhood of Steel has shown itself to be willing to work with other parties or even rivals to achieve a common or crucial goal. Such as the cooperation with the NCR to ensure the defeat of the [[Enclave]] following the destruction of the [[Control station Enclave]]. During the height of the [[NCR-Legion War]], the Brotherhood of Steel Mojave chapter displayed a willingness to sign a temporary truce with the NCR, as their ambitions in the Mojave were not at "crossed purposes."<ref name="ftr2">[[For the Republic, Part 2]]</ref>
[[File:FOTV Official Trailer Still 085.png|thumb|NCR and Brotherhood forces clash outside of [[Griffith Observatory]]]] 

If negotiations proceed, the Mojave chapter will send a representative, [[Todd (Fallout: New Vegas)|Paladin Todd]], to discuss the terms of the truce with the NCR's [[Cassandra Moore|Colonel Moore]]. The Brotherhood's truce came with a condition, Paladin Todd requested that the NCR return the power armor used by [[NCR heavy trooper]]s, which had been salvaged from their conflict together. In exchange for the return of their property, the Brotherhood would sign a temporary truce with the NCR in the Mojave.<ref>[[Todd (Fallout: New Vegas)|Paladin Todd]]: ''"This is unacceptable, we demand the immediate return of our power armor."''<br />[[Cassandra Moore|Colonel Moore]]: ''"A battle is about to be fought out there, to come here requesting our men to give up their armor is insane."''<br />Paladin Todd ''"It is not your armor, that power armor is the property of the Brotherhood of Steel and you have no right to claim it as your own."''<br />([[VMSHDBrotherhoodPaladin.txt|Paladin Todd]] and [[VHDColonelMoore.txt|Colonel Moore's dialogue]])</ref> They would also provide the NCR with combat support by sending some armored units to the [[Second Battle of Hoover Dam]].<ref name="ftr2" /> The Mojave chapter was willing to do this despite continued hostilities between the two in the west. As per their agreement, the NCR handed over all suits of salvaged power armor and in return the Brotherhood helped patrol [[Interstate 15]] and [[Highway 95]].<ref>[[Fallout: New Vegas endings|''Fallout: New Vegas'' endings]] (Slide 7: Brotherhood of Steel)</ref> 

By 2296, hostilities between the Brotherhood and NCR remnants continued despite the [[Fall of Shady Sands]]. The search for the cold fusion reactor had culminated in the [[Battle of Griffith Observatory]], in which Brotherhood forces from the Knights of San Fernando would emerge victorious.
