'''Henry's letter''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.
__TOC__
==Location==
This note is found in [[Camp Venture]], on a locker shelf in the main barracks.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=Dear [[Sarah (Fallout 76)|Sarah]],

I'm starting to think coming out to [[Camp Venture]] was a huge mistake. These people are totally nuts. Did you know they call themselves [[Brotherhood of Steel ranks|"Knights" and "Paladins"]]? What the heck is going on around here?

I thought if I joined the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Brotherhood of Steel]], I could learn to protect you and the kids, but all they want to do is create soldiers who are ready to die in battle. Maybe I should just quit and come home. Write me soon, I need to hear from you.

[[Henry (Brotherhood)|Henry]]
}}
