The '''Brotherhood of Steel''' (commonly abbreviated to '''BoS''') is a post-[[Great War|War]] quasi-[[religion|religious]] technocratic paramilitary order, founded in the immediate aftermath of the Great War by members of the [[United States Armed Forces]] and the [[United States government|government]]-sponsored scientific community. Originating in California, the organization has numerous extant chapters throughout the former continental [[United States of America|United States]], becoming the dominant force in many of these regions by the [[Timeline (2250-2300)#2290|2290s]].

The Brotherhood's original purpose was to preserve advanced technology and regulate its usage.<ref>The [[Chosen One]]: ''"{115}{}{Who are you guys?}"''<br />[[Matthew (Fallout 2)|Matthew]]: ''"{119}{}{We are a paramilitary organization known as the Brotherhood of Steel.}"''<br />([[FCFMATT.MSG|Matthew's dialogue]])</ref><ref>The [[Chosen One]]: ''"{201}{}{What do you know about the Brotherhood?}"''<br />[[ACE]]: ''"{221}{}{The Brotherhood of Steel is a paramilitary organization dedicated to the salvation of mankind through the proper use of technology. All other information is classified.}"''<br />([[Fsface.msg|ACE's dialogue]])</ref> This is because they believe humanity cannot be trusted with the means to destroy itself, and they acquire technology to prevent another apocalypse.<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Are you recruiting new Initiates?"''<br />[[Daniel Shin]]: ''"If we accept you as an Initiate - and that is far from certain - you would be pledging yourself to our order and our cause. Our order exists to locate, secure, and protect dangerous technologies. Humanity cannot be trusted with the means to destroy itself. We will not allow another apocalypse.''<br />([[Daniel Shin/Dialogue|Daniel Shin's dialogue]])</ref> Although they tend to be relatively isolationist, the Brotherhood has proved to be one of the most important organizations in the history of the wasteland, though their power, influence, and doctrine vary heavily from chapter to chapter.

The Brotherhood has been featured in every game and other entry in the [[Fallout series|''Fallout'' series]], in one form or another. This article focuses on an overview of the Brotherhood as it appears throughout the series, detailing the evolution of its doctrine and policies.

For information on specific Brotherhood chapters, see [[Brotherhood of Steel chapters]].
