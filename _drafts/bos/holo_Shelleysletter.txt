'''Shelley's letter''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.
__TOC__
==Location==
The note can be found at [[Camp Venture]], on the counter of the building at the weapons training range.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
[[Brant]],

Remember how crazy you said I was to join up with the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)|Brotherhood of Steel]] at [[Camp Venture]]? How you said I wouldn't be able to hack it, that I'd quit within a week? Well I'm writing to tell you that you were dead wrong. This is where I belong, side-by-side with people who give a crap about this world. [[Ted Wilson|Senior Knight Wilson]] treats me like I'm important, like I matter. Something you never did. I've found my calling, and I'm not coming home. The Brotherhood is my new family now. Now who's laughing?

[[Shelley (Fallout 76)|Shelley]]
}}
