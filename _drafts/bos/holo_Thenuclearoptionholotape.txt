'''The Nuclear Option''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] in ''[[Fallout 76]]''.

==Location==
The tape can be found in [[Fort Defiance]], on the fourth floor, on a nightstand in [[Elizabeth Taggerdy|Paladin Taggerdy's]] room.
