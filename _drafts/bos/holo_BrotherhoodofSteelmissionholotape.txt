The '''Brotherhood of Steel mission holotapes''' are [[Fallout: New Vegas holotapes|holotape]] {{In|FNV}}. They can be found on the corpses of a number of dead Brotherhood of Steel paladins scattered across the wastes. They can be collected at any time, but they are required to be found for the [[Fallout: New Vegas quests|quest]] [[Still in the Dark]].

==Transcripts==
===Mission statement XV-56===
The holotape can be found on [[Matiz|Paladin Matiz]], a dead Brotherhood of Steel paladin on the [[REPCONN headquarters]]' 3rd floor.
{{Transcript|text=
Mission Code: XV-56
Temporary Password: Lives to fight another day

Summary: Paladins [[Matiz]] and [[Lander]], you are to survey the [[REPCONN headquarters|ruins]] at coordinates 0.004, 9.265, search for the components we need, and retrieve what you can. As a secondary objective, compile a list of additional items at the site for future retrieval.

[[Nolan McNamara|McNamara]]
}}

===Mission statement KH-792===
This holotape can be found on the body of Brotherhood of Steel Paladin [[Raseleanne]], outside of [[Nellis Air Force Base]]'s main entry near some ruined houses.
{{Transcript|text=
Mission Code: KH-792
Temporary Password: And to dare to do it

Summary: [[Raseleanne|Paladin Raseleanne]], you and an escort are to investigate the [[Nellis Air Force Base|military base]] to the northeast for the components we need. We've had reports that the current occupants are fiercely territorial, but it's highly unlikely they have any weapons that can seriously threaten someone in full power armor. The threat level is considered minimal.

McNamara
}}

===Mission statement CB-03===
This transcript can be found on the body of [[Fairbanks|Paladin Fairbanks]], in an irradiated crater near [[Hidden Valley]]. Head right after exiting the bunker to a hole in the fence and follow the trail. A unique [[Centaur (Fallout: New Vegas)|centaur]] named [[Moe]] and additional centaurs are in and around the crater.
{{Transcript|text=
Mission Code: CB-03
Temporary Password: The better part of valor

Summary: Paladins [[Hughes (Fallout: New Vegas)|Hughes]] and [[Fairbanks]], I need you two to head north to the nearby [[Broadcast building|communications array]] and look for the components mentioned in the briefing. The mutants there have been reasonable in the past and may prove helpful.

McNamara
}}
