being "cured" and no longer being a super mutant. The [[Sole Survivor]] must assure him that he will not be forcibly cured. 
* Due to the encompassing range of his dislikes, Strong can be a difficult companion to garner admiration from as most of the dislike-triggering actions are sometimes hard to avoid when exploring ruins or negotiating with NPCs. On the other hand, since he likes killing things (and as all companions, time spent with the Sole Survivor), he is a good companion for exploring unmapped areas as long as one stays on the outside of places and/or avoids picking too many locks.
** A good way to boost Strong's affinity is to purchase guard dogs from the friendly Super Mutant [[Erickson]] on [[the Island]]. While Strong will dislike the meeting, if present, the dog or wolf the Sole Survivor purchases from Erickson for 250 caps will garner a Like trigger from Strong; purchasing the Mutant Hound will garner a Love trigger from Strong. However, he does not seem to Like or Love buying subsequent dogs, wolves or mutant hounds from Erickson, though.
** Another easy way to gain Strong's admiration is to pick the [[Cannibal (Fallout 4)|Cannibal]] perk. When performing quests that involve clearing out large numbers of raiders from a location, one must attempt to eat the various corpses. Saving and loading between feedings will allow one to bypass the waiting period between Like triggers.
** Defeating powerful enemies seems to gain a lot of respect from Strong.
** Progressing [[Duty or Dishonor]] has an opportunity to gain four likes in a row for Strong during the final encounter with [[D. Clarke|Clarke]].
** Strong gains a large amount of affinity from completing [[Taking Independence]], rewarded when [[Radio Freedom]] is powered up.
** Despite saying that someday all humans will be destroyed, Strong likes it when the Sole Survivor helps settlers, either independently or for the Minutemen. This is one of the most effective ways to get his approval as it is the only act of charity that he tolerates. He also likes choosing the "cocky" dialogue choice.
* Strong will often voice disapproval of the player using crafting stations or picking up junk items, saying that they are wasting time tinkering and the items they take are useless, but these actions do not have any actual effect on his affinity.
* Strong will occasionally voice his approval of the Brotherhood of Steel after joining, saying that the "metal men" are "worthy" even though he dislikes the Sole Survivor entering [[Power armor (Fallout 4)|power armor]] and will cause a Hate trigger if he witnesses the Sole Survivor joining them.
* During [[Arthur Maxson]]'s speech on [[the Prydwen]], Strong will refuse to enter the room and remain outside, not entering until it is over.
* Strong will voice approval if the Sole Survivor sides with [[the Institute]] and destroys the Brotherhood, stating that the super mutants can easily beat the Institute.
* Strong seems to like entering the set of [[T-45 power armor (Fallout 4)|T-45 power armor]] on the roof of the [[Museum of Freedom]] during [[When Freedom Calls]], but will disapprove of all others, saying "human look like robot."
* Strong can sometimes be seen interacting with the large cooking pots present in some super mutant camps (unusable to the player character), stirring the pot and tasting its contents.
* Although Strong refuses to actually enter a [[pillory]] if assigned to one, the player character will still get the trophy/achievement [[Time Out]] for the act of assigning him to it.
* Compared to all other companions, with a value of 24, Strong has the highest [[Strength (Fallout 4)|Strength]] attribute level.
* Strong often pronounces names that start with "Mac" as "Mack." For example, he calls Robert MacCready "Mack Ree Dea" and refers to the titular character of ''Macbeth'' as "Mack Beth."
* In ''[[Fallout: Wasteland Warfare]]'', Strong is part of the Super Mutants faction despite being released in a Survivors faction set.

==Behind the scenes==
* Strong is voiced by [[Sean Schemmel]], best known as the English teen and adult voice of [[w:c:dragonball:Goku|Goku]] in the ''[[wikipedia:Dragon Ball|Dragon Ball]]'' franchise. Schemmel also voiced male [[ghoul]]s and [[Marc Wilson]] in ''Fallout 4''.
* In localized versions of the game, Strong's voice is dubbed by Juan Carlos Lozano (Spanish), Kazumasa Katsura (Japanese) and Pietro Ubaldi (Italian).
