'''Strong''' is an [[Super mutant (Fallout 4)|Institute super mutant]] residing in [[Trinity Tower]] in [[the Commonwealth]] in [[Timeline#2287|2287]], thrown in a cell with wasteland poet [[Rex Goodman]] because he wants to obtain the power of the "milk of human kindness" from [[human]]s. He appears {{In|FO4}} as a possible [[Fallout 4 companions|companion]] of the [[Sole Survivor]].

==Background==
Like every other super mutant found in the Commonwealth, Strong was created by [[the Institute]] through their experiments with a modified strain of the [[Forced Evolutionary Virus]], though any specifics related to Strong's past humanity are unknown. By [[Timeline#2287|2287]], he is a member of a warband led by the mutant named [[Fist (super mutant)|Fist]] inhabiting [[Trinity Tower]] in the [[Back Bay]] neighborhood of [[Boston]]. At least one of his exploits during this time involved entering [[Fens Street sewer|the sewers]] under [[the Fens]] near [[Diamond City]] with his brothers, where they encountered, attacked, and [[Cannibalism|ate]] a group of humans.<ref>Strong: ''"Strong come here with brothers once. Ate a group of humans. Long time ago. No one human knows."''<br />([[CompanionStrong.txt|Strong's dialogue]])<br />{{small|'''Note:''' This dialogue only occurs if Strong is brought as an active companion when entering the [[Fens street sewer]].}}</ref>

Sometime before the Sole Survivor woke up in [[Vault 111]] on October 23, 2287, Trinity Tower was visited by radio host and aspiring poet [[Rex Goodman]] from the [[WRVR broadcast station]], who was attempting to reintroduce the super mutants to human culture by reading them the works of [[William Shakespeare|Shakespeare]], specifically ''Macbeth''. This inadvertently led to Strong becoming fascinated with the "milk of human kindness" from a line in Act 1, Scene 5 of the play. However, rather than its intended metaphorical reference, the super mutant took it quite literally and thought that it was referring to "milk" as a physical liquid that he could find and drink which would give super mutants the "secret power" of humans.<ref>[[Rex Goodman]]: ''"I came here to teach these poor, ignorant creatures culture. I started with Macbeth. I guess they found it funny. All of them except Strong here."''<br />Strong: ''"Strong learn secret to human power from Mack Beth. Milk of human kindness. Strong will find milk. Strong will drink milk. Strong will have secret power of humans."''<br />([[RexGoodman.txt|Rex Goodman's dialogue]])</ref>

When Strong began voicing his intentions to find the "milk of human kindness," his super mutant brothers grew annoyed with his appeals to the words of a human, and imprisoned him along with Rex in a cell at the top of the Tower. When asked about him, Rex states that the other mutants were planning to toss Strong off the tower, even taking bets on "how many times he would bounce."<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"What are the super mutants going to do with Strong?"''<br />[[Rex Goodman]]: ''"Throw him off the building. They're taking bets on how many times he'll bounce."''<br />([[RexGoodman.txt|Rex Goodman's dialogue]])</ref>

==Personality==
At a glance, Strong seems to fit the archetype of a typical Commonwealth super mutant: unintelligent, naturally violent, and carrying a lust for battle at all times. The major trait that sets him apart from most of his brethren is an ability to think critically: whereas most of his brethren dismiss humans as being physically weaker and thus inferior, Strong has come to notice that despite their physical weakness, humans have consistently managed to pose a serious challenge to super mutants and other respective dangers of the wasteland. Instead of acknowledging the two races have different strengths, however, he initially determined that if super mutants could discover the secret to humankind's ability to survive, then they could truly dominate the wasteland. This, combined with the above-mentioned misinterpretation of Rex Goodman's delivery of William Shakespeare, is the origin of Strong's drive to find the "milk of human kindness."

Through extended travels with the Sole Survivor, Strong may slowly realize that humanity's strength comes from its ability to come together to face threats, while super mutants tend to be divided and operate only on local levels. As a result, he will hope to find a way to instill this quality into super mutants, most likely through force. Despite his assurance of humans having a "secret power" his kind does not, Strong believes that there is much about super mutant society that makes them more worthy to inhabit the world, such as that every super mutant is a "brother" to the rest,<ref>Strong: ''"Human help brother. Super mutants help brothers."''<br>([[CompanionStrong.txt|Strong's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Strong: ''"Strong will bring brothers here one day. Make good home."''<br>([[CompanionStrong.txt|Strong's dialogue]])</ref> resources are shared equally between brothers,<ref>Strong: ''"Give what is needed. That Super mutant way. Human to human. Super mutant to super mutant."''<br>([[CompanionStrong.txt|Strong's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Strong: ''"Why keep and not share? Strong share with brothers. Make all super mutants stronger."''<br>([[CompanionStrong.txt|Strong's dialogue]])</ref> and when conflicts occur between brothers, they are dealt with straightforwardly through violence instead of what he sees as humanity's constant, boring need to talk about their problems.<ref>Strong: ''"Too much talk. Time to kill."''<br>([[CompanionStrong.txt|Strong's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Strong: ''"Walk and talk boring. Human should fight more."''<br>([[CompanionStrong.txt|Strong's dialogue]])</ref>

In terms of the major powers in the Commonwealth, Strong applies the usual super mutant view: respect determined through strength, both of the individual and the group.<ref>Strong: ''"{{tooltip|This human good fighter. Kill many brothers.|Stern}} Someday Strong smash."''<br>([[CompanionStrong.txt#94|Strong's dialogue]])<br>{{small|'''Note:''' This dialogue only occurs when swapping out Strong for [[Danse|Paladin Danse]] as the new active [[Fallout 4 companions|companion]].}}</ref><ref>Strong: ''"{{tooltip|Ghoul good fighter. Strong approve.|Concerned}}"''<br>([[CompanionStrong.txt#97|Strong's dialogue]])<br>{{small|'''Note:''' This dialogue only occurs when swapping out Strong for [[John Hancock|Hancock]] as the new active [[Fallout 4 companions|companion]].}}</ref> He respects the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 4)|Brotherhood of Steel]] only as far as they have proven to be a serious threat to super mutants that he would like to fight and possibly destroy.<ref>Strong: ''"Steel brotherhood! Strong hates brotherhood."''<br>([[CompanionStrong.txt#46|Strong's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Strong: ''"{{tooltip|Brotherhood! Strong fight! Strong kill all brotherhood!|Battle frenzy}}"''<br>([[CompanionStrong.txt#1353|Strong's dialogue]])</ref> Conversely, he does not hold much favor for either [[the Railroad]] or [[the Institute]] due to their more subversive methods of "sneaky-killing"<ref>Strong: ''"No sneaky killing. Humans should not kill humans that way."''<br>([[CompanionStrong.txt#1461|Strong's dialogue]])</ref> and association with the "fake men" of [[synth]]kind.<ref>Strong: ''"{{tooltip|Crazy humans. ==Interactions with the player character==
Strong is first encountered when the Sole Survivor tries to rescue Rex Goodman from Trinity Tower during [[Curtain Call]]. There, Strong has been caged alongside Rex, and aids them in defeating Fist and escaping from the tower in order to find the "milk of human kindness." Afterwards, Strong will wait at the bottom of Trinity Tower until hired as a companion.

===Companion===
* Strong is proficient with heavy weapons like [[Missile launcher (Fallout 4)|missile launcher]]s and [[Minigun (Fallout 4)|miniguns]], and melee weapons like [[Sledgehammer (Fallout 4)|sledgehammers]] and [[Super sledge (Fallout 4)|super sledges]].
* Strong will never crouch down even when the player character enters [[Sneak (Fallout 4)|Sneak]] mode themselves, meaning he is a poor choice of a companion when trying to be stealthy.
* Strong is limited to wearing [[Fallout 4 armor and clothing#Super mutant apparel|super mutant armor]], found in various super mutant strongholds throughout the Commonwealth (including several pieces found spread around Trinity Tower during Curtain Call).

===Effects of player's actions===
* Strong becomes available as a companion after completing [[Curtain Call]] by returning to the lobby of Trinity Tower along with Rex Goodman.
* As his affinity increases, Strong will admit that he has learned to respect the Sole Survivor.
* Upon reaching maximum [[affinity]] with Strong, he will say that he has made the decision to make the Sole Survivor his leader, granting the [[Berserk]] perk which offers a temporary 20% increase to [[Melee Damage]] when the player character's [[Hit Points|HP]] drops below 25% of their current total.
* Strong cannot be romanced.

===Other interactions===
* Strong will not comment if brought to [[Nate]]/[[Nora]]'s corpse in [[Vault 111]].
