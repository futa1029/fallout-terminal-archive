'''Max Posey''' was a student at [[Vault-Tec University]] that pledged himself to the [[Pi Kappa Mu]] fraternity.

==Background==
Max and his [[Pledge|friend]] were locked in the laundry room of the [[Pi house]] during a party as a final initiation test for the fraternity. The two were given [[Nukashine]] - Max drank his cup, while his friend dumped it out. Both sets of remains are found in the basement.<ref>[[Final initiation]]</ref>

==Appearances==
Max Posey's skeleton appears only in ''[[Fallout 76]]'', introduced in the ''[[Wild Appalachia]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]].
