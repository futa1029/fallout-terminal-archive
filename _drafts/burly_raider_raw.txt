The '''burly raider'''<ref name="audio description">{{FOTV|1|1}} audio description</ref> is a member of the [[Vault 32 raider|raider group]] that attacks [[Vault 33]] {{In|FOTV}}.

==Background==
A tall, shaven, and strong wastelander that was recruited along with the rest of the raiders by [[Lee Moldaver]] to infiltrate the [[Three Vaults]], specifically [[Vault 33]], by posing as residents of [[Vault 32]] and entering during their Triennial Trade ceremony. 

==''Fallout'' TV series==
==="[[The End (episode)|The End]]"===
At [[Lucy MacLean|Lucy]] and [[Monty (TV series)|Monty's]] wedding reception, he is seen seated next to [[Davey (TV series)|Davey]] and the [[elderly speaker]].

When Lucy enters [[Vault 33]]'s crop field during the raiders' attack, she sees the burly raider take a puff from an inhaler drug before fighting several [[Vault 33 dweller]]s with his bare hands. He passes next to the crouching [[Norm MacLean|Norm]], who remained undetected by the raider.

He is next seen carrying a captive armored [[Vault 33 guard]] to the inter-Vault door, where he is stopped by Vault 33 gatekeeper [[Chet (TV series)|Chet]]. The raider drops the guard on the floor, takes another hit from the inhaler drug, and lunges at Chet with a [[machete (TV series)|machete]]. However, the guard manages to grab his ankle, causing the burly raider to fall while at the same time the startled Chet accidentally releases the link between his [[Pip-Boy 3000 Mark V|Pip-Boy]] and the Vault door mechanism. This causes the door to close, killing the raider via bisecting him at the waist and splattering Chet in blood.
