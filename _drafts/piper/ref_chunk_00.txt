'''Piper Wright''' is a resident of [[Diamond City]] in [[the Commonwealth]] in [[Timeline#2287|2287]], delivering the news of current events as the owner, publisher, and sole journalist of the ''[[Publick Occurrences]]'' newspaper. She appears {{In|FO4}} as a possible [[Fallout 4 companions|companion]] of the [[Sole Survivor]].

==Background==
===Early life===
Born in a [[Wright hometown|small settlement]] located "way out" in the Commonwealth with her younger sister [[Nat Wright|Nat]] and their father, who served as a member of the local militia, Piper's "career" in journalism started when her father was found dead under suspicious circumstances. When she asked the militia captain, [[Mayburn]] to investigate her father's death, his dismissive attitude caused her to feel like he was toying with her. After fruitless attempts to get the town mayor to look into Mayburn, Piper took the matter into her own hands. Using a [[Stealth Boy (Fallout 4)|Stealth Boy]] from her father's weapon stash, she snuck into Mayburn's office undetected and uncovered the evidence she was looking for: [[Raider (Fallout 4)|raiders]] were plotting to sack the town, and Mayburn was in league with them. ===Diamond City reporter===
With their parents gone and Nat too young to work, Piper relied mainly on kindness and charity to survive. Eventually, the sisters saved enough money to relocate to Diamond City by paying for passage on a caravan.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"What happened after that?"''<br />Piper Wright: ''"{{Tooltip|We made due. Sis was still pretty young at the time and mom was... out of the picture, so we got by on the kindness of others for a while. Eventually I saved up enough to book us both passage with a caravan and then we moved on up to the big city. Called it home ever since.|Neutral}}"''<br />([[CompanionPiper.txt/COM|Piper Wright's dialogue]])</ref> However, life in the "big city" provided its own share of challenges; at the time of their arrival, Diamond City was plagued by corruption, lax security, and failing infrastructure (such as a gaping hole in the old stadium's protective walls that was "patched" by a bookcase furniture piece). Frustrated with this state of affairs and goaded by a desire to enact change, Piper founded and ran her own newspaper ''[[Publick Occurrences]]'' to raise awareness of the city’s problems.<ref name="FoundingPublick">Piper Wright: ''"{{Tooltip|I just wanted to right the things I thought were wrong. And when Nat and I first got to Diamond City, there was a lot of wrong.|Neutral}} {{Tooltip|Crooked guards, lousy infrastructure. Heh. There was a hole in the exterior wall that was patched over with a bookcase. One bookcase. That's it.|Amused}} {{Tooltip|I started the paper more as an act of desperation than anything else. It turned out, I wasn't the only one who wanted things to change.|Neutral}} {{Tooltip|After the first couple of editions, people may not have agreed with what I said, but everyone was listening.|Defiant}}"''<br />([[CompanionPiper.txt/COM|Piper Wright's dialogue]])</ref>

Despite her noble intentions, her career was fraught with danger and intrigue from the outset. Shortly after founding her newspaper, an article she wrote exposing the efforts of a caravan cartel to drive up food prices made her the target of their retribution. The cartel secretly arranged for an assassin to disguise as the bartender at the [[Dugout Inn]] and serve Piper a poisoned drink; once she realized, she immediately hopped the counter and chugged [[Vadim Bobrov]]'s [[Bobrov's Best moonshine|moonshine]] straight from the still to induce vomiting and remove the poison.
