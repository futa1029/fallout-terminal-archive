==Behind the scenes==
[[File:FO4 Piper Art Book.jpg|thumb|250px|right|From ''[[The Art of Fallout 4]]'']]
* The [[Form ID]] for Piper's companion affinity quest is {{ID|000BBD96}}.
* Designers who contributed to the writing of Piper's character included [[William Shen]], [[Liam Collins]] and [[Emil Pagliarulo]], who also worked on writing for [[Nick Valentine]] and [[Diamond City]] as a whole.<ref group="Non-game">[https://www.youtube.com/live/PsOjwbwA-X4?feature=shared&t=655 Design Leadership with William Shen - Indie Game Lunch Hour (10:55)]</ref>
* Piper's appearance was visualized by artist [[Ray Lederer]]. He stated that the green scarf he included in her design, in his own mental backstory, was knitted for Piper by [[Mama Murphy]],<ref group="Non-game">[https://www.facebook.com/rayledererart/photos/a.2604572796260577/2622951747756015/ Ray Lederer Art on Facebook]</ref> though in the final game, the two characters have no notable interactions together.
* Piper is voiced by [[Courtney Ford]], who worked on the voiceover for the character for over two years.<ref group="Non-game">[https://archive.ph/tvVhb Courtney Ford on Twitter]: ''"I worked on this game for over 2 yrs, and to see #Piper received w/ so much positivity has been overwhelming in the best of ways:) ❤️ 2/2"''</ref> Ford had previously played the nosy and unethical reporter Christine Hill in the fourth season of the American crime television series [[wikipedia:Dexter (TV series)|''Dexter'']] (2006-2013). Piper's characterization is largely similar to Christine's from the show.
** In localized versions of the game, Piper's voice is dubbed by Danai Querol (Spanish), Saori Seto (Japanese) and Chiara Francese (Italian).
* In the Creation Kit, the [[road goggles]] have the editor ID "ClothesPiperGoggles." Concept art of Piper depicts her wearing them pushed up on her forehead.
* In her concept art, it appears Piper would have had shorter hair. She also would have worn a fedora rather than a flat cap. One other concept design shows her with what appears to be an aviator's cap and camera equipment worn around the neck.
[[File:FO76 Not Piper Wright.png|thumb|250px|A Piper lookalike in ''Fallout 76'']]
* In ''[[Fallout 76]]'', players can purchase [[Piper's press cap]], [[Piper's road goggles]] and [[Piper's trench coat]] as apparel in the [[Atomic Shop]].
* Piper's interactions with Mayor McDonough seem to be inspired by those of [[wikipedia:Bob Woodward|Bob Woodward]] and [[wikipedia:Carl Bernstein|Carl Bernstein]] who famously reported on the [[wikipedia:Watergate scandal|Watergate scandal]] which eventually led to the resignation of [[wikipedia:Richard Nixon|Richard Nixon]] as US President. This is supported by Mayor McDonough's "I Am Not a Synth" speech, which paraphrases Nixon's infamous "I Am Not a Crook" speech.
* Piper's name may have been chosen as a pun on a Boston pronunciation of the word "Newspaper" (news-piper).

==Bugs==
{{Notable content
| {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} Piper will not move even if she is standing, near an enemy, shot at or interacted with at all. She can still be spoken to. She may be locked in a single position and just slide around. Fast traveling fixes this.{{Verify|verified}}
| {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} Piper cannot be romanced after idolizing the player character (max affinity), even if their Charisma is maxed. The key/button to select is there, but the option to romance is blank. {{Verify|verified}}
| {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} Piper will mysteriously disappear from the game completely. However, starting the quest [[In Sheep's Clothing]] will seemingly fix this.{{Verify|verified}} Also, she may reappear at the end of the quest [[Reunions]], trying to get an interview from Nick Valentine.{{Verify|verified}}
| {{Platforms|ps4,xboxone}} Piper will randomly dislike something out of nowhere, such as fast traveling, killing hostile enemies, picking unowned locks, and simply exploring. It is quite rare, but frequency increases after fast traveling to an area populated with enemies, and especially when the player character is in power armor (specifically the X-01 power armor), although these messages take an unusually long time to appear.{{Verify|verified}}
| {{Platforms|pc,ps4}} Sometimes when heavily injured, Piper will remain in the downed position indefinitely. She can still be spoken to, and will teleport with the Sole Survivor when traveling, via a loading zone or fast travel, but will not get up or walk. (Possibly caused by fall damage outside of combat, encountered at the [[Garden Terrace]]) Depleting her health (tested using the PC Kill command) then stimpaking her seems to fix the problem. Alternatively, loading a save prior to her injury solves this.{{Verify|Verified}}
| {{Platforms|pc,ps4}} If Piper is your romantic partner and is your companion and is wearing power armor when you are sleeping, her power armor sometimes has disappeared when you wake up. This seems to happen when you sleep in a narrow location where there is not enough space for her to step out of the armor. If you make her leave the armor before you sleep, the problem will not occur. {{Verify|verified}}
| {{Platforms|pc,ps4,xboxone}} If Piper is not made a romantic partner prior to the finishing conversation with her and [[Nick Valentine]] at the end of [[Reunions]], she sometimes cannot become a romantic partner because the conversation assumes you have not already met her. It is best to make her a romantic partner before starting [[Reunions]].{{Verify|verified}}
