'''Wilson Brother's Auto Repair''' is a [[Fallout 76 locations|location]] in [[the Forest]] region of [[Appalachia]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Background==
This building is a small pre-[[Great War|War]] auto shop once owned by [[Rich Wilson]], situated on the outskirts of [[Morgantown]].<ref group="Non-game">''[[Fallout 76 Vault Dweller's Survival Guide]]'' p.305: ''"'''30. WILSON BROTHER'S AUTO REPAIR'''''<br />''This small repair shop consists of three separate structures: a white house with a yellow door (and a basement safe), a garage and workshop area with workbenches, and a red house on the upper rocky ground. This house's cellar shows evidence of Mothman Cultist activity!"''<br />([[Fallout 76 Vault Dweller's Survival Guide Atlas of Appalachia]])</ref>

==Layout==
The location consists of two main buildings, the auto garage off the main road and a house behind it, on the upper portion of the hill. The garage itself contains both a [[Power armor station (Fallout 76)|power armor station]] and an [[Armor workbench (Fallout 76)|armor workbench]] in the main garage area. There is also a locked [[First aid (Fallout 76)|first aid]] pack on the wall right before the office of the shop.

Another partially destroyed building is located nearby, which contains a locked safe in the basement. Upstairs, jumping from the bed's headboard allows access to an attic containing a steamer trunk, a duffle bag, and an ammo box.

===Wilson home===
Up the hill behind the auto shop is the home of [[Rich Wilson]].<ref name=":0">[[Wilson's Bros. garage]]</ref> A Mothman poster is displayed prominently in the home, which Rich received as a gift from [[Jeff Lane]].<ref name=":0" /> There are many candles throughout the home, most of them stocked on shelves, unused.

The basement of the house contains a [[Cult of the Mothman|cult]] shrine, constructed from roots and human remains, with a sacrificed [[Mole rat (Fallout 76)|mole rat]] laying at its base. A white armchair faces the shrine, across the room. An IV stand by the chair indicates that whoever once sat there was receiving intravenous infusions of some sort.

In the other corner of the basement, there is a casket beneath the stairway. Two [[Empty blood pack (Fallout 76)|empty blood packs]] sit on a shelf nearby. There are also two buckets here.

An attic can be reached through a hole in the ceiling via jetpack, but the space is barren.

==Notable loot==
* [[Giant Teapot advertisement]] - Note, found on a metal shelf beside a broken water dispenser in the back room of the garage building.
* [[Fallout 76 keys#Bathroom key|Bathroom key]] - Found on a metal desk beside the [[Mothman Museum]] poster in the house. Opens the bathroom at the museum.
