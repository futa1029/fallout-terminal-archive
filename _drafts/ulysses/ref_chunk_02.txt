===The Big Empty===
Learning of the Courier's survival evidently tempted Ulysses back to his obsession with the Divide and America. Recognizing that the Divide's irregular weather patterns were manmade, he decided to track them to their source, inadvertently finding his way into [[Big MT]]. Along the way, he left behind color-coded markers in the shape of the pre-War U.S. flag, knowing that someone with the know-how and grit to survive would understand them, someone like the Courier. When he eventually entered the bounds of the crater, Ulysses discovered the Old World research facilities, and the [[X-17 meteorological station]] in particular, still active.<ref>[[Ulysses' logs#Ulysses log: Y-17.16|Ulysses log: Y-17.16]]</ref>

While looking for answers in the Big Empty, Ulysses was caught up in the conflict between the [[Circle of Steel]] assassin [[Christine Royce]] and her target, the rogue Brotherhood [[Brotherhood of Steel ranks#Elder|elder]] [[Elijah]]. At some point after Elijah spoke with the [[Think Tank (faction)|Think Tank]], he and Ulysses made contact. Hearing of Elijah's plans to get revenge against the NCR for the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Mojave chapter)|Mojave Brotherhood]]'s loss at [[HELIOS One]], Ulysses chose to point the rogue elder in the direction of the [[Sierra Madre]], considering it a place he would never go himself while knowing that it would in all likelihood end up as Elijah's grave.<ref>[[Torn journal page (Old World Blues)|Torn journal page]]</ref> Once Elijah escaped the crater, Ulysses located and rescued Christine from the [[Y-17 medical facility]] and nursed her back to health in a [[Ulysses' Point|nearby cave]].<ref>[[Patient log: Y-17#Patient log: Y-17.0|Patient log: Y-17.0]]</ref><ref name="y-17.5">[[Patient log: Y-17#Patient log: Y-17.5|Patient log: Y-17.5]]</ref> From Christine, he learned more about the Brotherhood of Steel, enough to decide they were not the ones with the power to reawaken America as he envisioned. In payment for his aid, Christine gave him an old recorder she had managed to repair, which he used to record personal logs detailing his philosophies and important views.<ref name="y-17.15">[[Ulysses' logs#Ulysses log: Y-17.15|Ulysses log: Y-17.15]]</ref>

In the middle of Christine's recovery, Ulysses decided to seek out the "voices" of the Big Empty, still believing that something more remained of the Old World and wanting to "hear history give its answer."<ref name="y-17.5" /> He entered the Dome at the heart of Big MT and confronted the Think Tank, including [[Klein (Old World Blues)|Doctor Klein]], who remembers him as "a melancholy fellow who asked a lot about history" and had "an interesting name."<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"I heard one of the previous visitors to the Dome spoke to you, asked you some questions."''<br />[[Klein (Old World Blues)|Dr. Klein]]: ''"HMM? ===A reunion===
With his newfound knowledge and determination, Ulysses decided to return to the Divide and use its "sleeping" missiles to reshape the wasteland single-handedly, much like the Courier had reshaped the Divide. By the time he went to the Big Empty, Ulysses had realized that waiting for the Mojave to claim his enemy's life didn't and/or wouldn't work. Knowing nothing of the Courier's current direction since he'd long thought them dead and spent years trying to find them, Ulysses determined that this time, they would have to come to him.<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"Maybe you better tell me who you are, and what you want."''<br />Ulysses: ''"{{Tooltip|I'm a courier. {{Tooltip|Courier Six...|Beat}} was Courier Six. In late 2281, Ulysses broadcasted a simple message, intended as a lure and a challenge all in one: the coordinates for the [[canyon wreckage]] and [[Pass to canyon wreckage|the pass]] beyond it, ending with the words "Courier Six. -Ulysses." He anticipated the moment when they would heed his call, wanting the Courier to come and witness the destruction of their new home in his reshaping of America, just as they did for him with reshaping the Divide. He wanted to host the Courier's revelation personally; the message itself is not important, but the meaning is, and so it had to be delivered in a manner that would not dilute its significance by any means.<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"I got your final message."''<br />Ulysses: ''"{{Tooltip|Didn’t think I’d be breathing to hear you say that. {{Tooltip|Message|Beat, “Still...”}} isn’t important, meaning is.|Slightly amused}} Had to speak of it, in case the words got lost in paper, ink, or other's voices.
