dialogue?' It happened so late in development that that didn't seem practical. Yes, if we had realized that earlier in development then probably, yes."''</ref><ref group="Non-game">[https://forums.somethingawful.com/showthread.php?threadid=3439576&userid=17931&perpage=40&pagenumber=9 Joshua Sawyer on Something Awful forums]: ''"Ulysses was cut because his character VO literally would not fit on the disc. His dialogue node count was something like 2x or 3x higher than any other companion, including Cass, who had the second highest (hers was ~615 IIRC and I think Ulysses was... ~1500??). I genuinely consider "original" Ulysses being cut as a big loss for F:NV overall because Avellone's original critique was right: we needed a Legion-sympathetic companion."''</ref>
** According to producer [[Jason Bergman]], Bergman himself made the final call to cut Ulysses. In addition to the reasons outlined above, localization would also have been a major issue due to the sheer size of Ulysses' script, as written by Avellone. Although there was some progress made on voicing Ulysses, he was never fully implemented in-game during development before being cut. The concepts for the character ended up being recycled for ''Lonesome Road'', but Avellone's first script, as originally written, was not.<ref group="Non-game">[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_bB1TtL0d-E A New Vegas Wedding & Other Musings with Jason Bergman - F:NV The Cutting Room Floor]</ref>
*** Regarding the differences between the original script for Ulysses and his final appearance in ''Lonesome Road'', Bergman said: "Ulysses was pretty much completely rewritten for ''Lonesome Road''. Before he was a companion, which meant he had to follow the choices the player made. Now he has a set story (multiple outcomes, but a set story) so it's a very different character."<ref group="Non-game">[https://web.archive.org/web/20200218164147/http://forums.bethsoft.com/topic/1223886-wheres-wal-jason-bergman-thread-2/page-2 Jason Bergman on the Bethesda Forums (archived)]: ''"Ulysses was pretty much completely rewritten for Lonesome Road. Before he was a companion, which meant he had to follow the choices the player made. Now he has a set story (multiple outcomes, but a set story) so it's a very different character."''</ref>
** Ulysses was the only character intended to be a companion that ended up not being one.<ref group="Non-game">[https://youtu.be/0pLft_FQPFo?t=13251 ''Fallout: New Vegas'' 10th Anniversary Charity Stream (reference starts at 3:40:51)]<br />[[Joshua Sawyer]]: ''"Basically the only character that was meant to be a companion that didn't become a companion was Ulysses. That's it. Any other character that you can think of was never meant to be a companion. [laughter]"''</ref>
* Ulysses was going to be found at [[Wolfhorn Ranch]] when he was still a companion in the base game, before being cut.<ref group="Non-game">[https://youtu.be/ALRO9EKeoAU?si=XDfqXouRPOsbwEhK&t=4475 ''Fallout: New Vegas'' 10th Anniversary Charity Stream Part 2 (reference starts at 1:14:35)]<br />[[Joshua Sawyer]]: ''"Yeah, Wolfhorn Ranch was supposed to be where you found Ulysses when he was a companion."''<br />([[Resources:Fallout: New Vegas 10th Anniversary Charity Stream/Day 2 transcript|Transcript]])</ref> The ''[[Fallout: New Vegas Official Game Guide]]'' mentions that the ranch was formerly owned by Ulysses, and several language localizations of ''Fallout: New Vegas'' maintain a variation of Wolfhorn Ranch's original name, "Ulysses' Camp."<ref name="Guide" group="Non-game" />
** [[Chopper]] was added to Wolfhorn Ranch after Ulysses was cut, so that the location would not be so empty. Joshua Sawyer also denied that Chopper would have been used by Ulysses; it was merely added to the location to have something unique after Ulysses' removal.<ref group="Non-game">[https://youtu.be/ALRO9EKeoAU?si=4cj9M5z9EaYnTHjT&t=4830 ''Fallout: New Vegas'' 10th Anniversary Charity Stream Part 2 (reference starts at 1:20:30)]<br />[[Joshua Sawyer]]: ''"Chopper, no. Ulysses would not have used Chopper. I think that was just placed there... like there was- there wasn't much at Wolfhorn Ranch, and when Ulysses was pulled as companion... I think that's when I was like, 'Oh, we should put Chopper there so there's something.'"''<br />([[Resources:Fallout: New Vegas 10th Anniversary Charity Stream/Day 2 transcript|Transcript]])</ref>
** According to Chris Avellone, the grave located on the hill near the ranch was added as a reference to Ulysses being cut from the base game. In-universe, however, the grave belongs to someone unknown. The grave was added by level designer [[Sydney Wolfram]].<ref group="Non-game">[https://archive.is/Puwqi Chris Avellone on Twitter]: ''"Grave = The memory of Ulysses dev-wise, in the game itself, it's unknown (one of our level designers, Syd, put it there)."''</ref>
* Jason Bergman confirmed that [[Roger Cross]] was always the voice of Ulysses, and had been cast in the role prior to Ulysses being cut from the game. Only the first recording session for Ulysses' base game dialogue was conducted with Cross, before it was decided that the character would be cut from the game. When Cross was brought back to re-voice the character for the game's add-ons, Bergman apologized to Cross for the initial cut, and joked that he (Bergman) was "still able to speak, so clearly [Cross is] a great guy. And a great actor."<ref name="Cross" group="Non-game">[https://web.archive.org/web/20200218164147/http://forums.bethsoft.com/topic/1223886-wheres-wal-jason-bergman-thread-2/page-2 Jason Bergman on the Bethesda Forums (archived)]: ''"It was always Roger. We even recorded his first session before cutting him from the main game. When we brought him back, I apologized and told him it was all my fault. I'm still able to speak, so clearly he's a great guy. And a great actor."''</ref>
* In the original version of ''Fallout: New Vegas'', the companion Ulysses was designed with a number of goals in mind:<ref name="GS" group="Non-game">[https://web.archive.org/web/20200529132908/https://www.gamasutra.com/blogs/WillOoi/20110917/8462/An_Interview_with_Chris_Avellone__game_designer_writer_and_former_unlucky_schlep__Part_3.php Gamasutra: Will Ooi's Blog - An Interview with Chris Avellone - game designer, writer, and former 'unlucky schlep' - Part 3]</ref>
** He had to reinforce the faction reputation mechanic, which Avellone thought was one of the key mechanics in the game.
** He had to react strongly to NCR/Legion conflict and the Courier's role in it, acting as a sounding board when possible.
** He had to be a Legion sympathetic character and explain Legion back story elements since none of the other companions were supportive of the Legion.
** He had to continually remind the player of Hoover Dam as the focus, and his backstory as the frumentarius who discovered the Dam and the NCR for the Legion incorporated this.
** He had to showcase myth elements. Ulysses was big about symbols, and his take on the flags of the NCR and Legion were also reflected in their champions (he viewed [[Lanius|Legate Lanius]] as an Eastern myth in the making, and he felt the Courier could achieve that same mythological status for the West or for the Mojave).
** He was to complement the cool visual design changes that [[Joshua Sawyer]] had included for other companions (similar to [[Raul Tejada]] and [[Arcade Gannon]], Ulysses would have the vest/flag changes, except it would depend on Courier's end faction allegiance when they completed Ulysses' vision quest).

===Development===
* The decision to re-introduce Ulysses as an overarching villain mentioned throughout three add-ons (''Dead Money'', ''Old World Blues'' and ''Lonesome Road'') only came near the end of ''Dead Money''{{'}}s development. By comparison, other add-on hooks (such as [[Elijah]]'s presence in ''Dead Money'') had been envisioned since the base game's development.<ref name="GS" group="Non-game" />
* Ulysses wears a facemask in-game because of engine limitations that could not support [[Chris Avellone]]'s original image for the character, so the development team designed a mask that would accomplish how they wanted Ulysses to look, while at the same time hiding lip syncing.<ref group="Non-game">[https://youtu.be/ALRO9EKeoAU?si=gDKx4Lmb68LWY2RE&t=4544 ''Fallout: New Vegas'' 10th Anniversary Charity Stream Part 2 (reference starts at 1:15:44)]<br />[[Joshua Sawyer]]: ''"Ulysses wears a face mask because this engine kind of had a lot of limitations to how faces work, and Avellone wanted a very specific look for Ulysses that the engine just wasn't up to doing, so they made a mask for him so that he could look exactly the way that we wanted him to look. Um, but it had to have a... It had to have, like, the mouth thing on there so that there wasn't lip syncing."''<br />([[Resources:Fallout: New Vegas 10th Anniversary Charity Stream/Day 2 transcript|Transcript]])</ref>
