'''Ulysses''' is a [[Fallout: New Vegas characters|courier]] and former [[Frumentarii|frumentarius]] of [[Caesar's Legion]]. He serves as the main antagonist of the ''[[Fallout: New Vegas]]'' [[Fallout: New Vegas add-ons|add-on]] ''[[Lonesome Road (add-on)|Lonesome Road]]'' as well as an unseen secondary antagonist in the add-ons ''[[Dead Money]]'', ''[[Honest Hearts]]'', and ''[[Old World Blues (add-on)|Old World Blues]]''.

==Background==
===The Twisted Hairs===
Ulysses was once a member of the [[Twisted Hairs]], a powerful tribe in the former state of [[Arizona]] known for their signature dreadlock hairstyles. They were sought out by the fledgling Caesar's Legion in [[Timeline#2247|2247]] and became its main scouting force during the conquest of the other tribes inhabiting Arizona. Ulysses, then going by an unknown tribal name, was one of the most successful scouts in the Twisted Hairs, traveling vast distances in search of the enemies of both his and [[Caesar]]'s tribes. His scouting ability was supplemented by his ability to live off the land, having acute knowledge of herbal remedies and medicines.<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"Any recipes you can teach?"''<br />Ulysses: ''"Depends what history has to say about them. Their tribal identity was erased and those who resisted were crucified along the sides of [[Interstate 40]] as a warning to the rest.

[[Vulpes Inculta]]'s pacification of the tribe at [[Dry Wells]], even though it was not their homeland, was a particularly painful moment for Ulysses, though his dedication to Caesar and the flag of the Bull prevented his desertion then.<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"Dry Wells is destroyed - that was your birthplace?"''<br />Ulysses: ''"No, opposite of that. Not revenge... just the way of things when you {{Tooltip|own them.|emph}}"''<br />([[NVDLC04Ulysses.txt|Ulysses' dialogue]])</ref> With the loss of his tribe, the man abandoned his former name and instead took the moniker of "Ulysses," alluding to [[Ulysses S. Grant]], "the man who fought during a time of two flags, and he had to make them one."<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"So you're the one who sent the radio message - Ulysses."''<br />Ulysses: ''"{{Tooltip|Not my given name, close enough.|Conceding to player's request, a little less challenging. Had to fight during a time when his world had two flags, and he had to make them one."''<br />([[NVDLC04Ulysses.txt|Ulysses' dialogue]])</ref> Thanks to his survival skills and his forceful personality, he quickly became an important, indispensable [[Frumentarii|frumentarius]] in service to the Legion, walking the wastes as a nondescript and unnoticeable courier. Meanwhile, he obeyed Caesar's rule to never kill any courier since, in fact, many couriers were spies for the Legion as well.

In [[Timeline#2274|2274]], after the [[New California Republic]] reached [[Hoover Dam]] coming west from [[New California]], Ulysses himself crossed the [[Colorado River]] from the east and was the first among the Legion to see the Dam and the flag of the two-headed Bear - a nation powerful enough to rival Caesar's own. Returning to his lord, Ulysses spoke with Caesar about Hoover Dam and the might of the NCR. The Old World symbol of the Dam became an object of obsession for Caesar, a symbol of a future for his Legion. Ulysses, however, believed Hoover Dam was an omen of Caesar's downfall, regardless of whether he won or lost against the NCR.

His prediction nearly came true at the [[First Battle of Hoover Dam]], when the Malpais Legate, [[Joshua Graham]], was defeated by the [[New California Republic Rangers|NCR Rangers]] led by [[Hanlon|Chief Hanlon]]. For his failure, Caesar had Graham covered in pitch by his [[Praetorian Guard]]s, lit on fire, and thrown into the [[Grand Canyon]]. Ulysses witnessed this firsthand, and the event cemented his belief that Caesar would pursue his conquest of the Dam and the [[Mojave Wasteland]] at any cost.<ref>The [[Courier]]: ''"Other Legates? After the Legion's disastrous defeat at Hoover Dam, Ulysses continued to travel as a courier, exploring the wastelands for Caesar. Knew you'd walked the West as I'd walked the East. Learned different lessons. And I would never have discovered the Divide without you."''<br />([[NVDLC04Ulysses.txt|Ulysses' dialogue]])</ref> Between [[Timeline#2274|2274]] and 2277, Ulysses tracked the Courier on another trek to the Divide and there, he discovered a community, itself also called "the Divide." Ulysses saw in this community, in his own words, "a nation taking its first breath," surrounded and shaped by the symbols of the [[United States of America|Old World]].
