'''Porter Gage''' is a [[Raiders (Fallout 4)|raider]] operating as the second-in-command to [[Colter|Overboss Colter]], leader of the [[Nuka-World raiders|raider alliance]] controlling the pre-War [[Nuka-World (location)|Nuka-World]] amusement park in [[Timeline#2287|2287]]. He appears {{In|FO4NW}} as a possible [[Fallout 4 companions|companion]] of the [[Sole Survivor]].

==Background==
===Becoming a raider===
Growing up on a small farming settlement with his parents, the young Porter witnessed firsthand as time after time, their family was forced to give into the demands of [[Raiders (Fallout 4)|raider]] gangs that harassed their home; every time he saw his parents kowtow and give in without a fight, and he grew to despise them for their weakness and refusal to resist. At the age of 12, he ran away from home during one such raid, in which he witnessed his parents cowering in fear of a single raider with a gun, deciding for himself he was going to escape the fate of living under the crushing heel of someone else.<ref name="gage1">Porter Gage: ''"{{Tooltip|Dunno about you, but I had this shit figured out early. Grew up in your average crap-hole settlement, with parents that meant well, but...|Neutral}} {{Tooltip|They were pushovers. Watched them get smacked around by Raider gangs for years, handing over whatever they had to keep their lives.|Irritated}} {{Tooltip|One day, I'm watching them cower in front of some punk with a gun, and it just hits me. "I ain't gonna end up like this," I says to myself.|Defiant}} {{Tooltip|So I bail. I'm what, 12 years old? Didn't matter. I'd seen enough of the world to know how shit works.|Confident}}''"<br />The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"That was really brave of you, Gage."''<br />Porter Gage: "''{{Tooltip|The hell it was. Survival, that's all. I knew if I stuck around, I'd wind up like them.|Defiant}}''"<br />([[DLC04Gage.txt#107|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref> Gage never found out what became of his parents, nor did he care enough to look, considering them just another pair of weaklings to be chewed up and spit out by the wasteland.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Where are your parents now?"''<br>Porter Gage: ''"{{tooltip|Who the hell knows? Dead, I'm sure. Probably a long time ago. Doesn't matter; hasn't mattered in decades.|Dismissive / Irritated}}"''<br>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref>

For the next few years, the teenaged Gage roamed the wasteland and got by via taking odd jobs, doing whatever he could to earn [[Bottlecap (Fallout 4)|caps]] and continue to survive. He later turned to caravan and scavenger work, and there he continued to observe the parasitic lifestyle of raiders who took from others and only cared for themselves. A change finally came for Gage at age 16, after the raid of a settlement he had been residing and working in; then and there, he decided that he would no longer fear or flee from the threat of raiders, instead he would become that threat as a raider himself.<ref name="gage2">Porter Gage: ''"{{Tooltip|I bounced around for a few years, taking whatever jobs I could to pay for food, one way or another.|Neutral}} {{Tooltip|I was a runner for a caravan for a while, did some scavenging, did odd jobs for settlements... Raiders took what they wanted, moved on, and that was that.|Confident}} {{Tooltip|Didn't take long before I finally figured out where I'd really been heading all along.|Amused}} {{Tooltip|Next time a gang came through, I joined. Worked my way up over the years, and now here we are.|Confident}}"''<br />([[DLC04Gage.txt#115|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref>

===Connor's gang===
The first gang Gage found after his epiphany had a decent body count and a lot of strength on hand, but Gage personally believed they were not using it wisely. Being young and naïve, he thought he could secure a place for himself by attempting to suggest different ways the gang could be improved and streamlined. Eventually, he was approached by the gang's leader, [[Connor (Nuka-World)|Connor]] "The Harvester," a title Gage personally thought was laughable. Much to Gage's surprise, Connor actually listened and executed Gage's ideas for improvement. From then on, Connor would regularly return to Gage for advice and opinions on his plans, telling him how much he trusted him and inflating the young Gage's ego.<ref name="gage3">Porter Gage: ''"{{Tooltip|You know, I made some bad calls when I was younger.|Apologetic}} {{Tooltip|I ain't saying I regret the choices I made, just that early on, I was a little starry-eyed.|Neutral}} {{Tooltip|First gang took me in when I was 16. After approximately a year of working alongside Connor and his gang, Gage came to the boss with a suggestion to make peace with a rival gang. Connor seemed agreeable to the idea, so Gage volunteered to be the negotiator in his stead. He arranged a meeting with the opposing gang on neutral ground with three of Connor's biggest crewmates backing him up.<ref name="gage4">Porter Gage: ''"{{Tooltip|After something like a year of working closely together goes by, we come up with this plan to make peace with a rival gang.|Confident}} {{Tooltip|We work the whole thing out in secret. An hour into the negotiation, Gage saw the rival gangers beginning to come around to the idea the truce was in their best interest. However, seemingly out of nowhere, there was gunfire and explosions. Gage began to panic, thinking that he just needed to wait until Connor came to sort things out and save him. Instead, as the chaos unfolded with no rescue in sight, Gage's hope turned to anger as he came to believe that Connor had set him up for death. In Gage's mind, Connor must have begun to fear him, thinking that Gage would usurp his role as leader after helping the gang rise to power. He reasoned that Connor only agreed to the negotiations so Gage could serve as a distraction while he and his men ambushed the rival gang, with Gage ending up as an accidental casualty. ===Nuka-World===
Sometime prior to [[Timeline#2286|2286]], Gage's raider career eventually led him to the outskirts of [[the Commonwealth]], where he became involved with a veteran raider named [[Colter]] and assumed a similar position as he had to Connor, though he remained wary of getting too involved. Sometime that year, Gage advised Colter to form a coalition between three powerful raider gangs (the [[Operators]], [[Disciples]] and [[the Pack]]) and have them take over the pre-[[Great War|War]] amusement park of [[Nuka-World (location)|Nuka-World]], particularly the prospering trading hub based out of [[Nuka-Town USA]]. Under Colter's direction guided by Gage, the raider army assaulted the trading hub in an event that became known as the "[[Fall of Nuka-Town USA]]".

Once the gangs had swept in, putting down any resistance and enslaving the survivors to continue operating the trading hub for the raiders' benefit, Gage continued to serve as an advisor and right-hand man to Colter, helping him into the role as the gangs' "Overboss." Over the next year, Gage repeatedly pushed for Colter to continue with their plans to claim the other areas of Nuka-World, however, Colter refused all requests to take any further action. Seeing what he perceived as the familiar streak of laziness that had destroyed other gangs now emerging in his leader, Gage grew dissatisfied with Colter's complacency and perceived incompetence. He later arranged a secret meeting with the gang leaders to plot a way to replace their overboss and finish the plan to fully take control of Nuka-World.
