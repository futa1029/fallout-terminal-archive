==Personality==
While Porter Gage has all the hallmarks of a typical raider, being keen to violent and cruel acts for both personal benefit and mere entertainment<ref>Porter Gage: ''"{{tooltip|Sounds like it's time to put bullets in faces.|SinisterSmile}}"''<br/>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Porter Gage: ''"{{tooltip|Now you're dead.|Just found someone he's been trying to kill / SinisterSmile}}"''<br>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Porter Gage: ''"{{tooltip|I can't lie - I've made messes bigger than this in my day.|SinisterSmile}}"''<br>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref> while disgusted by altruism and kindness,<ref>Porter Gage: ''"{{tooltip|Look, I backed you taking over as Overboss. Helped you get rid of Colter. I thought you could be the bad-ass the gangs needed. But, damn... Seems like you're determined to let me down. You're a pushover, going easy on chumps and even helping them.|Irritated}} {{tooltip|You gonna start acting like a Raider, or what?|Suspicious}}"''<br/>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Porter Gage: ''"Whole world's full of people like you. No wonder it's such a shit-show."''<br>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref><ref>Porter Gage: ''"{{tooltip|You going soft, boss?|Disgust}}"''<br>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref> he takes it a step further by actually being clever and cunning;<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"What sort of talents would you say you have, Gage?"''<br>Porter Gage: ''"{{tooltip|Aside from being a good shot and having a foul mouth? This shit needs to work out.|Confident}}"''<br>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref> he is able to look past the short-term reward of one battle to see the long-term gain of working with multiple strong gangs to take over a pre-War amusement park and turn it into the heart of a raider empire.<ref>Porter Gage: ''"{{tooltip|Let's take a step back, and talk big picture, okay? Nuka-World... Shit, this was the dream. Huge, built like a goddamn fortress. You run this, the world is yours.|Confident}}"''<br>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref> This stems from his lifelong raider career where he has witnessed more than one gang destroyed because of their own incompetence and shortsightedness, due in no small part to heavy chem addiction which also inspired his dislike of anyone who uses them.<ref name="gage7"/> His life experiences have taught him that it is better for his own sake to be the second-in-command of a raider gang than its leader, where he can exert a better degree of influence while greatly reducing the chance that any lowlife with even an inkling of ambition will make him their target;<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Seems like you've got a decent head on your shoulders. If I've got his ear, I can steer things in a direction that benefits me, and usually everyone else."''<br>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref> at the same time, he has learned to temper his own ambitions and always have a quick-exit strategy when his current allegiance proves to be a hazard, which leaves him lacking in the ability to genuinely trust another person in his life.<ref name="gage5"/>

When it comes to the three gangs he helped Colter pull together to take over Nuka-World, Gage sees the [[Disciples]] as crazy, bloodthirsty killers to a level above the average raider but knows that getting on their good side means that can be put to use; the [[Operators]] as spoiled, rich-kid snobs that nonetheless have instincts for both murder and making money; and [[the Pack]] as savages barely smarter than the animals they cage that still prove useful owing to their ferocity and obedience to shows of strength.<ref name="GageGangLikes">Porter Gage: ''"Yeah, all right... ===Companion===
* Porter Gage becomes available as a companion only after completing [[An Ambitious Plan]] by meeting the various Nuka-World raider bosses and then speaking with him again, also triggering [[The Grand Tour]].
* Gage is the only companion in ''Fallout 4'' who can act as a vendor to the Sole Survivor. However, this ability is limited as he will only sell while in Nuka-Town USA.
* Gage will not enter the bridge of the ''[[The Prydwen|Prydwen]]'' during [[Shadow of Steel]] as [[Arthur Maxson|Elder Maxson]] is giving his speech, a trait shared with [[Strong]].
* If Gage is given any items to carry in his [[inventory]], when he is later used as a vendor, he will offer them up for sale in addition to his normal inventory, making it possible to buy them by accident.
* Attacking any of the Nuka-World raiders and turning them against the player character will also cause Gage to be removed as a companion and become hostile.
* Gage can be romanced.

===Effects of player's actions===
* Gaining maximum [[affinity]] with Porter Gage grants the [[Lessons in Blood]] perk, which offer a permanent 5% increase to [[Experience Points|XP]] gained per kill and permanent +10 increase to [[Damage Resistance]].
* Until completing An Ambitious Plan and making him available as a companion, Gage is non-essential and can be killed, which will fail that quest and start [[Open Season]] without needing to talk to [[Mackenzie Bridgeman]] or to rescue [[Preston Garvey]] during [[When Freedom Calls]] and speak to him afterward.
* If one kills Colter without using the [[Thirst Zapper (Nuka-World)|Thirst Zapper]] (very difficult due to his armor's damage-reduction effect), Gage will still talk to them as if they did it the normal way.
* Despite wearing an eyepatch, if one gives him eyewear that removes it, there is nothing wrong with his eye.
** There is no way of putting the eyepatch back in place after giving him another piece of eyewear.
** If one engages in dialogue with him at certain angles, they can see his eye under the eyepatch.
* Unlike other companions, if one selects "Best Friends" or "Thank you" during his final affinity dialogue, Gage will not offer another opportunity to select "Romance" at a later date.
* When Gage is dismissed, he can only be sent to raider outposts. If no raider outpost exists, i.e. [[Home Sweet Home]] has not been started, he will automatically return to Nuka-Town.
* If the player character has progressed in a major faction's questline far enough to be considered a member prior to meeting Gage during An Ambitious Plan, he will have unique dialogue referencing their allegiance to the [[Commonwealth Minutemen|Minutemen]],<ref>Porter Gage: ''"You know, I've heard about you. In charge of the Minutemen, ain't ya? No idea why you'd waste your time with those has-beens. I ain't no genius, but far as I'm concerned, history already proved what they're after ain't gonna work."''<br>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref> the [[Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 4)|Brotherhood of Steel]],<ref>Porter Gage: ''"You know, I think I heard of you... some bigshot with the Brotherhood of Steel, right? They ain't that different from a Raider gang, you ask me. Just try and act all legitimate, but they still just take what they want."''<br>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref> [[the Railroad]],<ref>Porter Gage: ''"Ain't gonna lie, I've heard about you. Part of that Railroad, ain't ya? I don't give a damn about their little crusade, but guess I know you've got some fight in ya."''<br>([[DLC04Gage.txt|Porter Gage's dialogue]])</ref> and [[the Institute]].<ref>Porter Gage: ''"You know, I've heard of you, and that Institute of yours...
