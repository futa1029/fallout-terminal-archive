'''Robert Joseph "RJ" MacCready''' is the adolescent mayor of the child-exclusive settlement of [[Little Lamplight]] in the [[Capital Wasteland]] in [[Timeline#2277|2277]], with a minor role in the main story of {{In|FO3|noin}}.

By [[Timeline#2287|2287]], the grown-up MacCready has travelled to [[the Commonwealth]] where he works as a hired mercenary, selling his services from the backroom of [[the Third Rail]] among the [[Drifter (Fallout 4)|drifters]] of [[Goodneighbor]]. He appears {{In|FO4}} as a possible [[Fallout 4 companions|companion]] of the [[Sole Survivor]].

===Wandering the wastes===
When MacCready turned sixteen and left Little Lamplight for good per the colony's rules, he took to wandering and working as a gun for hire. In the course of his travels, he met a woman named [[Lucy MacCready|Lucy]], and the two fell in love. Lucy always had MacCready's back and helped him through the darkest of nights in the wasteland. With her at his side, MacCready felt he could press ahead no matter the horrors that faced him.<ref>The [[Sole Survivor]]: ''"Why would you say something like that?"''<br />Robert MacCready: ''"{{Tooltip|I guess I needed to tell someone how I felt about it.|Somber}} {{Tooltip|Damn, I miss Lucy. At some point, Lucy became pregnant, and she and MacCready had a son, [[Duncan (child)|Duncan]]. The family was happy for a time, but that ended when they attempted to camp out in an abandoned metro station, unaware that it was infested with [[feral ghoul]]s.
