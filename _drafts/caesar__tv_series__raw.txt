'''Caesar''' is the leader of a splinter group of [[Caesar's Legion]] residing in the [[Mojave Wasteland]] in [[Timeline (2250-2300)#2296|2296]]. He appears {{In|FOTV}}.

==Background==
After the death of the original [[Caesar]] (Edward Sallow) sometime between October [[Timeline (2250-2300)#2281|2281]] and 2296, the Legion eventually fractured into two factions fighting over who would become the heir to the vacant throne. Two men laid claim to the mantle of Caesar, considering one another to be a [[false Caesar|"false" Caesar]] and ordering their respective factions of [[Legionary (TV series)|legionaries]] to retrieve what they believe is the written will of Sallow that conclusively names his successor. By July 2296, both groups remain at a stalemate, even though their [[Caesar's Legion camp|camps]] are located just opposite one another; their forces prevent each other from retrieving Sallow's note, shooting at anyone who dares to try.<ref>[[Lacerta Legate]]: ''"The great founder of this Empire, he died. We've been at war ever since over who should be his heir. He wrote down the name of his successor. He placed it into his divine pocket. But every time we try to retrieve it, the false Caesar and his followers, they shoot at us. We are at a stalemate."''<br />({{FOTV|2|3}})</ref> 

The situation shifted in July 2296 after a [[The Ghoul|third party]] reignited the conflict between the two Caesar claimants' factions, causing a bloody [[Legion civil war|civil war]] to occur. This Caesar was killed amidst the fighting, and the other claimant evidently also died or at least no longer contested power, as the Lacerta Legate was able to seize control over the surviving legionaries in the aftermath.<ref>{{FOTV|2|8}}</ref>

==''Fallout'' TV series==
===[[The Profligate]]===
Lucy MacLean meets this Caesar when she is captured by his legionaries and taken to his side of the Legion stronghold, where she is also introduced to his right-hand man, the Lacerta Legate. She arrives right as they are in the middle of reviewing battle plans, with the Caesar mostly ignoring Lucy's presence.

Later, both Caesars are observed from afar by [[the Ghoul]] seated upon their respective thrones, causing the Ghoul to realize that the Legion has become divided into two factions.

Caesar is present in his tent when the Ghoul comes to bargain for Lucy's release, offering to tell the Legion the location of [[Rodriguez|Captain Rodriguez's]] small NCR camp in exchange (which he pitches as the "last holdout" of the NCR in the region), a deal they accept. Caesar himself is seen leading a war rally for his Legion declaring the final and complete eradication of their long-time enemy, but they are interrupted by an explosion instigated by the Ghoul. The explosion is mistaken for an attack from the other Caesar's soldiers, leading to fighting and gunfire breaking out.

===[[The Strip]]===
Some time later, as the fighting in the Legion camp continues, this Caesar's corpse is seen outside of his tent as the Lacerta Legate drags Edward Sallow's remains past him into the tent; he is shown to have been fatally impaled with a ''pilum'' spear. Shortly after the legate walks away, a legionary removes the spear from this Caesar's body to take as a weapon while returning to the fighting. After the fighting ends, the Lacerta Legate is able to take over as the new Caesar.
