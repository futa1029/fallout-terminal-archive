'''Doc Stanley''' is a [[Protectron (Fallout 76)|Protectron]] serving as a [[Fallout 76 vendors|vendor]] at [[the Whitespring Resort]] in [[Appalachia]].            

==Background==
Doc Stanley is one of the robotic vendors the Whitespring replaced their human staff with shortly before the Great War.<ref>[[The Whitespring 2080 Initiative]]</ref> He resides in the 'The Chemists' store of the Whitespring's mall area. 

This Protectron medic is responsible for selling recreational and medicinal chems to customers and also offers electroshock therapy to interested clients.<ref>Doc Stanley: ''"Electroshock therapy is available upon request."''<br />([[Doc Stanley/Dialogue#5|Doc Stanley's dialogue]])</ref>
