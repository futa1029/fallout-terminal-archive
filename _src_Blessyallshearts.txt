'''Bless y'all's hearts''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Wastelanders]]''.
__TOC__
==Location==
The tape can be found in [[Poseidon Energy Plant WV-06]]'s south expansion section during [[From Russia With Lev]]. It is on a small table in the topmost section, where [[Surge]] is posted.
