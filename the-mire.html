概要

沼地地帯 is one of the eight 地域s of アパラチア .

概要

沼地地帯 was once a remote, rural 地域 of アパラチア east of 荒れた境域, home to the フリー・ステイツ movement sparked by Raleigh Clay. Suspicious of the government's vault program and aware of the escalation of the Resource Wars, the group built private concrete bunkers scattered throughout the 地域, using the nearby ハーパーズ・フェリー as a staging area and supply point. The movement's conspiracy theories and ideals unnerved patriotic visitors and disrupted the small town's tourism.
Violence between the フリー・ステイツ and law enforcement drew the attention of the government. In June 2077, the public prosecutor pressed charges against the movement's founder, and, by November, the フリー・ステイツ openly seceded from the United States and retreated to their concrete bunkers.
After 大戦, 沼地地帯's forest and swamp environment began to mutate and grow deadly with each passing day. Exactly one year after 大戦, Vault 94 opened its vault door and sent ambassadors into the ウエイストランド to invite survivors back to the vault. In November 2078, an ambassador reached the town of ハーパーズ・フェリー. After hearing about the Vault, Mayor Miranda Vox sent a group of survivors led by Cole to investigate the ambassador's claims. Certain that the dwellers' hospitality was a trap, the group massacred the vault's leaders and attacked the vault's G.E.C.K. unit, triggering a massive 核 explosion. A thick fog poured from the vault's entrance, rapidly intensifying the area's swampy environment and mutating the 地域's thick foliage. The 荒れた境域 acted as a natural barrier, preventing 沼地地帯's expansion その方向には.
In 2079, Raleigh Clay gave the all-clear signal to フリー・ステイツ members and was contacted by Miranda Vox, with ハーパーズ・フェリー being in desperate need of assistance. Despite previous conflicts, the movement agreed to provide aid.
By 2081, the ブラザーフッド・オブ・スティール used the 戦前の Camp Venture ロケーション to provide survival training to members of the フリー・ステイツ. After intensifying requisitions from local survivors, the Brotherhood 放棄された the camp in 2094 to defend ディファイアンス砦 and the Thunder Mountain Power Plant. In August 2095, the Brotherhood finally fell as Thunder Mountain was overrun with スコーチ. Using sonic generator technology salvaged from Brotherhood outposts, the フリー・ステイツ set up lures in the 地域 to attract, trap, and kill scorchbeasts.
At some point in the pre-Plague period, the エンクレイヴ established a field lab at Tanagra Town, a neighborhood which had been lifted above the canopy by a towering pillar of strangler plants. Their research was ultimately 破壊された, but strangler plant seed pods were returned to エンクレイヴ Research Facility Site J for study.
In 2096, ハーパーズ・フェリー was attacked by スコーチ and the フリー・ステイツ retreated back into their bunkers throughout 沼地地帯, banning all efforts to recover or save what remained of the town or its inhabitants.
In 2102, the residents of Vault 76 began exploring 沼地地帯. It was known as a place to hunt cryptids. Late in the year, they gained access to the エンクレイヴ field lab at Tanagra.
In 2103, a group of villagers settled the 放棄された Treehouse Village under the leadership of Jennie Brown, renaming it the Retreat. The raider Dagger and her goons made their headquarters in a nearby cave, and began demanding tribute from the village on the threat of death. In the south, the Followers of the Winged One cult constructed a towering primitivist stronghold called Moth-Home. At a nearby camp dubbed Blake's Offering, cultists live among unusual strangler vine formations. Additionally, crashed astronaut Sofia Daguerre was found in a bunker by the Vault Dwellers.
In 2104, the Sunday Brothers' cabin was settled by moonshiners.

Environment

沼地地帯 is a dark, swampy 地域 home to twisting vines, large tree canopies, and tall grass. The thick foliage gives cover to the 地域's mutated creatures and makes the area one of the darkest at night. While 放射線 from 大戦 attributed to mutations, the 地域's red-hued environment is largely due to the explosion of Vault 94's G.E.C.K.
The strangler vines infest the entire 地域, especially along the river. Strangler hearts bloom from these vines, which are capable of infesting creatures and controlling them. These coarse red vines grow rapidly, and tend to rip buildings apart and lift vehicles off the ground.
沼地地帯's weather is primarily misty, though the 地域 may occasionally suffer from highly-放射性の radstorms. The weather has a 55% chance to be misty, 15% to be misty and rainy, 15% to be foggy, 15% to be clear, and 3% to be a radstorm.
