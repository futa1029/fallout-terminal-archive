'''Bruiser''' is a member of the [[Crater Raiders]] sub-group known as the [[Blackwater Bad Boys]] at [[Ohio River Adventures]] {{in|FO76WL}}.

==Background==
Bruiser once lived in a [[Vault]]; according to [[Fishbones]], Bruiser's memories of pre-[[Great War|War]] life before the Vault are muddled.<ref name="bones">[[Fishbones]]: ''"Anyways, Bruiser didn't remember much about his life before the Vault... not even his name. Been Bruiser ever since."''<br />([[Fishbones/Dialogue|Fishbones' dialogue]])</ref> Bruiser claims that he was, at one time in his life, a "warrior" who fought in an arena alongside a team of at least seventy, and that many people traveled to see him; he says that he wore heavy armor, while a war band played loud music and beautiful girls performed acrobatics nearby.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"What was your life like before all of this?"''<br />Bruiser: ''"I don't remember much, but I was a mighty warrior that fought in an arena. Hundreds of people came to watch me from all over. I wore heavy armor... and a band played war music... with drums and great, powerful horns. Oh, and beautiful girls! There were beautiful girls doing acrobatics."''<br />([[Bruiser (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Bruiser's dialogue]])</ref><ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"It sounds like you must have enjoyed it. You remember it well and fondly."''<br />Bruiser: ''"Those were great times. I had a team too. Real comrades. We marched into battle together to crush our enemies. There must have been seventy of us."''<br />([[Bruiser (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Bruiser's dialogue]])</ref> If the player character points out that his description of the event sounds similar to a game of [[Football|American football]], Bruiser will reject the notion, adamant about his memories as a fighter.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"I think you might have been playing a game called football."''<br />Bruiser: ''"Football? What's that?"''<br />Vault 76 dweller: ''"You work with a team to move a ball past your opponents defense to score points."''<br />Bruiser: ''"A game? No. I was a mighty combatant. That sounds like the lousiest game I've ever heard of. I bet those Foundation wimps play it. Weaklings."''<br />([[Bruiser (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Bruiser's dialogue]])</ref> Eventually, Bruiser's memories faded and he forgot even his own name. At some point, he left the Vault he lived in and ended up in [[Appalachia]].<ref name="bones" />

After helping establish [[the Crater]], [[Fishbones]], [[Gills]] and [[Blackeye]] were sent to establish an outpost at Ohio River Adventures, where they could coordinate the trade of [[Mirelurk (Fallout 76)|mirelurk]] products such as [[Mirelurk egg (Fallout 76)|eggs]] and [[Softshell mirelurk meat (Fallout 76)|softshell meat]]. While making the trip southwest, Fishbones woke up one night to Bruiser "beating the tar" out of Gills, who was on night watch. Bones figured that he had found Gills' replacement, as Gills could no longer walk from the beating he received. Bruiser, Bones and Blackeye now lead the outpost, though they have recently been having troubles with mirelurks attacking the docks.

Bruiser is not one for conversation;<ref>Bruiser: ''"Only thing better than a short conversation is no conversation."''<br />([[Bruiser (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Bruiser's dialogue]])</ref> he prefers to let his fists do the talking for him,<ref>Bruiser: ''"I let my fists do the talkin'."''<br />([[Bruiser (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Bruiser's dialogue]])</ref> makes liberal threats of violence against those who annoy him,<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"Ever consider meditating at the end of the day? Clearing your head and taking a deep breath?"''<br />Bruiser: ''"Jokes. Yeah, jokes are funny. Real funny until I push you off the dock."''<br />([[Bruiser (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Bruiser's dialogue]])</ref> and relishes in the chance to inflict physical harm.<ref>Bruiser: ''"I like to punch things, 'specially faces."''<br />([[Bruiser (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Bruiser's dialogue]])</ref><ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault 76 dweller]]: ''"Sometimes when I get angry I just count to 10 and say to myself, 'It'll be ok.'"''<br />Bruiser: ''"I could count to 10 and punch you in the face if you'd like."''<br />([[Bruiser (Wastelanders)/Dialogue|Bruiser's dialogue]])</ref>
