概要

In 2077, the unemployment rate in town was increasing due to automation put in place by the ホーンライト・インダストリアル Mining Company. A transition to complete automation would be mandatory if Ballot Measure 6 in the November 2077 election passed.
A second mining company Atomic Mining Services approached the town with promises to revitalize the area and reenergize its economy due to the discovery of valuable ultracite. After an earthquake damaged several homes, AMS began to evict those with property containing the ore, claiming "resource rights to the land." The townspeople revolted against those trying to evict them. The National Guard was called in due to the violence.
After 大戦, part of the U.S.S.A. spaceship conducting the Deep Sleep project crashed into a street in ウェルチ, containing the remains of three astronauts, Bernard, Nowak and Lee, who had been separated from their mission commander, Sofia Daguerre, during the reentry.
