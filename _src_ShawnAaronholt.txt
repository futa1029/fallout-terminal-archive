'''Shawn Aaronholt''' was a resident of [[Appalachia]] who died before [[Timeline#2102|2102]]. His corpse can be found in the back of a pickup truck at [[Aaronholt homestead]].

==Background==
Shawn Aaronholt lived with his brothers [[John Aaronholt|John]] and [[Lowell Aaronholt|Lowell]] at their family farm after the [[Great War|War]]. He disagreed with his brother Lowell's desire to travel to Chicago and instead wanted to travel south to see if any of their other relatives had survived the nuclear apocalypse. He died from radiation sickness before he could do so, after he and John couldn't find the key to the granary where the brothers had hoarded their medicine.<ref>[[Shawn's journal]]</ref>
