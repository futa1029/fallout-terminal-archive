概要

Built upon the wreckage of the Valiant-1 space station, the Crater is the largest raider settlement in アパラチア. It was constructed by Caleb Fisher and his crew, who, like many others, joined up with the surviving Diehards as they made their way back to the 地域. Crater maintains several crop fields, which supply the raiders with a steady food reserve.

レイアウト

The Crater is located in a large, shallow crater near the northeastern edge of 毒の峡谷. The space station appears to have fallen from orbit at a high velocity, devastating the surrounding area. The bulk of the station remains embedded upright in the center of the crater, with fragments scattered around it.
By 2103, the ロケーション has been turned into a raider settlement, adding scaffolding, amenities, and other decorations typical of raider architecture. The Crater Core is located in the center of the spaceship and has been turned into the raiders' headquarters. 舞台裏

* The design for the space station was developed independently of the B.O.M.B.-001 design and the premise of B.O.M.B.-002 from Van Buren. Lead artist Nathan Purkeypile initially came up with the shape based on iconic science fiction space station designs, and intended to use the design for a Fallout 3 DLC. After learning about the existence of the B.O.M.B.-001, Purkeypile briefly designated his design B.O.M.B.-002, before the late Ferret Baudoin informed him that the name was spoken for, thus the name Valiant-1 was chosen.
* Nathan Purkeypile designed much of the Crater's layout, lighting and cluttering.
* According to level designer Steve Massey, the crashed space station was selected as the site of the レイダー' settlement as the team had always wanted to do more with the space station since the game's initial release.
