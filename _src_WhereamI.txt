'''Where am I?''' is a [[Fallout 76 holotapes|holotape]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
The holotape can be found on a table in the picnic pavilion directly north of the [[Landview Lighthouse]], next to a dead settler laid over the table.

==Behind the scenes==
The chem addict in this holotape was voiced by [[Eric Lopez]]. The holotape itself was written by level designer [[Steve Massey]].<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20230514140258/https://i.imgur.com/lH8hAlF.png BGS_Steve on Bethesda Game Studios' official Discord]</ref>
