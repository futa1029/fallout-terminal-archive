'''Poole''' is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] in [[Vault 76]].

==Background==
Poole is one of the Mister Handies that serves Vault 76. He is in charge of Station #6 (Get a Job!) on [[Reclamation Day (event)|Reclamation Day]], where it offers a [[Pen (Fallout 76)|pen]] and [[Black-rim glasses (Fallout 76)|black-rim glasses]], and advice for the sorts of roles that [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] exiting the vault can play in the wasteland.

Poole has remained at this station even years past Reclamation Day, all because of the last few dwellers who are still staying in the Vault. He 
has grown tired of its role, and has taken to pleading for the stragglers to finally leave.
