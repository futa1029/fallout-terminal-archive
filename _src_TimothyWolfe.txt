'''Timothy Wolfe''' is a deceased [[Fire Breathers]] recruit found inside [[Belching Betty]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Timothy Wolfe was a member of the [[Scorchslayers]], a squad of Fire Breather recruits led by [[Maxine Ballard]]. He was confident in the squad's abilities going into their final exam inside the Belching Betty mine due to their prior experience fighting [[Scorched]].<ref>[[Scorchslayer's journal - Part 1]]</ref> However, he and fellow squadmate [[Andrew Rhodes]] were the first to be killed during the exam when [[Sylvester Tate]] failed to cover the squad's rear flank.<ref>[[Scorchslayer's journal - Part 2]]</ref>  

Wolfe's corpse can be found slumped against some machinery with his [[Submachine gun (Fallout 76)|submachine gun]] laid next to him.
