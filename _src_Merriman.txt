'''Merriman''' is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] living in [[Vault 76]].

==Background==
Merriman is one of the many Mister Handies that serve Vault 76. It is stationed at #4 (Join a Team), where it facilitates the distribution of [[Party hat (Fallout 76)|party hats]] to [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Dwellers]] exiting the vault.

By [[Timeline#2103|2103]], Merriman remarks on the straggler [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] who finally decide to wake up and leave the vault after being in there a whole year since [[Reclamation Day]].
