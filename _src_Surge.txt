'''Surge''', short for "Surgeon,"<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"What's your name?"''<br />Surge: ''"They call me Surge. As in surgeon, but I'm no doctor. I can put a round in a [[Mole rat (Fallout 76)|molerat]]'s buck teeth at a thousand meters though."''<br />([[Surge/Dialogue#320|Surge's dialogue]])</ref> is a member of the [[Crater Raiders]] residing inside the [[The Core|Crater Core]].

==Background==
Surge was trained to shoot from a young age "as soon as [she] could hold a pistol straight" by her father and became an expert marksman with surgical precision, earning her nickname. Her father was no pushover either, once killing three [[Yao guai (Fallout 76)|yao guai]] with only a pistol. However, even that could not prevent his death from heart failure.<ref>A [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"You look pretty comfortable with that rifle."''<br />Surge: ''"My Da had me shooting as soon as I could hold a pistol straight. He's gone now. Killed three yao-gaui{{sic}} with a pistol once, but couldn't stop his own ticker from quitting on him."''<br /> ([[Surge/Dialogue]])</ref> After he died, Surge wandered the [[Appalachian Trail]] and met [[Lev]] as he fought [[Super mutant (Fallout 76)|super mutants]], joining him and ending up at [[the Crater]].<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"How did you end up here?"''<br />Surge: ''"After my Da died, I walked the Appalachian trail. Figured why not. Spotted [[Lev]] in a firefight with some super mutants through my scope. Thought to myself 'now that’s a man I need to know better.'"''<br />([[Surge/Dialogue#315|Surge's dialogue]])</ref>

Like Lev, she dislikes [[Meg Groberg]]'s open-arms policy of allowing all sorts of mutants to join the raiders, believing that it will end up getting them killed one day. She derogatorily views [[Lucky Lou]] as a "walking corpse" and [[Gail]] a "green-skinned monster" and thinks both of them are unnatural freaks.

According to Meg, Surge never respected her as a leader. She also dislikes [[Johnny Weston]] and his propensity for cheating at cards. However, she is fond of [[Molly (Wastelanders)|Molly]] and her drinks.<ref>[[Bless y'all's hearts]]</ref>
