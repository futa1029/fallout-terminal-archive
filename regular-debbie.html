Regular Debbie

概要

Debbie was born and raised in Pittsburgh before the Great War. She worked in the city's heavy industry and was affiliated with the Pittsburgh Union. She was laboring in the factory where she was employed when the bombs dropped, but happened to be below ground in a lower level of the facility. This saved her from being killed in a direct strike, but radiation was still able to seep down and affect her. By the time she managed to free herself out of the rubble and get back to the topside, the process of ghoulification was already underway.

The first thing that Debbie did in the aftermath of the Great War was check on her family and friends to see if they survived. However, her parents' block had been completely obliterated, and she found her sister in a nearly unrecognizable state. Her own home had been destroyed, and Debbie was left without the people she cared about, or even her own skin.

However, Debbie survived in spite of this misfortune, as she found new places to use as shelter and scavenged for supplies, which resulted in her getting in more than a few fights. Although many left the city, those who stayed, including Debbie, worked to rebuild, and helped get many of the industrial factories running again. Some may have questioned the purpose of restarting these facilities, but Debbie says it gave the survivors something to work toward, giving them a sense of normalcy, not to mention the factories' manufacturing capabilities opening the doors to trade. It was around this time that Debbie earned her nickname: while working on a job post-War, Debbie was called "Ghoul Debbie" to differentiate her from another woman named Debbie, who was an unaffected human. She had a forceful "talk" (using her boots and fists) with the other workers who were calling her by this name, and emphasized the point that there would soon be more ghouls than humans due to the radiation in the area. This assertive rebuttal from Debbie led others to start calling her "Regular Debbie," which she liked, and the name stuck.

Due to heavy nuking of Pittsburgh in the Great War, the city had a significant ghoul population, both normal and feral. However, Debbie still experienced discrimination from people who were scared of ghouls and thought they would all eventually go feral. Although the normally-violent Fanatics gang had made promises to treat ghouls well, Debbie recognized this as a trap and saw how many ghouls who joined them turned into slaves or corpses, and avoided this fate for herself. Debbie says that being a ghoul has been hard, as she recognizes others' fears about going feral, but she tries to hold onto her own principles and make herself useful, at the same time rejecting anyone who gives her grief.

Despite trying to make her home a better place post-War, Debbie could not stay in Pittsburgh forever. The city became the Pitt, a violent hellhole that only got worse and worse, despite their efforts to the contrary. Debbie came to believe that her true home was long gone, and once she came to terms with that, she felt that it was time to leave. Heading out with no real direction in mind, she eventually came across a group of ghouls while on the road. They turned out to be the Revenants, who explained their history of having been hunted down and targeted, with which Debbie empathized. The Revenants told Debbie that they were on their way to set up a more permanent home for themselves and needed help with construction. Debbie found assisting the Revenants in exchange for a place to live to be a good deal. However, she has rejected multiple offers from leader Maddox Mullen to be a true member of the faction, as she does not think their secretive, raiding, outcast lifestyle is for her.

Nevertheless, Debbie remains an associate (albeit not a full-time member) of the Revenants and resides in Radiant Hills, where she helps build up the settlement as the leader of their construction efforts. This includes restoring the buildings above ground near the missile silo, as well as setting up fortifications and defenses for the area's perimeter. She has also casually interacted with Kevan Asherton's combat training, in which she showed up late but proceeded to strike every target in record time, showing exceptional marksmanship. However, she was also chastised by Kevan for "excessive personal conversations." Although she is glad to have left the Pitt behind, Debbie does still occasionally wonder about the old factory where she used to work, and hopes it is still under the control of the Union.
