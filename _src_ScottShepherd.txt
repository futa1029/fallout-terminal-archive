'''Scott Shepherd''' is a deceased member of the [[Responders]] and resident of [[Flatwoods]] in [[Appalachia]]. His body can be found at 2 Church Road in Flatwoods.

==Background==
Scott Shepherd was a nurse before the [[Great War]] who volunteered up with the [[Responders]] after the bombs fell, providing his services in exchange for supplies and housing. He was a paranoid person, as detailed in his [[Survivor story: Nurse Scott|survivor story]], believing that 9 days, 19 hours before the War he was attacked by aliens, and that they orchestrated the war and controlled who survived, who died, and everything in between.<ref>[[Survivor story: Nurse Scott]]</ref> He also had theories on other topics, mainly political and paranormal, which he shared with the other survivors.<ref>[[Flatwoods terminal entries#Shepherd, Scott|Flatwoods terminal entries, self-serve registration kiosk, people directory, Shepherd, Scott]]</ref>
