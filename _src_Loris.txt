'''Doctor Loris''' is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] at [[Vault-Tec University]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
A Mister Handy playing the role of the simulation vault's head physician, Doctor Loris is responsible for locking [[Kelemen]] in the reactor wing to prevent the spread of radiation in the vault. Unapologetic about his choice, Loris believes it was sacrificing one for the good of the many - a bone of contention between him and [[Brass|Head Engineer Brass]], who believes it to be nothing more than cold-blooded murder.
