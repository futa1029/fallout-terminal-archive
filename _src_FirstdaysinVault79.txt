'''First days in Vault 79''' is a [[Wastelanders holotapes|holotape]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Wastelanders]]''.

==Location==
The tape can be found in [[Vault 79]], on a desk in the overseer's office. After [[Secrets Revealed]] is completed, most sections of the vault become inaccessible and this item can no longer be retrieved.
