'''Cindy Holloway''' is a deceased graduate student whose remains can be found near [[Toxic Larry's Meat 'n Go]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Cindy's father, a police officer, was strict and constantly asked her what she was doing. Finding fellow student [[Michael Turner]] attractive and her father overbearing, she concocted a plan to both escape her father and seduce Michael by taking him camping near [[Berkeley Springs]],<ref>[[Cindy's diary]]</ref> unaware that he had only accepted because of his interest in recent [[sheepsquatch]] sightings in the area.<ref>[[C.H. Monthly, October]]</ref> When the trip came around, during their fall break week of October 11th-18th, her plans were interrupted when the sheepsquatch showed up for real, and killed both of them.<ref>[[Date night (Wild Appalachia)|Date night]]</ref>

==Appearances==
Cindy Holloway's [[Skeleton (Fallout 76)|skeleton]] appears only in ''[[Fallout 76]]'', introduced in the ''[[Wild Appalachia]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]].
