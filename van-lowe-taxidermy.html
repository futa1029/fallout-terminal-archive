概要

Van Lowe Taxidermy is a store within ルイスバーグ.

概要

Van Lowe Taxidermy was owned by Shelley van Lowe who inherited it from her parents when they died. She eventually sold it to the Bysshe Company when she left ルイスバーグ after her brother Calvin's disappearance. The store has a secret basement lab which is accessible by pressing the laundry day date on the wall calendar next to the laundry. This was built by the Bysshe Company for Calvin van Lowe on a weekend when his sister Shelley was out of town. It was here that Calvin tried to reprogram an assaultron to help him in his hunt for the legendary sheepsquatch, but misunderstood what the assaultron said when he gave it commands, and this led to it attacking him, mutilating his face, and causing him to go into hiding, joining the ブルーリッジ・キャラバン・カンパニー as one of their bodyguards for their supply caravans through Big Bend Tunnel under the alias Aries.
