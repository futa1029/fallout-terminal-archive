'''Mortimer''' is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] associated with [[the Crater]] [[Crater Raiders|Raiders]]. After the conclusion of [[Secrets Revealed]], he appears as a [[gold bullion]] [[Fallout 76 vendors|vendor]] in [[The Core|the Crater Core]].

==Background==
Mortimer joins the Raiders in an effort to obtain gold from trading with both them and the [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]]. He has great plans for the gold, from re-engineering a new body to force the robots at [[the Whitespring Resort]] to show him respect,<ref>Mortimer: "''Once I've enough gold to engineer a new body, those insolent robots at Whitespring will have to respect me.''"<br />([[Mortimer (Wastelanders)/Dialogue#47|Mortimer's dialogue]])</ref> to giving himself a gold paint job,<ref>Mortimer: "''I wonder how much gold bullion it'll take to re-engineer my body. I think the color will quite suit me, don't you?''"<br />([[Mortimer (Wastelanders)/Dialogue#48|Mortimer's dialogue]])</ref> to purchasing weapons modifications for himself,<ref>Mortimer: "''Perhaps [[Vault 79]] has some weapons modifications I could make use of.''"<br />([[Mortimer (Wastelanders)/Dialogue#49|Mortimer's dialogue]])</ref> and even to use it to acquire a fourth arm.<ref>Mortimer: "''A fourth arm could really show a certain robot I know who's boss.''"<br />([[Mortimer (Wastelanders)/Dialogue#50|Mortimer's dialogue]])</ref>

Despite being willing to trade with the Raiders, Mortimer looks down on them.<ref>Mortimer: "''Ah, beautiful gold, what plans I have for you. Not that any undesirables around here would ever understand.''"<br />([[Mortimer (Wastelanders)/Dialogue#54|Mortimer's dialogue]])</ref> He views the Crater Core as a "hole in the ground" unfit for "a Handy of my stature,"<ref>Mortimer: "''This hole in the ground is no proper living quarters for a Handy of my stature.''"<br />([[Mortimer (Wastelanders)/Dialogue#46|Mortimer's dialogue]])</ref> and despises the interior decorating therein.<ref>Mortimer: "''The decor here is lacking to say the least. I mean, really, a dead animal? Am I the only one concerned about the smell?''"<br />([[Mortimer (Wastelanders)/Dialogue#53|Mortimer's dialogue]])</ref> He also dislikes [[Ra-Ra]] for bothering him.<ref>Mortimer: "''Don't let that little girl near me. She keeps trying to make me play with her, and don't get me started on the fingerprints.''"<br />([[Mortimer (Wastelanders)/Dialogue#51|Mortimer's dialogue]])</ref>

===Effects of player's actions===
After the events of [[Secrets Revealed]], the last quest in the main ''Wastelanders'' questline, Mortimer will arrive at the Crater Core and become available as a merchant. He sells rare plans to the Vault Dwellers in exchange for [[gold bullion]], with plan rarity dependent on one's [[Wastelanders reputations|reputation]] with the Raiders.
