'''The Wayward interrogation''' is a [[Wastelanders holotapes|holotape]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Wastelanders]]''.

==Location==
The tape can be found at [[WV Lumber Co.]], inside the underground of the office, on the cabinet beside [[Roper]].
