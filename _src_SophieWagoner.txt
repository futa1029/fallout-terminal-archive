'''Sophie Wagoner''' is a member of the new [[Responders]] in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
She comes from Kentucky. She had a rather happy childhood and was raised in a commune with other children, with adults acting as their parental figures. She considered the other children, about two dozen of them, to be her siblings. Later, she realized that collective parenting was not the norm, and considered other peoples' lack of a community family to be "pretty weird" instead. She eventually wanted to see more of the world and went to West Virginia. She heard about the revival of the Responders and signed up.

In 2104, Sophie joined the new group at the Whitespring Refuge. Sophie was put in charge of a donation drive by [[Rucker]] and became well-liked by [[Orlando]].
