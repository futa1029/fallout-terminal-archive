'''Pennington''' is a [[Mister Handy (Fallout 76)|Mister Handy]] operating in [[Appalachia]].

==Background==
Pennington is one of the many Mister Handys that serve [[Vault 76]]. It was instructed by the [[Vault 76 overseer]] to stay at the entrance of the vault and direct all Vault Dwellers to head to [[Overseer's camp|her camp]].

When [[Lacey Drummond]] and [[Isela Mejia]] arrive at the vault's entrance in [[Timeline#2103|2103]], Pennington seems to be wary of them and other "non-vault sort." It asks the player character to verify that their intentions are civil.
