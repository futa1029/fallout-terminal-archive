'''Niraj Singh''' was a member of the [[Free States]].

==Background==
Niraj moved his family to a [[Abbie's bunker|bunker]] prior to the [[Great War]]. Two years after the bombs fell, Niraj helped the Free States in rebuilding [[Harpers Ferry]] after hearing from [[Raleigh Clay]]. He was working on an air filter contraption to help with the brutal storms.<ref>[[Restoring order (holotape)|Restoring order]]</ref> However, following a seven-year effort, Harpers Ferry was overwhelmed by the [[Scorched]] and Niraj fled back to the bunker with his family.

After that, Niraj devoted his time and effort to devising ways to fight the Scorched, for which he established the [[Scorched Detection System]].<ref>[[Abbie's bunker terminal entries#Niraj's terminal|Niraj's terminal]]</ref> He worked on the system with [[Ella Ames]] and he tasked her with delivering a [[Niraj's confession|holotape]] to his daughter [[Abigayle Singh|Abigayle]] in the event of his death.

Niraj was diagnosed with Parkinson's disease and kept his illness hidden from his children.<ref>[[Niraj's confession]]</ref> Knowing that he would not live long, Niraj taught his daughter so she could finish the Scorched Detection System. Niraj eventually died.
