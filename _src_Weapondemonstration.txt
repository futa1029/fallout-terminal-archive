'''Weapon demonstration''' is a [[Steel Dawn holotapes|holotape]] in the ''[[Fallout 76]]'' [[Fallout 76 updates|update]] ''[[Steel Dawn]]''.

==Location==
The tape can be found at [[East Mountain lookout]], only while [[Supplying Demands]] is active. It is on a black mat on the ground level of the lookout, near the [[Settler (Fallout 76)|settler corpses]].
