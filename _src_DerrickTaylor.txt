'''Derrick Taylor''' is a member of the [[Settlers (Wastelanders)|Settlers]] residing in [[Foundation]].

==Background==
Derrick was born and raised in [[The Pitt (Fallout 76)|Pittsburgh]], stating his roots go back to at least four or five generations.<ref name="Pittsburgh">[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Where are you from?"''<br />Derrick: ''"Pittsburgh. Born and bred. My roots went back there four or... five? Generations. Had to leave though. It's... Just don't go north. Trust me."''<br />([[Derrick Taylor/Dialogue#248|Derrick Taylor's dialogue]])</ref> After the [[Great War]], the city fell on the hands of [[raiders]], taking many civilians as slaves, including Derrick, who worked at rebuilding the city for their enslavers.

At some point, he met a raider enforcer by the name [[Elsie Taylor|Elsie]]. Both shared their distaste at their jobs, and eventually fell in love. They wanted to leave, and after having a child, [[Davie Taylor|Davie]], as a result of an unintentional pregnancy, they orchestrated an escape. Derrick hates guns, but that day Elsie made him use one, though he never hit anything he aimed at. They managed to escape, and during the next years, Derrick and his family always kept moving, never staying on the same spot for more than a week. Elsie considered that was not the way to raise a child, but all that finally changed when they arrived at [[Foundation]].<ref>[[Elsie's story]]</ref>

Ever since settling in Foundation, Derrick has had much work as a carpenter and constructor.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Can you tell me about any of the others?"''<br />[[Ward (Wastelanders)|Ward]]: ''"I'm not a gossip. Waste of time running your mouth about other peoples problems. But I can tell you that the Taylors are good people. Derrick is a fine carpenter. And Elsie know how to handle herself in a fight."''<br />([[Ward (Wastelanders)/Dialogue#163|Ward's dialogue]])</ref> He and Elsie are highly protective of their son Davie, as he is one of the first of the post-War generation. Derrick has little to say of Pittsburgh other than that [[Appalachia]] is leagues above the former city.<ref>[[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dweller]]: ''"Is the land here really that good?"''<br />Derrick: ''"Whole stretches of country up north is just cratered. And the few fields that'll grow anything, let's just say what comes  out of the ground makes the geiger counter go crazy. Appalachia's deadly -- but at least here we stand a chance."''<br />([[Derrick Taylor/Dialogue#245|Derrick Taylor's dialogue]])</ref> If questioned about his past in the Pitt, Derrick prefers not to talk about it, advising [[Vault Dweller (Fallout 76)|Vault Dwellers]] not to go north, ever.<ref name="Pittsburgh" />

==Behind the scenes==
* Derrick is erroneously referred to as "Derrick Hale" in the ''Fallout 76'' credits. This is because Derrick's voice actor, [[Neil Kaplan]], also voiced [[Emerson Hale]].
* Derrick appears to have an interest in cooking. Elsie mentions he baked a pie. [[Penelope Hornwright]] has some cut [[Founder's Hall terminal entries|terminal entries]] after moving to Foundation in which her terminal mentions Derrick brought her a casserole.
